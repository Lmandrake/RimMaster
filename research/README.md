# research/ — material we did not author, and our summaries of it

**Tier rule: the source is somebody else's; the summary is ours.** Reference
atlases, downloaded study maps, another creator's save torn down for technique,
mod dossiers for things we have not adopted. Carries the `Jawa/` vs
`RimMandrake/` split by the owner's ruling — a Star Wars species atlas is useless
in another playthrough; a teardown of how `.rws` files are structured is not.

## Why this is not `vendor/`

`vendor/` is content **installed in the game**. `research/` is content we
**studied**. A mod we run is vendor; a mod we read about and rejected is research.

## Why this is not `design/`

Design is what *we* intend. Research is what *exists*. The moment a research
document starts saying what our campaign should do, that part has become design
and should move — leave the summary here and put the intent there.

## 🔴 Payloads are gitignored; findings are committed

The downloaded `.rws` study maps, the PDFs' bulk, the zips — ignored. **The
teardown is the work product**: what the file format turned out to be, which node
is safe to edit, which grid is opaque. That survives the artifact and is why the
artifact was worth pulling.

⚠️ **A finding whose source artifact has gone missing is still a finding.** Mark
it measured-and-unreproducible rather than deleting it; it was verified when it
was taken, and re-deriving it costs full price.

`MODLIST.md` here is **hand-authored** — mods a reader needs to make sense of this
tier's material.
