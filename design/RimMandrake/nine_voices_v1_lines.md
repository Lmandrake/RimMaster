## Law over delivery (owner rulings, this sitting)
- **UNSIGNED, always.** No line ever displays a god's name. The crew learns
  the voices or doesn't. (Owner: "Unsigned always.")
- **The narrator runs alongside**: impersonal, no persona, explaining events
  plainly (`canon.yml` narrator). Two streams, never mixed — a god line is
  never narration, narration never has a temper.
- **Register**: Jawa pidgin — dropped articles, doubled words, blunt,
  passionate, short sentences. Model line (owner's own): *"Old machine work
  again! Three owners before you. Story goes on. Right right thing."*
- v1 is **felt, not heard** (cast bible §4): these lines ride pre-authored
  letters, event text and fragments. No dialogue until LLM_INGAME_WIRING_1.
