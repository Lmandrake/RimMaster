# Faction semi-permanent bases — a trust-earned place to put your stuff

**The owner's concept**: each major faction questline can earn the player a
**semi-permanent base opportunity** — a protected place to keep things that
is not the ship. "That's a neat metric: the faction-aligned semi-permanent
base opportunity that you can use to stick your stuff other than on the
ship."

His three worked examples, verbatim in substance:

| faction trust earned | the place | the protection | Antiquities hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rust Cathedral / droids** | use of a metal cathedral where players can leave things | **droid enforcers** guard it | leaving urns there makes the gods much happier (a sanctum, not a warehouse); the shard-reconstruction compute partnership (§8.1 of the Antiquities doc) lives here too |
| **Moisture Farmers** | a secret canyon base, with a small outpost outside to protect it | the outpost garrison | — |
| **Deepwater faction** | an underwater base opportunity | **trained deep-sea creatures** | — |
| **Geonosian Foundry Hive** (RULED 2026-09-04, the Alliance arc — canon_reintegration_plan.md C1) | the Hive builds the player a powerful protected base, in trade for ship/urn technology | the Hive itself — tunnels, bio spitters, numbers | **TRAGIC AND TEMPORARY BY DESIGN**: it seems almost too good to be true, begins to work, and then the Empire catches wind and utterly destroys their settlements. "Geonosians just don't understand politics at all." |

The pattern: one earned base per major questline, each with a
faction-flavored guardian mechanism, each functioning as protected off-ship
storage (and plausibly staging, healing, or trade posture — the design pass
decides how much beyond storage these do).

## Why this matters beyond flavor — the ship-footprint concern

The owner's paired worry, recorded here so it is not lost: **the gravship
may be too big**, such that landing it dominates or ruins the map
experience. Two compensators named so far: **very large maps**, or exactly
this — trust-earned off-ship places so the ship does not have to carry (or
land) everything, everywhere. The gravship/map thread owns the size
question; this seed owns the storage answer.

## Not designed yet

No mechanics, no quest shapes, no map placements are proposed here — this
file exists so the concept survives verbatim until its design pass. When
that pass runs it should read the Antiquities doc first (two touchpoints
already exist) and the ownership-fabric ruling (protected storage overlaps
with what ownership already models).
