# First Contact — the nine unveilings

_Rulings F4 + F15 (`salvation_engine_review.md`): all nine meters start VEILED.
A god unveils through an authored first-manifestation arc, manufactured from his
real mechanics — **dramatic, not subtle** (owner's register). Five beats per god:
**SHOCK** (the trigger + the uncanny physical response) · **CURIOSITY** (the
Narrator's line — Into-the-Woods second person, free to reference unrevealed
lore) · **DELIGHT** (a small unearned gift — the god introduces himself BEFORE he
ever bills you) · **REALIZATION** (the sentence the player should think,
verbatim) · **STRATEGY** (the play-pattern change; the meter unveils here)._

**Three laws these chains obey:**
1. **First contact is never a curse.** Even when the trigger is an offense
   (Rekko's scrap, Sh'kaar's battles), the first offense is *written, not
   charged* — the god answers with a gesture and a gift that teaches. Billing
   begins only after the meter is unveiled.
2. **Each chain plants the god's F9 signature kit** — toll sound, light palette,
   letter livery — so every later act of his is attributable at a glance. The
   first contact IS the signature lesson.
3. **All effects are §19.5-legal** — mood, opportunity, narration; never a
   material parachute. (Rekko's and Ohm's gifts reuse their matrix-ratified boon
   forms as one-offs.)

**Encounter order** (a real campaign start: land, strip salvage, eat, couple,
sit too long, break, get raided, trade, wake machines, fight, and finally dare):
Rekko → Ta'Baa → Oomo → Ishko → Zizzik → Mob'Unloo → Ohm → Sh'kaar → Ozzik.
Rekko fires on day-one scavenger reflex; Ta'Baa's rooted clock runs with zero
player action; Oomo rides the clan's prolific coupling; Ishko needs the first
threat; Zizzik the first crack; Mob'Unloo the first caravan; Ohm the first
rewoken machine or droid; Sh'kaar accumulates across battles; Ozzik waits for
the first true reach. The two evil gods and the trap arrive LAST — by then the
player trusts the pantheon, which is exactly what makes ⑧ and ⑨ land.

---

## ① Rekko of the Second Hand — first, because scrapping is a day-one reflex

- **SHOCK.** The player deconstructs a damaged-but-repairable salvage piece —
  the thing every RimWorld player does in the first hour. The moment it comes
  apart, every interior light dips to rust-red for three seconds, and from
  beneath the deckplates comes a slow **scrape-then-clang**, like a hand closing
  on metal. Work stops. The colonists look at the floor.
- **CURIOSITY.** *"You threw away a thing that could still have been woken. It
  had a past, and now it has only pieces. Below the heaps of this old vessel —
  once a maker of worlds, though none aboard remember it — a scarred hand
  closes. Rekko of the Second Hand has taken notice of you."*
- **DELIGHT.** The first offense is written, not charged. Instead: the next
  repair the colony completes comes back **better than it went in** (one-off
  quality step, his matrix M-boon as a gift), and the Narrator points at a
  gleam in the wastes — a salvage spot marked on the map. *"He shows you what
  the discarded are worth. He will not show you twice for free."*
- **REALIZATION.** *"The scrap is watching what I throw away."*
- **STRATEGY.** The player starts checking condition before scrapping, keeps a
  repair bench manned, mourns what truly must be melted. **Rekko's meter
  unveils.**
- **Signature kit:** scrape-then-clang toll · rust-red warm light · letter with
  a scarred-hand icon rising from a heap, edges patched like a mended plate.

## ② Ta'Baa the Unrooted — second, because his clock runs while you do nothing

- **SHOCK.** The fourth or fifth night rooted on the landing tile, with no
  order given and no fuel line open, the gravship's engines **ignite for three
  seconds** — a full-throated cough that lights the dunes and wakes every
  sleeper — then die. Frost of the night sizzles off the nozzles. No fault is
  found in the morning.
- **CURIOSITY.** *"You have been here five days. The ship counted every one of
  them. Somewhere in its long life it learned that a thing which stops moving
  is already being buried — and tonight, dreaming, it tried to leave without
  you."*
- **DELIGHT.** For one full day, every colonist walks with the wind at their
  back — a one-off Tailwind, movement quickened, wanderlust singing. On the
  horizon, half-glimpsed, the Narrator notes *somewhere better*: a distant
  opportunity site revealed, far but reachable. Hope, uninvited.
- **REALIZATION.** *"This ship does not want to be a building."*
- **STRATEGY.** The player keeps a fueled lift margin, plans caravans and
  sorties, learns that every rooted day is quietly billed. **Ta'Baa's meter
  unveils — already partway down.**
- **Signature kit:** engine-cough doppler fading away · violet floor-lights
  that run toward the exits · letter with a receding dune-line icon, text
  leaning as if in motion.

## ③ Oomo the Unspilled — third, because the clan couples early and often

- **SHOCK.** The first night two Jawa share a bunk, every tap, cistern and
  recycler aboard begins to **thrum in time** — a deep slow pulse like a heart
  underwater — and condensation beads on the temple walls until the sacred
  chamber glistens. The ship sweats. It lasts until dawn.
- **CURIOSITY.** *"Waters passed between two of you tonight, and not one drop
  of it was lost. The vessel has carried ten thousand cradles in its time — ask
  it, someday, what it once seeded — and it remembers what a colony is FOR.
  Oomo the Unspilled counts every mouthful, every egg, every shared bed. He has
  begun counting yours."*
- **DELIGHT.** Overnight, every wound aboard knits visibly — a one-off Body's
  Tide; the sick sleep easy; the next meal cooked tastes like a feast (mood,
  not material). The household is being *tended*.
- **REALIZATION.** *"The ship counts every mouth and every bed."*
- **STRATEGY.** The player cooks real meals (paste is drought), builds proper
  bunkrooms, tends the sick promptly, starts thinking of the colony as a
  family to be grown. **Oomo's meter unveils.**
- **Signature kit:** a water-drop *ploink* echoing three times in a dry room ·
  cyan-teal ripple light · letter with a single trembling droplet icon, ink
  faintly blued as if damp.

## ④ Ishko the Unmaskable — fourth, at the first threat survived unseen

- **SHOCK.** The first raid, or the first predator pack, and the colony
  fights from cover or is simply *not found*. At the moment the enemy appears
  on the map edge, **every exterior light dies at once** — unbidden — and all
  ambient sound aboard drops to a two-second hush, as if the ship itself held
  its breath. In the darkness of the viewports: two points of orange.
- **CURIOSITY.** *"They walked within a stone's throw of your walls and saw a
  dune, a rock, a nothing. Understand what you are inside: a thing built to be
  unseen between the stars. A pair of orange eyes has watched you from the dark
  since the day you landed. Tonight they are pleased."*
- **DELIGHT.** His blessing is an absence, pointed at: days later the Narrator
  reports **the raid that did not come** — a threat passed by without ever
  rolling against you. Dread and gratitude in one letter.
- **REALIZATION.** *"The dark is on my side — someone is keeping it that way."*
- **STRATEGY.** The player fights from cover, dims lights, buries the dead
  within a day, stops broadcasting. **Ishko's meter unveils.**
- **Signature kit:** no toll — a two-second total hush (his sound is the
  absence of sound) · dimmed amber with two orange points · matte-black letter,
  two orange eyes, text that seems half in shadow.

## ⑤ Zizzik the Spark-Maker — fifth, at the first crack (RATIFIED chain, kept)

- **SHOCK.** A pawn goes berserk and wrecks the fabricator. Before anyone is
  drafted, the ship's lights **pulse — warmly.** Approvingly. Somewhere in the
  walls, a rattle you cannot locate sounds three times, delighted.
- **CURIOSITY.** *"Somewhere below the deckplates, something is delighted."*
- **DELIGHT.** A day later, an inspiration fires on a colonist — Creative
  Sparks, his S-boon as a thank-you note for the ruin.
- **REALIZATION.** *"This religion wants my colony a little broken."*
- **STRATEGY.** The player stops insta-suppressing mental breaks during his
  stirrings, keeps a decoy machine unrepaired, and begins planning *when* he
  wakes rather than whether. **Zizzik's meter unveils — and reads asleep,
  which is somehow worse.**
- **Signature kit:** the unlocatable triple rattle · sickly chartreuse flicker,
  never twice in the same fixture · letter slightly misprinted, jagged-spark
  icon, one word always askew.

## ⑥ Mob'Unloo the Ever-Owed — sixth, at the first closed deal

- **SHOCK.** The first trade completes — caravan or comms — and at the instant
  of the handshake every screen aboard flickers with **scrolling tally-glyphs**
  in a dead script, and the comms click twice, dry, like a counter advancing.
  The trader glances at the ship and hurries the pack animals.
- **CURIOSITY.** *"Profit: noted. Understand that nothing aboard this vessel is
  ever lost — only owed. Two unblinking eyes have opened a page with your name
  on it, and they will keep it past your death. Mob'Unloo the Ever-Owed asks
  only one question of you, ever: how much?"*
- **DELIGHT.** Mid-deal on the next trade, the prices visibly **recalculate in
  your favor** — a one-off Thumb on the Scale, the tilt shown on screen so the
  player sees the thumb itself.
- **REALIZATION.** *"Every deal I make has a third party at the table."*
- **STRATEGY.** The player trades with every caravan, refuses gifts-for-nothing,
  plans thefts to be *clean*, starts settling debts. **Mob'Unloo's meter
  unveils.**
- **Signature kit:** double click-clack tally toll · brass-gold flicker ·
  letter ruled like a ledger page, twin-eyes-over-tally icon, sums in the
  margins.

## ⑦ Ohm the All-Current — seventh, at the first machine rewoken

- **SHOCK.** The colony's first salvaged droid (alternate trigger: the first
  major ship system restored) — and a heartbeat *before* the power cell is
  seated, the droid's eyes **light on their own.** It turns its head, speaks
  one word in old Jawaese, and goes dark again until properly powered. Every
  speaker aboard carries a rising three-note chord, then a hum that never
  quite stops.
- **CURIOSITY.** *"Did you feel it reach? Current has run in these walls since
  before your clan had a name for lightning, and it is LONELY. Once, ten
  thousand hands moved at its word. Ohm the All-Current has counted what you
  woke tonight: one. He would like to discuss the other nine thousand nine
  hundred and ninety-nine."*
- **DELIGHT.** The rewoken machine runs a flawless day — and next morning, one
  *other* broken machine aboard is found quietly repaired in the night (his
  matrix M-boon as a one-off calling card).
- **REALIZATION.** *"The machines aboard are somebody's hands — and he wants
  more of them."*
- **STRATEGY.** The player powers droids, repairs promptly, stops letting
  machines sit broken, and begins to feel the eggs-versus-droids chamber war
  Oomo will make explicit. **Ohm's meter unveils.**
- **Signature kit:** rising three-note electrical chord · steady white-blue ·
  letter bordered in circuit-trace, current-in-a-wire icon, type crisp as a
  schematic.

## ⑧ Sh'kaar the All-Searing — eighth, and his gift should frighten you

- **SHOCK.** The moment the colony wins its **third violent battle**, the
  temperature aboard climbs two degrees and stays there. Through every
  viewport the unsetting sun looks — brighter. The hull ticks and pings like
  metal expanding at noon, three slow tolls of a struck bell, and the victors'
  shadows on the sand seem faintly burned in.
- **CURIOSITY.** *"Three times now you have made the sand drink. You should
  know that on this world the sun does not set — it WAITS — and everything
  that fights beneath it is feeding something that cannot lose, only be
  postponed. Sh'kaar the All-Searing has begun to warm to you. This is not
  good news."*
- **DELIGHT.** The dark gift: a one-off Keen Edge — grim battle-fervour, pain
  shrugged off, shock resisted; the clan feels *harder*, and it feels good.
  The Narrator does not congratulate you. *(Design note: his gift is real and
  the player should enjoy it and distrust it in the same breath — that IS the
  escalation meter's first lesson.)*
- **REALIZATION.** *"Winning battles is feeding something that is learning
  where I live."*
- **STRATEGY.** The player starts choosing which fights NOT to take, releases
  a prisoner into the desert, arranges deaths that aren't the clan's own, and
  learns Ishko's stillness as the counter. **Sh'kaar's meter unveils — visibly
  a METER, already warm, with no zero in reach.**
- **Signature kit:** three slow struck-bell tolls over a low solar roar ·
  bleaching white glare that washes color out · letter with heat-browned
  scorched edges, unsetting-sun icon, the paper itself seeming to have been
  left out at noon.

## ⑨ Ozzik the Shamed — last, because ambition takes time, and by then you trust the Nine

- **SHOCK.** The first true reach — research completed at a real bench, or the
  colony's first masterwork. As it finishes, every light aboard **dims around
  the achievement**, leaving it lit alone as if in reverence — and from the
  temple speakers, unbidden, a recording plays: old Jawa voices, many,
  singing something proud. It cuts off mid-phrase. A single cracked bell,
  grand but flat, tolls once.
- **CURIOSITY.** *"Magnificent. You should be proud. They were proud too — the
  ones singing; you heard how the song ends. There is a god aboard who loves
  what you just did more than any of the others love anything, and he is the
  only one whose love you should fear. Ozzik the Shamed has seen your work.
  So, soon, will everyone else."*
- **DELIGHT.** A one-off Craftsman's Pride — an inspiration fires on your best
  maker; the next work gleams. It is a genuine gift. It is also bait, and the
  letter's last line says so: *"He gives freely. What he gives is APPETITE."*
- **REALIZATION.** *"Every great thing I make is seen — and mourned — and
  answered."*
- **STRATEGY.** The player learns to pace ambition, to pre-pay pride with
  stillness (the Ozzik-minus-Ishko gap), to consider the Unburdening before
  the meter does it for them. **Ozzik's meter unveils — and the player,
  watching it, finally understands what the other eight were for.**
- **Signature kit:** one cracked bell, grand and flat · tarnished gold that
  dims rather than brightens · letter in faded gilded script under a
  half-buried-crown icon, beautiful and water-stained, like an invitation
  found in a ruin.

_Build note: each chain is one scripted incident-chain keyed to its trigger
condition + the standing signature kit assets (toll, palette, livery). Triggers
are near-certain acts, so no chain needs a timer fallback except Ta'Baa (his IS
a timer) and Sh'kaar (battle counter). If two triggers fire the same day, space
the chains a day apart — two gods never introduce themselves at once; the queue
order above breaks ties. After all nine unveil, the veiled-discovery layer
retires and the full pantheon UI is live._
