# The Devotional Sacrifice Catalog — sacrifice-as-play (F11 ruling)

_Provenance: owner card-session 2026-08-30, F11 (`salvation_engine_review.md`):
competent play must have its own pious channel — not merely denying the gods
their grievances, but FEEDING them deliberately, at a real price, for a real
say in when and how they move. Every devotion below is §19.5-legal (moves
satiation/clocks/moods/events, never material) and SIGNED per F9 (the god
acknowledges receipt with his own tell — the player always knows it landed).
A devotion is voluntary, always available, and always costs something the
colony would rather keep. That is what makes it devotion and not exploit._

**Format per devotion:** NAME — ACT (concrete RimWorld verbs) · COST (what you
truly give up) · BUYS (which meter moves) · SIGN (the F9 receipt).

---

## ⑦ Zizzik the Spark-Maker — first and richest (the owner's exemplars kept)

_The competent colony starves him, and starving him only BANKS the wake. These
are the ways a well-run clan pays the entropy tax on its own schedule._

- **The Weathered Cell** — ACT: haul a CHARGED battery outdoors, unroof it,
  forbid it, and wait for weather; the short is the offering (the owner's
  exemplar). · COST: the battery and every Wd in it. · BUYS: shrinks the
  slumber bank a notch; small satiation feed; the wake, when it comes, is
  gentler. · SIGN: the arc flashes twice more than physics needs, and the
  rattle-you-can-never-locate falls silent for a day.
- **The Open Latch** — ACT: arrange a prisoner's "escape" — door left unlocked,
  guard schedule cleared (the owner's exemplar: intentionally letting someone
  go). A plan deliberately allowed to come apart. · COST: the recruit or
  ransom that walks away; he may return armed. · BUYS: bank shrink (a
  betrayal witnessed and savored); brief Mood lift. · SIGN: every door on the
  ship clicks its latch once, in sequence, stern to bow.
- **The Honored Break** — ACT: when a minor mental break fires, declare it
  honored — no arrest, no draft, no suppression; the break runs its full
  course (rite-toggle on the break event). · COST: the tantrum's damage, the
  binge's stores, the lost day. · BUYS: bank shrink scaled to what the break
  cost you; taboo-proof during his reign. · SIGN: the wrecked room's lamp
  flickers in his palette as the pawn comes to.
- **The Gift of Working Things** — ACT: the burnt offering formalized — select
  one FUNCTIONING machine and destroy it at the shrine-heart (matrix demand M,
  now always available). · COST: the machine, and Rekko's grief (a small
  `↓Rekko` rides every gift — feed one god, bruise another). · BUYS: the
  largest single bank payment available outside a full rite. · SIGN: the
  smoke curls against the ventilation draft.

## ① Ishko the Unmaskable

- **The Deep Berth** — ACT: bury EVERY corpse the map produces, raider and kin
  alike, in real graves within a day (burial designation; no burning, no
  rot). · COST: labor, graves, the corpses' leather and loot-time. · BUYS:
  steady satiation drip; detection-clock kindness. · SIGN: grave dirt settles
  overnight as if patted down by a hand no one saw.
- **The Hour of Stillness** — ACT: colony-wide stand-down — all pawns
  scheduled to rest or meditate, outdoor lights doused, one full hour, no
  work (matrix demand M as a devotion at will). · COST: an hour of every
  hand. · BUYS: satiation; a nudge down on visibility. · SIGN: a pair of
  orange eyes glimpsed in an unlit doorway, gone when looked at.
- **The Passed Cup** — ACT: let a hostile patrol or caravan cross the map
  UNENGAGED — no shot, no trap sprung, watched from dark cover the whole
  way. · COST: their loot, their gear, the safe kill forgone. · BUYS: large
  satiation (a threat that passes undetected is his sacrament); Sh'kaar's
  meter untouched — the fight that never happened. · SIGN: the Narrator
  counts their steps aloud, softly, until they are gone.

## ② Ohm the All-Current

- **The Idle Made Whole** — ACT: repair a machine you will never use — the
  ruin's dead autodoor at the map edge, the wreck's stripped console —
  repaired, powered if possible, and left. · COST: components and a
  builder's day spent on nothing useful. · BUYS: satiation (mercy shown to
  machines with no profit in it is the purest current). · SIGN: the repaired
  thing's light blinks once at dusk, aimed at no one.
- **The Choir Hour** — ACT: gather every droid, fully charged, idle in the
  shrine-heart for one hour — no tasks, power flowing, the hum shared
  (matrix demand L made a devotion). · COST: an hour of droid labor and the
  charge burned. · BUYS: satiation; his Mood steadies for days. · SIGN: the
  droids' idle chirps fall into unison for three beats.
- **The Day of Current** — ACT: one full day with every machine powered and
  no circuit browned out — declared in advance, held. · COST: fuel/Wd run
  hot all day; no austerity switching. · BUYS: satiation drip; breakdown
  luck kind while it holds. · SIGN: the ship's running lights ride a shade
  brighter than their setting.

## ③ Oomo the Unspilled

- **The Nursed Stranger** — ACT: take in a sick or wounded OUTSIDER (visitor,
  downed raider, refugee) and tend him to full health — meds, bed, the
  nurse's hours — then let him go. · COST: medicine, bed-space, the risk and
  the ransom forgone. · BUYS: large satiation (tending a fouled body's
  waters is his sacred work, doubled when nothing is owed you for it). ·
  SIGN: condensation beads on the water tanks though the air is dry.
- **The Open Table** — ACT: a feast where EVERY mouth on the map eats fine or
  lavish — colonists, slaves, prisoners, guests, no paste anywhere (matrix
  demand M, widened). · COST: the pantry, gutted. · BUYS: satiation; his
  curse-tier steps down one band if any is active. · SIGN: every bowl is
  found licked clean, even the ones no one finished.
- **The Overpaid Kin** — ACT: ransom a Jawa slave and pay MORE than asked —
  deliberately, publicly. · COST: the premium in silver; `↓Mob'Unloo` small
  (a bad deal on purpose — feed one god, bruise another). · BUYS: large
  satiation; the rescue imperative honored beyond duty. · SIGN: the freed
  kin's first drink aboard never seems to empty the cup.

## ④ Mob'Unloo the Ever-Owed

- **The Named Debt** — ACT: the rite names an old wrong the clan did a
  faction — then silver goes to them as PAYMENT, not gift (goodwill
  purchase, reframed and priced by the rite first; an unnamed gift remains
  his taboo). · COST: the silver. · BUYS: satiation; a haunting eases if the
  debt was a dead man's. · SIGN: a coin-chime rides the comms static at the
  moment it lands.
- **The God's Account** — ACT: seal a stockpile in his name — trade goods
  locked, never sold, never used, growing only (forbidden stockpile zone,
  audited at each rite). · COST: standing capital frozen out of every deal
  you'll ever want it for. · BUYS: satiation drip scaled to the account's
  worth; the Ledger Closes curse cannot touch a clan that banks with him. ·
  SIGN: the tally he keeps is heard, faintly, being written.
- **The Collected Grudge** — ACT: avenge a theft-from-us — raid or ransom the
  thief faction until equivalent value returns. · COST: the sortie's risk,
  Sh'kaar's meter fed by any fight it takes. · BUYS: large satiation (an
  unavenged theft is the wound that never closes). · SIGN: the recovered
  goods weigh in a fraction over what was lost.

## ⑤ Rekko of the Second Hand

- **The Woken Sleeper** — ACT: haul one ruined find to FULL repair and then
  install or wear it — give it a home, not a shelf (matrix tithe, at
  will). · COST: materials and hours on a thing you didn't need. · BUYS:
  satiation; deterioration kindness colony-wide for a span. · SIGN: the
  woken thing runs one shade better than its stats say it should.
- **The Mourning** — ACT: when scrapping is truly unavoidable, hold the small
  rite FIRST — the machine's funeral; only then deconstruct. · COST: the
  gathered hour; the scrap yields no faster. · BUYS: converts the scrap's
  `↓Rekko` to neutral — permitted grief instead of murder. · SIGN: the
  deconstruction sparks fall slow, like ash.
- **The Second Skin** — ACT: a pawn wears salvaged, tattered apparel as
  standing devotion despite the mood hit. · COST: that pawn's mood, every
  day. · BUYS: small steady satiation; the pawn is his — Rekko's boons find
  that pawn first. · SIGN: the tatters never quite finish falling apart.

## ⑥ Ta'Baa the Unrooted

- **The Left Behind** — ACT: at launch, deliberately leave one VALUABLE thing
  on the old map — chosen, named at the launch-rite, abandoned. · COST: the
  thing, forever. · BUYS: the launch spike enlarged; the next rooted-clock
  runs slower (you have proven you can let go). · SIGN: from the climbing
  ship, the left thing glints once, like a wave.
- **The Rehearsal** — ACT: a fuel-burning liftoff-and-return with no
  destination — the engines proven, the clan drilled. · COST: the fuel,
  wholly. · BUYS: satiation; despair thresholds ease (hope is his gift and
  readiness is hope made solid). · SIGN: the landing kicks up dust in the
  shape of the dune-line receding.
- **The Far Walker** — ACT: send one pawn on a long caravan loop that trades
  nothing and fetches nothing — out, around, home. The road kept alive. ·
  COST: the pawn's absence and rations. · BUYS: satiation drip while the
  walker walks; rooted-clock pauses for the duration. · SIGN: the walker
  returns with sand in his boots from a dune no map shows.

## ⑧ Sh'kaar the All-Searing — _the only calming verbs are deaths not your own_

- **The Kindled War** — ACT: cause a battle you do not join — lure manhunters
  into a raider camp, mortar-bait two enemies together, wake the hive under
  their feet. · COST: shells, bait, the risk of it turning home. · BUYS: the
  escalation meter drops per death he watches; his gaze feasts elsewhere. ·
  SIGN: the heat-shimmer on the horizon bends toward the far killing.
- **The Released** — ACT: a prisoner released into the open desert, unarmed,
  at noon (matrix calming verb, formalized as rite). His fate is the sun's. ·
  COST: recruit or ransom forgone; `↓Oomo` faint (a life poured out). ·
  BUYS: meter relief without a fight; doom-unease eases. · SIGN: the glare
  off the sand goes amber for the walking figure, and only for him.
- **The Feast of the Unowned** — ACT: the prisoner death-match (matrix lever;
  cataloged, never softened). · COST: the prisoners; `▲Zizzik` small;
  `↓Oomo` faint. The clan's hands stay clean and are not clean. · BUYS: the
  largest meter drop short of true war; a reprieve bought with atrocity. ·
  SIGN: the arena lights burn white through the bout and will not dim until
  dawn.

## ⑨ Ozzik the Shamed

- **The Unburdening** — ACT: the potlatch (F13 ruling) — wealth given away or
  ritually destroyed before witnesses; the pride-meter vented on purpose. ·
  COST: the wealth itself; `↓Mob'Unloo` (he hates every coin of it). ·
  BUYS: pride-meter vents; the Sh'kaar/Zizzik exposure bias eases. · SIGN:
  the tarnished crown, wherever his icon stands, sheds one flake of rust.
- **The Named Grief** — ACT: display one relic of the fallen age (Rakatan or
  ancient work, restored per the grief-valve) and hold remembrance before
  it. · COST: the hall it demands, the gathered hour. · BUYS: grief-side
  satiation with NO pride spike; the grief-pall eases when the clan lives
  small. · SIGN: for a breath, every pawn present stands straighter, and no
  one saw them do it.
- **The Dedication** — ACT: finish a great work IN ANOTHER GOD'S NAME —
  declared at start, sealed at completion (F13 ruling; capped once per
  reign). · COST: Ozzik's full credit forgone; a sliver of his insult
  banked. · BUYS: the satiation routes to the dedicatee; the pride-meter
  takes only a sliver. · SIGN: two gods' palettes share the lights for one
  evening — the dedicatee's warm, and his, thin beneath it.

_The disposable shrine-as-offering — a structure built to be walked away from,
valued by its summed work-hours — is a rite of intercession, not of one god's
devotion: it lives in `god_intercession_spec.md` (GOD_INTERCESSION_SPEC_1)._

_Design note: every devotion that bruises a second god does so on purpose —
the catalog obeys "no act is clean" (§2.0d). The player who devotes widely is
still choosing whom to owe._
