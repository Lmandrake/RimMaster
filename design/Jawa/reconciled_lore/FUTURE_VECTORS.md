# Future vectors — where v2 wants to go

Distilled from `design/V2_DREAMS.md` and
`research/Jawa/Star_Wars_RimWorld_Mod_Concepts.md`, aligned with what 2026-08-29
already started. The dream texts' design principle governs everything here:
**not one-to-one substitutions** (droid = colonist, sand = yellow terrain) but
different KINDS of thing — *droids are a different kind of agent, sarlaccs are
living locations, salvage is an economy, sand is an active medium* — so the
simulation produces Star Wars-shaped stories on its own.

## Already in motion (the vectors 2026-08-29 opened)

1. **Droidbrain → Droidworks.** The §28 flagship is no longer a dream: the
   platform is being built (`08_droids.md`). Its natural v2 tail: personality
   drift, the module-personality layer, wild-droid seek-a-master behavior, and
   the **Repair Shop quest pack** (visitors with broken droids, diagnosis,
   reputation — ruled a pack on top, not platform).
2. **The dungeons arc.** Assailant flesh dungeon + Forsaken vault design with
   the owner; the Sarlacc (confirmed buildable pit-gate rebrand) and They! ant
   nests slot in behind them as living-location dungeons. Build spec (site,
   thaw-gate, content palette, KCSG route, six-vault siting+grammar):
   `worldbuilding/dungeons_arc_spec.md` — creative lock-in still owed with the
   owner per this line.
3. **Research tree normalization** — the full-tree pass after droids land.

## The big standalone candidates (dream-text §28, judged against today)

- **The Living Desert** — burrowing ecology, sand swimming, thumpers, desert
  leviathans, night desert, weather with teeth. Pairs with the VAST-tier
  precedent already in-stack (the Leviathans sandworm: a world object with
  weather and music attached, not a spawn).
- **Junk Is Civilization** — salvage ontology, component provenance,
  jury-rigging. Much of its spirit already ships (the economy, Rekko); the v2
  piece is provenance and the salvage-built blaster tier (Makeshift's
  unreliable-burst verb reskinned to scrap blasters).
- **The Cantina Kitchen / Galactic Palates** — Star Wars food is ANIMAL food;
  live storage as a building; the same dish reading three ways by faith. The
  Jawa meet it in Hutt and Deepwater settlements rather than owning it. Art
  already exists (160 drawn creatures).
- **Galactic Underworld Reputation** — debts, favors, bounties, Hutt political
  economy; the natural deepening of the ledger pressure.
- **The Desert Below / Shipwreck Archaeology** — persistent subterranean
  layers; buried settlements; planetary deep history (the vaults already point
  down).
- **Holo-Deception** — projection and tactical misdirection; ties to the ghost
  layer the ship already carries.
- **Legend Generation / Recurring Characters / Cinematic Failure** — the
  Inhabited mod's memory layer is the substrate these want.

## Standing constraints on every vector

No worldgen, ever. No player psycast ceiling. Anti-exponential and §19.5 bind
v2 as hard as v1. A dream that needs a new world (giant-ant faction ticked at
creation; anything ideo-baked) must be decided AT the world screen or wait for
a fresh campaign — the freeze is a savegame.
