# The clan — Jawa society, The Salvation, and the nine gods

## The species

**`MandrakeJawa` is the only active Jawa xenotype** [owner 2026-08-14; measured
2026-08-19 — it strictly contains what the alternatives offered]. Small, hooded,
ink-black skin, glowing eyes, heat-comfortable to 46 °C, dark-vision, weak in
melee, skittish, all-male.

**Reproduction**: all-male, homosexual, egg-laying. Every Jawa carries both
organs (`SEX_AlwaysAphrodor` — Intimacy's ovipositor route is what actually
works; `Outland_EggLayer` survives as icon only [measured 2026-08-22]); any
Jawa can impregnate any other, and the one impregnated lays the egg. Fast
growth, rapid aging; children learn by shadowing working adults — no
schoolrooms.

**🔴 The Cold Nursery** [owner 2026-08-22]: a laid egg ruins above **32 °C**,
and adults are comfortable to 46 °C — a fourteen-degree window in which the
clan thrives while its own clutch cooks. **The clan cannot reproduce in the
open on its own homeworld.** Deep burrows, sandcrawler holds, or refrigerated
egg chambers are a biological necessity — a design pillar, not a bug. Do not
"fix" the 32 °C ceiling.

## Society

- **The clan does not recruit.** Membership is hatched, or earned by a slave
  through love (the love-gate — romance is the naturalization ceremony). Wim
  Ateeka is the living precedent.
- **Slavery is core and load-bearing.** Non-Jawa slaves are inventory — worked,
  then sold (the Hutts are the buyer). Jawa slaves are NEVER sold: kin are
  bought back at any price (*"a Jawa in chains is a debt on the whole clan"*),
  held with Jawa, love-gated in or kept. **The Jawa trade slaves; the Hutts
  keep them** — same institution, opposite relationship, neither is the good
  one [owner 2026-08-19]. Precepts ratified as-is: Slavery Acceptable + alien
  slavery Honorable; "sell swiftly" is deliberate ROLEPLAY, unenforced.
- **The breeding colony** [owner 2026-08-07]: unlimited-partner polyamory with
  **jealousy ON** — chaos, not harmony; the churn is the feature. Births are
  intentionally unbounded; the counterweights are a hard active-clan cap
  (~5–7 aboard; overflow fosters out to outposts), rapid aging and death, and
  edge-case honorable exile of the spent. Crew churn is purely demographic.
- **The mood economy**: any Jawa's death grieves the clan (even an enemy's,
  scaled); every Jawa acquired is a celebration. Bespoke ThoughtDefs, still
  hand-authored work.
- **Materiel doctrine — three axes, never conflated**: LOW QUALITY is tolerated
  as doctrine (proud to run junk); TAINTED is ignored entirely (scoped to the
  Jawa layer, not colony-wide); DISREPAIR is the opposite — it *bothers* them
  (worn gear auto-repairs via the adopted mod; unused gear simply isn't). That
  triad is what makes them tinkers, not hoarders.
- Never-Nudes: the body is covered; mating happens in total darkness (Ishko).
- Pets: MANY strange gross little creatures underfoot — pets, not herds; no
  creature becomes a meat/wealth printer.

## The nine gods

`divine_satiation_engine.md` (the Pantheon section; names LOCKED 2026-08-08).
Mechanics (satiation/Mood vectors, ritual participation bias, the council, the

| god | domain | the short of it |

| ① **Ishko the Unmaskable** | hiding, ambush, stillness | orange eyes in the dark; ranged-from-cover is pious, melee is exposure; death is the deepest hiding; never punishes a skipped rite |
| ② **Ohm the All-Current** | the living machine | resident of the ship; believes he IS the ship; lonely for his lost hands (droids); the Oracle who reports the other gods |

| ④ **Mob'Unloo the Ever-Owed** | trade, debt, bonds | ends ARE the ethics; successful theft is the perfect deal; being CAUGHT is the sin |
| ⑤ **Rekko of the Second Hand** | salvage, repair | scrapping the repairable is tragedy to be mourned; theft gives a thing a better home; restore ≠ transcend |
| ⑥ **Ta'Baa the Unrooted** | flight, hope | the launch is the holiest rite; despair is his one blasphemy; explosives are a door held open behind you |
| ⑦ **Zizzik the Spark-Maker** | malfunction, betrayal, breaks | inverted; starved he SLUMBERS and waking is inevitable — manage when, not whether |
| ⑧ **Sh'kaar the All-Searing** | the killing light, inevitability | EVIL; every battle wakes him hungrier (the escalation meter); calmed by a death that isn't yours |
| ⑨ **Ozzik the Shamed** | ambition, pride, grief | THE TRAP: you must court him to advance and his pleasure is exposure — high Ozzik feeds ⑦ and ⑧; his grief is the memory of a prouder Jawa age |

System properties that no single god states:
- **No act is clean** — the faith is a tug-of-war; the Council argues, never
  announces.
- **Four pressure-clocks** (Zizzik's slumber, Sh'kaar's escalation, Ta'Baa's
  rooted-erosion, Ozzik's pride-meter) are the anti-exponential pillar with no
  resource cap.
- **The prisoner pipeline**: one captive, five gods collecting differently.
- **The centre of gravity**: raid-and-return-to-darkness — hidden AND ready to
  flee, the only posture Ishko and Ta'Baa both bless; everything else is a
  rival program for the ship's future.
- **The win-path map**: Ohm+Sh'kaar → droid-army-by-force; Mob'Unloo+Ozzik →
  coalition/commerce (the Hutt-shaped road); Ishko+Ta'Baa+Oomo+Rekko → the
  humble survival bloc. Rekko⇄Ozzik (restore vs transcend) is the sharpest
  internal war. **There is no ambitious victory that doesn't wake Ozzik — the
  endgame IS the pride-crisis, by design.** Rituals are event-triggered and
  non-negotiable (the Principal's office); pleased gods speak often, angry ones
  rarely, neutral never — skipping rites sours everyone but Ishko.

Weapon doctrine from the faith: **ion over explosives** — Ozzik loves the
disable-and-take, despises the loud folly that destroys what could be taken;
personal ion is the Jawa identity itself (`07_physics_and_arms.md`).
