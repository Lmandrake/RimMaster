## 2. Jawa trap-sense — "I will profit from your preparations" (owner ruling)

Jawa are built paranoid and trap-minded: **they SEE enemy traps** (revealed on any
map they walk, no scan minigame) **and get a large disarm bonus** — and a disarmed
trap yields its components as salvage (the profit line). Mechanization options, one
to rule:

- **Option A — xenotype gene** (`Jawa_TrapSense`): always on, species-deep,
  works modless-simple. Cost: it's a flat racial, no theology in it.
- **Option B — god's blessing**: baseline modest bonus; FULL sight/disarm while
  Ishko is content or fronting (he shows his own kind of danger to his own kind
  of people). Theology-rich; cost: sometimes you step on a mine because your god
  sulked — reads unfair unless well-signed.
- **Option C — both, layered — ✅ RULED (owner, 2026-08-30)**: the gene gives sight + a solid
  disarm bonus always (paranoia is biology); Ishko's favor upgrades disarm speed
  and yield ("the dark shows its teeth to its own"). Mob'Unloo books each
  disarmed-trap salvage as a small `↑` (profit from another's preparation is the
  perfect deal's little brother).

## 2b. The light minify — stealing the unstealable

Jawa can uninstall and carry off an **unusually large set of otherwise
unstealable/unminifiable buildings** — as LOOT. The hard line (owner): **making
things super-strong via minify is not cool** — this is steal-to-salvage/sell/
re-home, never a mobility exploit for turrets/defenses in combat.

- Mechanism: a Jawa-only work type ("strip it") that converts listed unminifiable
  buildings into a minified item or a rich salvage bundle; long work time, needs
  the map pacified or the theft unseen (Ishko: get away clean).
- Bounds: a curated whitelist (production benches, mod machines, art, ship
  fittings), an explicit denylist (turrets and anything that would fight for you
  where it lands — re-installation of denylisted stolen goods is disabled;
  they exist to be sold, gifted, or fed to Rekko's restoration).
- Theology: `↑Rekko` (a better home) + `↑Mob'Unloo` (something for nothing) on a
  clean take; the §8b theft row already prices getting caught.

## 4. The build tier — the vacant register

**No snare, pitfall, tripwire, or caltrop exists in 584 mods.** The primitive
cunning register is ours alone.

- **a. Snare & tripwire family**: scrap-cheap, Rekko-priced (built FROM salvage),
  low damage but effects — slow, entangle, alarm (a tripwire that *tells you*
  beats one that hurts them; Ishko's early warning made of wire).
- **b. The pit family** — trigger on MASS, hold by BODYSIZE/HEALTH; 1x1/1x2/2x2
  covered variants + the **gated prisoner pit** (dug fast by Jawa or droids; the
  canon holding pen — a scavenger clan digs a hole, it does not build locked
  barracks on the sacred ship). **Full spec: `COVERED_PIT_TRAPS_1`** (filed on
  the owner's word, same sitting). Prior art note: the old "prisoner pit" mod
  concept was great fun and poorly written — we take the concept, not the code.
- **c. The droid-release trap**: Odyssey's `Building_TrapRelease*` precedent —
  a sprung trap that deploys a salvaged combat droid. Ohm and Ishko in one
  building: the hidden hand that is literally a hand.

## 5. Theology wiring (rows to land in §8b + matrix on ruling)

- **A kill by trap, no Jawa seen** → `↑Ishko` (large — purer than the ambush
  shot: the hand was never even present), no Sh'kaar meter feed (he was denied
  a battle).
- **A trap MISFIRES / springs on a colonist or ally** → `▲Zizzik` (the wrong
  spark in your own cunning).
- **Explosive trap kill** → the canon split: `↑Ta'Baa` (the door held open),
  `↓Ozzik` (the loud folly), `▲Sh'kaar` small (fire is fire).
- **Ion/EMP/net/gas capture-take** → `↑Ozzik` (disable-and-take), `↑Mob'Unloo`
  (value seized), `↑Ohm` when the take is a droid.
- **Disarming an enemy trap** → `↑Ishko` small + `↑Mob'Unloo` small (§2).
- **A raid broken by traps alone** (no shot fired) → a Council-worthy deed;
  candidate first-contact accelerant for Ishko.

## 6. Anti-frustration pass (where the un-fun dies)

1. **Jawa never trip their own traps** — it is their craft (allies/visitors still
   can: comedy and Zizzik both feed).
2. **Rearm automation** via research: a rearm work-giver with priority, and the
   HAZN bait-loop pattern generalized.
3. **Preparation beats memory-cheese**: tune/patch raider trap-avoidance so a
   *prepared dark* (unlit, concealed, Jawa-built) kill-zone is not path-avoided
   into irrelevance; visible daylight traps still are. (The Visibility stat is
   the natural hook when COLONY_VISIBILITY_STAT_1 lands.)
4. **Trap ROI scales**: the primitive tier is cheap enough to carpet; the
   capture tier pays in prisoners, not damage — so trap value tracks raid size
   instead of being outscaled by it.
