# Intercession — managing the gods against each other

_Owner ruling F13 (`salvation_engine_review.md`): "you must socially manage them
against each other, just like negotiating trades in a complex environment.
Don't manage them head-on: you will lose or grow oppressed." This spec makes
that a mechanic: **the shield you earn from one god against another**, and the
**disposable shrine** — the offering you build and walk away from. §19.5 holds
throughout: intercession buys standing, protection, and clock relief — never
material._

---

## 1. The shield

### 1a. Earning it — a relationship, never a transaction

A god is a **willing intercessor** for the clan only while BOTH hold:

- **Standing:** his satiation is Content or better (Exalted intercedes eagerly,
  Content occasionally). A Neutral god watches; a Slighted god enjoys the show.
- **Recency:** at least one deed+ in his domain inside the current reign or the
  one before. His favor must be *warm*, not archival — a god remembers who has
  been feeding him lately, not who fed him last winter.

🔴 **The begging rule (canon: "the one who begs has thrown away his hands").**
Offerings made *after a curse has begun to toll* (the F9 foreboding signature)
count for **nothing** — worse, they are begging: a small offense to the god
being supplicated AND to the god already angry. There is no panic-button. The
shield is built in good weather or not at all. This is the single rule that
keeps intercession a strategy rather than a health potion.

### 1b. Firing

When a curse (M or L) is rolled and would land:

1. The engine checks the **intercession table** (§2) for gods positioned
   against the offended god.
2. Among eligible intercessors (standing + recency, §1a), the one with highest
   effective favor may step in — probability by band (Exalted ~likely,
   Content ~occasional; tune at build).
3. **Effect:** the curse is cancelled (Exalted intercessor) or downgraded one
   tier (Content). It is *replaced* by the intercessor's **warning-look
   signature moment**: the offended god's toll begins… and the lights shift to
   the intercessor's palette; one Narrator line, e.g. — *"Sh'kaar's glare
   gathers on the ridge — but the dark thickens, and a pair of orange eyes
   opens between you and the light. Ishko says nothing. He is looking at
   you."*
4. **A debt opens to the intercessor.** His next demand arrives one tier
   heavier, named in the letter, due within a window. Paid → the relationship
   deepens (small satiation gift). Unpaid → *he* sours by the debt's size, and
   he will not step in again this reign. The shield is a loan, not a gift.

### 1c. Limits

- **Allies do not cross.** A god never intercedes against a canonical ally
  (Zizzik–Sh'kaar feed together; Mob'Unloo–Rekko love the same theft;
  Ohm–Ozzik keep the ion accord). Table in §2 is exhaustive.
- **Patience.** Each intercession inside a rolling window lowers that god's
  willingness (he is a neighbor doing you a favor, not a bodyguard). The third
  ask in quick succession fails with his own displeasure — you have made him a
  servant, and no god is that.
- **The evil gods are targets, never shields.** Zizzik and Sh'kaar can be
  interceded *against*; neither can be recruited. There is no bargaining the
  wrong spark onto your side.
- **Ozzik intercedes — and his shield is the trap.** Statecraft is his
  instrument; brokering peace between gods is exactly the game of Empire he
  loves. But every Ozzik intercession feeds the pride-meter ("saved by
  cleverness" is still pride), so his protection *is* exposure on layaway. The
  cheapest shield in the pantheon, and the most expensive.
- **One voice per curse.** No stacking; the Council does not pile on mercy.

## 2. The intercession table

_Derived strictly from each god's canon block ("how he regards the others").
Format: offended god → who may shield the clan from him, and the argument the
intercessor makes; then who never will._

| Offended | May intercede | The argument he makes | Never — and why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **① Ishko** | Ta'Baa; Oomo | "They are already gone — the seen camp is an empty camp" (movement moots exposure); "the family shelters its own" | Sh'kaar (hates him; wants the clan seen) |
| **② Ohm** | Rekko; Ozzik | "The broken are mourned, not abandoned — give them time to mend it"; "grander machines are coming — be patient, All-Current" (the ion accord) | Oomo (the chamber-war: eggs vs droids) |
| **③ Oomo** | Mob'Unloo; Rekko | "Book the refused waters as waters *owed* — the ledger holds what the heart dropped"; "need excuses, grief pays" (his mercy-for-necessity generalized) | Ishko, Ta'Baa (both want the family small — they will not plead for its increase) |
| **④ Mob'Unloo** | Oomo; Rekko | "The bond is worth more than its price — let this one stand unpaid"; "the thing found a better home; call the loss a gift" (the theft alliance, inverted) | none structural — but he *charges* whoever intercedes (a debt transferred, not forgiven) |
| **⑤ Rekko** | Ta'Baa; Mob'Unloo | "They could not carry it — flight excuses the scrapping" (the pillar-brothers); "the salvage was realized as value, not murdered" | Ozzik (the sharpest war — see below) |
| **⑥ Ta'Baa** | Ishko; Oomo | "They stay because the dark is deep — hidden is not rooted" (the one reconciliation posture); "the brood must sit before it can fly — eggs do not travel" | Ozzik (despises his explosives; wants the rooted grandeur Ta'Baa flees) |
| **⑦ Zizzik** | Ohm; Rekko | "The right spark holds this wire" (the mirror — the engine room is Ohm's ward, where the name is never spoken); "the twice-mended does not break twice" | Ta'Baa (fears him above all — will not stand in that doorway) |
| **⑧ Sh'kaar** | Ishko; Ta'Baa | "The dark against the sun" — the canonical evasion; "run — the sun cannot chase" (two of canon's three moves against him, made mechanical; the third, *abandon the plan*, is not an intercession — it is Ozzik's grief, accepted) | Zizzik (the two evil clocks feed together) |
| **⑨ Ozzik** | Ishko; Mob'Unloo | "The pride is covered — what is not seen cannot be envied" (the Ozzik−Ishko gap arithmetic, F13-C, spoken as theology); "glory converts to goods; sell the triumph before it draws fire" | **Rekko — NEVER.** Restore-vs-transcend is the sharpest internal war; his refusal is an authored signature moment: *"The scarred hand does not rise. Rekko watches the Shamed one reach for you, and turns back to his heap."* |

**Design note:** every intercessor pairing doubles as a teaching instrument —
the warning-look line states the *relationship*, so twenty intercessions in,
the player has learned the whole feud-web without reading a codex.

## 3. The disposable shrine — the offering you walk away from

_Owner, F13: "building a structure in the local map that you then walk away
from offers an artistic offering far more potent than 'leave some gold on the
ground and call it a day.'" The shrine-as-offering-itself._

### 3a. Dedication and the heart-post

A shrine begins with a **heart-post** (one cheap buildable), placed and
dedicated to ONE god. Dedication starts the ledger: **only construction work
performed after the heart-post stands, inside its radius, counts.** (This is
the anti-exploit: you cannot re-designate last year's dining room as an
offering. The god watched you build it for yourself.)

### 3b. Worth — measured as ruled

Summed at the moment of abandonment:

- **Work-hours** (primary): total pawn labor invested inside the radius —
  devotion measured in sweat, exactly as ruled. A crude vast thing built by
  many hands can outweigh a small rich one.
- **Silver-equivalent** (secondary): market value of structures + contents.
- **Footprint**: enclosed/claimed area.
- **Room stats** (the baked-in scorers): impressiveness, beauty, wealth of the
  enclosed space — free to compute, hard to fake.

**Per-god weighting** — each god reads the same shrine differently:

| God | Weights heaviest | Flavor |
|---|---|---|
| Ozzik | impressiveness + silver | a monument; grandeur or nothing — and yes, building it feeds his meter *while* venting it (net calibrated at build) |
| Rekko | % salvaged/reclaimed materials + work-hours | a heap given honor; new-bought stone insults him |
| Ishko | concealment: under-mountain, unlit, unseen from map edge | a hidden holy place no enemy will ever find |
| Ta'Baa | **the walking-away itself** | worth multiplied when abandonment is by LAUNCH — the leaving is the liturgy |
| Oomo | beauty + a water source enclosed | a garden left for the desert to drink |
| Ohm | powered machinery left running inside | a room that hums to no one |
| Mob'Unloo | **appalled** — unless booked as debt payment | value discarded is sin; dedicate a shrine *as settlement of a named debt to him* and he accepts the write-off |
| Zizzik | dedicated by neglect: built, then left to DECAY unmaintained | its collapse is the offering (see DEVOTIONAL_SACRIFICE_CATALOG_1) |
| Sh'kaar | burned at abandonment | fed to the sun in one blaze — calming, and §19.5-dark |

### 3c. Abandonment, and what it buys

- **Trigger:** map-exit (launch/caravan departure) — the natural gravship
  liturgy — or on-map: home-area removed and no colonist enters for N days.
- **It must be truly left.** Re-entering to harvest, deconstruct, or shelter in
  it later is **desecration**: a large offense to the dedicatee, worth more
  than the shrine ever paid. The gods remember what was given.
- **Buys** (never material): a large satiation gift to the dedicatee ·
  intercession recency + a patience refresh with him (§1a — the one *legal*
  way to warm a shield fast, and it is slow and expensive by nature) · clock
  relief where the dedicatee owns a clock (Ta'Baa's rooted-erosion partially
  forgiven; Ozzik's meter vented per F13; Sh'kaar's escalation cooled by the
  burning).
- **The begging rule still applies:** a shrine rushed after the toll has begun
  is begging in masonry. Worth zero, offense taken.
