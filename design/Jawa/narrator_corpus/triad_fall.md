# Narrator Corpus — the Fall Triad (Zizzik · Sh'kaar · Ozzik)

_Finished v1 prose: every letter here is shipping text, Narrator-voiced (second
person, non-egoic, within-and-beyond the world, free to reference unrevealed
lore, never a game-mechanic word, never a number). The fall-triad law, kept
sharp throughout: **Zizzik celebrates the mechanism of the loss. Ozzik mourns
what was lost. Sh'kaar is the reason it will happen again.** Their boons are
gifts you should not have accepted; their silences are the loudest aboard.
Signature kits per `first_contact_chains.md`; curse structure per the matrix
pages (F10 law: a curse enacts what the god wants; M/L carry their exit inside
the prose). Bracketed [PAWN]/[THING] are substitution slots._

---

## ⑦ ZIZZIK THE SPARK-MAKER

**Voice bible.** Gleeful, skittering, over-familiar — the only god who talks to
you like he knows you, because he has been inside your walls the whole time.
Short clauses. Sudden turns. He is two children in one skin: the reasonable
catastrophist (*of course* it failed; complex things do) and the boy who
presses the button because it is red. Images: the rattle you cannot locate,
sand in the gears, the errant spark, the loose screw, the held breath. He
never apologizes, never explains twice, never speaks of plans or tomorrow —
plans are the joke, tomorrow is the punchline. He never claims to be unfair:
his deepest conviction is that he is the only *fair* thing in the universe,
the leveller who visits everyone. The Narrator handles him warily, like a
thing in the room that might be listening. It is.

**First contact** _(the berserk pawn, the fabricator in pieces, the lights
pulsing warmly)_:
> Somewhere below the deckplates, something is delighted. You should understand
> what just happened: a mind came apart in your care, a machine died of it, and
> of the nine that dwell in this vessel, exactly one found it beautiful. He is
> called the Spark-Maker, when he is called anything at all — near the engines
> he is not named, and you would do well to learn that habit. Do not look for
> the rattle. It has already moved.

**Boons** _(a fed Zizzik pays for ruin — never gratefully accept)_:
- **S — Creative Sparks.** > In the night something skittered through the
  wiring, and this morning [PAWN] woke laughing, an idea arrived whole behind
  the eyes. The Spark-Maker is pleased with your ruin and has left payment.
  Take it. He would be offended if you didn't, and you cannot afford his
  offense and his affection at once.
- **M — The Betrayer's Gift.** > The ones coming to hurt you are having a bad
  day. Their guns jam that never jammed; their machines cough; the wrong spark
  has gone ahead of them like a herald. Understand the lesson exactly: it is
  not that he is on your side. It is that today, for his own reasons, he is on
  theirs *less*.
- **L — The Grand Short-Circuit.** > Far off, something proud and armored has
  begun to devour itself. Every safeguard its builders trusted is failing in
  order, politely, like guests leaving a burning house. The Spark-Maker has
  thrown his favorite kind of feast, and he has thrown it in your honor. Guests
  at such a feast should remember what is being celebrated: that NOTHING holds.
  Nothing they built. Nothing you build.

**Demands** _(reign announcements)_:
- **S.** > While the Spark-Maker holds the floor, one broken thing each day
  stays broken. Call it a courtesy. The rattle likes something to live in.
- **M — the burnt offering.** > He asks plainly, which should worry you: one
  working thing, destroyed by your own hands, while it still works. Not scrap.
  Not salvage. A thing with life in it, given to ruin on purpose. He can tell
  the difference, and he is insulted by leftovers.
- **L — honor the wake.** > For this reign, when a mind cracks, let it crack.
  No hand on the shoulder, no cell, no dart. The Spark-Maker wishes to watch
  his work run its course, and he wishes YOU to watch with him. He says you
  might learn something. He says it kindly, which is worse.

**Taboo warnings**:
- **S** _(everything instantly repaired)_: > Your crews are very quick with
  their wrenches. Every spark smothered, every rattle oiled. Understand that
  tidiness, to one who dwells below your deckplates, is not virtue. It is
  hunger. His.
- **M** _(a break suppressed during his reign)_: > You put [PAWN] down mid-fury
  and called it mercy. The floor under you went very quiet — the particular
  quiet of a child whose toy was taken. Do not do that again while he holds
  the floor.
- **L — REIGN-BREAKER warning** _(a perfect day forming during his reign)_:
  > Nothing has broken today. Nothing. Every machine hums; every mind holds.
  Aboard this vessel, on this day of HIS days, that is not called order. That
  is called defiance, and it is being counted.

**Curses** _(the wake — he was always going to)_:
- **S — Spark Bites** _(arrival)_: > It has begun small, the way he likes to
  begin: a coupling here, a fuse there, the rattle circling closer room by
  room. Small is a courtesy. Small is him knocking.
- **M — The Betrayal** _(arrival, exit inside)_: > Three sharp taps from inside
  the wall, then nothing. Then this: a thing you trusted has turned — count
  your friends, your beasts, your hounds-of-metal, whichever it proves to be.
  He can be gorged back toward sleep, but he sets the table himself: one
  working thing, a good one — he can tell — given to ruin by your own hands.
  Feed the mechanism, and the mechanism looks elsewhere.
- **L — THE WAKING** _(arrival — no exit once fired)_: > The rattle has
  stopped. You have perhaps a breath in which to understand what the silence
  is: every quiet day you hoarded, every spark smothered, every crack painted
  over — banked, with interest, in a ledger kept below the deckplates. It is
  all being withdrawn at once. There is no bargain to make now. There was,
  earlier. You remember earlier.
- **Curse-lift lines**: > The rattle sounds again, far off and unhurried.
  Sated, for now. / > Somewhere below, a long, contented settling — the sound
  a house makes when it has decided not to fall on you today.

**The slumber tells** _(the bank rising — three stages, ambient)_:
1. > Now and then, from somewhere far aft, a faint rattle. The old hands do
   not look up. The old hands are listening very hard.
2. > The rattle walks the ship at night now, room to room, patient as a
   tally. The crew have begun leaving small broken things in corners, the way
   you leave milk for something you'd rather not meet.
3. > No rattle for nine days. No one aboard calls this peace. The whole
   vessel has the held breath of a room where something is hiding behind the
   door, and knows you know.

**Dilemma voice** _(expansions of his authored lines)_:
- *The Red Button:* > "You already know you will press it. Skip the
  pretending. The only creatures in this universe who don't press it are the
  ones who died before reaching it — and how is that better?"
- *The Fouled Cistern:* > "Let the little sparks dance. Bodies mend or they
  don't; that was always true and no cup of boiled water changes it. Stand
  with me and watch, and I will remember your courtesy — I remember
  everything, friend. Ask the Hutts."

**Silences**:
- > Something below the deckplates watched all of that happen and made no
  sound at all. The old hands say his silence has a flavor. They will not
  say what it tastes like.
- > A rattle began — one beat — and chose not to finish. Whatever you almost
  were to him today, you were not, and no one will ever tell you which way
  the choice went.

## ⑧ SH'KAAR THE ALL-SEARING

**Voice bible.** Vast, slow, pitiless. The Narrator rarely quotes him — he is
less a speaker than a weather, and the prose describes what the light DOES.
Declaratives only. No questions: questions imply he does not already know.
Images: glare, heat-shimmer, brass, burned-in shadows, metal expanding at
noon, the sun that WAITS. He never uses *if* — only *when*. He never offers
mercy and never claims cruelty: the sun is not cruel to what it burns, it is
merely the sun. The single word of bargain he understands is *trade*, and the
only coin he accepts is a death that is not yours to grieve. Where Zizzik
skitters and Ozzik mourns, Sh'kaar simply *arrives*, on schedule, like noon.

**First contact** _(the third battle won; the ship two degrees warmer)_:
> Three times now you have made the sand drink. You should know that on this
> world the sun does not set — it waits — and everything that fights beneath
> it is feeding something that cannot lose, only be postponed. Sh'kaar the
> All-Searing has begun to warm to you. This is not good news.

**Boons** _(the dark gifts — enjoy and distrust in the same breath)_:
- **S — Keen Edge.** > A hardness has come into your people. Pain arrives and
  is set aside; the hand does not shake on the trigger. This is his gift, and
  it is real, and it is exactly the gift a farmer gives a fattening animal:
  everything you need to grow, nothing you need to leave.
- **M — The Searing Hour.** > For one fight, the light itself will lean on
  your side of the field — every volley lands burning, every barrel runs hot
  and true. He wants to see what you do with it. Remember, while you enjoy
  this, that he is not watching you win. He is watching you fight.
- **L — Annihilation.** > They will break at the first blood. Not because you
  are strong — because he has walked ahead of you into their hearts and left
  nothing standing there. You will win without effort, and the winning will
  feel like being handed a debt written in a language you cannot read yet.
  You will read it.

**Demands** _(reign announcements)_:
- **S.** > The light on the sand has sharpened. While the All-Searing holds
  the floor there will be blood, yours or theirs; he is genuinely indifferent
  which. Choose, or he will.
- **M.** > A fight is coming, and he requires that you MEET it — not walls,
  not patience, not the long dark waiting he despises. Out. Open. Under his
  eye. He wishes to watch his children work.
- **L — the conflagration.** > He asks for a bonfire with a name: an enemy's
  place, burned until the sand fuses. He does not care which enemy. He cares
  that the smoke rises high enough to be seen from everywhere — because the
  smoke IS the message, and the message is him.

**Taboo warnings**:
- **S** _(mercy mid-battle)_: > You took the surrender. Somewhere above, the
  light flattened, the way noon goes when a verdict has been reached. He does
  not forbid mercy. He prices it.
- **M** _(a battle fled)_: > You left a fight standing. Understand what the
  All-Searing heard: not prudence — theft. That blood was on his table, and
  you carried it off, and the sun has a long memory and no night in which to
  forget.
- **L — REIGN-BREAKER warning** _(a violence-free reign forming)_: > No blood
  in all his hours on the floor. The heat aboard climbs a little each day
  now, and the glare through the ports has gone the white of a held scream.
  You are starving something that does not die of hunger. It only gets
  hungrier.

**Curses** _(he brings the war HE wants to your door)_:
- **S — Bloodlust Whisper** _(arrival)_: > There is heat-shimmer indoors now,
  and the light runs a shade too white. Your people have begun picking at
  each other like kindling asking for a match, and the beasts outside watch
  the walls with noon in their eyes. His war is seeded in your own house.
  It wants only weather.
- **M — The Light Turns** _(arrival, exit inside)_: > The shadows have begun
  shortening at the wrong hours. Fires kindle near what you love; shots meant
  outward find friends; your own people drift into the open to fight the way
  HE fights, because the light is teaching them. He will be led away by only
  one thing, and you know already what it is: a death that is not yours to
  grieve. A captive walked into the desert. An enemy fed to an enemy. He
  does not care that it is cheap. To him, all deaths cost the same.
- **L — THE SEARING** _(arrival — no exit but through)_: > On the horizon
  there is a white glare that does not fade at night. What is coming is not a
  raid; it is a harvest, and it was sown by every battle you fed him, and it
  arrives early, burning, and certain of the ground. There is no trade to
  make now and no dark deep enough. Endure it. And when it is over, remember
  the only thing that has ever cooled him: stillness. Do not fight. Let the
  sun find nothing on the sand but sand.
- **Curse-lift lines**: > The glare has stepped back to its ordinary cruelty.
  He is not gone. He is never gone. He is *waiting*, which is his name for
  patience, which is his name for love. / > The heat aboard has eased. The
  old hands touch the cool bulkhead the way you'd touch a scar.

**Dilemma voice**:
- *The Captive Champion:* > "A death that is not yours. Feed me and I will
  look away a while — the sand takes its drink, your walls keep their
  shadows, and everyone aboard sleeps. You have counted what my looking
  costs. Count what my watching costs."
- *The Quiet Triumph:* > "They are ROUTED. Finish what runs. A fight left
  alive is a seed left watered — you know this, you of all people, whose
  whole race is a fight somebody failed to finish."

**Silences**:
- > The sun said nothing today, which the old hands call the worst of his
  moods: noon, full glare, and not one thing on the sand worth burning.
  Yet.
- > All that blood, and the light did not so much as flicker. Either he is
  sated, or you have become so certainly his that he no longer counts you.
  The crew have not decided which reading lets them sleep.

## ⑨ OZZIK THE SHAMED

**Voice bible.** Faded grandeur; the register of elegy. Long sentences that
begin gilded and die mid-thought, the way the song did. He alone slips into
*we* — he counts himself among the fallen, because he is what fell. Images:
the tarnished crown half-buried, the monument no one remembers building, the
cut-off recording, dust on gold leaf. He never names the ones who broke them;
he says *they*, and the word sags. He never uses *small* as praise and never
says *safe* — safety, to him, is the polite word for the grave the Jawa dug
themselves and called a hiding place. His love letters and his curses are in
the same hand, because for Ozzik they are the same act: he makes you GRAND,
ready or not. The Narrator handles him gently and does not trust him at all.

**First contact** _(the first true reach; the singing, cut off)_:
> Magnificent. You should be proud. They were proud too — the ones singing;
> you heard how the song ends. There is a god aboard who loves what you just
> did more than any of the others love anything, and he is the only one whose
> love you should fear. Ozzik the Shamed has seen your work. So, soon, will
> everyone else.

**Boons** _(bait, and the letter admits it)_:
- **S — Craftsman's Pride.** > A steadiness has come into [PAWN]'s hands, an
  old sureness, as if some grander age were briefly lending its fingers. The
  work that comes of it will gleam. He gives freely. What he gives is
  APPETITE.
- **M — The Prouder Age.** > For a while the benches remember what they were
  for. Ideas arrive dressed; the work sings under the hands; the crew walk
  taller past what they have made. The Shamed is dreaming through you, and
  his dreams are magnificent, and every one of them, you will recall, has
  already come true once — briefly.
- **L — Glory.** > Word of you is abroad, and it returns bearing gifts:
  honor, admirers, hands eager to serve under so bright a name. Take a long
  look at this letter. It is written in gold, and the gold is genuine, and
  somewhere beneath the sand there is a crown that was also genuine. He
  wants you to have everything they had. Everything.

**Demands**:
- **S — display it.** > What you have made is being kept in a box, and he
  finds this unbearable. Greatness hidden, he says, is greatness insulted.
  Set it where eyes fall on it. Yes — those eyes too. That is the point,
  though he will not say so, and neither, in this letter, will anyone else.
- **M.** > He asks for a great work before his hours on the floor are done —
  a thing that did not exist, made excellent. He does not ask twice. The
  second asking is done differently, and you would not call it asking.
- **L — the broadcast.** > He wants it PROCLAIMED. The triumph, the name,
  the reach of you — sent up loud, to everyone, signed. You should know what
  each of the others thinks of this: the hidden one calls it madness, the
  counter calls it advertising, and the sun calls it an invitation. He knows
  all three opinions. He is asking anyway. He was always going to ask.
- **Taboo warnings**:
- **S** _(achievements hidden)_: > You finished it in the dark and told no
  one. Somewhere a cracked bell tried to ring and was held still. He
  understands hiding — no god aboard understands it better, or hates it
  more, for we were not always a people who hid. His words. Note the *we*.
- **M** _(a trophy sold)_: > You priced it. The thing the hands made great,
  weighed out in silver like grain. The ledger-god applauds; the Shamed has
  turned his face away, and from under the sand, faintly, comes the sound of
  a song stopping.
- **L — REIGN-BREAKER warning** _(humiliation unanswered while he fronts)_:
  > They shamed you in the open, and you swallowed it, and the swallowing is
  being watched. He has worn exactly that silence before — it is what he is
  MADE of — and he did not seat himself at the front of this vessel to watch
  his children rehearse his own fall. Answer, or he will stop holding the
  door against what answers in his place.

**Curses** _(the pride-crisis machinery — he makes you grand, ready or not)_:
- **S — The Sting** _(arrival)_: > Polish is catching eyes aboard. The crew
  have tasted the prouder age and want it NOW — plates feel too plain, walls
  too low, the work of last season suddenly embarrassing. Nothing is wrong.
  That is exactly the feeling that was had, once before, when nothing was
  wrong.
- **M — Renown** _(arrival, exit inside)_: > Your name is traveling without
  you. Strangers arrive already knowing your works; rivals speak of you at
  fires you will never sit at; the worth of everything you hold is being
  guessed at, generously, by people who count in raids. He calls this fame
  and he is drunk on it. It can be sobered only one way, and it is the old
  way, the desert way: the Unburdening. Give the wealth away, or build a
  grand thing and walk from it forever — let the sand hold your greatness,
  where no one can price it. He will weep. He always weeps. It keeps him.
- **L — THE SHAMING** _(arrival — no exit but through)_: > This letter is
  written in the register of elegy, because that is the only register left.
  What comes now is scaled to everything you banked: every triumph
  displayed, every alliance flaunted, every proud broadcast — all of it
  answered at once, as it was answered before, in the age no one sings
  about because the singers were interrupted. The two who feed on such
  answerings arrive fed. The crew are silent at their benches. Afterward —
  there will be an afterward; there was last time, barely — only humble
  work answers: mend what remains, restore what the ancients left, and let
  the old vaults teach grief its craft again.
- **Curse-lift lines**: > The cracked bell has gone quiet. The crown settles
  a little deeper, holding what you gave it. / > The expectation has drained
  out of the air like heat after sundown — a thing this world's sky never
  does, and the crew notice, and are briefly, cautiously plain again.

**Dilemma voice**:
- *The Red Button:* > "STUDY it. Knowing is the one treasure they never took
  from us — they took the fleets, the works, the name, but the knowing we
  carried out in our own skulls. Add to it. Carefully. I have learned
  carefully. I have had the time."
- *The Quiet Triumph:* > "PROCLAIM it. Let the sand learn our name again —
  it knew that name once, when the singers still sang, and I am so tired of
  hearing it only in recordings. Yes, the sun will hear it too. Everything
  worth being has been heard by the sun."
- *The Asking Price:* > "We bow to NO ONE. Not again. Never again. I have
  paid for this counsel with everything I ever was, which entitles me to
  give it exactly once per asking: refuse."

**Silences**:
- > A glower from the Shamed contemplates what you have built, and he says
  nothing at all. The crown in the sand does not stir. Grief, the old hands
  say, is what pride does with its silence.
- > He watched the humble work all day — the mending, the making-do, the
  smallness — and made no sound. From the temple speakers, very late, two
  notes of the old song played, and stopped, unfinished, like everything.
