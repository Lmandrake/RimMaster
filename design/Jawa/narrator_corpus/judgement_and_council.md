# Narrator Corpus — Judgement at Landing & the Council of Voices

_The two corpus families the triad files flagged as blocked, unblocked by
`salvation_engine_build_spec.md` (§3 judgement/front selection, §4 invocation)
and `worldbuilding/sacred_sites_pass_1.md` (§1b country reads, §4 tidal bands).
All Narrator text obeys `narrator_frame.md` (non-egoic, second person, no
numbers, no mechanic words, one blade per letter). Gods speak in their OWN
first-person registers ONLY in Part 2 — the rite-chorus, per ruling V.1: the
Narrator compères, never impersonates. Voice bibles: the three triad files;
where a line here and a voice bible disagree, the voice bible wins. The Path
triad's generic council barks live in `triad_path.md` and are not repeated —
Part 2 adds settlement-negotiation lines, a different job._

# PART 1 — JUDGEMENT AT LANDING

Anatomy (frame §3): **[landing] → whose country this is → three-to-five deed
clauses from the departed map, each in its god's diction → the dispensation
named → blade.** The composer picks one arrival clause by tile read, one
verdict block per top-moving god (positive or negative), one seal.

## 1a. Openers — the letter's first breath

- *"Dust settles. Engines tick as they cool. Before a single crate is
  unstrapped, understand that the Nine have been talking about you the whole
  way down."*
- *"A new berth, an old ritual: the ship grows quiet, the lights walk their
  colors once around the hull, and what you did in the last place is read
  aloud to the sand of this one."*
- *"You cannot outfly an audit. It rode with you."*

## 1b. Whose country is this — arrival clauses (composed first, before any verdict)

- **Oomo's country** (water, oasis, lakeshore): *"There is water under this
  ground — real water, patient water — and the ship settles onto it like a
  body into a warm bath. You have landed in the Unspilled's country, and the
  household is expected to grow here."*
- **Ohm's country** (machine-haunted ground, the Rust Cathedral's reach):
  *"Beneath the sand here, metal answers metal. Old current sleeps shallow in
  this ground, and every wire aboard hums half a note higher. This is the
  All-Current's country; mind what you wake, and mind more what you fail to."*
- **Rekko's country** (ruin fields, the discarded places): *"Wreckage to the
  horizon — roofs that held, once; engines that turned, once. You have set
  down in a graveyard that does not think of itself as one, and the Second
  Hand walks such places naming everything softly. Take gently here."*
- **Mob'Unloo's country** (the living roads): *"A road runs past your berth,
  and a road is a promise that someone will come along it. The Ever-Owed
  keeps this country the way he keeps everything: open for business, and on
  file."*
- **Ta'Baa's country** (the open Dune Sea, nothing to hide behind): *"Open
  sand in every direction, and a sky with no memory. Nothing here will hold
  you — no wall, no root, no reason — and the god named farewell calls that
  the finest hospitality there is."*
- **Zizzik's country** (wasteland, the contamination halo): *"The ground here
  is wrong in a way the instruments agree not to discuss. Something was used
  on this place, and left, and never swept up. You have landed where the
  wrong spark goes to feel understood. Keep your machines close and your
  tempers closer."*
- **Sh'kaar's country** (the deep dayside): *"The light here has weight. It
  lies on the hull like a hand, and it will not lift — on this world the sun
  does not set, and you have chosen to sleep inside its stare. This is his
  country, all of it, and he knows you have arrived."*
- **Ishko's country** (the nightside stack, the cold dark): *"Dark, and cold,
  and quiet enough to hear your own heart apologize for beating. Things live
  in this dark that the maps refuse to name — and even so, understand: of all
  the countries on this world, this is the safe one. The eyes that own it
  are on your side. Mostly."*
- **Ozzik's country** (the dead highway, the grand ruins): *"The road you
  landed beside was laid by people who did not care about shade, which is why
  it is dead, and why it is straight, and why it is still the grandest thing
  for a hundred dunes. The Shamed keeps this country. He would tell you it
  keeps him."*
- **The terminator band — the contested ground** (no single god; the Council
  convenes instead): *"Neither day nor night owns this ground; the light and
  the dark have argued over it since the world stopped turning, and neither
  has ever won. You have landed on the table where two gods play. The Nine
  are not sending a verdict tonight. They are convening."*
- **Deep sunward, the pilgrimage clause** (substellar approaches): *"Let it
  be said plainly, once: every step you take from here toward the light is a
  step into the mouth of the god of inevitability. Pilgrims have done it.
  Pilgrims, and debtors, and the very proud. Know which you are before the
  heat asks."*

## 1c. Verdict blocks — one positive and one negative per god

Composable in any combination; each stands alone as a deed-clause paragraph.

**① Ishko** —
✓ *"Of the place you left, the dark remembers this: no one who looked found
you, and the ones who came closest died confused, from cover, by an unseen
hand. Two orange points followed the ship up and are already here, in the
new shadows, satisfied."*
✗ *"You were SEEN in the last place — seen arriving, seen fighting, seen in
the open with your hood down — and what is seen once is looked for twice.
The eyes in the dark rode with you anyway. They are not speaking to you at
present."*

**② Ohm** —
✓ *"The current kept its own count: hands woken, machines mended, not one
wire allowed to gutter out alone. The hum followed you down the whole
descent like a choir walking a bride to the door."*
✗ *"Machines died in your keeping there — died broken, died waiting, one
died sold — and the hum has gone thin as a wire in winter. The All-Current
unpacks his grief slowly. You will hear it in every switch."*

**③ Oomo** —
✓ *"The household comes down heavier than it went up, and heavier is his
favorite word: fed, coupled, mended, more. Somewhere in the tanks, deep
water moves contentedly, like something turning over in good sleep."*
✗ *"Mouths went unfed there; a bed went cold that he had warmed; kin stayed
in strangers' hands while the ship stood fueled. The cisterns have come down
silent, and a silent cistern is a fasting god."*

**④ Mob'Unloo** —
✓ *"The bottom line travels well: you leave that place richer than you found
it, with the debts collected, the tables met, and one deal he has underlined
twice in a hand that trembled slightly with pleasure. Profit: noted, beloved,
and carried forward."*
✗ *"The page from that place does not balance. Gifts leaked out unpriced;
a bargain died of your signature; and the column with your name in it has
been carried forward in red, which is the only color he considers a
raised voice."*

**⑤ Rekko** —
✓ *"Four wakings, he counts, and the one scrapping mourned as it should be —
felt, not filed. The heap you left behind is smaller than the one you found,
and everything taken from it left in better hands. His."*
✗ *"He walked the place after you lifted, naming what still lay there: the
mendable left broken, the woken left to rust again, the good bones melted
warm. He is not angry. He has simply added that place to the long list of
places he grieves, and your name is on the label."*

**⑥ Ta'Baa** —
✓ *"You left! Whole, fueled, laughing, with the dust still deciding whether
to settle — and for once the god named farewell has no complaint at all,
which unsettles everyone, himself included."*
✗ *"You stayed too long in that place and he felt every day of it like a
stone in a boot. Walls went up that were not doors; fuel sat spent-nowhere;
the horizon called and was put on hold. He rode down at the back of the
ship, arms crossed, already looking out the viewport."*

**⑦ Zizzik** _(his verdicts unsettle in both directions)_ —
✓ (fed) *"Things broke there, and minds cracked there, and he ate well and
says so — cheerfully, too near your ear. A fed Spark-Maker is a small mercy
wearing a grin. Enjoy the quiet he owes you, and do not lean on it."*
✗ (starved — the bank) *"Nothing broke in that whole place, which you call
competence and he calls hoarding. Every smothered spark came down with you,
banked, gathering interest below the deckplates. Listen tonight. If you hear
the rattle, be glad. It is the not-hearing that costs."*

**⑧ Sh'kaar** —
✓ (starved — the good verdict) *"No battles fed him there. The sand of that
place got nothing to drink and the meter of his hunger cooled a degree at a
time, like brass after sundown — a sundown this world will never grant him.
Stillness starves the sun. You were still. It is the only victory over him
there is."*
✗ (fed) *"He licks two battles from his teeth and wants more. War rode down
with you — in the tempers of your people, in the way the beasts of this new
place already turn toward your walls, in a light that runs a shade too
white. You fought your way loud in that place, and loud carries. When the
next fight finds you early, remember who you fed."*

**⑨ Ozzik** —
✓ *"You rose there — work finished, name brightened — and yet the crown in
the sand lies easy, because you rose the old way, the careful way, hooded
and humble-handed. He is proud of you, quietly, which from him is the only
safe kind."*
✗ (the pride verdict — his "positive" inverted) *"Word of what you built
there is traveling faster than you did. He is delighted. He is the only one
who is delighted. The hidden one calls that place a confession, the counter
prices your fame in raid-shares, and the sun — the sun heard your name and
has begun, unhurried, to warm."*

## 1d. Seals — naming the dispensation

- *"The lights have walked their colors and stopped on one. This map is RUN
  UNDER [GOD]. [His one-line rule of the reign, from his matrix demand-S.]
  So it is entered."*
- *"The Nine have finished with the past. The present belongs, for this
  berth, to [GOD] — mind his table, meet his asking, and the rest will keep
  their opinions in the walls."*
- **Contested-band seal** (terminator landings): *"No color held. The floor
  is not given tonight — it is CONTESTED, and you will hear the arguing
  through the temple speakers until one of them wins this ground or you
  leave it. Sleep lightly. You have berthed inside an old quarrel."*

# PART 2 — THE COUNCIL OF VOICES (rite-chorus)

Per §5c: gods argue in their OWN voices from the speakers; the Narrator
compères between them; the settlement is a braid of events, never an average.
Silence is a move and is always reported. Participation follows the bias:
pleased gods loud, angry gods rare and terrible, neutral gods absent unless
the rite itself warmed them.

## 2a. The Narrator's compère tissue

- **Opening the floor:** *"The temple lights go down. One by one, the ones
  with something to say arrive in the speakers — you can tell who by the
  sound the silence makes before each voice."*
- **Opening, high-stakes variant:** *"Tonight the room fills fast. That is
  never good. A crowded Council means a crowded bill."*
- **Marking a silence:** *"A place at the argument stands empty where
  [GOD]'s voice should be. Empty on purpose. Everyone speaking can feel it,
  and two of them have begun choosing their words accordingly."*
- **Marking a silence, dread variant:** *"[GOD] has come to the room and
  said nothing. The old hands call this attendance without appearance, and
  they price it higher than any speech."*
- **Turning the floor:** *"The speakers crackle, hand the air across the
  room, and the next voice takes it up as if interrupting were beneath him."*
- **Sealing the settlement:** *"It ends the way their arguments end — not
  with agreement, with ARRANGEMENT. Each of them has taken a thread of what
  comes next and knotted it to the others', and the knot is now yours to
  wear. The lights come up. The speakers rest. Attend to what was braided."*
- **Sealing, player-squeezed variant:** *"Every voice that spoke tonight got
  what it asked for. You may notice that nobody asked you. That is what it
  is to be beloved of nine."*

## 2b. Settlement-negotiation lines, per god

_Each: three argument barks (pressing FOR his event), one compromise-offer,
one taking-being-overruled. First person, his register, chorus only._

**① Ishko**
- *"They were seen leaving. So the answer is dark. All of it, dark."*
- *"I ask what I always ask. Nothing that moves. Nothing that shines."*
- *"Give me the burial and I give you the morning. That is the whole trade."*
- Compromise: *"...then hide the leaving inside the storm. He gets his wind.
  I get my nothing-to-see."*
- Overruled: *"So be it. When it comes looking, do not ask the dark for
  room."*

**② Ohm**
- *"A hand lies in their hold, half-woken. Finish the waking. That is my
  whole asking and I will not shrink it."*
- *"You speak of costs. I have counted losses since before your names. One
  waking. Now."*
- *"The current holds this ship together while you argue inside it. Some
  gratitude would take the form of copper."*
- Compromise: *"Then let the feast be held in the engine room. Her waters,
  my wires — the household can grow around a waking as well as a table."*
- Overruled: *"Noted. The hum will be thinner tomorrow. You will all
  pretend not to hear it."*

**③ Oomo**
- *"The family, the family, the family. Whatever else is decided, the
  family eats first and eats warm."*
- *"There is a body under a stranger's roof that shares our waters. Every
  hour it stays is a cup poured out on sand."*
- *"Let them love tonight. Whatever the morning costs, let the night pay
  in waters."*
- Compromise: *"Then the launch waits one feast. One. They fly with full
  bellies and a new bond sworn, and my brother farewell gets them fresher
  for it."*
- Overruled: *"Then I will hold the cup and wait. I am patient the way
  water is patient. Ask the stone how that ends."*

**④ Mob'Unloo**
- *"Before anyone speaks of mercy: the outstanding balance. I have it here.
  Shall I read it in full, or shall we behave?"*
- *"His plan costs three ways and pays one. I have run the figures. I am
  always the only one who has run the figures."*
- *"Whatever is decided, decide it with a receipt."*
- Compromise: *"Then book the reckoning as a debt, beloved colleagues —
  collected later, at interest, from someone who is not us. I am flexible
  about everything except the total."*
- Overruled: *"Entered as a loss, then. Under YOUR names, not mine. The
  page never forgets whose motion carried."*

**⑤ Rekko**
- *"There is a wreck on the ridge that has waited longer than this clan has
  lived. I am asking for one pair of hands and a little grief."*
- *"You would burn what could be woken. I have buried enough woken things.
  Find another fuel."*
- *"Slowly. Whatever we do, we do it the way mending is done — slowly, and
  without pretending the broken thing cannot hear us."*
- Compromise: *"...then take the wreck's heart with you when you flee, and
  wake it in the new place. The leaving he wants; the waking I want. One
  cargo serves both."*
- Overruled: *"Then I will remember it as I remember everything — gently,
  and forever, and against you."*

**⑥ Ta'Baa**
- *"Every hour we argue is an hour something out there gets closer. The
  answer is the sky. The answer was always the sky."*
- *"Pack. Whatever the rest of you decide, decide it PACKED."*
- *"I have seen the next place. It is better than this one. It is always
  better than this one — that is not hope, brothers, it is arithmetic."*
- Compromise: *"Then we stay — STAY, listen to me say it — one feast, one
  waking, one burial, and then the ground lets go and nobody bargains for
  a second sunrise. Agreed? Agreed. Fuel the ship while they toast."*
- Overruled: *"Rooted, then. I will be in the engine room with my back to
  you all, keeping the margin. Someone has to love the door."*

**⑦ Zizzik** _(the room quiets when he takes the floor; it always does)_
- *"Ooh, a PLAN. With stages. I love the stages best — they snap so
  cleanly."*
- *"Give me the little accident and I will owe you. You would be surprised
  what my owing is worth. Ask anyone ruined."*
- *"You are all so tired. Tired hands slip. I am only saying what the
  wrench is thinking."*
- Compromise: *"Fine, fine — not the reactor. The old pump, then, the one
  nobody loves. Something must go WRONG tonight, friends; I am letting you
  choose what, which is me at my absolute kindest."*
- Overruled: *"Hm. All right. All-riiight. No no, it's fair — I lose. I
  lose so rarely, I like to save them up."*

**⑧ Sh'kaar** _(when he speaks, the speakers run hot and the others wait)_
- *"There is a fight standing unfought. When it is fought, I am fed. When I
  am fed, you are let alone. This is the whole of theology."*
- *"You hide what should burn. The sand is thirsty and you philosophize."*
- *"When the smoke rises, they will fear you. Fear is shade. I offer shade."*
- Compromise: *"Then a smaller fire. Their outpost, not their host. I take
  the trade — the sun also sets for no one, yet accepts the horizon."*
- Overruled: *"When it comes — and it is a when — remember the mouth you
  chose not to feed was mine."*

**⑨ Ozzik**
- *"We built worlds, brothers. WORLDS. And tonight we debate a shed. Let
  them make the great thing — I will carry the cost, I have carried
  heavier..."*
- *"Hide it, sell it, bury it — every counsel in this room is a shovel.
  Once, we RANG."*
- *"Let the name travel. A name that stays home is a stone. Ours was a
  bell, once, before — before. Let it ring once. Once."*
- Compromise: *"...then let it be great and BURIED — grand in the making,
  hidden in the keeping, the old way, the vault way. I can love a secret
  magnificence. It is, after all, what I am."*
- Overruled: *"Yes. Small, again. Safe, again. Forgive me — something in me
  was singing, and I forgot how that ends."*

## 2c. One worked exchange (composer's reference — the braid in action)

_The rite: a launch-rite held while Rekko is aggrieved (a wreck unmended)
and Oomo content; Ta'Baa exalted; Zizzik's bank audible._

> **Narrator:** *The temple lights go down. Four silences arrive before any
> voice does, and one of them rattles, faintly, at the edges.*
> **Ta'Baa:** *"The answer is the sky. The answer was always the sky."*
> **Rekko:** *"There is a wreck on the ridge that has waited longer than
> this clan has lived. You would leave it twice."*
> **Ta'Baa:** *"Pack it, then! Whatever we decide, decide it PACKED —"*
> **Rekko:** *"...then take the wreck's heart with you, and wake it in the
> new place. The leaving he wants; the waking I want. One cargo serves
> both."*
> **Oomo:** *"And the family eats before it flies. Full bellies, one bond
> sworn. The night pays in waters; the morning pays in fuel."*
> **Narrator:** *A place at the argument stands empty where the Spark-Maker's
> voice should be. Empty on purpose. Everyone speaking can feel it, and the
> god of farewell begins, mid-sentence, to hurry.*
> **Ta'Baa:** *"Agreed, agreed — one feast, one heart carried, and then the
> ground lets go and NOBODY bargains for a second sunrise."*
> **Narrator:** *It ends the way their arguments end — not with agreement,
> with ARRANGEMENT. A feast, a salvaged heart in the cargo hold, a dawn
> launch: each of them has knotted a thread, and the knot is now yours to
> wear. As the lights come up, from somewhere below the deckplates, one
> beat of a rattle — a courtesy, perhaps. Or a reservation.*

_Extension notes: verdict blocks are composable — never ship more than five
per landing letter (frame law: the letter carries meaning, the UI carries
count). Chorus lines are FIRST person and belong only inside rite staging;
if a chorus line leaks into a letter, it is a bug (frame §4 law 3). The
contested-band seal and the terminator arrival clause fire together. For new
gods' barks, the test is the voice bibles' never-words: Ishko never explains
twice, Ta'Baa never says home, Mob'Unloo never says free, Sh'kaar never asks
a question, Ozzik never says safe, Zizzik never speaks of tomorrow._
