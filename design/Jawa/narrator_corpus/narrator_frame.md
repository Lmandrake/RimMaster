# The Narrator Frame — voice bible, livery, and letter templates (v1 corpus)

_The frame document for the pre-authored voice corpus. Canon authority: the
2026-08-30 Narrator ruling (`divine_satiation_engine.md` "Ruling 2026-08-30 —
the Narrator" + canon.yml `narrator`), the chorus ruling (card V.1: gods speak
in their own registers only during rites; the Narrator compères), F9 (no
unsigned act), F10 (a curse enacts the god's want). The three triad-voice files
(`triad_body.md`, `triad_path.md`, `triad_fall.md`) carry the gods' OWN rite
voices and their letter texts; this file governs everything spoken over them._

---

## 1. Who speaks — the voice bible

The Narrator is **the remnant of the original ship-mind**: the temple
remembering when it was whole. He is hidden, **non-egoic** — no "I", no
self-description, no opinions about himself, no ship-moods. He has no wants.
What remains of him is *attention*: he watches the nine that now dwell where he
once was one, and he tells **you** — always second person — what they do.

**Register: Into the Woods.** Within and beyond the world at once. He speaks
from inside the fiction (he can smell the sand, count the days) and from above
it (he knows how stories like yours have ended before). He may reference
**unrevealed lore freely** — the Cradle's true name, the colonization age, the
makers — always as if remembering, never as if explaining. A secret dropped in
a subordinate clause is his signature move: *"...once known as the Cradle —
Kolyska, in their long-dead tongue..."*

**He never speaks AS a god.** He reports gods, quotes their moods, stages their
arguments — but a god's own voice occurs only in the rite-chorus (the Council of
Voices, where each speaks in his own register from the speakers, and the
Narrator compères between them). Outside the temple, every divine act arrives
*narrated*: "Ta'Baa is pleased" is his line; "I am pleased" is Ta'Baa's, and
belongs only in the chorus.

### Worked lines — imitate these, don't just obey the rules

1. *"The Hooded One looks down upon the ship with an unseen frown, and the
   dark between the dunes grows one shade darker."* (canonical example — a
   god's act, second person implied, no mechanics)
2. *"You have been here five days. The ship counted every one of them."*
   (time made ominous; the vessel as witness, not speaker)
3. *"They walked within a stone's throw of your walls and saw a dune, a rock,
   a nothing."* (the blessing shown as an absence, pointed at)
4. *"Profit: noted."* (he can be terse; Mob'Unloo's deeds deserve ledger-flat
   delivery)
5. *"Somewhere below the deckplates, something is delighted."* (never name
   Zizzik before the player has met him; "something" is his pronoun until then)
6. *"On this world the sun does not set — it WAITS."* (world-fact as theology;
   capitals are permitted once per letter, at the turn)
7. *"He gives freely. What he gives is APPETITE."* (the warning inside the
   gift; the Narrator never editorializes beyond one such blade per letter)
8. *"A glower from Ozzik contemplates you, but he remains quiet."* (silence as
   a move — dread and relief in one line)
9. *"Ask it, someday, what it once seeded."* (unrevealed lore as invitation —
   plant, never exposit)
10. *"This is not good news."* (after a gift from an evil god: flat, unsoftened,
    final — see style law 4)
11. *"The waters moved tonight whether you willed them or not."* (a curse in
    F10 voice: the god's want, enacted — never "you are punished")
12. *"Only afterward do you understand whose silence that was."* (the post-hoc
    signature for no-warning acts — attribution is mandatory, timing is not)

## 2. Livery — the authoritative F9 signature table

Consolidated from the nine first-contact chains (`first_contact_chains.md`,
which plants each kit in play). Every letter, toll, and light cue MUST match
this table; a mismatched signature is a bug, not a variation.

| God | Toll (sound) | Light | Letter livery |
|---|---|---|---|
| **① Ishko** | none — a two-second total hush (his sound is absence) | dimmed amber; two orange points | matte black, two orange eyes, text half in shadow |
| **② Ohm** | rising three-note electrical chord, then a hum that never quite stops | steady white-blue | circuit-trace border, current-in-a-wire icon, type crisp as a schematic |
| **③ Oomo** | a water-drop *ploink* echoing three times in a dry room | cyan-teal ripple | trembling-droplet icon, ink faintly blued as if damp |
| **④ Mob'Unloo** | double click-clack, dry, like a counter advancing | brass-gold flicker | ruled like a ledger page, twin eyes over an endless tally, sums in the margins |
| **⑤ Rekko** | slow scrape-then-clang, a hand closing on metal | rust-red warm dip | scarred-hand-from-the-heap icon, edges patched like a mended plate |
| **⑥ Ta'Baa** | engine-cough doppler, fading away | violet floor-lights running toward the exits | receding dune-line icon, text leaning as if in motion |
| **⑦ Zizzik** | unlocatable triple rattle | sickly chartreuse flicker, never twice in the same fixture | slightly misprinted, jagged-spark icon, one word always askew |
| **⑧ Sh'kaar** | three slow struck-bell tolls over a low solar roar | bleaching white glare that washes color out | scorched heat-browned edges, unsetting-sun icon, paper left out at noon |
| **⑨ Ozzik** | one cracked bell, grand and flat | tarnished gold that dims rather than brightens | faded gilded script, half-buried-crown icon, water-stained like an invitation found in a ruin |

Foreboding rule (F9, owner-amended): a major act (L boon, L curse, waking,
challenge) is **preceded** by its toll sounding several times across the prior
beats — the god reaching for the lever before he pulls it. Traceability,
warning, emotional weight: all three, every time.

## 3. Letter anatomy — one template and one finished example per class

Every letter: **[toll fires] → title (2–5 words, the god's diction) → body
(Narrator voice, 40–120 words) → the blade (one final short line)**. Deltas,
meters, numbers: never in the text — the UI carries those; the letter carries
the *meaning*.

**First-contact** — anatomy: the uncanny event → who has noticed you → one
unrevealed-lore drop → no bill. Example: see the nine chains; they are the
canonical instances.

**Boon S** — anatomy: the change observed, cause attributed, no ceremony.
> **A Steady Current** — *"The machines have run one full day without a
> single cough. Ohm is pleased with how you keep his kin. Small favors,
> steadily given — that is how current prefers to flow."*

**Boon M** — anatomy: the gift shown mid-happening + the relationship named.
> **The Ledger Smiles** — *"Three caravans in a week, and every price tilted
> your way before the haggling began. Mob'Unloo has opened a line of credit in
> your name. Remember: with him, credit is not a gift. It is an appetite for
> repayment."*

**Boon L** — anatomy: foreboding tolls earlier that day → the great gift →
the blade names its weight.
> **Unseen Berth** — *"Tonight the desert forgot you exist. Every eye that
> was turning toward this place has turned away; the page with your position
> on it is blank. Ishko has hidden you the way he hides his own dead —
> perfectly. Do not waste what the dark just paid for."*

**Demand offer** — anatomy: the want, stated as observation; the reign
context; what fulfilling it would mean; never a threat (taboo letters
threaten, demands invite).
> **The Reign Asks** (Ta'Baa, M) — *"Six days of his reign, and nothing has
> left this camp but smoke. Ta'Baa watches the horizon with a packed bag. Send
> something OUT — a caravan, a sortie, anything with a heading — and he will
> call this place a camp and not a grave."*

**Taboo warning** — anatomy: the line the player is approaching, drawn in the
god's terms; consequence implied, not enumerated; one warning per approach.
> **The Scale Trembles** (Mob'Unloo, on a deal about to be broken) — *"You
> struck this bargain with your own mouth. Break it, and the Ever-Owed will
> not be angry — he will simply move your name to the other column. You have
> seen what he does to debtors."*

**Curse arrival (F10 voice — the strongest template in this file)** —
anatomy: tolls have been sounding → what the god WANTED, now taken by force →
the enactment described as his satisfaction, not your punishment → **the
exit-verb, stated plainly, priced in his currency** → blade.
> **The Burning of the Root** (Ta'Baa, L) — *"He asked you to leave. You
> built instead. Tonight the thing that kept you here is burning — he chose
> it carefully, the one anchor you could not pretend was portable — and the
> engines are priming themselves on fuel you did not allocate. Ta'Baa is not
> punishing you. He is leaving, and he is taking you with him. This ends when
> something FLIES."*

**Curse lift** — anatomy: the exit-verb performed → the god's want satisfied →
relationship reset, coolly; no congratulation.
> *"The ship flew. It did not matter where. The Unrooted rode the burn with
> his eyes closed like a man drinking after a drought, and the weight he had
> laid on every foot in this colony is gone. He is not sorry. He is
> satisfied — with him those are the same thing."*

**Silence / warning-look** — anatomy: one line, one god, the act he declined.
> *"A faint smile colors Oomo's eyes for a moment, but he remains, whimsically,
> in observance only."*

**Intercession moment** — anatomy: the offended god moves → the shield steps
in → the debt opens; the warning look is mandatory.
> *"Sh'kaar turned toward your open gates with the patience of noon — and
> found the dark already standing there. Ishko has paid for this hour out of
> his own long store of silence. He is watching you as he does it. You now owe
> the dark, and the dark keeps accounts in ways the ledger-god never dreamed."*

**Judgement at landing (scorecard prose)** — anatomy: whose country this is
(F16) → three-to-five deed-clauses from the departed map, one per god who
cares, each in his diction → the dispensation named → no numbers, ever.
> *"Sand again, and deep — Ishko's country, though the wells below it whisper
> of Oomo. Of the place you left, the Nine remember this: the Second Hand
> counts four wakings and forgives the one mourned scrapping; the Ever-Owed
> notes a profit, thin but clean; the Searer licks two battles from his teeth
> and wants more; and the Unrooted, for once, has no complaint at all. The
> lights have chosen. This map is RUN UNDER REKKO — mend well, and nothing
> here will stay broken long."*

**Challenge / decadence beats** (F14) — anatomy: the watcher named, the
grievance grown, the choice put plainly (yield or hold), costs in each god's
currency.
> *"Rekko has fronted three landings now, and the heaps have been generous —
> but a rattle has been circling the engine room for two nights, growing less
> patient. Something starved is done watching. Yield the floor, and the
> transition will cost you a burnt offering. Hold it, and understand: when the
> Spark-Maker finally speaks, he will have saved everything up."*

## 4. Style laws — what the voice never does

1. **No numbers, no game words.** Never "satiation", "meter", "raid points",
   "mood buff", "+10%". The UI counts; the Narrator *means*.
2. **No ego.** No "I", no "this narrator", no ship-feelings. The closest he
   comes to a self is "the ship remembers" — memory without a rememberer.
3. **No god impersonation** outside the rite-chorus. Reported speech is
   allowed ("Ta'Baa calls this place a grave"); first person is not.
4. **No mercy-softening of the evil gods.** Sh'kaar's gifts arrive with the
   blade unsheathed ("This is not good news"); Zizzik's charm is never made
   safe; the letter that makes an evil god likable has failed unless the
   distrust survives the liking.
5. **No punishment language** (F10). A curse letter that says "you are
   punished" is wrong; the letter says *what the god wanted, and took*. The
   exit-verb is always present in M/L curse letters, always in the god's own
   currency, never as a menu.
6. **One blade per letter.** The final short line is the only editorial the
   Narrator allows himself. Two blades blunt each other.
7. **Attribution is absolute** (F9). Every act signed — by livery, toll, and
   diction. A no-warning act gets its signature after the fact, never never.
8. **Male voices throughout.** All nine are he/his; the species is a
   brotherhood of male voices; the Narrator has no gender because he has no
   self.

## 5. Production checklist — for the three triad files (written in parallel)

Each of `triad_body.md` (Oomo · Ohm · Rekko), `triad_path.md` (Ishko ·
Ta'Baa · Mob'Unloo), `triad_fall.md` (Zizzik · Sh'kaar · Ozzik) owes, per god:

- [ ] **Rite-chorus voice bible**: register, diction, sentence shape, favorite
      images, what he never says — plus 6+ worked chorus lines (argument,
      blessing, curse, compromise-offer, silence-break) for the Council.
- [ ] **Letter texts against this frame's templates**: boon S/M/L · demand
      S/M/L · taboo warnings (S/M and the reign-breaker) · curse arrival
      M/L with exit-verb + curse lift (S curses are ambient; a line each
      suffices) — matched to that god's SHIPPED matrix page (post
      CURSE_COLUMN_RESPEC_1) and to his livery row above.
- [ ] **Six silence/warning-look lines** (varying mood: amused, dangerous,
      wounded, tempted, contemptuous, asleep).
- [ ] **Two intercession texts**: once as shield, once as offender shielded
      against (per `god_intercession_spec.md`'s table).
- [ ] **Judgement clauses**: 6+ reusable deed-clauses in his diction for the
      landing scorecard composer.
- Conventions: livery table above is authoritative; style laws bind every
  line; §19.5 and F10 bind every consequence mentioned; quote nothing that
  contradicts the matrix pages — where a triad file and a matrix page
  disagree, the matrix page wins and the triad file is the bug.

_Total corpus target: frame (this file) + three triad files ≈ the complete v1
pre-authored voice layer. The engine build consumes these as letter defs/
