# The Knowing — the integration-letter register

_The register for every ANTIQUITIES INTEGRATION letter: the moment a read
urn's restored fragment enters the collective Knowing of the gods. The
Narrator speaks it; the gods react within it; the ship never explains._

---

## 1. The law of the register

**The ship never explains it in this language** — no letter, tooltip or bar
may say "database," "restoration," "pattern-match," "techprint" or any
systems word. The Narrator intones what the mechanism does as what the gods
FEEL. The owner's exemplar, verbatim, the tone target for every integration
letter:

> *"As the ancient lore enters the collective Knowing of the gods, its
> truths radiate outward like an organizing principle. Where they are
> needed, they bind and mend. Where there is dissent, they withdraw. What
> is left is less cracked, less fragmented, and the patterns restored find
> yet other places where relevance guides. To remember one thing is to
> remember others too, or at least recognize their absence."*

Frame rules inherited whole from `narrator_frame.md`: second person, always;
no "I"; unrevealed lore in subordinate clauses, remembered rather than
explained; the Narrator reports gods and never speaks as one.

## 2. The shape of an integration letter

Three movements, a few sentences each:

1. **What was read** — the piece's read-narrative echo, one image of it
   carried forward ("the nine hundred birds have found their sky").
2. **The Knowing** — the radiation, in the exemplar's register: what binds,
   what withdraws, what recognizes its absence.
3. **The gods' weather** — one to three gods react FROM THEIR OWN VANTAGE
   (content may please or displease them; the wholeness pleases all, to
   varying degrees — net positive, always). When a techprint radiates, it
   is movement 3's last line, as a gift noticed rather than a system
   firing: *"and somewhere aft, a door that would not open remembers what
   it is for."*

## 3. The nine, at an integration — reaction sketches

Keyed to the urn grammar's registers and domains (antiquities §4.3, axes A
and D). Content reactions vary; wholeness-pleasure is universal.

| god | vantage at an integration |
|---|---|
| **Ishko** (hiding) | Blesses the Doctrine itself — a civilization that survived by hiding is proof of his whole theology. Deepest pleasure at null-bands and withheld names: *"Ishko studies the unpainted band a long time, and approves of what is not there."* |
| **Ohm** (living machine) | Surges — every integration is the Body knowing itself better. Displeased only by funerary registers that mourn machines left dead: he wants them BACK. |
| **Oomo** (water/rationing) | Takes the genealogies and the census registers — who was owed what, who carried whom. Twitchy at records of waste; soothed by the tide-mechanics domain (water, counted and kept). |
| **Mob'Unloo** (debt/trade) | Reads every piece as a ledger finally produced for audit. The LOST ledger is HIS book; a Recovery Raid is his theology — the deal unwound at cost. Unsettled, briefly, by fragments where the dead paid debts no one collected. |
| **Rekko** (salvage/repair) | The register's warmest voice: reading is mending, restoration without transcendence — pride-neutral by ruling. Wounded only when an integration reveals a piece the clan sold unread: he counts the absence aloud. |
| **Ta'Baa** (leaving) | Restless: takes CARTOGRAPHY and the roads. Every map fragment feeds him and worsens him — *"Ta'Baa has learned another way out of here, and hums it."* |
| **Zizzik** (malfunction) | Stirs at Assailant-shadowed content — erasure motifs, the eaten figure, the silhouette painting itself. Gleeful where the record shows the ancients' own systems betraying them. Slumbers through comfortable civic pieces. |
| **Sh'kaar** (exposure/war) | Feeds on the war-grief registers; the darker fractal layers, when fluency finds them, are HIS vindication — he knew what the grief was hiding. The reversal's arrival should read partly in his rising weather. |
| **Ozzik** (ambition/pride/grief — the trap) | The Helix's mirror: he covets what they covet and grieves what they forged. Pleased by monumental domains; stung by every piece proving the makers were greater than the clan will ever be — then proud, bitterly, that the clan is the one reading. The Temptation quest speaks in his voice. |

not the Memory-Core recognition letters (`ShipMemory`'s Keyed strings —
