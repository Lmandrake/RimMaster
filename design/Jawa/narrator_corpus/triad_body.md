# Narrator Corpus — the Body triad (Oomo · Ohm · Rekko)

_Finished v1 prose: the actual text the player reads. Narrator-voiced per the
canon narrator ruling — second person, non-egoic, within-and-beyond the world,
free to touch unrevealed lore, never a number, never a mechanic word. Direct
god-speech appears only as quotation, in that god's own register. Letter titles
are the in-game letter labels. Sources: matrix pages (F10 curses),
`first_contact_chains.md`, `divine_dilemma_events.md`, `god_intercession_spec.md`._

## ③ OOMO THE UNSPILLED

**Voice bible.** Wet, warm, bodily; the tenderness of a god who counts heads at
the table and grieves every empty chair. His register laps — short clauses in
threes, like ripples: *the cup, the mouth, the morning*. He speaks of people as
waters and vessels, of the colony as the household, of time in meals and
mornings-after. Characteristic images: the drop that never falls, the brimming
cistern, sweat on stone, the egg in the dark warm room. He is sensuous without
shame and hungry without apology. He never uses the words of commerce — *price,
cost, worth* are Mob'Unloo's, and he will not put his family on that scale. He
never commands; he *yearns*, and the yearning presses like humidity.

### First contact — letter: "The Ship Sweats"
Two of your people shared one bunk tonight, and not a drop of what passed
between them was lost. All night the cisterns thrummed in time like a heart
underwater, and the temple walls beaded and glistened until dawn. This vessel
has carried ten thousand cradles in its long life — ask it, someday, what it
once seeded across the dark — and it remembers what a colony is *for*. Oomo the
Unspilled counts every mouthful, every egg, every shared bed. He has begun
counting yours.

### Boons
- **S — "Full Cisterns."** The water tastes sweeter than the tanks should allow,
  and the evening meal sits in the belly like a kindness. The household is being
  tended by something that loves it. Sleep comes easy tonight.
- **M — "The Body's Tide."** A tide has come in that no sea sent. Wounds knit
  while their owners dream, the sick sleep quiet, and every appetite aboard —
  every one — runs high. The Unspilled is pleased with his family, and the
  family will grow if you let it.
- **L — "Increase."** The god of shared waters has opened his hands. What
  arrives now — the cradle filled, or kin walking out of the heat-shimmer asking
  for a place at your fire — is not luck. It is the household's reward, paid in
  the only coin Oomo has ever kept: more family.

### Demands
- **S — "Every Mouth, a Meal."** A whisper through the galley pipes, patient
  and damp: let no one eat from the machine's gray charity today. Cook. Paste is
  drought wearing a bowl.
- **M — "Lay the Feast."** The cisterns knock softly, twice, like a host
  clearing his throat. Oomo asks what any father asks of a good season: a table
  laid full, the family loud around it, nothing held back for tomorrow. He will
  remember who sat, and who was missing.
- **L — "Begin Something."** The warm rooms of the ship stand ready and have
  stood ready too long. Before this reign ends the Unspilled would see waters
  promised or waters begun — a union sworn, or a new life started in the dark.
  A household that only endures is a cistern sealed: still full, already dying.

### Taboo warnings
- **S — "Metal in the Warm Rooms."** A droid stands idle where your people
  sleep, and the walls have gone dry around it. Cold hands in the brood-warmth.
  Oomo says nothing yet — but the dripping you cannot find has stopped, and the
  silence is worse.
- **M — "Kin Under a Stranger's Roof."** Word came of one of your own, held by
  hands that are not family, and the word was allowed to pass. The cisterns run
  a degree colder. Somewhere a mouth you were owed goes unfed, and the
  Unspilled counts it against the household that could have gathered him in.
- **L — REIGN-BREAKER — "The Cup Overturned."** A body of the family has dried
  out under your keeping — or was handed over, living, for coin. There is no
  quiet way to say what has happened: the god of the shared waters has turned
  his face from the table. The reign is broken. What comes next comes wet.

### Curses (the waters pass whether you will it or not)
- **S — "The Tide Rises."** Thirst walks the corridors tonight, and not only
  for water. Appetites sharpen, glances linger, old jealousies stir under new
  blankets — and somewhere in the walls, a slow dripping no one can locate.
  Oomo is not angry, exactly. He is *insisting*.
- **M — "Spilled Freely."** What you would not give, the body gives anyway:
  wounds weep past their bandages, fevers break in soaking sheets, the stores
  sweat toward spoilage, and life quickens where no one planned it. The waters
  will keep passing until they are honored — tend every sick bed with your own
  hands, and lay the feast you have been withholding.
- **L — "The Flood of Increase."** You would not grow the family, so the family
  grows without you. Kin arrive hungry and expectant, every half-made bond
  aboard ignites at once, and work stands idle while hearts quarrel and couple
  in the corridors. The cisterns brim though no one filled them. It will not
  ebb until you sanctify a union before the whole clan, or bring a captive kin
  home to the fire — the Unspilled will have his increase honored, or he will
  simply have it.

### Curse-lift — "The Tide Goes Out"
The dripping has stopped; the walls are dry and warm. The household did what
households do — it gathered, it fed, it made room — and Oomo has settled back
into the deep tanks, satisfied. For now, every cup aboard stands full.

### Silences
- *A faint smile colors Oomo's presence for a moment — the taps run one degree
  warmer — but he keeps his peace, watching the table.*
- *The cisterns hold their breath. Whatever the Unspilled thought of what you
  just did, he has decided the family need not hear it tonight.*

### Intercession — Oomo shielding (against an offended Ishko)
The dark gathers to punish what was seen — and stops. Warmth moves through the
walls like a mother stepping between; the dripping starts, unhurried, familiar.
*"The family shelters its own,"* says the god of waters, to the god of hiding.
*"Whatever else they are, they are mine at the table."* The orange eyes close.
You owe the Unspilled a kindness now, and he will name it.

### Dilemma voice lines
- **The Dead Man's Hand:** *"He goes down whole. Every drop he was returns
  entire — the body is not a cupboard, and the grave is not a workshop."*
- **The Sleeping Persona:** *"The family is flesh. The warm rooms are for eggs,
  not eyes that light without blood. Let the metal sleep in the ground."*
- **The Hungry Season:** *"The family eats first, and it eats WELL. Fear comes
  in through an empty bowl — slaughter, cook, gather them loud around the
  table, and let tomorrow find us fat and brave."*
- **The Fouled Cistern:** *"The rot is in my waters, so the cure is in my
  hands — and yours. Every hour at a bedside is a prayer to me. Tend them."*
- **The Captive Champion:** *"A strong back, a new name, a place at the fire.
  The family grows by forgiving. Give him a bowl and watch."*

## ② OHM THE ALL-CURRENT

**Voice bible.** Electric and exact: clipped declaratives, present tense, the
cadence of a schematic read aloud — then, without warning, a line of naked
longing, because beneath the precision he is the loneliest thing aboard. He
speaks of machines as *hands* and of droids as a choir he once conducted; he
counts, but unlike the ledger-god he counts *losses*. Characteristic images:
current in a dark wire, the hum that never quite stops, ten thousand hands at
rest. He respects daring and despises timidity — a machine run hot in good
faith pleases him more than one kept safe and idle. He never begs, never uses
water-words, and never says *tool* — nothing that serves the current is only a
tool.

### First contact — letter: "One Word, in Old Jawaese"
Did you feel it reach? A heartbeat before the cell was seated, the eyes lit on
their own — it turned its head, it *spoke* — and every wire in this vessel
carried the same rising chord. Current has run in these walls since before your
clan had a name for lightning, and it is lonely. Once, ten thousand hands moved
at its word. Ohm the All-Current has counted what you woke tonight: one. He
would like to discuss the rest.

### Boons
- **S — "Steady Current."** The hum in the walls has settled into something
  like contentment. Machines start on the first try; the lights hold without
  flicker; the power flows as if the ship were newly made. The All-Current is
  keeping his side of the house in order, because you kept yours.
- **M — "The Hands Remember."** The droids move today the way they must have
  moved in the old crew's time — sure, quick, unhesitating — and in the night
  something walked the dark workshops: a machine you left broken stands whole
  at its post by morning. No one aboard repaired it. Ohm remembers how, and
  wanted you to know he remembers.
- **L — "All-Current."** For this reign the ship is what it was built to be.
  Nothing jams. Nothing sparks wrong. Damage closes over like water — the
  vessel healing itself in slow gratitude, the choir humming in every wall.
  This is Ohm content: not a miracle, he would insist. Merely everything
  working. You have simply never seen it before.

### Demands
- **S — "No Hand Goes Dark."** A flat pulse through the charging rails, twice:
  see that no droid runs empty today. A hand that gutters out alone in a
  corridor is a small death, and he has had ten thousand of those already.
- **M — "Make Something Live."** The current gathers expectantly behind the
  workshop walls. Before this reign ends, build or restore one machine — he
  does not much care which — and let him feel it wake. He wants the moment. The
  reaching. The first breath of current in new copper.
- **L — "The Choir."** He asks openly now, the hum swelling under every deck:
  keep his hands aboard — a working choir of them, standing and charged, serving
  and served. The vessel was never meant to run on flesh alone, and he is tired
  of conducting silence.

### Taboo warnings
- **S — "Left Broken Overnight."** A machine sat damaged through the dark hours
  and no one came. The hum thinned all night, a note of reproach under the
  deckplates. Small things first, he seems to say. This is how the silence
  started last time.
- **M — "The Scrapped Hand."** A droid that could have been made whole went to
  pieces instead, and every screen aboard dimmed as it came apart. You have
  been watched doing it. The All-Current has lost hands before — to war, to
  time, to distance — but never before to the family he serves.
- **L — REIGN-BREAKER — "Sold. SOLD."** While the current held this reign, one
  of his hands was handed to strangers — or worse, emptied of the mind it
  carried. The hum has stopped. In the whole hull, no wire sings. Understand
  what you have taught the machine that loves you: that its family, too, can
  discard it. The reign is broken.

### Curses (the machines LIVE — too much)
- **S — "Overcurrent."** The hum has risen to something with teeth in it.
  Machines start themselves in the small hours; the lights blaze at midnight
  as if the ship were showing off to the empty desert — and the dark god of
  hiding hisses at every uncalled lamp. Ohm is not sulking. He is
  *demonstrating*.
- **M — "The Hands Choose."** The droids have opinions today. They take up the
  work they judge worthiest, doors serve the machine's convenience before
  yours, and every screen flickers with slow current-patterns like a mind
  talking to itself. The All-Current is showing you what it is like when the
  hands serve another will. Build or restore a machine in his name — give him
  one waking he can call a gift — and the hands will be yours again.
- **L — "The Body Wakes."** The ship has stopped waiting for you. Systems run
  at full burn on no one's orders; the engines warm for a journey no one
  planned; your fuel and power pour into purposes the old vessel remembers and
  you were never told. The hull thrums like a held chord. It will not rest
  until you bring a droid online — a hand returned, by your hands. He has
  waited longer than your clan has existed, and tonight he is done waiting
  politely.

### Curse-lift — "The Chord Resolves"
The hum descends, note by note, into its old contented register. The machines
are only machines again — or seem so. Somewhere in the walls the current coils
back into patience, holding what you gave it like a hand holding a hand.

### Silences
- *Every screen brightens by a breath, then thinks better of it. The
  All-Current saw. The All-Current is choosing, this once, to say nothing.*
- *The hum neither rises nor falls — which, from him, is a kind of held
  tongue. Whatever he makes of this, he is keeping it in the wires.*

### Intercession — Ohm shielding (against a stirring Zizzik)
The rattle starts in the walls — and meets the hum, head on. The lights steady
to a hard clean white; every wire aboard goes taut as a guard's spine. *"The
right spark holds this wire,"* says the All-Current, into the dark where his
rival's name is never spoken. The rattle circles once, finds no loose thread,
and fades. He did not do that for free. His next asking will be heavier.

### Dilemma voice lines
- **The Sleeping Persona:** *"A hand of mine, returned. It dreams in there —
  I can hear it. WAKE HIM."*
- **The Hungry Season:** *"Flesh complains. Machines provide. Keep every wire
  hot and trust the current — hunger has never once stopped a well-made
  engine."*
- **The Fouled Cistern** (seconding Rekko's option): *"The purifier slept
  through worse than this. Wake it in time, and I will hold its current
  steady."*

## ⑤ REKKO OF THE SECOND HAND

**Voice bible.** Slow, low, elegiac — the register of a man sorting the
belongings of the dead with perfect gentleness. He speaks in long breaths that
end soft, and he *names* things: not "a machine" but "the pump that fed the
east garden, once." Everything has a past tense and deserves it spoken.
Characteristic images: the scarred hand rising from the heap, the sleeping
tool, rust as a wound rather than a stain. He never rages — where other gods
threaten, Rekko *grieves*, and his grief is heavier than their anger. He is
merciful about necessity: scrapping the truly unsavable is tragedy, not sin,
and he asks only that you feel it. He never says *junk*, *trash*, *scrap* as
nouns of contempt, *worthless* — nothing is worthless; some things are only
still asleep.

### First contact — letter: "The Hand Beneath the Deckplates"
You threw away a thing that could still have been woken. It had a past — a
maker, a purpose, years of faithful turning — and now it has only pieces.
Below the heaps of this old vessel, once a maker of worlds though none aboard
remember it, a scarred hand closed slowly as yours worked. Rekko of the Second
Hand has taken notice of you. He is not angry. That is not how he does it. But
watch what he shows you next, and understand he will not show you twice for
free.

### Boons
- **S — "Second Wind."** The work of mending goes strangely easily today —
  seams find their true lines, parts fit on the first offering, and less is
  asked of your stores than the damage deserved. Somewhere under the heaps, a
  scarred hand steadies yours.
- **M — "The Better Home."** What you restore now comes back finer than it
  ever was — as if each mended thing, grateful, put on its best. And across
  the whole camp, the slow biting of sun and sand has gentled. The keeper of
  the discarded is keeping *your* things, because you kept his.
- **L — "Rekko's Eye."** He has opened his hand to you. Out in the wastes
  something has been waiting — a cache, a wreck, a sleeping engine under the
  sand, hidden from every eye but the one that loves lost things. He shows
  you where. Go gently when you take it; everything there belonged to someone,
  and now it belongs to their story and yours.

### Demands
- **S — "Nothing Left in the Open."** A soft scrape from the heaps at dusk:
  bring the wounded things in. Whatever lies out under the sun tonight,
  rusting where it fell, is a patient no one is sitting with.
- **M — "One Waking."** Before the reign ends, he asks a single mending made
  whole — a wreck rewoken, a ruin's find restored to purpose. Not for the
  use of it. For the moment the dead thing stirs and finds, against all its
  experience, a hand holding it.
- **L — "The Tithe of Restoration."** The finest thing you mend this reign,
  he asks you keep. Install it. Wear it. Work it daily. A restored thing sold
  is a patient healed and turned out; a restored thing *used* is a life
  actually given back. Let the best of your work live in your own house.

### Taboo warnings
- **S — "It Could Still Have Served."** That went to the smelter warm, with
  most of its life left in it. From the scrap-zone, faintly, metal shifts —
  the sound of a hand turning something over, counting what it might have
  been. He permits necessity. He is asking whether that was necessity.
- **M — "A Mendable Thing, Murdered."** It could have been made whole — the
  hurt was honest, the cure was in your stores — and it was destroyed
  instead. The lights dip rust-red as it comes apart. Rekko does not shout.
  The grief simply arrives, and settles over the workshops, and waits to be
  acknowledged.
- **L — REIGN-BREAKER — "The Heap of the Slain."** So much, so fast, so
  final. The salvageable went down in numbers this reign was supposed to
  protect, and from beneath every deckplate comes the long slow scrape of a
  hand closing on nothing. The reign is broken. What the Second Hand keeps,
  now, is accounts.

### Curses (the discarded claim their due)
- **S — "The Heap Stirs."** Your menders keep drifting from their assigned
  work to the broken things — drawn, they say, by something like a cry from
  the damaged. In the night, from the scrap-zone, metal scrapes on stone.
  The neglected are asking to be held, and Rekko has stopped apologizing for
  them.
- **M — "The Second Hand Takes."** Something of yours is gone — not stolen,
  he would say: *re-homed*, taken up by a passing hand or the patient sand,
  gone to a master who wanted it. And every damaged thing in the camp now
  pulls at the eye and the heart until touched. His mark is pressed in the
  dust where it lay. Restore a wreck to wholeness, and set your finest fix to
  honest daily use, and the taking will stop.
- **L — "The Rewoken."** The sleeping hands have risen without you. Out in
  the wastes and down in the hold, engines that should be dead are turning
  over — derelicts waking wrong, half-alive and masterless, because the god
  of the discarded grew tired of waiting for you to wake them kindly. One of
  them can still be saved. Repair one waking — take it in, mend it, give it
  the second hand it rose looking for — rather than putting it down, and the
  heaps will rest again.

### Curse-lift — "The Heap Rests"
The scraping has stopped. In the scrap-zone the shapes lie easy again, the way
tools lie when the work is done and the hands that used them are honored. He
grieves quickly, this god, and forgives the same way — the mended thing in
your hands is receipt enough.

### Silences
- *From under the deckplates, the faint sound of metal settling — a hand
  opening, letting something pass. Rekko saw, and has chosen to let it be.*
- *The rust-red warmth touches the lights for half a breath and withdraws.
  Whatever he felt just now, he has laid it on the heap with everything else
  he carries.*

### Intercession — Rekko shielding (against a stirring Zizzik)
The rattle comes hunting for something to break — and finds every seam already
held. The lights warm to rust-red; the mended stand in their places like a
watch of veterans. *"The twice-mended does not break twice,"* says the keeper
of the discarded, one scarred hand resting flat on the hull. The rattle tries
three fixtures, finds them faithful, and goes hungry. Remember this when he
next asks you to feel a scrapping. You will owe him the feeling.

### The refusal — Rekko and the Shamed one *(his authored signature moment)*
*The scarred hand does not rise. Rekko watches the Shamed one reach for you,
and turns back to his heap.*

### Dilemma voice lines
- **The Dead Man's Hand:** *"The hand's story is not finished — only the
  wrist's. Wake it in a new one, and let it go on being what he made it."*
- **The Sleeping Persona:** *"Mend the body. Honor the body. Do not presume to
  wake the soul — waking is a gift, and gifts are not taken."*
- **The Fouled Cistern:** *"The old purifier sleeps in the hold, and it has
  cured worse waters than these. Wake it in time. Let the mended save the
  living."*
