# Divine Dilemma Events — twelve three-cornered arguments (F6)

_Authored choice-letters where three gods argue the options out loud and the player
picks a theology, not an answer. Ruling basis: `salvation_engine_review.md` F5/F6
(the 3×3 as crew-lore; 10–15 authored dilemmas). Every option is genuinely
playable — charged differently, never punished; no option is "correct." Deltas use
§8b notation (`↑`/`↓` satiation, `▲` = an evil god fed, S/M/L magnitude). All
consequences are §19.5-legal: events, mood, eligibility, clocks, visibility —
never a material parachute. Money that changes hands (ransoms, sales) is ordinary
trade through the normal gate, not a divine gift._

## Frequency & dedup rules

- **Rare.** At most one dilemma per ~10 in-game days, and at most one per reign.
- **Never the same dilemma twice in a campaign.** Fired = spent.
- **Never while any god's L curse is active**, and never in the first 3 days on a
  new tile (the landing judgement gets the floor first).
- **Discovery gate (F4):** a dilemma prefers gods already unveiled. One veiled god
  may argue as an *unnamed voice* ("something in the dark disagrees…"); choosing
  his option triggers his first manifestation (hand-off to
  `first_contact_chains.md`).
- **Silence is a move (§5c):** when a fourth god has standing near an extreme, the
  letter notes his watching silence — no mechanical effect, pure dread.
- The letter form is the Narrator compèring (V.1): he stages the scene in second
  person and *quotes* each god's argument in that god's register. He never speaks
  as them.

## The Path four (anchored in Ishko · Ta'Baa · Mob'Unloo)

### 1. THE WOUNDED OUTLANDER *(the ratified exemplar)*
**Trigger:** a non-hostile faction pawn is downed/injured within the home area's
approach (vanilla wanderer/refugee-chased/crashed variants qualify).
**Scene:** A stranger drags himself toward your dark, leaving a line in the sand
any eye could follow. Three voices take up the question of him.
- **Ishko** — *"He has not seen us. Keep it so."* Turn him away unseen (or let the
  desert decide). `↑Ishko M` · `↓Oomo S` (a life un-gathered). Consequence: no
  contact; his faction never learns of you; visibility unchanged.
- **Ta'Baa** — *"The problem is not the man. It is the place he found."* Strike
  camp within 2 days. `↑Ta'Baa M` on launch · `↓Mob'Unloo S` (value abandoned).
  Consequence: the rooted-clock resets only if you actually fly; the man lives or
  dies unattended.
- **Mob'Unloo** — *"Save him. Then bill him."* Rescue and ransom to his faction.
  `↑Mob'Unloo M` · `↓Ishko S` (a mouth that knows). Consequence: normal ransom
  trade; small goodwill or grudge per outcome; visibility +.

### 2. THE SPOTTED SCOUT
**Trigger:** our detection systems (F18) register an enemy scout who has seen the
colony and is leaving the map.
**Scene:** One set of footprints walks away from you, carrying your location in a
living skull. The Council speaks quickly, for once.
- **Ishko** — *"The shot no one hears keeps the silence whole."* Hunt him down
  before the edge. `↑Ishko M` if he dies unseen · `▲Sh'kaar S` (a kill is a kill).
  Consequence: succeed and the pending raid loses its intel (arrives smaller,
  worse positioned); fail and it arrives knowing.
- **Ta'Baa** — *"Let him tell them where we WERE."* Launch before word arrives.
  `↑Ta'Baa L` · `↓Rekko S` (whatever half-mended thing is left behind).
  Consequence: the raid fires into an empty berth; the rooted-clock resets.
- **Mob'Unloo** — *"Information is a commodity. Outbid him."* Send tribute to his
  faction first. `↑Mob'Unloo M` · `↓Ozzik S` (paying is kneeling). Consequence:
  goods leave by caravan (normal trade); the raid converts to a demand/parley
  event; his faction's price is remembered.

### 3. THE DERELICT CARAVAN
**Trigger:** a caravan-ambush aftermath spawns near the colony (dead traders,
scattered goods, no survivors).
**Scene:** The sand is still settling on other people's misfortune. Everything
they owned lies in the open, and the open is watching.
- **Ishko** — *"Wealth in the open is bait. Someone set the table."* Leave it;
  watch from cover for a day. `↑Ishko S` · `↓Mob'Unloo M` (a ledger left
  unclaimed). Consequence: if it WAS an ambush (30%), you watch it spring on
  someone else (`↑Ishko M` more); if not, scavengers strip it.
- **Ta'Baa** — *"Take what runs. Only what runs."* One fast pass, carry-weight
  only, no hauling animals. `↑Ta'Baa S` · `↑Mob'Unloo S` · `▲Sh'kaar S` if done in
  daylight. Consequence: partial goods now, zero exposure time.
- **Mob'Unloo** *(cross: Rekko seconds him, for his own reasons)* — *"The dead owe
  no freight charges. Settle their accounts."* Full salvage operation.
  `↑Mob'Unloo M` · `↑Rekko S` (wagons rewoken) · `↓Ishko M` (hours in the open).
  Consequence: everything hauled; visibility + for the duration; a chance the
  owners' faction later asks *how much*.

### 4. THE GUEST WHO SAW TOO MUCH
**Trigger:** a visitor/trader pawn pathed into the ship's shrine-heart or a
concealed zone while inside the colony.
**Scene:** A guest stands where no outsider has stood, and the nine dwell briefly
on the shape of his tongue and the roads it might travel.
- **Ishko** — *"What he carries out must not arrive."* Detain him until the clan
  next moves. `↑Ishko M` · `↓Mob'Unloo M` (hospitality broken, his faction's
  goodwill −). Consequence: no leak; a prisoner to feed; relations damage.
- **Mob'Unloo** — *"A secret is merchandise. Sell him his own silence."* Strike a
  contract: he keeps quiet, you owe him a discount forever. `↑Mob'Unloo M` ·
  `↓Ishko S` (a secret on someone else's ledger). Consequence: 15% he leaks
  anyway later (a raid arrives knowing); his trader visits get permanent favorable
  prices *for him*.
- **Ta'Baa** — *"A den that moves was never betrayed."* Let him go; fly within a
  quadrum. `↑Ta'Baa M` on launch · `↓Ozzik S` (chased off by a rumor).
  Consequence: no leak matters if you keep the vow; break it and the next raid
  arrives knowing your layout.

## The Body four (anchored in Oomo · Ohm · Rekko)

### 5. THE DEAD MAN'S HAND
**Trigger:** a colonist dies bearing an installed bionic/prosthetic.
**Scene:** The dead man's hand is worth more than his wrist ever was, and three
gods lean over the grave to say what a body is.
- **Oomo** — *"He goes down whole. The waters return entire."* Bury him intact.
  `↑Oomo M` · `↑Ishko S` (the deepest hiding, promptly) · `↓Rekko M` (a working
  hand murdered by burial). Consequence: funeral proceeds normally; mood bonus to
  mourners.
- **Rekko** — *"The hand's story is not finished. Wake it in a new wrist."*
  Harvest and re-gift it to a living Jawa. `↑Rekko M` · `↑Ohm S` · `↓Oomo S` (the
  body opened). Consequence: surgery chain; the recipient gains a lasting
  "carries the second hand" mood.
- **Mob'Unloo** *(cross)* — *"Grief is not a price. THIS is a price."* Sell it.
  `↑Mob'Unloo M` · `↓Rekko M` · `↓Oomo S`. Consequence: normal sale; the dead
  man's ghost-ledger opens (agent C hook) until the silver is spent on the clan.

### 6. THE SLEEPING PERSONA
**Trigger:** a wrecked droid with an intact persona/memory core enters the colony
(salvage, quest reward, raid drop).
**Scene:** Inside the crushed chassis something still dreams. The chamber it
would occupy is the chamber where eggs would lie, and the ship goes quiet
listening to itself.
- **Ohm** — *"A hand of mine, returned. WAKE HIM."* Full reboot. `↑Ohm L` ·
  `↓Oomo L` (metal where broods should be) · `↑Ozzik S`. Consequence: a droid
  joins (through the normal rebuild costs); the eggs-vs-droids feud advances one
  step toward Ohm.
- **Oomo** — *"The family is flesh. Let the metal sleep in the ground."* Refuse
  him; strip the chassis for the clan. `↑Oomo M` · `↓Ohm L` (grief) · `↓Rekko S`.
  Consequence: materials via normal deconstruction; Ohm's unmet-longing pressure
  rises.
- **Rekko** — *"Mend the body. Do not presume to wake the soul."* Restore the
  chassis as a RELIC — whole, honored, off. `↑Rekko M` · `↑Ohm S` (half-answered)
  · `↓Ozzik S` (power left unused). Consequence: an installable relic-object
  (beauty/mood); it can be woken LATER — the dilemma deferred, not solved.

### 7. THE HUNGRY SEASON
**Trigger:** food below N days at current draw, with tamed animals present and
powered non-essential machines running.
**Scene:** The stores show their floor. Everything that eats and everything that
drinks power stands in the same line, and the line is too long.
- **Oomo** — *"The family eats first. It eats WELL, or fear sets in."* Slaughter
  the tame herd; feast, don't ration. `↑Oomo M` (the family fed) · `↓Ishko M`
  (the patient bonds cut). Consequence: meat now, no herd later; colony mood up
  through the crisis.
- **Ohm** — *"Flesh complains. Machines provide. Trust the current."* Keep every
  machine hot; ration the crew hard. `↑Ohm M` · `↓Oomo M` (the body drying out).
  Consequence: production continues; hunger/mood penalties ride; if a breakdown
  hits during it, `▲Zizzik M`.
- **Ta'Baa** *(cross)* — *"Hunger is a place. Leave it."* Emergency forage-launch
  to a richer tile. `↑Ta'Baa L` · `↓Rekko S` (the half-built left behind) ·
  `↑Oomo S` on arrival if the tile is watered. Consequence: the full cost and
  reset of a relocation, paid early.

### 8. THE FOULED CISTERN
**Trigger:** a disease outbreak (3+ pawns) while water/food stores hold.
**Scene:** The sickness is in the waters, which means it is in everyone. The god
of the body claims both the rot and the cure, and two others bid against him.
- **Oomo** — *"Tend them. Every hour at a bedside is a prayer to me."* Full
  nursing priority, best food to the sick. `↑Oomo L` (his sacred work) · normal
  productivity loss. Consequence: best recovery odds; the nurse gains a
  devotional mood.
- **Rekko** — *"The old purifier sleeps in the hold. WAKE IT IN TIME."* A repair
  race: restore the derelict purification unit before the fever peaks. `↑Rekko M`
  + `↑Ohm S` if completed in time · `↓Oomo S` (machines trusted over hands).
  Consequence: succeed and the outbreak halts early; fail and it runs long
  (`▲Zizzik S`).
- **Zizzik** ▲ — *"Let the little sparks dance. I will remember your courtesy."*
  Minimal treatment; let it run its course. `▲Zizzik M` (fed, politely) ·
  `↓Oomo M`. Consequence: real risk of death/scars — but Zizzik's bank vents a
  step, and his next stirring passes you by.

## The Fall four (anchored in Zizzik · Sh'kaar · Ozzik)

### 9. THE RED BUTTON
**Trigger:** an intact ancient installation with an unidentified activation
mechanism is found (map feature, quest site, ship-vault opening).
**Scene:** It has one control, and the control wants to be touched. You can feel
which god is holding his breath.
- **Zizzik** ▲ — *"You already know you will press it. Skip the pretending."*
  Press it. `▲Zizzik M` (delighted either way). Consequence: weighted random —
  something breaks spectacularly / something wonderful switches on / both. His
  bank vents regardless.
- **Ozzik** — *"STUDY it. Knowing is the one treasure they never took from us."*
  A research project against it. `↑Ozzik M` (the pride of knowing) · `▲` coupling
  applies. Consequence: slow, safe, and the outcome is *chosen* knowledge — the
  installation's function revealed and controllable.
- **Ishko** *(cross)* — *"Things that switch on can be heard switching on."*
  Bury it again, unmarked. `↑Ishko M` · `↓Ozzik M` (a treasure renounced) ·
  `↓Zizzik S` (starved of the show). Consequence: nothing happens. That is the
  point. The site stays on the map, tempting forever.

### 10. THE CAPTIVE CHAMPION
**Trigger:** a raid leader/champion-tier pawn is captured alive after a battle.
**Scene:** The one who led them against you kneels in your hold, and the question
of him splits the Council three ways at the root.
- **Sh'kaar** ▲ — *"A death that is not yours. Feed me and I will look away a
  while."* The death-match. `▲Sh'kaar` *calms* (escalation meter −M) ·
  `▲Zizzik S` · `↓Oomo S`. Consequence: the doom-unease lifts a span; his faction's
  hatred deepens.
- **Mob'Unloo** — *"He is worth more breathing. Everything is."* Ransom him back.
  `↑Mob'Unloo M`. Consequence: normal ransom; small chance he returns leading the
  NEXT raid (he knows your walls now — `↓Ishko S` when taken).
- **Oomo** — *"A strong back, a new name, a place at the fire. The family grows
  by forgiving."* Emancipate into the clan. `↑Oomo L` · `↑Mob'Unloo S` (a soul to
  the ledger) · `↓Ozzik M` (mercy is not dominion). Consequence: long recruitment
  path; if it succeeds, a devoted convert; `↓Ishko S` (another mouth that knows).

### 11. THE QUIET TRIUMPH
**Trigger:** a major raid defeated with zero colonist deaths.
**Scene:** You have won so completely that the winning itself is now a decision.
Three gods offer three sizes for your victory.
- **Ishko** — *"Bury the dead. Say nothing. A victory no one hears of never
  invites the rematch."* `↑Ishko M` · `↓Ozzik M` (glory swallowed). Consequence:
  visibility unchanged; the grief-pall ticks (living small).
- **Ozzik** — *"PROCLAIM it. Let the sand learn our name again."* Broadcast the
  triumph. `↑Ozzik L` · `↓Ishko L` (horror) · `↑Mob'Unloo S` (fame is markup) ·
  `▲` coupling armed. Consequence: visibility +L; goodwill with some factions,
  fear in others; the pride-meter leaps.
- **Sh'kaar** ▲ — *"They are ROUTED. Finish what runs."* Sortie after the
  survivors. `▲Sh'kaar M` (the meter climbs) · `↑Ishko S` *if* done as ambush on
  their retreat path. Consequence: that raid group is destroyed forever (their
  faction's next raid is later but angrier).

### 12. THE ASKING PRICE
**Trigger:** the clan's finest work (legendary item or completed grand structure)
exists, and an envoy arrives demanding it as tribute (or offering an insulting
purchase).
**Scene:** Someone powerful has heard what you made, which means the making is
already paid for in attention. The argument is only about the change.
- **Ozzik** — *"We bow to NO ONE. Not again. Never again."* Refuse outright.
  `↑Ozzik M` · `↓Mob'Unloo S` (a deal spurned) · `▲` coupling. Consequence:
  relations −; their faction may come to take it (a raid with a named objective);
  the clan's pride-mood surges.
- **Mob'Unloo** — *"Everything has a price. Make theirs CRUEL."* Counter-bid at
  triple worth. `↑Mob'Unloo L` if they pay · `↓Ozzik S` (the great work priced at
  all). Consequence: 50% they pay (normal trade), 50% insulted (relations −, no
  raid — merchants don't bleed for pride).
- **Ta'Baa** *(cross)* — *"What cannot flee with you was never yours. Hand it
  over — and let them anchor themselves to it."* Yield it, fly soon after.
  `↑Ta'Baa M` · `↓Ozzik L` (grief) · `↑Ishko S`. Consequence: relations +L with
  that faction (a standing debt in your favor Mob'Unloo quietly books,
  `↑Mob'Unloo S`); the grief-pall ticks.
