## 1. What it is

Under the coldest mountains on the planet the crust is thickest and slowest, and slow
cold crystallization grows big. The voids the crystals grew in are the Deeps: enclosed,
warm-by-comparison, humming, lit from within by **lanternstone** — the blue native
crystal that carries light — and pastured by glow-fungi that the crystals eat. Nothing
here has a body in the ordinary sense. The oldest minds in the caverns cannot move at
all, and so they wear what the dead brought down with them. Nobody reaches this place
without excellent technology; that is why the caverns are full of it, walking.

## 2. Planetary position

**Beneath the deep nightside × the crust anomaly.** Not a surface regime at all: the
Deeps take their energy from the aurora's ground currents overhead (piezoelectric
lattices wired to the sky, §3), their matter from the solutions that seep down (fuel
snow, ammonia), and their light from what grows in the dark.

## 3. Driving forces

### Crystal geology

The nightside's high, cold, thick crust is where crystallization runs slowest and
largest. **Lanternstone** (owner-ratified name) is the native blue crystal — luminous,
volatile, alive at the top of its size range. Among it, rarer, **kyber**: quiet where
lanternstone glows, cold where it hums — **the reason the Rakata came: they had mastered
kyber technology, and this planet has it.** A third, rarer still: the **mindstone**
(working name, owner's rename owed) — the strangely sentient crystal of §4.

### Crystalline life, from physics

- **Piezoelectric nerves**: stress makes voltage in a crystal lattice — a crystalline
  nervous system is real physics — fed by the auroral ground currents above
  (`the_propane_lakes.md` §3): the Deeps are wired to the sky, and a reconnection storm
  is a feast day.
- **Accretion**: they grow from the solutions seeping down; **cleaving** is birth (the
  donor's huge crystal "large enough that a smaller crystal could break off" is a
  parent) and, done fast, violence.
- **Photonic metabolism**: they eat light. The glow-fungi are their pasture; **your
  lamps are a feast** — the ruled darkness mechanic made literal: *we are blazing beacons
  begging to be messed with.*
- **Resonance**: a lattice talks by singing; the caverns hum.
- **The shatter**: death is volatile (donor lore kept).

### The well-provisioned dead (owner's ruling)

**Nobody can get here without high technology**, so every ruin and every corpse was —
or still is — superbly equipped: exo-suits, droids, power cells, cutting gear. And the
Deeps' minds cannot move. They wear what came down.

## 4b. Weather and conditions

Enclosed: `Calm` only (donor). **Darkness is the standing condition** (ruled v1): light is
carried, rationed, and read by everything alive. **Collapse** (ruled v1): dust trails and
sand piles warn; the caverns grumble; Geonosian sonic does not trigger it. The hum.

## 5. Always true

- Every Deep is reached by one of two mouths (§8) and is **persistent** — the same cavern
  every visit, never regenerated (ruled).
- Lanternstone glows; kyber is quiet; the mindstone is aware.
- Light draws what lives here; the well-lit expedition pays.
- The dead are well-equipped, and some of them walk.
- The Rakata mined here; later high-technology parties tried; the Junkers **occasionally
  try and usually fail to get far enough** (owner's ruling).

## 8. Inhabited objects and the plot that lives down here (owner-authored, 2026-09-06)

### The two mouths (owner: "that's probably enough for now")
- **Natural emergences** — lanternstone breaking the surface where a void reaches the
  roof: a geode open to the aurora, glowing from below. *Lantern Deeps* is named for the
  light these leak.
- **Old mineshafts inside ruined mining facilities** — Rakatan kyber mines and the later
  high-technology expeditions that followed them; the shafts still descend; the
  facilities still hold the gear of the well-provisioned dead.

### Who came
The **Rakata**, for kyber. Later parties with excellent technology (the Helix? Imperial
survey? — owner's call at `KYBER_TRADE_PLOT_1`). **The Junkers**, occasionally, usually
failing before they reach a mine (owner).

### 🔴 The mechanoid origin (owner, verbatim in intent — `MECHANOID_ORIGIN_CANON_1`)
A special droid mind made from the mindstone is extremely intelligent and capable
because *it is not really a droid any more — a new race.* **That explains what the
mechanoids and the Rust Cathedral are: a very different form of AI, not so artificial
after all, and the Deeps' crystal minds are their "wild cousins."** They may **talk to the
player**, asking for help and defense against **"the insane enslaved one"** — the Rust
Cathedral — which seeks to mine them all up and use them in its never-ending hunger.
Somewhere out here: **a mechanoid production facility still working**, restocking the
**Forgotten Sentries** (owner's spelling "Senties" read as Sentries — confirm) at a very
slow rate — or a slumbering one the Rust Cathedral would like turned back on. **Restore
its "true children" and it might no longer love its Free Droids quite so much.**

### 🔴 The kyber trade (owner — `KYBER_TRADE_PLOT_1`)
Selling kyber is **extremely illegal to the Empire**: heat rises substantially per sale;
**the Hutts' positive interest rises** alongside. It may also bring **"alleged" Jedi
calling from the Moisture Farmers**, asking how it was obtained — and a small plot in
which some kyber is **"donated to the cause" and smuggled offworld at a rendezvous** to
help the Rebellion *elsewhere* (🔴 still no way to help it here: they lost, that's it).

## 9. Artistic theme

**"A blue lantern burning under the mountain, and a dead miner's suit walking toward it."**

- **Light:** lanternstone blue as the only ambient; glow-fungi pastures; the carried lamp
  as a spotlight everything turns toward; kyber's quiet glint; the mindstone's
  unsettling steadiness.
- **Palette:** blue-on-black, fungal grey and green, the metal and ceramic of superb
  dead technology, the orange of the imported crystal.
- **Silhouette language:** facets, geodes, lattices; a Creep's slow spread; a Cleaver's
  fracture-line motion; an exo-suit standing too still.
- **Sound:** the hum, rising to a Chorus; the click of a suit's servo; the shatter.
