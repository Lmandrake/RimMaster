## 1. What it is

From orbit it is the bluest thing on the planet; underfoot it is colorless. A plateau of
the oldest ice on Ash'karr, scoured bare by cold wind draining off higher ground, its
"sand" ice grain, its "snow" that same sand blowing. **Nothing here is blue** — pure ice
and hydrocarbon nanoparticles scatter the red away and leave the blue (owner's ruling).
It is usually clear, and silent, and beautiful, and almost empty. Then the ablation line
gives something up — a meteorite, a hull, a freeze-dried body that fell from orbit, or a
cocoon that has waited since the war — and the beauty has a price tag on it.

## 2. Planetary position

**Deep nightside regime (eternal dark, near-zero energy budget) × the ablation anomaly.**
A blue-ice field in the real sense: ancient compressed ice exposed where katabatic wind
strips the frost — and, as on every such field, **a concentrator of what fell from the
sky**: the flowing ice carries buried objects up, and ablation leaves them on top.
Broken into lobes (§0) so it never rings the antistellar point.

## 3. Driving forces

### The phase boundary

🔑 **The biome's median temperature is propane's condensation point** (−42 °C). Butane
(−1 °C) and pentane (+36 °C) are liquid-to-solid here — which is exactly why the local
biology stores energy in them; methane (−161 °C) stays gas — which is why it is the
exchange metabolite. Everything alive here lives two degrees on the warm side of a phase
line. Two degrees the other way, and the propane pools: that is PropaneLakes, next rung
down, same mechanism.

### The haze, and the "snow"

- **The Haze** (owner-authored): usually it is clear — much less shelter than the crags —
  but sometimes a haze descends: **cryogenic hydrocarbons precipitating out of the
  atmosphere** at their dew point, not yet enough to pool, the propane lakes' mechanism
  in its first stage.
- **Ice-sand drift** (owner's ruling; the donor's BlueSnow re-skinned): it still "snows"
  here — but it is **water-ice sand blowing in the wind**, piling against structures,
  eroding the landscape, burying and unburying.

### Hydrocarbon biology (owner-ratified, in full)

🔴 **No water-based life.** Plants and animals run on **hydrocarbon plumbing**: no
glucose; metal-organic compounds trapped in cell membranes, a hydrocarbon soup for blood;
**butane and pentane as the power-storage molecules**; **methane exchange as the basis of
metabolism**, with some fast creatures toying with the extreme energy of oxidation.
Catastrophically reactive in a warm oxygen environment — the extreme cold keeps them
stable, more or less. Without the polar water molecule, transport and chemistry are
simply less efficient — so **plants stay small**, and they are staggeringly beautiful:
**transparent**, needing no light and so growing **fractal branches for gas exchange** in
place of leaves — ferns, dandelions, fuzzballs. Cold enough that starting them burning is
very hard; when they do go, **it is not a fire, it is an explosive chain reaction.**

### The ice hands up the weapon (owner-ratified)

The Horrors' cold-storage forms are **in the ice**: the war's "**spore forms**" of the
Assailant weapon — **hardened for siege, building and vehicle attack**, not the organic,
fleshy, infectious forms the warm sides harbor. One weapon, many forms, fluid DNA, one
program: **eradicate the ecosystem via destructive assimilation, with templated
destructive forms to draw upon.** They emerge **after thawing or on trigger events** —
explosive release at proximity using stored energy reserves — and the reserve is the
biome's own chemistry: a crysalis is a butane charge with a program. 🔑 **Mining is
warming**: cut an ice block, the quarry face crosses the phase line, and the thing that
waited releases.

## 4. How the biology adapted

**The admission test:** hydrocarbon-metabolic, cold-stable, warm-reactive. Nothing
water-based lives here.

- ⭐ **The Swallowers** (herbivores, owner's mechanism): they close their mouths over a
  plant, tear it up **whole-rooted**, and swallow — keeping oxygen, and hence the risk,
  away until the anaerobic gut. Slow, sealed, armored; wound one in the wrong place and
  it goes up.
- ⭐ **The Burners** (owner-ratified): the fast oxidizers — **their bodies blaze with a
  halo of blue fire when they move extremely swiftly or fight hard**, and **they go up in
  an explosion if wounded too greatly.** Boom beasts from hell, skinned right from
  physics. They run hot and die young.
- **The Pickers**: scavengers working the ablation line where the sky's debris surfaces.
- **The flora**: transparent fractal ferns, dandelions and fuzzballs — every one a charge
  of liquid butane; wild only (§6).
- **The spore forms** (injected, `HORRORS_RAIDING_FACTION_1`): not residents — cargo in
  the ice.

## 4b. Weather

- **Clear** — the common state; less shelter than the crags.
- **The Haze** — cryogenic hydrocarbon precipitation (re-skin of BlueSnow's *substance*).
- **Ice-sand drift** — the "snow": blowing ice sand, drifts against structures, erosion.
- **Ice fog** (re-skins of Blue Fogstorm / Purple Fog): diamond-dust at −40 — ranged
  accuracy and *range* reduced, movement slowed. The sensor-shadow theme's second home.
- 🔴 No rain (R-H1: greatest altitudes only; this is not one).

## 5. Always true

- Nothing is blue up close; the color is scattering, and it is real from a distance.
- The ground is ancient water ice; the sand is ice; the snow is sand.
- The ablation line surfaces the fallen: **meteoritic metal, crashed wreckage,
  freeze-dried corpses that fell from orbit** — and the war's cocoons.
- Every native organic is a fuel charge stable only in the cold; warmth is detonation.
- The Horrors are not residents; they are delivered — by thaw, or by trigger.
- The quarries have been tried before, and it is all still here.

## 6. Never true — 🔴 HARD BANS (linter-checkable)

1. 🔴 **No water-based plants or animals** — a def with an ordinary water metabolism
   resident here is a violation (grimpepper and the donor zoo evicted).
2. 🔴 **No cultivation of the hydrocarbon flora** (owner: beyond the scenario's scope;
   also the infinite-generator guardrail) — a growable/sowable def is a violation.
3. 🔴 **No warm-safe hydrocarbon organics** — plant, produce or creature-product defs
   lacking the warm-detonation behavior are a violation; you never bring one indoors.
4. 🔴 **No bullseye** — the Blue Desert never occupies all bearing sectors of its arc
   band; the lobe mosaic (§0) is law.
5. 🔴 **No second highland biome** — Bluefire Mountains (`BiomeGRimphire`) is NOT used;
   the ring's highland sectors go to the caverns.
6. 🔴 **No Horrors settlement, and no bioweapon-toned ground** — the spore forms are
   injections; the war-legacy split holds.
7. 🔴 **No rain** (R-H1).
8. 🔴 **The recognizability rule applies**; the icon carve-out protects mynock and
   neebray from the legacy cast.

## 7. Uniquely available

- ⭐ **Blue ice — mineable water-ice blocks of distilled-level purity** (owner: "golden,"
  quite valuable): the top row of the water taxonomy (`WATER_KINDS_TAXONOMY_1`). And the
  loop it carries: **quarry → warmth → thaw → siege form.** A horror pops up out of an
  ice quarry.
- ⭐ **The fallen** — meteoritic metal, crashed wreckage, orbital freeze-dried corpses
  (the Empire's orbital holdings are the unwitting supplier): salvage concentrated by
  the field, free for the finding, guarded by whatever thawed beside it.
- **Hydrocarbon flora as fuel** — wild-harvest only, cold-handled only; a chemfuel
  windfall that explodes if you warm it.
- **Burner fuel glands / Swallower gut-oil** — the fauna's own storage chemistry as
  harvestable fuel, at the fauna's own risk.
- **Ice fog as cover** — knife-and-sensor country by different physics than the crags.
- **The quarries' epochs** — the archaeology of every civilization that tried this.

## 8. Inhabited objects

- ⭐ **Injectable ancient ice quarries** (owner-authored): not the first time anyone
  thought of it. **Waves of different civilizations trying to mine the ice, drink the
  propane** — all still here, frozen in time, **alien script covering everything in
  unknown warnings.** Each quarry a tableau of one epoch's attempt and how it ended.
  Nobody who can read the warnings mines; everybody who mines learns what they said.
- **The weapon layer** (injected, `HORRORS_RAIDING_FACTION_1`): crysalises in the ice,
  sinkholes, nests — surfaced by ablation, triggered by warmth.
- **Fall debris fields** — the ablation line's harvest, and the freeze-dried dead.
- **Drift-buried ruins** — structures the ice sand has piled against for centuries,
  eroded on the windward face, entombed on the lee.

## 9. Artistic theme

**"Extremely blue from far away; colorless up close."**

- **Light:** starlit clear as the baseline — the widest sky on the planet; the Haze as a
  soft blue-white descending veil; ice fog glowing faintly; the Burners' **halos of blue
  fire** the only warm light for a hundred kilometers.
- **Palette:** scattering-blue at distance, glass-clear and grey underfoot; transparent
  flora refracting; rust and char where the fallen lie; the alien script's unknown color.
- **Silhouette language:** fractal — ferns, dandelion-heads, fuzzballs, transparent and
  small; sealed slow Swallowers; the sprint-and-blaze of a Burner; drifts leaning on
  ruined quarry gear; a cocoon face in a cut ice wall.
- **Motion:** ice sand streaming; the haze settling; the sudden violence of a detonation.
- **Sound:** wind over ice; the hiss of drift; the crack of a thaw; then the boom.
