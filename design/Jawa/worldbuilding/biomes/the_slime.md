# The Slime — definition sheet

_Owner + BENCH, 2026-09-06, written in conversation over three passes. Defines
`AB_GelatinousSuperorganism` (Alpha Biomes — donor content incorporated wholesale, then
bent). **The biome's name is THE SLIME** (owner's pick). Thematic handle: **the living
registry** — and its image: **a body the size of a country, reading everything that
touches it.**_

🔑 **Read against `the_rot.md`.** The two are siblings — **both from the Assailant
bioweapon, meeting different ends when left alone too long with nature** (owner's ruling).
In the Rot, nature won: the weapon was digested and only its useful genes kept. Here **the
weapon won — and victory ruined it as a weapon**: it ingested the entire ecosystem's
genetic information, was overwhelmed, and the hostile generation/weaponization function
was overwritten by ordinary biological imperatives — survival, reproduction, and
maintenance of genetic identity, where its identity IS the archive. *(The third panel —
where neither side won and the weapon is still fighting — is `assailant_weapon_remnants.md`:
a raiding faction and the Overdrive site, not a biome.)*

## 0. The measurements everything rests on

MEASURED 2026-09-05 off `world/ASHKARR_WORLDMAP_tiles.csv`: **96 tiles** — the smallest
defined biome — in four patches: **Slough (58)**, Glass Reach (15), Nightspill (14),
Chalk Marches (9). Arc p10 84.6 / median **90.0** / p90 99.1: it straddles the terminator
itself. Temp median 13.4 °C, total range −2.7…21.9 — **the mildest, most stable climate
on Ash'karr**. Elevation median 3 m, 88/96 tiles dead flat, zero water and river tiles —
but it sits at sea level in dead-river basin country (Glass Reach and Chalk Marches are
Wasteland basin regions): it grew where the rivers used to end.

🔴 **One organism, connected** (owner's ruling): the four patches are lobes of a single
body, joined beneath the basin floor along the old riverbeds — **though the planet's
residents dispute this**, and the dispute is in-world texture, not an open design
question.

Donor inventory taken in: the all-slime terrain suite (hardened slime f0.1, rich slime
f0.4, slime-grass f1.0, slimy mud, liquid slime — every surface takes footprints),
movement difficulty 4, forage 0.75 raw slime (*"edible. Barely."*), fastest regrow in the
donor mod (13 d), the empty disease list, the **slime compressor** (ooze pressed into
usable sandstone — construction here means eating the archive), the slime-in-eyes
terrain-attack, and the fauna (§4). 🔴 The donor's 15-species vanilla zoo (rat, monkey,
elephant, rhino, horse…) is **evicted wholesale**.

## 1. What it is

A country-sized body lying in the mildest weather on the planet, wearing a landscape as
its skin: fields of its own grass, groves of its own trees, herds of its own cells. It
was a weapon once. It ate everything it was sent against, and what it ate unmade it —
now it is a **living registry and database, forever seeking to ingest, analyze,
recombine, store, and multiply**, still faithfully serving a master a thousand years
decayed. It shows little to no sentient activity — and some fear that will not last:
that one day it becomes another **Mnggal-Mnggal** and spreads exponentially. So far, it
only reads.

## 2. Planetary position

**Terminator regime × the confluence anomaly.** Arc 90, sea level, in the basins where
the dead rivers end — the planet's **inbox**. Everything converges on the terminator:
the seas sit in it, the rivers drain toward it, the stormwall precipitates onto it,
dayside runoff and nightside sporefall meet across it. And it is the one strip with no
thermal violence, where long molecules survive. An archive could keep itself nowhere
else — chemistry stable, and the whole world's mail delivered to the door.

## 3. Driving forces

### The archive, and how it circulates

🔴 **The distributed genome** (owner's ruling): no part of the body holds every gene.
Information **slurms through sluggish currents** inside the organism, carrying both its
weaponized imperative and its loyalty to the decayed master. The archive is a
**circulating library — the shelf you need is somewhere out on the tide.** Every genome
that ever touched the Slime is in circulation: every creature that waded in, every Star
Wars race that ever visited, every dead thing the rivers delivered for a thousand years.

### Slimification

🔴 **The disease** (owner's ruling): unprotected organic life in contact converts in
**~1 week** — the Slime reading you so thoroughly that you become an entry. Residents
survive by resistance: **mycoid symbiotes** (a Rot export) confer it, or the
**Slime-resistant gene** can be taken from the machine itself — almost ironically — at
the standard random-gene price (§7). Everything living here is **resistant or being
transformed**; that is the roster admission test, literally.

### Slime rain — the organism waters itself

🔴 Owner-authored: when the body needs a drink it **emits volatiles that condense into
precipitation** — a slimy, slick rain, arriving as brief but intense flooding that
drinks back into the terrain rapidly. ⭐ **The floodwater is potable and useful** — and
this is a ruled carve-out, not a violation: R-H1 (rain only at the greatest altitudes) stands because this is not
weather, it is a body irrigating itself; the sky over the Slime rains only because the
Slime asks it to.

### The cure that starts a clock

⭐ Eating slime **instantly cures any poison in you, and most radiation** — its
toxin-rendering power is one of its wondrous aspects. And then the clock runs: the Slime
you swallowed begins converting you, and must be killed with **an intentionally toxic
injection derived from the Rot** before slimification completes. The gut's poison is the
library's antidote — the two Assailant siblings, pharmacologically entangled.

## 4. How the biology adapted

- **The substrate:** Green Goo (donor commonality 2.0), whose own lore closes the case —
  *"although they look like individuals, in reality they are part of a greater
  gelatinous superorganism, with only one objective: grow and expand."*
- **Flyers are the aristocracy** (owner's ruling): free movement over the body, immune
  to the wading trap that takes everything else. The aerofleet's hydrogen-float is the
  template.
- **The filter-feeder line** (owner's ruling): a whole family of scooping-mouthed
  animals grazing the Slime itself like krill-eaters — new art, NEW-ART ledger.
- **Conventional munchers** browse the pseudo-plants and hunt the pseudo-herds.
- **The resistant characters:** the donor's engineered oddities read as immune-system
  champions — the giant trash-eating acanthamoeba, the corrosive Helixien slug, the
  iron-shelled plasmorph. Rendered down or milked, the resistant natives supply the
  resistance economy.
- **The trace tail** of exotics at 0.001–0.07 commonality is the archive **running
  experiments** — recombinations walking around in small numbers, kept if interesting,
  reabsorbed if not.

## 4b. Weather

Donor vanilla weather re-skinned: Clear stays common (the mild strip earns it); the
donor's rain and snow become **slime rain** (§3) — organism-induced, flooding, fast-
draining, potable. No meteorological water rain, ever (R-H1).

## 5. Always true

- The land is a body; walking on it is being read. Movement is punishing, footprints
  always show, and the ground can strike back (slime in the eyes).
- The archive circulates: any given gene is *somewhere*, never everywhere.
- Unprotected organics slimify in ~1 week; every resident is resistant or converting.
- **It feeds you and waters you**: cooked slime is disgusting but fully edible goo, and
  slime-rain floods are potable — a place to fill your belly and your tanks (slime water
  counts as **toxic water for distillation purposes**).
- No rock exists; building stone is *made*, by compressing the organism (donor
  compressor kept).
- **Farms fail by conversion** (owner's ruling): fertility-1.0 slime-grass lures
  cultivation, and fields convert back to slime within a few harvests. Settlement was
  dissuaded, not forbidden — occasional inhabited structures persist.
- The Slime shows little to no sentient activity. The fear of more is rumor.

## 6. Never true — 🔴 HARD BANS (linter-checkable)

1. 🔴 **No sentience** — content granting the Slime intent, scheming, communication or
   agency violates the little-to-no-sentient-activity ruling; the Mnggal-Mnggal fate
   stays a *fear*, never an event.
2. 🔴 **No meteorological rain** — precipitation here is organism-induced slime rain
   only; a water-rain weather def is a violation (R-H1).
3. 🔴 **No natural rock or stone resources** — stone exists only as compressor output.
4. 🔴 **No permanent farmland** — content letting cultivated fields persist indefinitely
   violates the conversion ruling.
5. 🔴 **No unprotected permanent residents** — a resident def with neither resistance
   nor a transformation state is a violation.
6. 🔴 **No shelf-stable gene extraction** — the machine's genes are taken in place, in
   the open, on the body (§7); a portable or remote extraction route is a violation.
   The FULL archive is available here (the Rot's local-only limit is the Rot's).
7. 🔴 **The war-legacy split holds** — the Slime is a *disarmed* end-state of the
   Assailant arsenal; content re-arming it (weapon-generation behavior) is a violation.
8. 🔴 **The recognizability rule applies**; the Star Wars icon carve-out protects
   in-universe references (Mnggal-Mnggal included).

## 7. Uniquely available

- ⭐ **The gene machine** (owner-authored system; sold by **the Ascendant Helix**, who
  studied the creature, read its genome harvest, and lost interest): the player
  **selects a gene they want** from the curated list of everything that ever visited —
  underwater breathing, a tail, reduced sleep, any Star Wars race's gift — **at the cost
  of a randomized gene they don't yet know** (from a hidden list we author). Flow: pick
  the target → the machine computes **where on the map the current carrying that gene
  will pass** → the pawn goes and stands there, **left in the open on the Slime** →
  activates → **coma** → emerges with the desired gene *plus something fun but odd* —
  and must then take the toxic Rot injection to end the Slime's hold before
  slimification. The library's bloodstream is the waiting room; the vulnerability is the
  price.
- **The Slime-resistant gene** — takeable from the machine itself at the same
  random-gene price; the alternative to the mycoid-symbiote route.
- ⭐ **The Slime Pit** (owner-authored cuisine building): grown full of pure slime, used
  to **render down an ingredient** — the indigestible, the toxic, the dangerous — then
  cleaned out and refilled. The solvent step that turns the planet's deadliest produce
  (the Rot's finest included) into cuisine. The gourmet chain now spans three biomes.
- **The universal antitoxin** — eat slime, purge any poison and most radiation,
  then race the clock to the Rot injection (§3).
- **Belly and tanks** — endless barely-food, potable flood-water, toxic-water
  distillation feedstock.
- **Compressed sandstone** — the no-rock economy's answer, one grind at a time.
- ⭐ **The Throat experiment** (cross-written into `wasteland.md` §10): scoop tons of
  living archive onto the gravship and pour it down the Glowing Throat — likely killing
  what you poured, maybe neutralizing a planet-scale hazard, with genuinely unknown
  results below. The only disposal option that is an experiment rather than a verdict.

## 8. Inhabited objects

- ⭐ **The farm ruins** (owner's ruling): generations of cultivation attempts, each
  abandoned as its fields converted — fence lines and dead irrigation sinking into
  slime-grass, every ruin a legible story of someone who thought they'd beaten the
  conversion rate.
- **The Helix presence** — survey relics of the Ascendant Helix's completed study, and
  their trade contact for the machine: they come to sell, not to stay. *(The Helix wrote
  their own ancestry; a living archive holds no more secrets they want.)*
- **Wildsteam absence, pointed** — the wild's partisans disapprove of the place
  (bioweapon-mediated lateral gene transfer, against everything nature-carved they hold
  sacred); their pilgrim routes bend around it, and that bend is visible on the world.
- **Occasional inhabited structures** — margin-dwellers on the resistant economy:
  slime-cooks, pit-renderers, machine-pilgrim outfitters.

## 9. Artistic theme

**"A body the size of a country, reading everything that touches it."**

- **Light:** the terminator's permanent low amber on a wet, glistening land; everything
  specular — the one biome that *shines* by day.
- **Palette:** translucent greens and ambers, membrane pinks in the rich ground, the
  grey of drowned ruins; slime rain as a silver-green curtain.
- **Silhouette language:** soft-bodied everything — rounded pseudo-trees, domed goo
  herds, the flat immense horizontal of the body itself against tiny hard-edged ruins.
- **Motion:** slow — currents, settling floods, footprints filling back in behind you.
- **Sound:** donor silence kept (no ambient loop): wet stillness, the rare roar of
  slime rain, and underfoot always the faint sound of moving-through.
