# The Greentide — definition sheet

_Owner + BENCH, 2026-09-06, written in conversation over four rounds and declared
ready by the owner ("This is superb. Write it up!"). Defines `BiomeCypreJungle`
(GRiNDTerra Biomes — "Cypre" was the donor's tree name and dies with the eviction).
**The biome's name is THE GREENTIDE** (owner's pick). Thematic handle: **water is the
only argument** — and its image: **a green ribbon of total war steaming through
Extreme Desert sun, growing visibly while you watch.**_

🔑 **Read against `weeping_stones.md` and `the_cracked_lands.md`** — the three
water-laws of the dayside are a ladder: the oasis (scarcity → sacred peace), the
canyon (hidden water → ambush), the Greentide (permanent plenty → open free-for-all).
🔴 **There is NO truce here** (owner, emphatic): *"This is NOT an oasis, it's an open
free-for-all."* Everything is eating or being eaten, mating or laying eggs, growing
visibly or dying trying.

## 0. The measurements everything rests on

MEASURED off `world/ASHKARR_WORLDMAP_tiles.csv` (2026-09-05 prep tables): **191 tiles
— and every single one is a river tile.** That is the biome in one number: no river,
no jungle. Arc 27.8→53.6 (median 44.6 — the sun **+45° up**, Extreme Desert energy).
Temp median **44.8 °C** (37.3..64.4). Elevation median 313 m, but nearly half the
tiles are large-hill/mountainous/impassable (39+78+8) — **gorge country**, not flat
jungle. Regions: Dune Sea 70, Scald Spine 62, Dew Belt 31, Hollow Verge 13, Anvil 9,
Dew Horn 6 — two green groves flanking the Scald plus ribbons out into open sand.
Wildsteam holds two seats here on the owner's own coordinates: **Oilpalm** (tile
4271, 58.6 °C, on the river — the clan's hottest) and **Warthorn** (tile 16641,
1,128 m, river + CaveLakes — its remotest).

Donor inventory (workshop 3537211820, read from the mod XML): **kept in spirit** —
animalDensity **3.5**, the highest donor density on the planet (§4 explains why it
is accidentally right), forageability 1.0, diseaseMtbDays 50 (the planet's sickest
air — also accidentally honest), the custom terrain family (CypreJungleMud/Soil/

(deer, panther, elephant, raccoon, timber wolf, monkey…). **Replaced** — the GRim

## 1. What it is

A gallery jungle hugging every mile of the rivers that fall off the Scald's
rain-peaks — a green ribbon a few tiles wide through country that gets Dune Sea sun,
visible from the dunes as a line of steam. The rivers steam **all the time**; the
air holds more water than the river does. *"You don't sweat, the air sweats you"*
(owner) — survival unprotected is as hard here as in the open desert, by temperature
overwhelm of the opposite kind. Under the steam, everything grows at a speed you can
watch, everything eats everything in the open, and the crashing of trees — cracked,
gnawed, or simply shouldered over — is the background percussion of the place.

## 2. Planetary position

Mid-dayside river anomaly, arc 28–54. **At +45° the ground receives Extreme Desert
energy — the only variable that changed is the river, and the result is the most
furiously productive ground on the planet.** The Greentide is Ash'karr's thesis
stated as landscape: water is the only argument that matters here. The rivers rise
on the Scald's storm-peaks and leave the Contagion's country **sterilized and
clean** (R-H7) — the best water on the dayside enters at the top of this biome.

## 3. Driving forces

**Maximum sun × permanent water = growth faster than anything can be, except the
things that eat it.** Three consequences run the place:

- **The steam.** Extreme Desert sun on open water boils off a permanent weather
  layer (owner: *"Not fog: STEAM"*) — and mercifully, the steam deflects part of the
  sun, which is what makes the biome livable for anything that needs moisture.
- **The inversion lid.** The gorges hold river-cooled saturated air capped by the
  Scald's superheated dry downwash — cool-wet below, hot-dry above, so the steam
  *cannot rise*. It roils at ground level instead (§4c).
- **The dying river.** The river grows steadily saline as it flows, **visibly
  thickening and reducing as it breathes itself into the superheated air** (owner).
  Upstream: cold, clean, sterile. Midstream: the broad living reaches. Downstream:
  milky water, crusted margins, brine-adapted horrors. The terminus is not a mouth
  but **a river grave — a salt pan where the last water gives up.** (And the Free
  Droid Enclaves take saline water by preference, per the owner's same-day ruling —
  a droid enclave at a river grave is doctrine-perfect.)

## 4. How the biology adapted

**The admission test: grows or eats at Greentide speed, carries no truce, and is not
a terrestrial animal.**

- 🔴 **This is `EXPLOSIVE_PLANT_GROWTH_1`'s daily home.** Everywhere else visible
  growth needs a flood or an event; here it is the weather. **Darkness brings
  roots. Light brings leaves and branches. The only thing that repels them is dry
  heat** — alien to everything that lives here save as the lethal domain of the
  desert beyond (owner, near-verbatim).
- **The fruit yearns to be eaten** (owner): huge, sweet, everywhere, loaded with
  digestive accelerants so the eater digests fast and passes seed quickly — free
  calories with a tax (hunger returns fast, filth follows, a brief waddle). The
  ground is carpeted in filth and sprouts, which is the strategy *working*.
- **Trees grow until they crack and fall, sometimes reaching absurd sizes first**
  (owner) — and §7b takes the ladder further.
- **The fauna machine**, as sorts (actual animals ride the full assignment pass):
  - **The Gnawers** — huge insects chewing at the tree bases, indifferent to being
    predated; the food pyramid's floor and one of the three fellers.
  - **The Shatterers** (owner) — megafauna that knock trees over *as a way of
    life*: feeding, shouldering through, brawling in rut. A herd passing your
    treeline is a weather event; a provoked Shatterer is a living siege engine.
  - **The Brakes** — big fast-metabolism grazers mowing the growth line. The
    donor's animalDensity 3.5 is what a jungle that must be eaten *requires* —
    and grazing suppresses encroachment, so a penned herd is a living defense.
  - **The Lungers** — huge aquatic predators, submerged and invisible until the
    lunge; crossing water is the scariest routine act in the biome and bridges are
    precious infrastructure.
  - **The Swingers** — nimble canopy dwellers crossing above the river to escape
    danger… or falling into it (owner).
  - **The Fliers** — absolutely everywhere, screaming out their calls (owner).
- Three fellers, one fall: **gnawed from below, cracked from within, shattered from
  the side** — the C# fall event is built once and paid for three times.

## 4b. Survival — the inversion of the desert's problem

At 45 °C in saturated air, sweat does nothing: **wet-bulb overwhelm**. The desert
kills you dry; the Greentide kills you wet; the same planet demands two opposite
kits, and a Jawa trader sells both.

- **Gear**: wicking/sealed/dry-air equipment, a different protection tree from the
  desert's shade-and-cooling line.
- ⭐ **The elevated-thirst races come home.** The water doctrine's leash (Wookiee,
  Trandoshan, Rodian and kin) comes OFF here — the moisture-hungry species of the
  galaxy congregate in the Greentide, and its settlements hold the planet's densest
  crowd of big wet aliens. Already paid for by ruled doctrine.
- **Homes are made by going DOWN** (owner): round mud homes, cooler caverns of wood
  and earth — sited in **the habitable band**: far enough from the water that it
  does not cook you, not so far that you desiccate. Thick-walled, innately cool,
  **windows avoided**. *A strange way to live.*
- ⭐ **The dry-air blower** (owner's machine, three jobs in one): a downward-gushing
  curtain of hot dry air over each doorway that (1) repels plant encroachment —
  dry heat is the one alien thing, (2) repels animals — same instinct, and
  (3) dries the room behind it, switching off the wet-bulb multiplier. Fueled or
  powered; when it fails the green notices within hours. Every Greentide structure
  is legible by its shimmering doorways.
- **Fire is not the tool.** Saturated growth barely burns (low Flammability across
  the flora roster) — the desert answer fails here, keeping the blower load-bearing.

## 4c. Weather — the steam kit

- **The Roil** (standing) — roiling, ground-hugging, HOT fog (owner), waist-to-chest
  deep, hiding the *floor* while the canopy stands clear. Semi-limited visibility;
  the Lungers and floor-predators hunt inside it. Nothing else in the game hides
  the ground but not the world.
- **Steam devils** (event) — real physics (they spin over hot water): a cold
  downdraft off the peaks punches the lid and shear whirls up wandering
  scald-vortices. **Scald** is a new damage type — a wet burn that ignores
  flammability and flame-armor, fells weak trees, drags the Roil up into a column.
- **Breaklight** (rare) — the inversion *breaks*: the steam blanket rises at once
  and the biome stands naked under the full 45° sun for a few hours. Everything
  scrambles for shade. A reverse-storm: **clarity as the disaster.**
- **Underlight rain** (ambient) — steam condenses on the canopy and drips forever:
  under the trees it is always lightly raining. The soundscape's floor; the fire
  answer stays dead.

## 5. Always true

- Every tile is a river tile; the green exists only where the water runs.
- The steam runs always; the air holds more water than the river.
- Growth is visible — inch by inch over an hour — and **plants will grow into and
  block your doors** (owner). Darkness brings roots; light brings leaves; only dry
  heat repels.
- The river is saltier every mile; every river ends in a grave.
- Trees fall constantly, by gnaw, crack, or Shatterer; distant crashes are the
  biome's percussion.
- The habitable band holds: too close cooks, too far desiccates.
- Food is enormously abundant, and every export has a tooth in it somewhere.

## 6. Never true — 🔴 HARD BANS (linter-checkable)

1. 🔴 **No truce, ever** — no def, description, or story element imports the oasis
   peace; the Greentide is an open free-for-all (owner, emphatic).
2. 🔴 **No vanilla-Earth fauna or flora** in wildAnimals/wildPlants (the standard
   eviction; terrestrial-analog shapes allowed, names are not).
3. 🔴 **No high-Flammability native flora** — saturated growth does not burn; a
   flammable Greentide plant def is a violation.
4. 🔴 **No windowed native architecture** — Greentide settlement templates build
   windowless domes with blower doors; a windowed template violates §4b.
5. 🔴 **No dry ground beyond the band** — the biome's maps do not contain desert
   terrain pockets that break the wet-bulb rule for free.
6. 🔴 **No safe standing water** — deep water cells always carry Lunger risk;
   a swimmable-safe river reach contradicts §4.

## 7. Uniquely available

- **Greenwood** — the fallen wood lying everywhere: burnable, buildable, weak,
  smoky, *abundant beyond counting* (owner: "not quality hardwood but burnable and
  buildable... a major resource"). The desert buys it by the crawler-load.
- **True hardwood** — only from the heart of fallen giants; the price gap between
  the two woods tells the biome's story.
- **Sap and resin** — tapped from trees growing too fast not to bleed: glue,
  lacquer, waterproofing, and **rendered chemfuel** — the dayside's fuel economy.
- ⭐ **Greentide extract** — the growth agent bottled: the explosive-growth
  mechanic in a jar. Force a crop, rush a treeline, sabotage a doorway. The
  biome's killer export.
- **Eggs** (everything is laying), **chitin** (Gnawer plate — mid-tier material),
  **medicine** (the most biochemically frantic pharmacopoeia on the planet), meat
  and fruit beyond any other biome's dreams.
- **Fresh water, upstream** — sterile off the peaks; a hauling economy to the
  desert, and where you draw along the river's length matters.

## 7b. The Greatboles — living towers you mine

The tree ladder runs: normal trees (fall) → giants (crack, fall, hardwood jackpot)
→ **Greatboles**: terrain-scale living towers, multi-cell, too big to fall. Their
heartwood is **mineable like stone** — you dig chambers INTO the tree: naturally
roofed, superbly insulated, invisible from outside, the most alien base-type in the
campaign.

🔴 **And it seals back** (owner's rule): the tree regrows heartwood into any
unclaimed tunnel over days. A pawn or furniture in the way gets the squeeze — a
creaking warning, then crush damage and forced ejection, the tree pushing you out
like a splinter. **Unless you paint the inside with the toxin sealant**, which the
regrowth refuses to cross: painted chambers persist, unpainted shortcuts close
behind you, and a lapsed sealant job means your storeroom is being slowly digested.
Wild Greatboles are dungeons — something else usually painted its own chambers
first, and still lives there. The scavenger fantasy nobody else does: **squatting
inside an organism that is actively trying to heal you out of it.**

## 8. Inhabited objects

- **The Wildsteam Clan** — home ground (Require-severe water, devastating at home,
  +35): Oilpalm and Warthorn plus steam-works culture; safe harbor for the player,
  bathhouses, boiler tech. Elevated-thirst species crowd their halls.
- **Native/Wildsteam architecture**: windowless round mud domes in the habitable
  band, blower doorways shimmering, root-causeway approaches; Greatbole hollows
  where a bole stands near a seat.
- **Droid enclaves at the river graves** — electrolysis on the salt crust, wanting
  only solitude (§3; owner's potable-water ruling).
- **Jawa presence**: trade, not settlement — both survival kits sold, greenwood
  caravans out, extract and hardwood premium goods; crawlers park at the band's
  edge.
- **The causeway network** (§8b) is the road system, and the fights happen on it.

## 8b. The floor

- **Churnmud** — root-torn, beast-trampled ground between the giants: brutally
  slow, it **swallows dropped items** (dig them back out) and **mires** the unlucky
  (slowed, escalating, until pulled free). Under the Roil you cannot see which mud
  is the hungry kind.
- ⭐ **Root causeways** — the giants' roots are the roads: raised, firm, fast,
  winding networks radiating from each great tree. All natural pathing follows
  them — organic highways nobody built, narrow, over hidden mud, contested by
  everything that also prefers dry footing. And the encroachment engine means **the
  roads slowly move between visits.**

## 9. Artistic theme

**"A green ribbon of total war, steaming, growing while you watch."**

- **Light:** white steam-glow — the sun as a diffuse furnace above the Roil;
  Breaklight's naked glare as the rare terror; green-black canopy shade dripping
  underlight rain.
- **Palette:** every green at once, white steam, black mud, the river's gradient
  from glass-clear upstream to milk and salt-crust downstream.
- **Silhouette language:** vertical giants and Greatbole towers; root causeways
  fanning like veins; the Roil erasing everything below the waist; blower doorways
  shimmering on round domes.
- **Motion:** constant — growth you can see, herds, swingers overhead, fliers
  wheeling; the fall of a tree as punctuation.
- **Sound:** the loudest biome on the planet — scream, chew, drip, creak, crash —
  and its scariest signal is **silence**: the ambient ducks out moments before the
  apex predator arrives. Players learn to fear a quiet jungle.
