# The Forsaken Crags — definition sheet

_Owner + BENCH, 2026-09-06, written in conversation over two passes. First rung past the
dryland ladder. Thematic handle: **the Dark that seeps like cold** — and its image:
**obsidian teeth in a fog that light cannot cross, lit only by what grows.**_

🔑 **This sheet defines the def `AB_RockyCrags`** — Alpha Biomes' "Forsaken Crags." The
donor's content is **incorporated wholesale, then bent to this world** (owner's ruling):
its perpetual-dark mechanic, its two-toned bioluminescent flora, and its thirteen-species
nocturnal fauna suite are the starting roster, re-grounded in the atmospheric physics below.

## 0. The measurements everything rests on

"4,440" in the grammar README was wrong and is corrected). Arc 99→121 — the sun **10° to

🔑 It **interleaves with the Wasteland's dark scour in the same named regions** (Gray

## 1. What it is

A country of obsidian-black stone twisted into shapes no wind carved, drowned in a darkness
that behaves like a substance: it fills the air, it pools, it parts without warning into
pockets of impossibly clear air, and it closes again. Light does not fail here for lack of
a sun — light is *eaten in transit*. The only reliable illumination is biological: a carpet
of glowing grass, blue darklight gammas, yellow giant flora — the land lit like a harbor at
night, by its own lamps. Nothing about the great planetary circulations survives contact
with this place; the wind arrives only as sudden violence and leaves as sudden stillness.

## 2. Planetary position

**Past-terminator twilight regime × TWO anomalies: relief, and the Dark.**

- **Relief:** the crag ranges are the wall the nightward-spilling surface air breaks
  against — the place the planet's laminar flows die (§3).
- **The Dark:** a unique atmospheric condition — **not unique to this world, only rare in
  the galaxy** (owner's ruling) — arising where the two atmospheres meet over cold, torn
  ground. It is why the biome's visitors come (§8).

## 3. Driving forces

### The Dark, and how it vanishes (owner-ratified mechanism)

Two families of particle, both real references:

- **Cold variants — tholin rime.** Fractal organic aggregates (Titan-haze chemistry:
  thousands of monomers in an open snowflake structure, enormous optical cross-section per
  gram), coated in condensed **butane and propane** — a hydrocarbon rime.
- **Warm variants — daysmoke.** Flash-frozen water and aerosols carried off the dayside.

Together they obscure light almost totally. And the central mystery has a mechanism, now
canon: **the Dark does not leave — it folds up.** In an ambient-temperature pocket the
hydrocarbon coating sublimates and the fractal aggregate *collapses* into a compact grain;
optical cross-section drops a hundredfold while the mass stays airborne. Mysteriously clear
air, no evidence of what caused the opacity lingering a few meters away — nothing but a
thermometer can tell the clear pocket from the wall of Dark beside it.

### The collapse is the erosion

Where the Dark dies, its compacted grains settle with their acid chemistry and **etch
downward** — a slow, insidious rain eating the land. The famous obsidian-black twisted
formations are causal, not decorative: **the wild rock is the trail of everywhere the Dark
has been clearing for ten thousand years.** Where a standing clear-pocket has sat over one
spot geologically long, the etch goes deep — and the deepest is **the great chasm on the
planet's terminator**, the one massive feature of its kind (name and worldmap placement
owed).

### Wind: laminar → turbulence (canon, both sheets)

🔴 **No steady wind, ever.** The great planetary cycles do not pass through this country —
they END here. Laminar flow hits the relief and the Dark's temperature mosaic and shatters
into micro-turbulence: wild vortices dancing among the crags, suddenly icy-still, then
blasting. 🔑 **Downstream corollary (written into `wasteland.md` §3):** the crag country
kills the wind's coherence, so what escapes nightward carries nothing.

### Power: gusts and seeps — ⛔ NO geothermal (owner's ruling)

The dayside atmosphere is oxidizing; the nightside hydrocarbon chemistry is reduced — the
two together are a fuel-air interface at planetary scale, and the crag turbulence is the
mixing engine. Every vortex stirs a fresh charge of reactants; **bizarre chemistry seeps**
are where the mixture percolates into the etched rock and reacts slowly in the dark. Energy
here is delivered in *pulses*: a gust is a meal, a stillness is a fast.

## 4. How the biology adapted

**The donor roster is the population** (owner's ruling: incorporate all, then modify) — a
single transplanted family, nocturnal to the bone, lore-native to *"a RimWorld orbiting a
very faint star."* Nightling (quill-throwing apex predator) · nightram · nightmule · night
ave (the domestic line) · murkling scavenger packs · crepuscular beetle · shadow charger ·
thunderox · dark vandal · dusk prowler · sand prowler · dusk rat.

- **Chemotrophy on the mixing boundary.** The base of the web runs flameless-combustion
  metabolisms fed by the gust-stirred reactant mix and the seeps. Life keeps the gust
  rhythm: frantic in the blast, dormant in the still.
- **The light-makers are the farmers' infrastructure.** Gamma-family plants emit blue
  **darklight**; the giant flora burn yellow; the **Giant Gamma** is a natural sunlamp
  strong enough to grow crops under; **Giant Stikehr** mushrooms survive only where the
  mists protect them; **Radagast** glow-berries double as the gammas' growth medium.
  🔑 The flora is a lighting economy you cultivate — the biome's answer to farming in a
  land where the sky grows nothing.
- 🔴 **The darkbeast** (owner's ruling): it **electromagnetically interacts with the
  particles**, flaring them out and making them more robust, and it captures a **trailing
  halo of Dark around itself even where the Dark normally cannot form**. It does not make
  the Dark — it wears it. A moving knot of blindness on the map.
- 🔴 **The dusk rat stays, the art goes** (owner's ruling): it is a rat-analog that humans
  named "rat" for its **behavior** — kitchen-soiling, everywhere-following — never its
  appearance. Graphic redo owed to the NEW-ART ledger; until then its sprite is a
  placeholder in violation.
- **Suspiciously placed.** Some of these creatures are adapted to this terrain, and this
  one biome, *too* well — as though set here (§8).

## 4b. Weather — two atmospheres birthing reactions alien to both

The donor's weather table, re-skinned to the physics (`Clear` stays at commonality **0**):

- **The Dark** (donor `AB_ForsakenNight`, the permanent state): near-black "day" sky,
  ranged accuracy ×0.6, ambient glow below crop threshold. Gust cycles run *inside* it.
- ⭐ **The Clearing** (owner-authored, new, rare): illumination briefly RISES — the Dark
  folds away wholesale and the actual map shows itself, naked and lit — then it simply
  folds back. The one moment you see what country you have been living in.
- **The etching drizzle** (re-skin of donor `AB_ForsakenRainyNight`): the insidious rain
  made visible — 🔴 it is collapsed-grain chemistry, NEVER water (R-H1 stands).
- **Reaction storms** (re-skin of donor `AB_ForsakenThunderstorm`): the two atmospheres
  igniting into chemistry native to neither — light and violence with no terrestrial name.

## 5. Always true

- The Dark is present, material, and temperature-structured; clear pockets are real,
  sharp-edged, and unexplained to everyone in-world.
- All illumination that matters is biological or artificial. Light is the currency.
- Wind arrives only in gusts; stillness is the default between them.
- Sensors, gunsights and long-range anything degrade in the gunk — **this is one of the
  few biomes that measurably slows Imperial pursuit** (owner's ruling): no orbital eyes,
  no long lances; pursuit drops to walking pace and short knives.
- Wind-grasping technology breaks often — the Dark fouls it and the gust-shear snaps it;
  repair is a way of life.
- The rock is black (forced `AB_Cragstone`), the terrain dark sand, dark mud, dark gravel.
- The fauna is nocturnal and dark-adapted, one transplanted family.

## 6. Never true — 🔴 HARD BANS (linter-checkable)

1. 🔴 **No geothermal** — no vents, no thermal springs, no geothermal power content.
2. 🔴 **No steady wind** — any weather def, mechanic or prose giving this biome sustained
   laminar wind is a violation; gust-pattern only.
3. 🔴 **No water rain** (planet-wide R-H1) — the drizzle is chemical; a water-rain def
   here is a violation.
4. 🔴 **No permanent Clear** — `Clear` weather commonality stays 0; illumination rises
   only as the rare, brief Clearing.
5. 🔴 **No clean sensing** — content granting unimpeded sensors, targeting or overwatch
   through the Dark violates the pursuit ruling.
6. 🔴 **No sun-dependent resident life** — a def needing real sky light to live here is a
   violation; growth runs on glow flora or technology.
7. 🔴 **The Forsakens never appear** — no faction, no pawns, no structure attributable to
   them with certainty. Cryptid only (§8); an on-screen Forsaken is a violation.
8. 🔴 **The recognizability rule applies**; the Star Wars icon carve-out protects icons;
   the dusk rat is admitted under the behavior-named exemption pending its art redo.

## 7. Uniquely available

- **Farmable light** — the darklight/yellow-light flora economy; the only biome where a
  lamp is a crop and a Giant Gamma is a sunlamp you found, not built.
- ⭐ **The sensor shadow** — the place to run to when the Empire is close. Refuge priced
  in darkness, breakage and cold.
- **Tholin harvest** — the Dark's fallen grains are concentrated organic feedstock;
  chemfuel-adjacent industry from air.
- **Gust power** — wind energy in violent pulses: turbines plus storage plus a standing
  repair bill; the breakage economy is the price of the watt.
- **Ultima fibers** — the giant septimum's hardened line, per donor lore.
- **The chasm** — the deepest etch on the planet; what waits at the bottom is unwritten.

## 8. Inhabited objects

### The Forsakens — the whispered visitors (owner's ruling, 2026-09-06)

An **unnamed race that comes here FOR the condition** — the Dark is rare in the galaxy, and
they seek it. They are visitors, not residents: not organized or present enough to even
mention, and **not seen in living memory**. They are this planet's Loch Ness monster, its
Bigfoot — sworn to by nobody credible, believed by everybody a little.

But some of the creatures do seem uniquely, suspiciously adapted to this one biome, *as
though placed*. And the fear that dares not say itself aloud: **that they are the Zabraks —
the "Nightbrothers" — bringing this strangeness with their twisted Sith associations.**
These rumors are merely whispered, to ensure they do not come to pass.

### Everything else

- **Wind-farms, working and wrecked** — snapped rotors, spare-part shrines, the repair
  culture's waystations; a working turbine is tended like an animal.
- **Seep-works** — crude chemistry taps sunk where the reactant mix percolates; the
  biome's power wells, and its explosions.
- **Fugitive holds** — smuggler caches and bolt-holes of everyone the Empire ever chased
  into the sensor shadow; some still occupied, some now just supplies in the dark.
- **The chasm** and whatever generations of the desperate have lowered into it.
- **Rumor-sites** — places where something was found too neatly arranged: a creature den
  too well provisioned, a stone circle nobody admits to. Forsaken evidence, deniable.

## 9. Artistic theme

**"Obsidian teeth in a fog light cannot cross, lit only by what grows."**

- **Light:** two-toned bioluminescence — blue darklight gammas against yellow giant flora,
  a harbor-at-night palette; the Dark as a visible medium with knife-edge clear pockets;
  the Clearing as the rare, almost religious reveal of the whole map.
- **Palette:** obsidian black, glow-blue, lamp-yellow, hydrocarbon-frost grey on the rime
  faces.
- **Silhouette language:** twisted, etched verticals — rock that reads dissolved, not
  carved; turbine skeletons; the darkbeast as a hole in the glow with a halo.
- **Rhythm:** icy stillness broken by violence — sound and motion in gusts, then nothing.
- **Sound:** silence as the baseline; wind arriving like an impact; night-insect ambience
  (donor); the tick of etch-fall on stone.
