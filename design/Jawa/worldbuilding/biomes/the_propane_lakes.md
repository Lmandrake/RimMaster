## 1. What it is

The far end of the world: a black sea of liquid fuel under the brightest sky on the
planet. Not dark — starlit and curtain-lit, the aurora strongest here of anywhere,
because this is where the magnetosphere slams back together after its rude shock
upstream. It snows fuel. The plants are crystal and grow overnight from what falls. The
fast things run in blue flame because nothing moves fluidly here without burning. And
beneath the sea, over the crater where the first infection struck, is a war lab that was
never supposed to exist — built to study a weapon that was already loose, by people who
still felt strong and had no time left.

## 2. Planetary position

**Antistellar regime (zero solar energy; the magnetotail's reconnection footprint) × two
phase lines (propane pools at −42, ammonia is liquid −78…−33) × the machine anomaly**
(§3). A disc at the pole, lobed around it; never a ring.

## 3. Driving forces

### Two solvents

- **Umbra — the propane sea.** Below the dew point, the Blue Desert's haze **pools**: the
  antistellar cap is the planet's fuel tank (ruled). Propane-in-liquid gives the
  nightside's butane/pentane biochemistry a *medium* — so it gets big.
- **Ammonia Flats — the polar solvent returns.** Ammonia is liquid across this biome's
  whole range and it is *polar* — the classic water-analog. The largest region is the
  nightside's chemical oasis: **ammonia-based life**, efficient where the Blue Desert
  starved.

### The aurora — the magnetosphere reconnecting (owner-ratified physics)

🔴 **The substellar point is the planet's north magnetic pole** — unusual, and it speaks to
planet-level engineering (§8). Consequences: the day face takes the sun's radiation with
minimal shielding; the field the sun drapes along the planet **collapses in reconnection
over the antipode** — Umbra sits under the precipitation footprint of the whole
magnetosphere. Hence **the strongest, brightest aurora on the planet**, most of the clear
time, and **curiously high radiation** at both poles. Effects, ruled: **psychological and
physical** — surges in electrical circuits, and ⭐ **the electrojet tap** (§7).

🔴 **Umbra is the crags' tholin factory** (owner-ratified cross-flow): auroral and particle
chemistry in a hydrocarbon atmosphere manufactures the fractal organics that fill the
Forsaken Crags with the Dark. The Dark is aurora-ash, blown toward the terminator
(`forsaken_crags.md` §3).

### Ignition has an engine

A propane sea under an electrojet: induced ground currents, static off blowing ice sand,
dry lightning. The lab beneath the surface has survived ten thousand years because nothing
hot has touched that lake since the war; a spacecraft's thruster is the first (§8).

### The machine (owner's ancient history, 2026-09-06 — verbatim in intent)

The planet's fate was to be **a tremendous factory — a mega-project among mega-projects.
Not terraforming: terramanufacture.** What they were going to build no longer matters.
They had only begun — the sub-surface structures, the self-assembling megastructures, the
**planet-scale power dynamo** — when the Assailants came and everything became surviving
them. Since then it is **a one-tenth-built dream slowly grinding down, falling down,
collapsing inward on itself over the millennia.** 🔑 **The sub-solar point needed all the
power they could possibly channel into the planet** — that is why the magnetic pole is
there: the dynamo's intake, the Rust Cathedral its remnant. **The lab was never supposed
to be there**: a war lab built to study the weapon *after* it was deployed and active,
before they realized that, despite still feeling strong, there was no time left. And
every interaction taught the Assailants to adapt — **mutual learning, mutual
destruction.**

## 4. How the biology adapted

**The most alien life in the cast, save the oceans** (owner's ruling). The admission test:
hydrocarbon- or ammonia-metabolic, cold-loving (R-H10), not an icy dayside analog.

- ⭐ **The Burners, ascendant** (owner: "yet more powerful here and almost required"): the
  blue-fire halo is not a sprint trick here, it is **locomotion** — such is the cold that
  fluid movement is impossible without burning. Always in flame when moving. The apex of
  the Blue Desert's line, and the most beautiful killer on the ice.
- **Ammonia life on the Flats**: the **Frostbound Behemoth** (donor 1.0) is what the
  polar solvent builds when it has a medium; the **Terramorph** belongs to the Flats'
  mineral chemistry.
- **The sea's exotics** (ruled): the V-wake creature and its kin — propane-native,
  agitated by pumping, attacking pawns and pipe on sight.
- **The Frostmite** (donor 1.0): the corpse-sweeper working the Blue Desert's fallen dead,
  rolled downhill — the pole's undertaker.
- **The Blizzarisk**: hyperweave webs strung across the ice — the one luxurious harvest.
- **The Slurrypede** — kept, re-read: a bio-mechanoid "built to feed prisoners
  efficiently" is **lab fauna**, the containment facility's feeder, still running.
- **Lab guardians** (injected): the mechanoids and ancient dangers that return as you
  near the Impact Site (`MECHANOID_BIOME_PRESENCE_REVIEW_1`).
- **Crystal flora**: partially inorganic, regrowing daily from the fuel snow; the rime
  nodules' euphoric sacs. 🔴 **None of it is kyber** — kyber goes to the caverns
  (owner, 2026-09-06).

## 4b. Weather

- **Aurora-clear** — the common state. 🔴 **No visibility blockage** (owner): starlight
  and curtain-light; ranged fire works; the sky is the most beautiful on the planet.
- **Fuel snow** — hydrocarbon and ammonia precipitation; the crystal flora's feed.
- **Dry lightning** — over a fuel field.
- **Reconnection storms** — the aurora's violent nights: radiation up, circuits surging,
  the tap over-producing; mood two-sided (awe and dread).
- 🔴 No rain (R-H1: greatest altitudes only).

## 5. Always true

- The sea is fuel; every spark is weather; nothing hot has touched it since the war.
- The sky is bright: aurora most of the clear time, strongest on the planet, and it
  irradiates.
- Circuits surge; the tap harvests; both from the same sky.
- The fast life burns to move; the slow life dissolves in ammonia or propane.
- The dead of the Blue Desert arrive here and are eaten.
- Nothing native survives the crossing dayside (R-H10).
- The machine is under everything, one-tenth built, still collapsing.

## 7. Uniquely available

- **Fuel by the pipe-length** — the biggest single deposit on the planet, refueled in
  place (ruled), priced entirely in cold survival.
- ⭐ **The submerged adventure** — the ship lowered into fuel (ruled); what lies on the
  bottom is §8.
- ⭐ **The electrojet tap** (owner-ratified): long grounded wire loops across the ice
  harvesting auroral ground currents — **~a tenth of a solar array's output for the same
  footprint, extremely cheap in time and materials** — and surge-prone: the sky that feeds
  it fries unprotected circuits on a reconnection night.
- **Rime-nodule euphoric** — a mild drug that grows overnight.
- **Crystal wood, hyperweave silk.**
- **The ending** — the one biome with a *before and after* (§8).

## 8. Inhabited objects

- ⭐ **The ancient war lab** (owner's rulings, `ANCIENT_WAR_LAB_1`): **beneath the propane
  lake's surface, directly over the Impact Site where the first infection occurred** —
  where the Assailants were first contained and studied. Great care required: ignition
  from spacecraft interaction or anything extremely hot. And **plentiful ways to
  permanently change the map**: the lake becomes **a massive fresh crater and a
  ripped-open ancient lab, shielding intact, amazingly** — on a frozen world that is
  otherwise never allowed to change.
- **The collapsing machine** — sub-surface structures and self-assembling megastructures,
  one-tenth built, grinding down: the dynamo's conduits, the intake's far end.
- **Lab guardians** — mechanoids and ancient dangers, the last watch.
- **Prior taps** — the wire-loop ruins of everyone who learned the aurora pays.
- **The fallen** — the Blue Desert's dead, arrived, and the frostmites at them.

## 9. Artistic theme

**"Aurora curtains over a black mirror sea, with blue flame running on the ice."**

- **Light:** the brightest nightside sky — green and violet curtains reflected in liquid
  fuel; starlight on crystal; the Burners' blue halos streaking; a reconnection storm as
  a sky on fire that never reaches the sea. Until it does.
- **Palette:** black mirror, aurora green/violet, crystal-clear and frost-white, the blue
  of running flame; then, after the ending, the raw grey of a fresh crater.
- **Silhouette language:** translucent crystal flora; the wake on the sea; wire loops
  strung to the horizon; the megastructure's collapsed geometry under everything.
- **Sound:** the crackle of the tap; the hiss of fuel snow; the aurora's inaudible hum
  everyone swears they hear; the boom.
