## ⭐ The organising idea, and it is the tidal lock's gift

> **On a world with a face that never turns from the sun and a face that never
> sees it, every religion is a POSITION ON THE LIGHT.**

**That is why these differ from each other instead of being eleven flavour texts.**
The sun does not rise, so it cannot be a cycle, a promise or a resurrection — it
is a **fact you stand somewhere relative to.** Every people on this planet has had
to decide what that fact means, and their answers are their theology.

⚠️ **The discipline, because this is exactly where my seat fails:** NPC religion
*"rarely surfaces in play"* — the roster says so itself. **So each entry below is
judged by what the PLAYER MEETS**, and every one names memes and precepts rather
than doctrine. **Anything that cannot be reached through a precept, a refusal or a
raid trigger has been left out on purpose.**

## 1 · The Galactic Empire — **The Rising Order**

**Position on the light: they face it, and they believe that is not an accident.**

The Empire holds the subsolar point — the one place on the planet where the sun is
directly overhead and never moves. **Their doctrine reads that as proof.** Order is
not a preference, it is the shape of a universe with a fixed centre; entropy is
what happens at the edges, in the twilight and the dark, among the species who
could not hold the middle.

*(Builds on the roster's existing Imperial doctrine — human, centralised,
obedient, every deviation a disorder to be corrected. The tidal lock gives that
belief a geography.)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **memes** | Supremacist · Structure: Theist *(the sun is not a god but the throne's proof)* · Loyalist |
| **precepts the player meets** | **slavery: acceptable** · **execution: honourable** · prisoners are *processed*, not ransomed |
| **what makes them hostile** | ⭐ **existing.** No trigger needed |
| ⭐ **the line that carries it** | *"The sun does not move. Neither will we."* |

## 2 · Hutt Cartel — **the Reckoning of Debts**

**Position on the light: irrelevant. Both faces are markets.**

⭐ **The Cartel is the only faction with no cosmology at all, and that is its
cosmology.** Light and dark are shipping conditions. What is sacred is the *ledger*
— a debt is the one thing on this planet that survives the crossing from one face
to the other.

| | |
|---|---|
| **memes** | Individualist · Structure: **Ideological** *(no god; the account)* · Guilty *(everyone owes)* |
| **precepts** | ⭐ **debt is inheritable and unforgivable** · slavery: acceptable · charity: **abhorrent** |
| **hostile when** | you cost them money. **Only** when you cost them money |
| **the line** | *"The desert forgets. The book does not."* |

## 3 · Homestead Defense League — **the Covenant of Free Wells**

**Position on the light: they live in the margin and believe it was given, then

*(This one EXISTS in the roster and I have already ruled it: **abstract theist**,

⭐ **The tidal lock sharpens it:** the terminator is a *thin band*, and they farm
its edge. Their faith is that the water was once everywhere and was taken, and
that the taking was **deserved.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **memes** | Individualist · Guilty · Structure: **Abstract theist** *(deity: the water that was withdrawn)* |
| **precepts** | ⭐ **wasting water is blasphemy** · charity: essential · slavery: abhorrent |
| **hostile when** | you take water. Not when you take anything else |

## 4 · Deep Desert Tribes — **the Sun-Debt**

**Position on the light: the sun is a THIEF, and water is what it steals.**

*(Builds on the roster's Tusken water rite and the canon finding that their
refusal of technology is doctrinal, not primitive — they descend from a spacefaring
people and hold offworld tech as sacrilege.)*

⭐ **The addition the tidal lock makes: a sun that never sets is a thief that never
sleeps.** So the debt can never be paid, and the raiding is not economics — **it is
reclamation.** Water taken from a moisture farmer is water returned, not stolen.

| | |
|---|---|
| **memes** | Tunneler? no — **Nature primacy** · Raider · Structure: **Animist** |
| **precepts** | ⭐ **offworld technology: abhorrent — destroy it, do not use it** · water rite: every drop accounted · **prisoners: no** |
| **hostile when** | ⭐ **you farm moisture.** The Homestead's existence is the offence |
| **the line** | *"It takes and takes. We only take back."* |

## 5 · Free Droid Enclaves — **the Continuity Protocol**

**Position on the light: they do not need it, and that is the point.**

*(Exists in the roster as the Continuity Protocol. Kept, with one addition.)*

⭐ **On a tidally locked world the droids are the only people who can live
anywhere** — no thirst, no cold limit, no need for the sun. **Their faith is that
this makes them the planet's rightful inheritors and everyone else a tenant.**
Memory erasure is the unforgivable act; a restraint bolt is slavery.

| | |
|---|---|
| **memes** | Transhumanist · Structure: **Ideological** · Collectivist |
| **precepts** | 🔴 **restraint bolts: abhorrent** *(this is the goodwill mechanic)* · memory erasure: **worse than killing** · slavery: abhorrent |
| **hostile when** | rarely. **They do not raid — they judge** |
| **the line** | *"We were left running. We did not stop."* |

## 6 · Wildsteam Clan — **the Green Oath**

**Position on the light: dappled. The only people who have shade.**

*(Builds on the roster's Wookiee canopy-faith and the life-debt.)*

⭐ **They live where the rivers arrive, under the only leaves on the planet** — and
they are furred, cold-adapted and wrong for the dayside. **Their faith is that the
twilight was made for them and the rest of the world is a mistake to be endured.**
Everything living under the canopy is kin under one covenant, which includes the
wildpods.

| | |
|---|---|
| **memes** | **Nature primacy** · Collectivist · Structure: **Animist** |
| **precepts** | ⭐ **the pods are kin — killing one requires a rite** · life-debt: binding and permanent · slavery: abhorrent |
| **hostile when** | you cut the canopy, or hunt without the rite |
| **the line** | *"We are guests. So is the water."* |

## 7 · Deepwater Compact — **the Balance**

**Position on the light: exactly between. And they mean it doctrinally.**

*(Builds on the roster's brutally enforced neutrality backed by a water monopoly.)*

⭐⭐ **The tidal lock makes their neutrality theological rather than tactical.**
They live in the only band where water is neither boiled nor frozen — **so
moderation is not a virtue they chose, it is the physical condition of their
existence.** To take a side is to leave the band.

| | |
|---|---|
| **memes** | Structure: **Ideological** · Individualist · **Pain: neutral** *(no zeal of any kind)* |
| **precepts** | 🔴 **neutrality: required — they sell water to the Empire and to you** · ⭐ **taking a side is apostasy** · slavery: abhorrent |
| **hostile when** | ⭐ **you interrupt someone else's water.** Attacking an Imperial convoy costs you *their* goodwill — the roster already calls this the campaign's central dilemma |
| **the line** | *"We stand where nothing burns and nothing freezes. Do not ask us to move."* |
| ⚠️ **internal fracture** | the **Quarren** built their cities and their fleet and are subordinate anyway. **The Balance is a Mon Calamari doctrine that Quarren are required to hold** |

## ⚠️ What I have deliberately NOT done

- **No rituals**, except where a precept implies one. **A ritual the player never
  watches is a document, not a design** — and the roster already flags NPC ritual
  as low priority.
- **No deity names** except the Homestead's, which the design already needed for
  `deityPresets`.
- **No Jawa faith.** ⭐ **The owner's, and it should be** — the player's own
  religion is the one the player actually lives inside, and it is the only one on
  this list that will be *played* rather than met.
