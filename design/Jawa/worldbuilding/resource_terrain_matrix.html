Resource × Terrain Matrix

Every catalogued resource assigned a four-axis role per terrain, rebuilt against the locked 15-terrain set (6 bands). This is the arbitration pass the master catalogue set up — it supersedes the coarse §3A partition table and the earlier 7-column matrix with concrete, per-tile picks. Cells that repeat the desert_world_design.md §2C locked anchor allocations are anchored; alien/dark/faction columns draw on the §2B fills and verified 1.6 defNames. Blank (·) = present but ordinary; (—) = essentially absent / not applicable.

All Family-11 items are non-buildable (enemy-owned or environmental) → pillar-clean by construction; each functions as a timer/toll, raising a tile's cost and character without raising the player's ceiling.

Deliberately not partitioned (sourced from labor or traders, not terrain): kibble / meals / blocks / refined chemfuel (labor outputs) · silver, luciferium, hyperweave-as-trade, neutroamine (trader goods) · hemogen / animal-blood feed (no sanguophage in our roster) · ice / snow-melt (no polar tile in rotation). Jade is the baseline décor stone gems out-class — present everywhere, notable nowhere.

How to read the loop: every terrain shows at least one ① (reason to come), at least one ② (the next need it hands you), usually a ③ (wealth that tempts lingering), and a ④ (what ends the tenancy). No column is ① in everything; no column is without a ②. That closure — not any single cell — is the design working. The 15 columns group into 6 bands so the desert sea (DD/AR/FC/SF) reads as one contiguous ocean with the other bands as its scattered islands of purpose.
