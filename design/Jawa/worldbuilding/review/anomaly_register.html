Anomaly Entity Register

All 29 entities, with art recovered from the wiki because Anomaly's textures ship inside a Unity bundle. Each carries a recommendation and the reason for it. Recommendations, not decisions — yours to overturn.

Read this before the cards — it changes most of them

🔴 The void monolith sets bringAlongOnGravship: false. It is one of only two defs in the DLC that mention gravships. Anomaly's whole seven-stage progression is anchored to a map, and this campaign's premise is a clan that leaves maps — so shipping it whole means shipping an arc the player may structurally be unable to finish.

⭐ The lever is AnomalyPlaystyleDef — three settings, picked at world creation and switchable in-game afterwards. AmbientHorror turns the monolith line off, makes threats rare and keeps the codex; the DLC stays loaded and stops driving a progression. My recommendation. Full Disabled throws away free atmosphere; full Standard ships an uncompletable arc competing with the Empire for the campaign's spine.

⭐ And the tone problem has an answer that costs nothing: the Forsakens. The dark biome already names a vanished race that terraformed this world. Read the surviving entities as their leavings and Anomaly stops being a second mythology bolted onto the first. No def changes, no art, no writing.

⚠️ One hard constraint if you keep the monolith line anyway: Basic-tier slack is exactly one. Level 2 needs seven of the eight Basic entities, so cutting two hard-blocks the whole progression.

KEEP

12

Reads as something old and wrong left behind — which is the frame we already chose.

sightstealer

Sightstealer

Invisible clawed screamers that gather to hunt.

⭐ The single best fit. Something that stalks the dunes at night and is never seen is exactly what a dead world's leavings should feel like.

noctol

Noctol

Black-carapace beast, deadly in dark, crippled by light.

⭐ Belongs in the forsaken crags specifically. A predator that light hurts is a reason the dark places stay dark.

noctolith

Noctolith

Pillar that spawns noctols while darkness holds.

Keep with the noctol — it is the reason they arrive, and it reads as Forsaken hardware.

metalhorror

Metalhorror

A metal parasite hidden inside a host until it cuts its way out.

⭐ Machine-horror, not flesh-horror. Reads as Forsaken automation gone wrong, which is the frame we already chose.

nociosphere

Nociosphere

An archotech sphere that radiates pain and grows more active.

A Forsaken artefact by any other name. Suppressible, capturable, and it explains itself without gore.

revenant

Revenant

An invisible spindly thing that hypnotises one sleeper a night.

Folkloric rather than visceral — its own text calls it outlander folklore. That register survives the setting change.

unnatural corpse

UnnaturalCorpse

A waxy corpse that will not stay where you put it, and follows one pawn.

Quiet and uncanny, and it has no body art of its own — it wears the corpse of someone you knew. Costs nothing, disturbs everything.

golden cube

GoldenCube

A warm golden cube that people become unable to put down.

Uncanny without a drop of blood, and its spawn chance is already zero — it only arrives if you provoke it.

twisted obelisk

WarpedObelisk_Mutator

Mutates the pawns that approach when it activates.

The three obelisks are the best Forsaken artefacts in the DLC: silent objects that do something when you meddle.

corrupted obelisk

WarpedObelisk_Duplicator

Duplicates a colonist.

All three share one description on purpose, so the player cannot tell which they have found. That is good design and it is setting-neutral.

warped obelisk

WarpedObelisk_Abductor

Abducts pawns into the Labyrinth.

⚠️ The only route into the Labyrinth. Keeping it keeps a pocket map that has nothing to do with a desert — accept or cut deliberately.

revenant spine

RevenantSpine

A dormant revenant in object form; hold it and it comes back.

Keep with the revenant. A thing you can choose to destroy or choose to wake is a good object to leave in a base.

YOUR CALL

1

Genuinely balanced. I have said which way I lean and why.

harbinger tree

Plant_TreeHarbinger

A flesh-covered tree that eats corpses and grows a grove.

**Your call.** It is a plant, not a monster, and a single grove is memorable — but 'feeds on corpses' is the body-horror register. I lean keep as a landmark.

CUT

13

Body horror with dedicated organic art. No reading of these is Star Wars.

shambler

Shambler

Corpses reanimated for a few days, then they expire.

**Zombies are the loudest possible 'not Star Wars'.** They are also the cheapest tonally, since they wear ordinary pawn art. If any body-horror line survives, this is the one I would sacrifice first.

ghoul

Ghoul

A person made into a mindless melee weapon that eats raw meat.

A player *capability*, not an encounter. The clan turning its own people into meat-eating attack animals is a different campaign from the one we are writing.

gorehulk

Gorehulk

A swollen human that fires keratin spines from its skin.

Dedicated organic art and the most explicitly gory silhouette in the set. Nothing about it can be read as Forsaken.

chimera

Chimera

A disfigured bear-composite, vicious without limit.

A generic monster with no idea behind it. It adds a threat register we already have four of.

devourer

Devourer

Leaps, swallows a person whole, and regurgitates what is left.

Pure body horror, and the one entity whose *mechanic* is the horror. Cut.

fingerspike

Fingerspike

Dog-sized single-clawed flesh sprinter.

The fleshbeast line is five variations on cancerous meat. It is the most viscerally organic content in the DLC and the furthest from a desert of machines.

toughspike

Toughspike

Armoured spike-thrower whose corpse liquefies.

Same line, same verdict.

trispike

Trispike

Three-limbed; splits into fingerspikes when killed.

Same line, same verdict.

bulbfreak

Bulbfreak

Slow claw-bruiser that bursts into a horde on death.

Same line, same verdict.

dreadmeld

Dreadmeld

A gargantuan amalgam that tunnels through rock.

Undercave only, so it goes with the pit gate.

fleshmass heart

FleshmassHeart

A spreading cancer organ that consumes the map.

A slow map-eating infestation competes directly with the campaign's own pressure systems, and it looks like nothing in Star Wars.

fleshmass nucleus

FleshmassNucleus

The archotech core left when the heart dies.

Goes with the heart.

pit gate

PitGate

A hole down to the Undercave pocket map.

An underground cave campaign inside a desert-and-gravship campaign. ⚠️ It is also monolith-gated, so the playstyle choice below removes it anyway.

VoidMonolith

🔴 **Sets `bringAlongOnGravship: false`.** It anchors to a map; our premise leaves maps. Turned off by the playstyle, not cut by hand.

void structure

VoidStructure

Part of the monolith endgame. Off with it.

void node

VoidNode

Part of the monolith endgame. Off with it.
