Species Register

Every species assigned to a faction, grouped by where it belongs. Each carries what it looks like, its place in galactic society, and its heat tolerance — which on this planet decides who can live where. Revised to the owner's corrections, 2026-08-13.

What changed in this revision

🔴 Humans are now explicit and they are the backbone. The Empire is 75% human stormtroopers plus an elite human officer caste, with other species as support mercenaries rather than members. The Homestead is significantly human. Blackstar is human — Mandalorian is a culture, not a species, so its Mandalorians are humans and adoptees who wear the plate. The Hutt Cartel gains a small human minority.

🔴 The Jawa Trade Moot is 100% Jawa. Ugnaught moved to the Junkers, Chadra-Fan to the Homestead. No passengers.

⭐ Rakata moved to the Deepwater Compact — they are amphibious, so the deep water is where they would actually be. A better fit than the gene-cult, and it gives the Compact a precursor secret.

⭐ Rare Yoder among the Homesteaders — not a population, one sheltered oddity in a farming community that does not discuss him.

⚠️ The duplicate Blackstar and Wildsteam sections were my bug, not a design choice: my grouping split on the "also in" separator, so "Blackstar · Hutt Cartel" and "Blackstar Company" became two groups. Fixed — each species now appears once, under its primary faction, with secondaries listed on the card.
