Species Register

The Star Wars species actually loaded in this install, grouped by the faction they belong to rather than alphabetically — because assigning them is the job. Each carries what it looks like, where it sits in galactic society, and its heat tolerance, which on this planet decides who can live where.

🔴 Read this first: every species is loaded THREE TIMES

Three overlapping Star Wars xenotype mods are active, not one — [BTD] Xenotype REMIX (70), Star Wars Xenotypes by guy762 (58) and Outer Rim – Galactic Diversity (44). 80 distinct species across 172 defs.

🔴 They are not identical, and the differences bite. Jawa exists as BTD_Jawa, guy762_xenotype_jawa and OuterRim_Jawa — the first two grant +20 °C heat tolerance, the third only +10. On a desert world that is not a rounding error.

⇒ The design must name ONE canonical def per species or pawn generation will roll all three. Each card below carries my recommendation. Default: BTD — it is the most complete set and carries the strongest desert genes. Two exceptions are noted where guy762's version is better.

⚠️ Outer Rim's versions are cosmetic-only — head shape and skin, almost no stat genes. Fine as a fallback, wrong as the canonical choice for a campaign where temperature is a mechanic.

What is NOT here, deliberately. The install also carries ~170 non-Star-Wars xenotypes — Big and Small's giants and trolls, Alpha Genes' demons and phytokin, Det's spliced menagerie, Yautja, and 1,073 auto-generated animal-people from a sapient-animals mod. None belong on this planet and they are listed for suppression, not distribution. Two are worth a second look regardless: impid (vanilla, +20 °C, made for desert life) and saurid (arid-flavoured but mechanically penalised at both temperature extremes — a trap).
