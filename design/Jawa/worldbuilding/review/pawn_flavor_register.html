Pawn Flavor Register

The brief. Round 6 volume mandate is in: every faction carries ≥5 childhoods (one COMMON default), ≥10 adulthoods (one COMMON), and a ≥15-trait pool (existing-mod picks marked pool-pick + the faction's own designed traits). Most round-6 entries cite the Inhabited cast character that inspired them. Every piece of pawn-flavor content for the campaign — shipped backstories and traits, designed-but-unbuilt items, the reflavored ISEKAI leveling traits, and the parked droid set — one row each, grouped by faction. Everything is pre-filled KEEP: you only disagree. Decisions: keep (stays as designed) · rework (right idea, wrong shape — say what in the note) · cut (out of the campaign) · ship-next (promote into the next XML batch) · parked (droids: waits on DROID_SYSTEM_EMBRACE_1).
Criterion: rows are pre-decided for coherence with the design doc — that ranks fit, not what is fun for you. Overrule freely; your notes are worth more than your agreements.
Posture: default is KEEP — a row you never touch ships as designed. Nothing is stripped by silence.
Rules I invented (overturn in one line): ① v1 shipped exactly 2 childhoods + 3 adulthoods per faction — a uniform clip, never ruled. ② Only ISEKAI's 15 loud-fiction traits were reflavored; its 32 genre-neutral RPG traits (Iron Will, Glass Cannon…) stay as shipped. ③ The rank ladder's fiction is Bounty Hunters' Guild threat ratings — my frame. ④ Two wrong-fiction backstories (RBM_Roamer, SH_MED_MedievalAlchemist) were spared the cut because one campaign pawn each carries them.
