Research Tree Propositions — the weapon & ship tech re-cut

Three shapes to react to. Nothing here is ruled; every node name, tier and count is a proposal. Exact survivor-row mapping is the sitting's work.
Brief — what this page is and how to read it

The current tree scatters weapon research across 77 mods (515 rows; KotOR alone carries 89 — more than vanilla). Normalization picks one chain per idea and re-points survivor unlocks onto it, the same move the turret pass made for buildings. Below are three re-cuts of the same content: A groups by theme (form of harm), B by power with ship split from personal, C re-balances so every group gives comparable value per research spent.

All three carry the same source-gated layer: chains that cannot be researched with points at all — they arrive from places in the world. Memory Core is canon-backed; the other three are labeled candidates.

Cost model (applies to every proposition): research is expensive for the Jawa. PROPOSAL mechanisms: techprint-pattern items from traders (a chain tier needs N traded prototypes/analyzed items before points can be spent), and high point costs offset by prototype turn-ins. The knobs are per-node; the model is the same everywhere.

Counts marked ~ are approximate absorbed-row/unlock estimates from the census; the sitting firms them.

The source-gated layer — present in all three propositions

Chains no amount of points can buy. Knowledge arrives from somewhere, and the tab shows a locked chain whose unlock condition is a place, a deed, or a deal.
