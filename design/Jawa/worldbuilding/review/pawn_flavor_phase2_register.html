Pawn Flavor Phase 2 Register

The brief. PAWN_FLAVOR_PHASE2_PROSE_1 — lore-prose pass over every ThoughtDef, MentalBreakDef and XenotypeDef in the COMMON tier (403 + 22 + 72 = 497 rows; reachable in ordinary play on the live 585-mod set per infrastructure/output/pawn_flavor_phase2_census.csv). OCCASIONAL and DORMANT tiers are deliberately OUT of this pass — future work, not dropped. Every row already carries drafted Star-Wars/Jawa-scavenger replacement prose, current vs proposed side by side — you only disagree. Decisions: approve (ship the draft as-is) · tweak (right idea, wrong wording — say the fix in the note) · reject (redo later, not this pass) · skip (not reviewed — ships as drafted, same as approve, but distinguishable from an active approval).
Field shape varies by def type: ThoughtDef/XenotypeDef carry label+description. MentalBreakDef's actual prose lives on the linked MentalStateDef (label, the letter that announces it, the recovery message) — two entries (Catatonic, RunWild) have no linked MentalStateDef and carry only a label.
Posture: default is APPROVE — a row you never touch ships the drafted prose. Nothing is stripped by silence; this is a draft awaiting sign-off, not a whitelist.
How the drafts were made (overturn in one line): 497 rows split across 9 parallel drafting passes (7 ThoughtDef batches by mod, 2 XenotypeDef batches) plus the 22 MentalBreakDef rows hand-drafted against the real MentalStateDef fields via RimSage. Each batch got the same style guide: desert scavenger register, mechanical meaning and mood direction preserved exactly, first-person present-tense for thoughts, Star Wars proper nouns used sparingly. Fifteen rows that came back too close to the original vanilla wording were rewritten by hand afterward. Xenotype entries are re-voiced as the Jawa clan's own practical bestiary entries, not Wookieepedia paraphrase — species facts kept, framing changed.
