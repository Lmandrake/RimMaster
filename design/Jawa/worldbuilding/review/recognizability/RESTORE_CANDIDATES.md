# RESTORE candidates — cut creatures that are genuinely strange

Conservative by construction: the cut pile was cut largely FOR familiarity, and it holds up.
Of 270 cut rows, only **8 creatures** scored STRANGE on the art. Five of those are defensible;
three are not, and are listed so the owner can see the whole set rather than a filtered one.

**A starved biome is never a reason to admit a recognisable animal** — nothing below is
argued from biome need.

## Would defend

| # | name (defName) | mod | read as | why it passes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Segnosaurus (`JRWSegnosaurus`) | Jurassic Rimworld | clawed feathered biped, unnameable | This IS the owner's own example — a therizinosauroid. Huge claws, feathered, reads as a monster, not a dinosaur. |
| 2 | Holcorobeus (`JRWHolcorobeus`) | Jurassic Rimworld | purple horned beetle-beast | Sprite reads as an alien armoured thing; the name lies in the safe direction (the desc calls it a rhinoceros, the art does not). |
| 3 | hydrazoa (`IronScruff_Hydrazoa`) | Primordial Geysers | tendrilled white worm | No terrestrial referent at all; a hunting form nobody can place. bodySize 1.0, a real predator. |
| 4 | jellybird (`IronScruff_Jellybird`) | Primordial Geysers | floating jellyfish-cloud | Hovering, abstract, stinging. Alien silhouette; nothing on Earth flies like it. |
| 5 | Termitotron (`JRWTermitotron`) | Jurassic Rimworld | orange mech-insect | Weakest of the five. Reads as machine-insect rather than an animal — fine in Star Wars register, but it is a big bodySize-3.0 bug and the art is coarse. |

## Would NOT defend (cut STRANGE, but leave cut)

- **thrumwolf** (`GR_Thrumwolf`, VGE) — unnameable only because it is a thrumbo/wolf chimera;
  it is incoherent rather than alien.
- **humanoid paragon** (`GR_ParagonHumanoid`, VGE) — a fleshy humanoid mass; body horror, not
  a creature design.
- **chickenffalo** (`GR_Chickenffalo`, VGE) — a grey masked blob at bodySize 0.2; unreadable
  because the art is poor, not because the creature is strange.

## One finding worth the owner's eye

**Therizinosaurus itself is cut, and its art does not earn a restore.** The owner named the
therizinosaurus as the type-case of a dinosaur that reads alien — but this mod's
`JRWTherizinosaurus` sprite hides the claws and reads as a small sauropod (BORDERLINE).
Its sibling **Segnosaurus** is the sprite that actually carries that look. If the owner wants
the therizinosaur silhouette on Ash'karr, Segnosaurus is the def to restore, not Therizinosaurus.
