_Generated 2026-09-05 from `mechanics.json`. Serves amendment 1 of `design/Jawa/worldbuilding/creature_recognizability_rule.md`: **recognizable + unique mechanics = REGENERATE ART + RENAME**, not cut._

**180 creatures.** Live (un-cut), in-scope, recognizability verdict OBVIOUS or BORDERLINE, and mechanically unique.

Ranked by mechanic quality (a weighted sum over the evidence in `mechanics.json`, +2 for OBVIOUS because an obvious creature is the one that MUST be redrawn, and up to +3 for drawn size per amendment 2).

Each row: **what the new art must NOT look like** = the Earth referent the recognizability pass recorded. **What it COULD look like** = the creative hook, derived from the mechanic.


⚠️ **Droids and mechanoids.** `Outer Rim - Droid Depot` entries appear here because the recognizability pass judged them in-scope creatures. For a Star Wars campaign an astromech reading AS an astromech is the point, not a defect — the Earth-familiarity rule does not obviously govern them. Rule on that class before spending art on it.
