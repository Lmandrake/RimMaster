# Star Wars iconic creatures — the reskin/recreate catalogue

## Why this doc exists

`creature_recognizability_rule.md` disqualifies a RimWorld creature that reads as
a recognisable EARTH animal — "that's a rhino," "that's a housecat" is a CUT, no
matter how good the art is. **In-universe, that logic inverts.** Owner,
2026-09-05, verbatim: *"Iconic Star Wars status protects completely."* A bantha
that instantly reads as a bantha is the campaign working, not failing — the goal
for a Star Wars creature is maximum recognisability AS THAT THING, the mirror
image of the Earth-animal cut rule.

That makes a missing icon an **opportunity**, not a gap to shrug off: reskin an
existing RimWorld creature into it, or recreate it from scratch. This document
is the survey that opportunity-spotting runs against — every iconic Star Wars
creature findable across film, TV, and games, tagged by how well it fits
Ash'karr (desert scavenger world, tidally locked, deserts/caves/seas/nightside,
Jawa clan) so the highest-value gaps surface first.

**Fit tags:**
- **FIT-CORE** — desert, cave, sea, or scavenger-coded; belongs on Ash'karr with
  little or no stretch.
- **FIT-POSSIBLE** — works with a reskin, a habitat stretch, or because the
  species is already canonically habitat-flexible.
- **FIT-POOR** — genuinely iconic, but tied to the wrong world (ice, forest
  moon, city, open ocean, deep space) — included for completeness per the
  brief, not proposed for use as-is.

Each entry: name · appearance(s) · homeworld/habitat · size · role · one-line
silhouette · **FIT tag** · canon note where thin or contested. Organised by
habitat: desert, cave, sea, air/space, other (galaxy-wide or wrong-world).

This is a **research catalogue, not a ruling** — nothing here is authored into
the game, and nothing here is a decision about what gets built. That call goes
to the owner's design-review loop.

## Desert / arid (Ash'karr's core biome)

**Bantha** — films (OT onward), solid canon. Tatooine (also seen elsewhere).
Huge, ~2–3 m at shoulder. Mount/livestock for Tusken Raiders and moisture
farmers. Massive shaggy quadruped with long curling horns, elephantine gait.
**FIT-CORE.**

**Dewback** — films (*A New Hope*), canon. Tatooine. Large, rider-sized (~2 m+).
Mount (Imperial and native use). Green scaly reptile, thick hide, slow but
heat- and sand-resistant. **FIT-CORE.**

**Ronto** — films (background, prequels/OT), canon+Legends. Tatooine. Taller
than a bantha. Pack beast — Jawas' own mount. Long-necked, small-headed,
elephantine legs; poor eyesight, keen hearing/smell. **FIT-CORE** — this is
the Jawas' actual canonical mount, arguably the single highest-priority gap if
Ash'karr doesn't already have an equivalent.

**Eopie** — films (*The Phantom Menace*), canon. Tatooine. Medium-large. Pack
animal (moisture farmers, pod-race pit crews). Long neck, four
tusks/horns, camel-like gait. **FIT-CORE.**

**Jerba** — Legends/comics (horse-analogue). Tatooine. Horse-sized. Beast of
burden, associated with Tusken Raiders in some depictions. Shaggy fur,
elongated head, two crooked horns. **FIT-CORE.** *Canon-thin — Legends/comics
only, no confirmed current-canon appearance.*

**Krayt dragon** — canon (*The Mandalorian* S2) + Legends taxonomy (canyon,
greater, other subspecies). Tatooine. Canyon krayt ~10 m tall/30 m long;
greater krayt 100 m+. Apex predator/hazard. Massive armored reptile with a
horned crown ridge; its bones are Sand People religious markers and its teeth
arm Boba Fett's helmet. **FIT-CORE** — arguably THE flagship desert predator.
*Subspecies taxonomy is mostly Legends; only "krayt dragon" generically plus
the Mandalorian's example are solid current canon.*

**Sarlacc** — films (*Return of the Jedi*), canon, reinforced by *Book of Boba
Fett*. Great Pit of Carkoon, Tatooine. ~100 m buried structure, only the maw
visible. Stationary ambush hazard, digests prey slowly (century-scale digestion
is a Legends detail that may not be canon anymore). Buried beaked maw ringed
with tentacles and inward-slanting teeth. **FIT-CORE** — an iconic desert-pit
hazard, highly ownable as a map feature. *Crosses over with cave/underground —
it is a buried structure, not a surface animal.*

**Womp rat** — dialogue-referenced (*A New Hope*, "not much bigger than two
meters") + fully designed in Legends/games. Tatooine. Size wildly disputed —
Luke's boast implies something huge, nearly every visual depiction shows
something dog-to-fox sized. Pest/vermin, canyon-dwelling; preyed on by
massiffs and anoobas. Hairy monstrous rodent-analogue. **FIT-CORE** — perfect
Jawa-adjacent vermin icon. *Size is genuinely contested canon, not settled —
treat as an open design choice.*

**Massiff** — Legends/games (KOTOR-era onward). Tatooine and other worlds.
Dog-sized to larger. Predator of womp rats, sometimes tamed as a guard beast.
Reptilian/canine hybrid, spiked features. **FIT-CORE.**

**Anooba** — Legends. Tatooine. Wolf-sized. Pack-hunting scavenger/predator —
takes down rontos and eopies in numbers, preys on scurriers. Wolflike canine.
**FIT-CORE** — a scavenger clan's natural antagonist.

**Worrt** — Legends/games, glimpsed briefly in *A New Hope* background.
Tatooine. Small (toad- to horned-lizard-sized). Ambush predator/pest.
Toad/horned-lizard-like desert amphibian. **FIT-CORE.**

**Scurrier** — Legends. Tatooine. Small, rodent-sized. Prey species/pest,
flees and bites when cornered. Small scavenging desert rodent. **FIT-CORE** —
good "infests the camp" vermin.

**Steelpecker** — canon (*Battlefront II*, Jakku Starship Graveyard). Jakku.
Bird-sized. Pest/scavenger — pecks open downed-ship hulls for what's inside.
Scrap-metal-colored bird with a reinforced, armor-cracking beak. **FIT-CORE**
— an off-the-shelf "space vulture," extremely on-theme for a scrap-based clan.

**Profogg** — Legends/games. Tatooine. Medium rodent, larger than a scurrier.
Burrowing pest, builds underground warrens. Horned snout, digging claws.
**FIT-CORE.**

**Gorg** — canon+Legends. Native to Tatooine desert oases/burrows/sewers (a
Naboo/Rori subspecies later grew large and carnivorous in swampland — not
relevant here). Small amphibian. Livestock/pest — aestivates in a hardened
saliva cocoon through drought. Small, unassuming amphibian. **FIT-CORE** — a
genuine desert-oasis amphibian with drought-survival biology built for a
"seas/aquifer on a desert world" niche; a somewhat overlooked high-value pick.

**Vulptex ("crystal fox")** — films (*The Last Jedi*). Crait — a salt-crust
**desert** world, often mentally filed as "ice" because of the visual but
mineral/arid in composition. Small pack animal. Wild scavenger. Fox silhouette
coated in crystalline bristles that shed light when disturbed. **FIT-CORE** —
correctly classified here as arid/mineral desert, not the "wrong world" bucket;
a strong precedent for a desert critter design.

**Luggabeast** — canon (*The Force Awakens* era, featured in *Battlefront* and
Jakku scavenger material). Jakku. Large quadruped, bigger than a human.
Cybernetically-augmented pack beast — literally a scavenger's mount, wearing a
scrap-metal exoskeleton/yoke harness for hauling cargo sledges (used by Teedo
and Niima Outpost traders). **FIT-CORE** — near-perfect Jawa-clan parallel: a
beast of burden that is already part scrap.

**Kath hound** — games (KOTOR, SWTOR), Legends-rooted. Dantooine grassland
(arid plains, not true sand desert). Dog-sized to large "bull" variant.
Predator/pest, pack hunter, sometimes domesticated as a guard dog. Horned,
wolf-like canine. **FIT-POSSIBLE** — habitat is plains/scrub rather than true
desert, but a horned pack predator reskins onto arid terrain with little
friction.

**Orray** — films (*Attack of the Clones*, Geonosis arena), canon. Geonosis
(arid, rocky, hive-world). Medium-large, ridden. Mount (Geonosian picadors ride
them to dodge acklay/nexu in the arena). Strong leathery reptile, quick gait.
**FIT-POSSIBLE** — Geonosis reads more "rocky hive" than sand desert, but arid
enough to stretch.

**Roggwart** — canon (*The Clone Wars*, Grievous's pet). No confirmed desert
homeworld — kept as an exotic pet, not native to an arid world. Large.
Predator/hazard. Long-tailed, curved-horned, thick-skinned. **FIT-POOR** — not
desert-associated; listed for completeness only.

**Sandwhirl** — Legends, thinly sourced (mentioned as having killed/scattered
a jerba herd). Tatooine. Possibly an environmental sand-vortex hazard rather
than a lifeform. **UNCERTAIN** — flag as a hazard, not confirmed fauna, absent
better sourcing.

**Tauntaun** — films (*The Empire Strikes Back*), canon. Hoth — **ice**, not
desert. Medium mount, reptomammal, hunched bipedal-esque gait, cold-adapted.
**FIT-POOR** — included per brief despite the biome mismatch; the only
plausible relevance is a nightside/cold-analogue reskin if Ash'karr's nightside
ever needs a native mount.

## Cave / underground

**Rancor** — films (*Return of the Jedi*) + *The Clone Wars*, *The Mandalorian*,
*Book of Boba Fett*, *Fallen Order*, canon. Native Dathomil (also seen on
Felucia, and already canonically kept in a pit on Tatooine — Jabba's palace).
~5–8 m bipedal. Pit predator/hazard, sometimes tamed as a guard beast
(Nightsisters). Hulking reptilian biped, small eyes, huge jaws, thick hide.
**FIT-CORE** — already canonically present on Tatooine; a strong, iconic
cave-hazard fit.

**Rakghoul** — SWTOR (canon-adjacent MMO; core plague lore is Legends-rooted
from KOTOR), found in tunnels **beneath Tatooine** as well as Alderaan and
Corellia. Human- to large-sized, varies by mutation stage. Hazard — a
disease-spread, tunnel-dwelling swarm threat, not a natural species. Mutated,
distended-jawed, disease-bloated humanoid-animal hybrid. **FIT-CORE** —
literally already sourced to under-Tatooine tunnels; an excellent
underground-hazard fit for a desert scavenger world.

**Kinrath** — games (KOTOR, KOTOR II), Legends. Found in Dantooine's Crystal
Cave and Kashyyyk forests. Large, person-sized or bigger (viper kinrath is
tougher/venomous; a "kinrath queen" hive-boss variant exists). Cave
predator/hazard, venomous. Giant multi-legged arachnid. **FIT-CORE** — a
straightforward poisonous cave-arachnid reskin.

**Dianoga ("garbage squid")** — films (*A New Hope*, trash compactor), canon+
Legends. Not ocean-native — dwells in stagnant water, sewers, garbage
compactors, cisterns, fetid pools galaxy-wide, opportunistically following
filth wherever it exists. Scale ambiguous but implied large enough to threaten
a person; single visible eye-stalk tentacle, rest submerged. Pest/hazard,
eats organic waste. **FIT-CORE** — thematically ideal for a Jawa clan's
cisterns, qanats, and cave-pool water infrastructure: "watch your water" is a
built-in gameplay hook.

**Mynock** — films (*The Empire Strikes Back*), canon+Legends. Deep
space/asteroids; roosts in caves and inside space slugs. ~1.2 m wingspan.
Parasite/pest — chews power cables, drains energy. Leathery bat-like wings, no
eyes, needle mouth. **FIT-POSSIBLE** — a cave-roosting energy/cable parasite
reskins well as a scrap-camp hazard.

**Rakat/"energy spider"** *(likely "ginntho," thin sourcing)* — background
only (*Revenge of the Sith*, Utapau), possibly Legends-only naming. Utapau
sinkhole city. Large enough to walk/ride on a leash. Exotic pet/beast of
burden. Eight legs, feathery antennae, web-spinner. **FIT-POSSIBLE** — cave/
sinkhole predator-turned-pet, reskinnable as a working or guard beast. *Canon
thin — background-only, no on-screen name confirmed; treat as contested.*

**Wampa** — films (*The Empire Strikes Back*), canon. Hoth ice caves — drags
prey to its lair, hangs it from the ceiling. Large (>2 m) bipedal apex
predator. Shaggy white fur, jagged teeth, long claws. **FIT-POSSIBLE** — the
biome (ice) is wrong, but the behavior template (ambush, drag prey to a cave
lair) transfers cleanly to a desert cave predator with fur dropped for
chitin/hide.

**Nexu** — films (*Attack of the Clones* Geonosis arena), canon. Native to
Cholganna (jungle moon), also found on Zhanox/Vodran/Saleucami. ~4.5 m long,
<1 m tall. Arena predator, pack hunter. Catlike, four red eyes (two infrared),
spiked quill mane, whip tail split at the tip. **FIT-POSSIBLE** — native
habitat is jungle, but the arena-ambush role and saber-cat silhouette adapt
plausibly to a canyon/cave ambush predator. *Note: no source confirms a
Cato Neimoidia cave association — its correct canon home is Cholganna.*

**Oggdo Bogdo** — game, canon (*Jedi: Fallen Order*, Bogano cave/tunnel).
Large oversized amphibian miniboss. Cave hazard/predator. Black warty skin,
orange speckles, long prehensile tongue. **FIT-POSSIBLE** — cave-ambush
template transfers; the amphibian look is a stretch for an arid world but
could reskin as scaled/chitinous.

**Rabid Jotaz** — game, canon (*Jedi: Fallen Order*, Zeffo ruins/tunnels).
Massive ape-like tunnel brute. Hazard/miniboss. **FIT-POSSIBLE** — generic
enough silhouette to reskin as a tunnel-dwelling brute.

**Gundark** — dialogue reference only (*The Empire Strikes Back*, "mad as a
gundark") + Legends/EU art. Homeworld contested (Vanqor in some canon
mentions, jungle/rocky Outer Rim worlds in Legends). Large, ape-like, said to
be immensely strong. **FIT-POOR/UNCERTAIN** — never shown fully on screen in
live-action canon; treat any visual description as approximate, not confirmed
design canon.

**Akk dog** — Legends/EU (comics). Native to Haruun Kal (jungle) but used
galaxy-wide, including in desert-set EU stories, as a domesticated Jedi or
civilian companion/guard beast, occasionally a mount. Speeder-length, reptilian
dog-like quadruped, lightsaber-resistant hide. **FIT-POSSIBLE** — the species
is explicitly habitat-flexible in its own lore, so a desert-adapted reskin is
low-friction. *Mostly Legends; sparse in current canon.*

*(Sarlacc is also cave-adjacent — see Desert section; it is filed there as the
primary habitat.)*

## Sea / aquatic

**Firaxan shark** — games (KOTOR, Legends-rooted) + light canon reference (a
tooth used as a lightsaber ornament, Iskat Akaris). Manaan's global ocean,
especially the Hrakert Rift; one boss individual ("the Progenitor") is
enormous. Large apex predator, territorial, weak to sonic pulses. Shark
archetype. **FIT-CORE** — a direct apex-predator fit for Ash'karr's actual
seas. *Physical description beyond "shark" is canon-thin.*

**Sando aqua monster** — films (*The Phantom Menace*), canon+Legends. Naboo
Abyss (planetary-core seas). 160–200 m, apex predator. Feline-muscled sea
serpent, finned clawed grasping limbs, cavernous mouth. **FIT-POSSIBLE** — the
homeworld is wrong (Naboo), but the concept (colossal ambush predator in a
"sea" hazard zone) is a strong donor for an Ash'karr sea set-piece hazard.

**Colo claw fish** — films (*The Phantom Menace*), canon+Legends. Naboo Abyss.
Second-largest Naboo sea predator. Crocodile-like head, long snout, stalked
eyes, jaw that unhinges to swallow prey bigger than its own head. **FIT-
POSSIBLE** — strong reskin donor; the unhinging-jaw silhouette is distinctive
and habitat-portable.

**Opee sea killer** — films (*The Phantom Menace*), canon+Legends. Naboo
Abyss. ~20 m mid-tier predator, itself preyed on by larger sea monsters.
Crustacean/fish hybrid. **FIT-POSSIBLE** — a mid-tier sea-predator concept,
habitat-portable.

**Gorg (Naboo/Rori subspecies)** — see the Tatooine desert form under Desert
(FIT-CORE); the swamp-mutated large carnivorous form is not desert-relevant
and is noted only as trivia, not a separate catalogue entry.

**Thala-siren ("sea sow")** — films (*The Last Jedi*), canon. Ahch-To coastal
cliffs/ocean. ~18 ft. Livestock-like — females harvested for green milk. Large
pinniped, big flippers, long snout, basks on cliffs. **FIT-POSSIBLE** — the
world is wrong (wet/temperate), but a coastal livestock pinniped is a strong
direction for an Ash'karr sea-village milk animal.

**Aiwha ("air whale")** — films (*Attack of the Clones*), canon. Kamino
oceans/skies, ridden by Kaminoans. Large winged cetacean, amphibious flier.
Mount. **FIT-POSSIBLE** — Kamino is a rain-ocean world, but the "sea mount
that also crosses to air/land" concept could stretch into a sea-to-cave-lake
working mount.

**Yobshrimp** — mentioned only in passing alongside the three Naboo Abyss
predators above. **UNCERTAIN** — could not confirm an independent physical
description; likely background/incidental naming only.

**Dianoga** — see Cave/Underground (its actual niche is cisterns/sewers, not
open sea); cross-referenced here because it is sometimes filed as aquatic.

**GAP note:** no strong Star Wars precedent was found for a creature native
specifically to an **underground aquifer or cave-sea**, as distinct from open
ocean or garbage water — the closest analogue is the dianoga (opportunistic
cistern/sewage dweller). If Ash'karr's seas are meant to include a subterranean
component, that niche looks under-served by canon and may be a genuine
invention opportunity rather than a reskin one.

## Air / space (borderline habitat — included for completeness)

**Purrgil** — canon (*Rebels*, *Ahsoka*, referenced in *The Mandalorian*).
Habitat is **deep space**, not sea or sky — semi-sentient migratory "space
whales" that travel through vacuum/hyperspace breathing rare Clouzon-36 gas.
Massive (capable of carrying/relocating starships). Bluish-purple streamlined
body, four hind tentacles, bulbous head. **FIT-POOR** — despite "whale"
naming, this is not an aquatic or planetary creature at all; no habitat
transfer is possible for a ground-based colony.

**Howler / summa-verminoth** — canon (*Solo*), lives in the Akkadese Maelstrom
(a gas cloud near Kessel), not a landable habitat. ~7,500 m, multi-eyed
tentacled leviathan. Space hazard. **FIT-POOR** — no terrestrial habitat to
map onto. *"Howler" as a distinct species name did not resolve independently
of the summa-verminoth — likely conflation; treat as UNCERTAIN.*

**Convor** — *The Clone Wars*/*Rebels* era, canon. Already multi-planet
(Takodana, Malachor, Atollon, a Kashyyyk moon). Small, owl-like avian with a
prehensile monkey-style tail. Companion/omen bird, mildly Force-sensitive.
**FIT-POSSIBLE** — already habitat-flexible across several worlds; a desert
scavenger-bird variant is a light stretch.

**Porg** — films (*The Last Jedi*), canon. Ahch-To ocean cliffs. Small,
flightless-looking stocky seabird, flat beakless face, big eyes, webbed feet.
Pest/livestock (also eaten). **FIT-POOR** — a specifically wet, cliffside
seabird niche; low reskin value, and heavily over-exposed as a meme creature.

*(Steelpecker is a bird but is filed under Desert, its actual canon habitat —
Jakku — since that is the higher-value classification.)*

## Other — galaxy-wide mounts/livestock/pests, and iconic-but-wrong-world

These are creatures famous enough that an average fan would name them, but
either native to the wrong biome (ice/forest/city/jungle/swamp) or explicitly
habitat-flexible across many worlds. Habitat-flexible ones are strong
reskin donors regardless of their canon homeworld; habitat-locked ones are
included per the brief and marked FIT-POOR with the reason stated.

**Reek** — films (*Attack of the Clones*), canon. Already found on multiple
worlds **including Tatooine**. Large. Livestock/arena beast — thick rhino-like
quadruped, horned frill, tusks, aggressive when meat-fed. **FIT-POSSIBLE** —
already canonically present on Tatooine; a strong bantha-adjacent livestock
reskin candidate.

**Varactyl** — films (*Revenge of the Sith*), canon. Utapau (sinkhole world).
Large mount ("Boga," Obi-Wan's steed). Lizard-bird hybrid, colorful neck
plumage, vertical cliff-climbing claws. **FIT-POSSIBLE** — the canyon/cliff-
climbing mount concept transfers cleanly to a canyon or mesa desert.

**Blurrg** — *The Clone Wars*/*Rebels*/*The Mandalorian*, canon. Homeworld
uncertain (Ord Mantell region). ~2 m biped. Notoriously stubborn pack
mount/beast. Stocky reptilian biped, small arms, wide toothy mouth. **FIT-
POSSIBLE** — a habitat-agnostic "stubborn pack lizard" concept, reskins
easily.

**Nekko** — game, canon (*Jedi: Survivor*). Koboh — rugged, rocky frontier
world with mixed biomes. Large rideable quadruped, climbs steep/slippery
slopes, chainable jump. Sturdy lizard/dog-hybrid mount built for rough
terrain. **FIT-POSSIBLE** — Koboh is frontier rather than true desert, but the
"sturdy climbing mount" niche reskins cleanly for canyon terrain.

**Voritor lizard** — Legends (*Star Wars Galaxies* bestiary). Dantooine
plains, also Dathomir. ~2 m. Solitary ambush predator, ridden as a mount by
Nightsister "Sisters of the Voritor." Fan-like dorsal spike, slow stalk then
explosive charge, mildly venomous. **FIT-POSSIBLE** — a plains reptile mount/
predator with a desert-adjacent silhouette.

**Fathier** — films (*The Last Jedi*). Homeworld unspecified, used at Canto
Bight (urban racetrack). ~3 m at shoulder. Racing mount/abused livestock.
Sleek equine with a broad flat head and lizard-like features. **FIT-
POSSIBLE** — a habitat-neutral space-horse concept, reskins onto a desert
racing or pack mount easily.

**Orbak** — films (*The Rise of Skywalker*). Kef Bir (ocean moon). Large
mount (Resistance cavalry). Shaggy-maned horse-like steed with curling tusks.
**FIT-POSSIBLE** — equine mount silhouette transfers regardless of origin
world.

**Kybuck** — Legends. Kashyyyk prairies. Deer-sized, fast (~90 km/h).
Livestock/status animal, domesticated by chieftains. Horned deer-like runner.
**FIT-POSSIBLE** — a generic fast desert-antelope analogue, habitat-neutral
concept.

**Shaak** — Legends/*The Clone Wars* background. Naboo grasslands. Plump
quadruped. Livestock (meat, hide, ambergris). Rounded body, thin legs, small
head, mushroom-like silhouette. **FIT-POSSIBLE** — generic domesticated
livestock, transfers to desert herding easily.

**Tooka cat** — Legends/canon references. Homeworld Adoris, but lives
galaxy-wide including seedy urban settings. Small. Pest/companion feline,
wide color variation. **FIT-POSSIBLE** — already established as living
almost anywhere; a strong candidate for a Jawa-camp pest/companion.

**Gizka** — game (KOTOR), Legends. Found "throughout the galaxy" — notably
infesting the Ebon Hawk, with a Tatooine-set arc in KOTOR. Small,
frog-lizard hybrid. Pest — reproduces explosively, overwhelms ship stores/
ecosystems. Comically wide-eyed and harmless-looking despite plague-level
breeding. **FIT-CORE** — thematically ideal vermin/pest for a scavenger
clan's warrens or ship stores, and already has a Tatooine-arc appearance.

**Akk dog** — see Cave/Underground (FIT-POSSIBLE, habitat-flexible).

**Acklay** — films (*Attack of the Clones*), canon. Vendaxa (amphibious
world). Gigantic (~4 m+). Arena predator/beast. Bony carapace, six clawed
insectile legs, grasping pincer-hands. **FIT-POOR** — a striking silhouette
but wrong biome (amphibious swamp); low reskin value without heavy rework.

**Loth-cat** — *Rebels*/*Ahsoka*, canon. Lothal plains. Small. Pest/companion
predator, small serval-like striped cat with oversized ears. **FIT-POOR** —
its identity is tied specifically to Lothal's Force mythology; a generic
small desert feline pest is an easy reskin, but not this particular icon.

**Loth-wolf** — *Rebels*, canon. Lothal. Horse-sized. Rare Force-sensitive
guardian/mount, grey wolf with faint blue Force-glow eyes. **FIT-POOR** — tied
specifically to Lothal's Force mythology; wrong world.

**Kowakian monkey-lizard** — films (*Return of the Jedi*, Salacious Crumb) +
games. Kowak (jungle). ~70 cm. Pest/companion "court jester" creature, small
reptilian biped, floppy ears, cackling laugh. **FIT-POOR** — jungle world, but
the tiny scavenger-camp pest archetype is an easy generic reskin (not this
specific icon).

**Nuna** — canon background, Naboo swamps. Small gamebird, plump and
flightless-looking. Livestock/pest. **FIT-POOR** — swamp-specific and
canon-thin; description is closer to "space chicken" than a distinctive
silhouette.

**Roba** — Legends. Aralia (also Taanab, Talus, Endor) — forest moons and
farmland, not desert. ~900 kg pig-like livestock/prey animal (hunted by
Ranats). **FIT-POOR** — explicitly non-desert in every cited habitat.

**Veermok** — Legends (*Star Wars Galaxies*-era). Naboo forest (aggressive
subspecies on Eriadu). Mid-sized, solitary, highly aggressive. **FIT-POOR** —
wrong biome, and canon specifics are contested/thin beyond "aggressive loner."

**Boma beast** — game (KOTOR II), Legends. Dxun jungle moon. Small to very
large, apex jungle predator (preyed on in turn by the larger zakkeg). Shaggy,
ravenous quadruped, tamable via Force Beast Control in-game. **FIT-POOR** —
jungle moon, wrong biome.

**Zakkeg** — game (KOTOR II), Legends, mentioned alongside boma. Dxun jungle.
Larger than a boma. Apex predator. **FIT-POOR/UNCERTAIN** — jungle biome, and
details beyond "bigger than boma, kills them" are thin.

**Binog** — game (*Jedi: Fallen Order*), canon, background-only. Bogano
waters/mainland. Very large — its skeleton is an explicit visual homage to the
classic krayt dragon skeleton. Passive background fauna, never fought.
Amphi-mammalian, seen mostly at a distance or as a skeleton. **FIT-POOR/
UNCERTAIN** — swamp-ish world, barely characterized; included for
completeness and the krayt-skeleton trivia only.

**Peko-peko** — Legends (*Star Wars Galaxies* bestiary; a "toxic peko-peko"
tameable mount variant exists). Naboo's Gungan swamps. >2 m beak-to-tail.
Tameable mount, clawed-wing reptavian that climbs/glides through swamp
canopy. **FIT-POOR** — swamp/forest world, not desert.

**Tauntaun, Wampa** — see Desert and Cave sections respectively (both
FIT-POOR/FIT-POSSIBLE, ice-world originals).
