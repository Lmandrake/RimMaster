Biome Roster for Review

Every biome in the confirmed / candidate stack, grouped by the role it would play in the "mostly-desert, highly-volcanic, rare-water-ringed-by-vicious-jungle" world. The purpose of this list is to decide how many columns the resource × terrain matrix needs — the current matrix collapses four or five real biomes into one "alien/dark" column, and two of its columns (river, salt flat) are actually landform/terrain states, not biomes. Grounded in biome_terrain_palette.md (defNames verified from 1.6 sources except where marked).

Two structural notes before you read:  (1) River valley and salt flat aren't BiomeDefs — a river is a landform (Geological Landforms) that can occur inside several biomes, and a salt flat is an ExtremeDesert/Desert tile with a hardpan terrain set in Map Designer. They still deserve matrix columns (they play distinct four-axis roles), but they're authored differently than a biome. (2) The six locked anchors from §2C are marked ANCHOR — those already have decided allocations; everything else is a candidate to add.
