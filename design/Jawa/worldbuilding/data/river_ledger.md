## The water story, in eight lines

1. Ash'karr does not rain. The dayside's water is made in one place: **the Scald**, a boiling
   crater sea in the substellar Anvil, fouled at heart and clean in its breath.
2. Its steam climbs the **Cratercrown** and the **Dew Horn** and falls as the planet's only rain
   on the Contagion's red peaks; the runoff leaves those valleys red and arrives below sterilized
   (R-H1, R-H7). And by the owner's ruling the Scald itself is **perched** — a lake a mile
   above the desert, spilling through **one notch in the Spine** as the largest river on the world.
3. So every river sources high — at the Scald or on a Contagion peak — and runs downhill to
   wherever a tidally-locked desert lets it stop.
4. Nine of sixteen never reach a sea. Extreme Desert sun on open water boils it off mile by
   mile; the river thickens, salts, and **dies in a grave** — a salt pan where the last water
   gives up (`the_greentide.md` §3). Droid enclaves squat the graves.
5. Only the great ones reach a coast: one to the **Twilight Sea**, two creeks to the **Grey Sea**,
   whose brackish **Miasma** deltas are the lifeboat of a dying sea.
6. The **terminator** is where the seas lie, so a river that reaches the sea has crossed the whole
   dayside gradient: cold clean source, broad living Greentide reach, milky brine, mangal muck.
7. In the deep dayside a "river" can be something else: the Rust Cathedral's eight river tiles are
   **coolant canals**; the Scarlands' three are the ancients' leaking plumbing.
8. The Jawa read rivers as **news**: a flood is a salvage strike, a grave is a droid market, a
   delta is a flotsam beach. The names below are what the crawler crews call them.

## The rivers

### R01 — the Wynd · LargeRiver · 102 tiles · terminal basin
**Source.** Twenty-two heads on the Cratercrown's Contagion peaks (highest 2,021 m) and the
Forge-type highs (Volcano/LavaField) — the Scald's breath, condensed, red, and sterilized on the
way down. **Course.** The Greentide's spine: 74 of its tiles are this river — the living reach,
steaming, Shatterers and Lungers, root causeways, the Wildsteam's home water. It brushes the
Forge's ash skirts, then runs out through Hollow Verge into Pyrelands grass. **Fate.** Dies at
1 m in the burning savanna — the **Ashgrave Pan**: a salt crust the standing burn walks over,
droids at the crust. The Greentide's "every river ends in a grave" made literal at scale.
**Character.** The dayside's great dying river — glass-clear at the top, milk and brine at the
end. Data and lore agree (no Scald contact).

### R02 — Duskflow · HugeRiver · 91 tiles · reaches Twilight Sea AND the Scald
**Source (lore).** The Scald's notch — the perched lake spilling ~5,000 units through the Spine —
joined by Dew Horn tributaries (1,473 m). **Course.** Greentide gorge, a dry Shrubland crossing
(Dew Belt), the Fever Wood mangals, Greentide again, then the Miasma proper: 22 mangrove
tiles of stilt-roots and breath-tide. **Fate.** The **Twilight Sea** (tile 3450) — the one
river on Ash'karr that runs source-to-sea whole. **Character.** The planet's artery; the
Wildsteam pilgrim road; the flotsam yard's supplier.
⚠️ **Conflict.** The data has four separate arms of R02 *entering* the Scald (tiles 17342,
15175, 2931, 1310, all −350 m) and one arm reaching the Twilight Sea; the ruling wants ONE
outflow through ONE notch. Reconcile: one of the four Scald contacts is the Notch (recommend
17342, the LargeRiver-class edge); the other three are Spine runoff *into* the pan and stay
inflows. Which is a repaint call.

### R03 — Rustwave · HugeRiver · 32 tiles · reaches the Scald
**Source (lore).** The Scald's Anvil rim. **Course.** Greentide, then eight tiles of
**coolant canal** through the Rust Cathedral (ruled: not water — coolant, eels, the hum), three
tiles of the Scarlands' leaking plumbing (its sheet flags them: nothing should flow there), a
Greentide reach, and a mangal stand. **Fate.** Dies at 71 m in a Dune Sea mangrove sump — a
Miasma in miniature with no sea behind it. **Character.** The river that runs *through the
machine*: highest measured flow on the planet (28,936), and the Jawa will not drink below the
Cathedral. ⚠️ **Conflict.** Data: source at 71 m, mouth in the Scald. R1 reading: Scald →
sump. If R02 is the Notch, R03 must be an INFLOW (a perched lake has one spill), and its
"source" reverts to the 71 m mangal end — a swamp feeding a canal feeding a crater, which reads
badly. Recommend: R03 is the **coolant loop**, not a river at all — the Cathedral draws Scald
water, and the Scald end is an intake. Owner rules.

### R04 — the Braid · Creek · 11 tiles · terminal basin
Three gorge heads (991/733/461 m) braid into one Greentide creek in the Dune Sea and die at
84 m without leaving the gorge. A nursery reach for Gnawers; no faction water. Sibling of
R14 (same gorge country, lat −16..−22).

### R05 — the Feverwash · River · 11 tiles · reaches the Scald
**Lore.** Off the Scald's Fever Wood rim, a Greentide run into a Fever Wood mangal where it
dies at 26 m — a brackish sump the Wildsteam count among their pilgrim channels. Twin of R06.
⚠️ Data has it entering the Scald at 8493; see Conflicts.

### R06 — the Stiltwater · River · 10 tiles · reaches the Scald
**Lore.** Same rim, same fate (13 m, three mangrove tiles of stilt-roots). The pair are the
Scald's two "short spills" — or two inflows, depending on the notch ruling. Data: enters the
Scald at 19369.

### R07 — the Redthread · Creek · 7 tiles · terminal basin
Rises red on a Dew Horn Contagion peak (1,329 m), sterilizes on the descent, threads the
Shrubland's leaning fuzz and dies in the Long Sand at 459 m — a Cracked-Lands-style hidden
water: Moisture Farmers' cistern country. Data and lore agree.

### R08 — Dewdrop · Creek · 7 tiles · terminal basin
Two Cratercrown heads (551/387 m), a Greentide creek, dies at 1 m in the **Dew Belt pan**
(tile 11915). Its grave is shared with R15's (11914) — one salt pan, two mouths; a droid
enclave's obvious seat.

### R09 — the Greyfall · Creek · 6 tiles · reaches the Grey Sea
Ashfall Range Contagion peak (1,720 m) to the Grey Sea in six tiles: the steepest fall on
the planet, through Shrubland into the Deepwater vigil's shrinking brine. Agrees with lore.

### R10 — the Shortfall · Creek · 6 tiles · terminal basin
Its neighbour's twin (1,342 m head, same range) that dies at 1 m *beside* the Grey Sea and
never reaches it — the Jawa joke is the name. Agrees with lore.

### R11 — the Chime · Creek · 5 tiles · reaches the Grey Sea
Ashfall Range peak (1,627 m) through Shrubland and one Cracked Lands tile — water chimes ring
in the crack before the flood — to the Grey Sea at 16897. Agrees with lore.

### R12 — the Catch · Creek · 5 tiles · reaches the Scald
Two heads on the Cratercrown (1,587/1,407 m) through the Contagion and Greentide into the
pan at 11943 — the **steam-catch runnel**: the Scald's breath falling straight back into the
Scald. ⚠️ Data: inflow. R1: outflow. Recommend it stays an INFLOW in the lore (a 5-tile creek
climbing 1,900 m out of a lake cannot be told), the cleanest case for "the Scald's outflow is
its steam."

### R13 — the Hushwater · Creek · 5 tiles · terminal basin
A Shrubland creek in the Dune Sea's sunward tail near the Anvil (612 m → 1 m): fog-fed,
silent, dies in sand. Agrees with lore.

### R14 — the Muck · Creek · 5 tiles · terminal basin
A Dune Sea gorge head (1,045 m) running a Greentide slot to 99 m. Sibling of R04.

### R15 — the Sprout · Creek · 3 tiles · terminal basin
Cratercrown foot (392 m) to the Dew Belt pan (11914, 1 m) — R08's grave-mate.

### R16 — the Mist · Creek · 2 tiles · terminal basin
Two tiles, 287 m to 145 m, Greentide. Two steps and it is gone.

## Conflicts — data vs the perched-Scald ruling

1. **Every Scald contact reads as INFLOW** (9 edges, 8 distinct shore tiles, 5 rivers; the
   Scald is −350 m, the planet's floor). The ruling says the Scald spills through ONE notch. The
   frozen `the_scald.md` says "eight rivers in, none out." Reconciliation offered: the eight
   shore contacts ARE the sheet's "eight rivers" (R02×4, R03, R05, R06, R12); ONE of them is
   the Notch (R02's 17342 edge) and the ruling's "largest river" is **Duskflow** to the
   Twilight Sea; the other seven stay inflows. This amends `the_scald.md` §3/§5 by one word
   ("seven in, one out") and leaves R1 intact.
2. **R03 (Ironwater)**: max flow 28,936 — numerically the largest — but 32 tiles ending in an
   inland sump. If "largest" means flow, R03 is the Notch and R02 is not. Recommend length +
   sea-reach decides (R02).
3. **R12 (Catchrun)**: outflow reading is physically untellable; keep inflow.
4. **Foliage**: the ruling says Scald rivers carry Feralisk jungle / Greater Swamp; the paint
   carries the Greentide (`BiomeCypreJungle`). The Greentide sheet's lush-rule amendment already
   owns dayside river lines — no action, noted for the sitting.
5. Tile 11183 (flow 41, Wasteland, no link) is nobody's river — a spring or a data gap; unnamed.
