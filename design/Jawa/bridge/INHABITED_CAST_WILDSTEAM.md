# `Inhabited` — cast 07: the Wildsteam Clan

_Authored 2026-08-20. **Faith: `the Green Oath`** — `Structure_Animist` · `NaturePrimacy` ·
`TreeConnection` · `Collectivist` · `AnimalPersonhood`. **Taboo: cutting a living tree.**
Climate per §5.9: ⭐ the only faction that plants — write the smugness, then everyone it costs._

> *Everyone else on this planet eats what somebody else dropped. These people eat what they
> grew, and they will not let you forget it, and the growing is done with a blade in the
> other hand.*

### 🔎 DECIDE's pass
✅ **Accepted.** The smugness is split across three faces so it never becomes one joke —
Rrowhaan gives food away and *owns you for it*, Ohrra makes you climb nine hundred steps for
it, and Ossa, the convert, simply cannot stop counting it.
⭐ **And the cost is properly paid**: Drell is shunned for doing the killing everyone eats,
Rooda is punished by relentless public kindness, Rrogh is buried alive in life-debts, and
Pell Yoon is feeding the neighbours against a ruling and cannot keep it to herself.
⭐ **`Nikko the Sap-Namer`** is the best single idea in it: a fifteen-year-old given the
naming of saplings as a joke nobody stopped in time, who has ruled that no tree she has named
may be taken for timber — **that is all four thousand one hundred of them, the clan's entire
timber policy is now a teenager, and she is winning.**
✅ The Ithorian Law of Life shapes three characters *differently* (a ledger, an absurd
four-day etiquette, and a man too kind and dull to notice he is giving the clan away), and
none repeats the Hutt cast's `Denno Fash`. No fourth "duty continued after its object is gone."
Traits verified. No `Pyromaniac`.

## GREENMOUTH — the river-town, where the Scald's trunk enters the vicious jungle

**Elder Rrowhaan** · Wookiee · m · 212
`traits: Gourmand, GreatMemory, Abrasive`
childhood: born under the only four trees his grandmother managed to keep alive.
adult: forty years refusing to sell a single grain of anything to anybody.
> He will not take your money. He will pile food in your arms, in public, loudly, until you cannot carry it, and roar down anyone who tries to pay — and then he remembers, forever, in order, exactly what each of you owes him. Eleven factions call him generous. Three of them are terrified of him.

**Bekka Sunn** · Cathar · f · 27
`traits: Masochist, Brawler, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: put on the web-line at nine because she was the only child who did not scream.
adult: cuts feralisk silk off the crop rows at night, alone, by choice.
> She comes back at dawn shaking with venom, grinning, fur matted flat, and announces the night's bite count to the breakfast shed like a score. She is on four hundred and something. She has twice gone out on a rest day and lied about where she was going.

**Vaal Oom-Tikaa** · Ithorian · m · 88
`traits: GreatMemory, Abrasive, Ascetic`
childhood: taught the Law of Life at six and never found a single exception to it.
adult: seed-warden. Nothing leaves the vault without his hand on it.
> He keeps a running count of what every person he has ever met owes the world — two plants for every one taken, and he has never lost a number in sixty years. He will tell you yours to your face, alone, quietly, because airing it to anyone but you would be filthy manners. *"You are ninety-one trees behind. I have seeds. You have not asked correctly."*

**Ossa Krail** · human · f · 34
`traits: Jealous, Gourmand, TooSmart`
childhood: a homestead nine kilometres from green, on stored water, counting days.
adult: married in at nineteen for the food and runs the drying sheds better than anyone born here.
> She weighs every crate in and every crate out and then weighs them again, and she watches what you put on your plate — openly, with her whole face — and she cannot stop and knows she cannot. The born-green treat food as air. She has never once managed to.

**Tikk of the Underline** · Ewok · m · 31
`traits: CreepyBreathing, GreatMemory, Abrasive`
childhood: raised in the root-galleries under the rows; did not stand on open ground until eleven.
adult: runs the whole trap-line at branch height and has never once used the paths.
> He drops into your conversation from four metres up, mid-sentence, and he has been there the whole time. The spore mask makes him whistle wetly on every exhale, which is how the jungle knows he is coming and you do not. He considers walking on the ground a slovenly habit and says so to elders.

**Grahnn** · Wookiee · f · 178
`traits: Delicate, TooSmart, Kind`
childhood: a bad bleeder in a family of jungle-cutters, kept indoors and resented for it.
adult: the finest grafter in the clan and has not set foot outside the glasshouse in thirty years.
> Two and a half metres of Wookiee doing needle-work with hooked tools the size of a fingernail, because a scratch puts her in bed for a week and a fall would kill her. Every food crop on this river came off her bench. She has never seen the fields they grow in and asks about them constantly, of everyone, forever.

**Bo Kesh** · Yttakin · m · 44
`traits: ShootingAccuracy(TriggerHappy), Abrasive, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: pulled out of a Hutt slaver's hold at seven and never made a secret of it.
adult: riverwarden. Nine skiffs on the bottom of the Scald, all of them crewed at the time.
> Cheerful, sunburnt, enormous, and he will open up on a slaver boat before the hail is finished. He also let a feralisk carry off two-thirds of the herd last season without lifting the rifle, and argued the whole town round to agreeing with him, and would do it again tomorrow. Animals are kin. Blackstar are not.

**Mother Ulu** · Togruta · f · 61
`traits: AnnoyingVoice, GreatMemory, Gourmand`
childhood: montrals came in early; she could hear a hollow gourd across a field at nine.
adult: walks every row before every harvest and is never wrong about a single plant.
> She hums as she goes — a flat subsonic pulse you feel behind your eyes rather than hear — and humans who stand near her for an hour get nosebleeds and have learned to move. She has been asked to stop for forty years. She has explained, forty years running, that she cannot see without it.

**Nessa Duun** · human · f · 52
`traits: GreatMemory, Ascetic, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: unremarkable. She would tell you it was, at length, if asked.
adult: works the Greenmouth ferry. Nineteen years, four crossings an hour.
> Before every single crossing she delivers the safety briefing, word for word, four minutes, to the same eleven people who cross twice a day and have it memorised. Nobody has ever talked her out of it. Nobody has ever drowned on her ferry either, and she connects these two facts in a way she will also explain.

## KETTLE STATION — a boiler-house on stilts in the meridian's poison marsh

**Warden Zesh Vool** · Lasat · m · 52
`traits: GreatMemory, Brawler, NaturalMood(Depressive)`
childhood: his world was cleared while he was offworld buying rope. He has the receipt.
adult: took the Oath at thirty, which meant taking the arithmetic with it.
> Two metres of purple-grey muscle who plants two trees for every life he has ended and keeps the tally nailed to the station wall where anyone can read it. He is four hundred and six behind. He is polite, he is quiet, he plants every morning before light, and he knows perfectly well he will die owing.

**Nnu Pell** · Ithorian · f · 44
`traits: Ascetic, GreatMemory, Delicate`
childhood: raised where a grievance goes to its cause and to no one else, ever.
adult: marsh-station botanist. Slow hands, perfect results, four decades of them.
> When someone wrongs her she walks — four days across poison marsh, on foot, alone, in a rebreather — to say one sentence to the one person it belongs to, and then walks four days back. She has done it eleven times. She has never once complained to anyone else about anything, and the station finds this unbearable.

**Hakko Vurr** · Yttakin · m · 38
`traits: DrugDesire(ChemicalFascination), TooSmart, Gourmand`
childhood: ate something he shouldn't have at four and has been chasing it ever since.
adult: catalogued three hundred and eleven marsh fungi. Method: eat a little of each.
> He has been clinically dead twice, both times on purpose, both times taking notes right up to the point where the hand stopped. He is the reason this station can eat anything at all out here. He is also visibly, permanently high on something he found on Tuesday and will offer you some.

**Tenn Ghosh** · Togorian · f · 46
`traits: TooSmart, Abrasive, Ascetic`
childhood: built her first turbine at eleven, on a world where the women build everything.
adult: designed the steam plant the entire clan is named for and believes none of their religion.
> She calls the Oath a very good crop rotation with a hat on, out loud, to Elders, at dinner. They keep her because without her nothing on this marsh has pressure, water or heat, and she keeps them because her mate comes to the station for one month a year and eleven months of solitude suits her exactly.

**Boggo Umm** · Ewok · m · 19
`traits: SlowLearner, Brawler, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: given a glider at eight and never asked for anything else.
adult: hunts marsh-things off the updrafts with a stone-tipped spear he made himself.
> He is fast, he is lean, and there is genuinely nothing behind the eyes. He has refused the clan's rifles on a doctrine he invented personally and cannot explain twice the same way, he has been told forty times that the mycotic caps are what is making him ill, and he nods, and he agrees, and he eats them.

**Drell Ashk** · Cathar · m · 33
`traits: Ascetic, NaturalMood(Depressive), Brawler`
childhood: watched the rite performed once at eleven and understood that somebody has to.
adult: the only person at Kettle Station who will slaughter an animal.
> He does it correctly, with the full rite, and everyone thanks him properly, and no one will sit at his table. He eats standing at the rail. He has never asked for this to change and he has never asked to be relieved, and every time a new family arrives he watches to see how long the politeness takes.

**Ssura Kadd** · Togruta · f · 25
`traits: FastLearner, Abrasive, Delicate`
childhood: learned the marsh's whole sound-map in a season, which nobody else has managed.
adult: station pathfinder. Never lost. Has lost four other people.
> Her montrals read the bog through the mist like a lit road, so she moves at a walk where everyone else crawls, and she genuinely cannot conceive that the rest of you are not simply being slow on purpose. She snaps at stragglers. She will not shorten her stride. Two of the four were found.

**Shoo Bann-Aloo** · Ithorian · m · 61
`traits: SlowLearner, Kind, Ascetic`
childhood: soil.
adult: soil, at Kettle Station, for thirty-four years.
> The translator collar is set an octave too low, so an hour of drainage layers, root nitrogen and compost temperature arrives as a warm subterranean rumble you feel in the floor. He will explain the whole system to any visitor who asks, free, in full — which means he has given four rival factions the only genuine secret this clan possesses and has never once noticed.

## THE STAIRS OF VAR ULLU — nine hundred cut steps, terrace gardens, and the hot spring at the top

**Warden-of-the-Stair Ohrra** · Lasat · f · 58
`traits: Abrasive, Jealous, BodyMastery`
childhood: carried water up these stairs from the age of five, like everybody.
adult: decides who is permitted to buy food and at what price, and has for twenty years.
> Every buyer carries their own goods up all nine hundred steps while she climbs beside them, at fifty-eight, without breathing hard, chatting. She has never made an exception — not for a Hutt factor, not for an Imperial quartermaster, not for a dying man's family. Complain on the way up and the price goes up with you.

**Grandmother Yekk** · Wookiee · f · 301
`traits: GreatMemory, Ascetic, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: three hundred years ago, and she will not discuss it.
adult: she is the seed record. There is no other copy and she has forbidden one.
> Four hundred and six species — germination, depth, season, soil, the lot — held entirely inside one very old head, because paper burns and paper gets stolen and she has watched both happen. She recites eleven a day to a bored fourteen-year-old. The Elders have begged her for forty years. She is going, and she knows it, and she has not moved an inch.

**Nikko the Sap-Namer** · Ewok · f · 15
`traits: GreatMemory, Kind, Abrasive`
childhood: is still it, mostly.
adult: given the naming of the saplings at seven as a joke that nobody stopped in time.
> Four thousand one hundred trees on these terraces have names, she knows every one, and she will walk you round and introduce you personally for as long as you fail to escape. She has also ruled that no tree she has named may be taken for timber. That is all of them. The clan's entire timber policy is now a fifteen-year-old girl and she is winning.

**Kass Vekk** · Cathar · m · 40
`traits: Cannibal, Kind, Ascetic`
childhood: ordinary, on a homestead that ran out of water when he was twenty-nine.
adult: teaches the terrace children their letters, their plants and their rites. Beloved.
> He crossed four hundred kilometres of deep desert with six other people and arrived alone, and he said what he had done before anyone asked him, on the first day, standing at the gate. The Oath took him: a life handed back is a debt, and he has been paying it for eleven years in seedlings and schoolwork. The children adore him. Visiting traders have to be warned in advance.

**Rooda Tann** · human · m · 47
`traits: Jealous, Abrasive, NaturalMood(Depressive)`
childhood: an outsider's child, always the last one served, and he counted.
adult: caught siphoning the spring into a private plot in the ninth year of a drought.
> The judgement was that his plot be watered by the clan, forever, at their expense, and tended if he falls ill, and he must accept it. That was six years ago. Every single morning somebody cheerfully waters his vines in front of him, and he stands there and takes it, and it is destroying him, and there is no appeal because nobody is being unkind.

**Rrogh** · Wookiee · m · 96
`traits: Brawler, Kind, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: pulled a cousin out of a Blackstar pen at nineteen and got a taste for it.
adult: carries people out. Thirty-one so far, across nine liberation raids.
> Under the Oath a life handed back is a transfer of ownership that never expires, so thirty-one freed people now belong to him and follow him and will not be dismissed — up the stairs, into the baths, to the latrine. He is hiding in a shed on the fourth terrace. They have found the shed. He is going out again next season anyway.

**Pell Yoon** · Yttakin · f · 33
`traits: Kind, Gourmand, SlowLearner`
childhood: fed everything that came to the door, including things that bite.
adult: terrace cook. Six years of quietly running grain down to the Homestead farms.
> The Elders ruled against feeding outsiders who will not take the Oath, and she has broken that ruling every month since, and she will tell you about it inside the first hour of meeting you because she is warm, delighted with herself, and constitutionally incapable of holding anything back. Everyone knows. Everyone is waiting for someone else to be the one who says it.

**Yorrum Pell** · Yttakin · m · 35
`traits: Kind, AnnoyingVoice, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: happy. Four brothers. He'll cover them too.
adult: terrace irrigation, second shift, and he has four children of his own now.
> He is genuinely pleased to meet you, he has their names and ages ready, he will ask about yours, and then he will tell you about the four of them for forty minutes in a bright nasal drone with no natural pause anywhere in it. There is no twist. There is no darkness underneath. There is no way out of the conversation.
