# `Inhabited` — cast 01: the Hutt Cartel

_First 25, authored 2026-08-19 as the tone check. Written to the format in
`INHABITED_DESIGN.md` §5.8 and the work-rules in `INHABITED_SPECIES_TEXTURE.md`._

**Format, per the measured evidence:** two paragraphs each · one strong skill · one trait
that visibly causes trouble (⛔ never pyromaniac) · **at least one relationship** · a name
worth saying · one conspicuous hole left unfilled.
**Family quota:** no more than two per family per cast. **The dull ones were written first.**

Three places, so the roles actually spread: **Kessek Refinery** · **Dock Nine** ·
**the Ledger House**.

---

# KESSEK REFINERY — 9

### Bruk Oleen · Ugnaught · shift foreman · `dutiful`
Bruk won the right to this floor at twenty, tusks against his cousin Ferrin, and the terms
of that duel are the last thing he will discuss. He runs the crack tower on a rhythm nobody
wrote down: four taps on the rail means slow, six means stop, and a new hand who cannot hear
the difference is put on drums until they can. He has been here eighty-one years. He expects
to be here another hundred.

Tell Bruk his work malfunctioned and you will not be spoken to again — not shouted at,
simply removed from the category of people who are spoken to. His wife Missa keeps the
fungus tunnels behind the mess and they have not shared a shift since the Cartel bought the
tower. When a line ruptured last winter and took two of his crew, he logged it, capped it,
and had the tower running before the bodies were up. *"I have spoken."*

### Ferrin Oleen · Ugnaught · sump-clearer · `hilarious`
Bruk's cousin, and the man he beat. Ferrin holds the Kessek record for the longest single
breath-hold in the settling sump — four minutes and something, witnessed, disputed only by
people who were not there. Nobody has beaten it. Nobody has tried. He mentions it roughly
once a shift, to whoever is nearest, including droids.

He is a spectacularly good sump-clearer and a spectacularly bad drinker, and the two facts
arrive together most evenings. He has never once said he should have won the duel; he says
instead that the sump is the only part of the refinery that would notice if he left, which
everyone understands to be the same sentence. He and Bruk have not spoken directly in
sixty years and share a mess table every single day.

### Tenno Vurr · Chagrian · mess cook · `bizarre`
Tenno cannot taste and has never once let that stop him. He runs the refinery mess by smell
alone — tongue out, flick, adjust — and the crew agree the food is better than it has any
right to be, in a way none of them can explain and he has never been asked about. He takes
no break. He carries his capsules in a belt pouch and eats them standing, in about nine
seconds, twice a shift.

He files his lethorns every morning with the same ridged strip of hull plate and will not
use another. Ferrin once hid it as a joke and Tenno did not work for two days; the strip was
returned and nobody has touched it since. He is disturbed by how the drudges live and has
been quietly overfeeding the worst-off for eleven years, which he does not consider charity
and would deny if asked.

### Wesh · human · drudge, tank deck · `utterly boring`
Wesh has worked the tank deck for nine years and can tell you the serial prefix of every
vessel on it. He is not proud of this and does not think it interesting; it is simply what
is in his head instead of other things. He arrives early because the walk is unpleasant in
full light. He leaves last because he is slow packing up.

His sister Adda works Dock Nine and they eat together on the sixth day. That is the whole of
his social life and he considers it sufficient. When the line ruptured and took two of the
crew, Wesh was forty metres away; he moved the unaffected drums back from the heat, which
was the correct thing to do, and then finished his shift. He has never mentioned it.

### Ossuk Rell · Muun · tallyman and shop-keeper · `thwarted`
Ossuk failed the third examination at nineteen. He does not say which one and the
particulars are not available. He came out here where the arithmetic is beneath him and does
it perfectly, and the Cartel has never had a discrepancy on this tower in the eleven years
he has held the book. He runs the little oil shop off the gate — official stock, faction
prices, no haggling, receipts for everything.

He is appalled by the crew and cannot hide it: by the waste, by the drinking, by the way
Ferrin dresses. He is also the only person who visits Ferrin when the drinking gets bad,
arriving with a cloth and a bucket and an expression of total disgust, every time. He has
three hearts and uses two of them to keep his hands warm on the night gauge round, and he
is embarrassed to be seen doing it.

### Jat Hessik · Rodian · pipe-fitter, night gang · `heartbreaking`
Jat is cold-blooded and works nights, which is a joke the day crew have made to his face for
six years and which stopped being funny to him in the first month. He wraps in three
salvaged thermal layers and still shakes by the fourth hour. He is the best fitter on the
tower and everyone knows it, including him, and he has never once asked to be moved to days.

He will not say which clan he is from. Ask twice and he will change the subject; ask three
times and he will remember it. There is a woman on the day gang who wears a scent-damper
because she says the refinery gives her headaches, and Jat has never mentioned that he can
tell she is from a house his family has a debt with, and that she almost certainly knows
who he is.

### Margrave Sarrick · Togorian · yard guard · `devoted`
Sarrick came offworld eleven years ago looking for his mate and has not found her. He works
gate and yard for the Cartel because the Cartel moves people and cargo across the whole
dayside and he sees every manifest that comes through Kessek. He has read them all. He will
read them all again.

He is enormous, silent, digitigrade, and the crew adore him in the way people adore weather.
He has never drawn on anyone at Kessek. He carries a scimitar he made himself and has twice
refused offers for it that would have paid a year of passage. When the line ruptured he
carried both bodies up the stair himself and set them down gently and went back to the gate,
because the gate was his post and nobody had relieved him.

### Poul Adden-Adden · Ortolan · junior stockman · `grand`
Poul was pushed out of his family unit at seven, as is customary, and traded to a household
that wanted a musician and got him instead. He is twenty-three, works stock, and introduces
himself with both names every single time, including to people he has worked beside for
years. He is under the firm impression that he is management-track. Nobody has corrected him
because nobody has the heart.

The compressor deck hurts him — genuinely, physically, his ears are fat-storage organs that
hear into the subsonic — and he works it anyway because it is the post nearest the office
and he believes he is being observed. He took this job for the meals and would take a worse
one for better meals, a fact he does not consider a weakness and will explain to you.

### Little Hessa · human · water-runner, age eleven · `tender`
Hessa carries water up the tower stair, eleven flights, all shift, and has learned every
crew member's preference: Bruk cold, Tenno warm, Wesh whatever is nearest, Ferrin more than
his share. She is paid in scrip and food and she is not, on paper, employed. She belongs to
the tower the way the tower belongs to the Cartel, and neither arrangement is written down.

Her mother worked here. Nobody on the current shift was here then, or nobody says they were.
Sarrick lets her sit at the gate in the worst of the heat, and when a Cartel factor came
through last year and asked whose child that was, Sarrick said *"mine"*, in front of nine
witnesses, and the factor wrote it down and moved on.

# DOCK NINE — 8

### Adda Wesh · human · loadmaster · `dutiful`
Wesh's sister, older by four years, and the only person at Dock Nine permitted to tell a
Hutt factor that a manifest is wrong. She does it about twice a season, in writing, and has
been right every time. Her authority rests on nothing but that record and she guards it like
a bond.

She is abrasive in a way that has cost her two promotions and does not appear to trouble
her. She drinks with the night gang, wins, and does not let anyone forget it. On the sixth
day she eats with her brother and talks about nothing, which she says is the best meal of
the week and which he has never realised she means literally.

### Gand · Gand · dock clerk · `thwarted`
He has no name. He had a surname once — earned, witnessed, entered — and gave it back after a
consignment went to the wrong world on his signature. Nobody at Dock Nine understands what
that means. They think it is a bit he does. He signs every docket "Gand" and is asked, most
weeks, to write it properly, and every week he writes it the same.

He sleeps perhaps two hours in six days and can bank more against a long job, which makes
him the most useful clerk on the dock and the one nobody can find at night. He has told
Sarrick, once, what the surname was. Sarrick has not repeated it, and the two of them do not
otherwise speak.

### Buba Nokk · Herglic · freight handler · `hilarious`
Buba clears his blowhole before saying anything he considers important, which is a Herglic
habit that has become, at Dock Nine, an involuntary comedy: the whole shed goes quiet at the
*hauum*, and then he says something like *"the small crates go on top."* He knows. He does it
anyway. It may be the only power he has.

He gambles his wages, all of them, every payday, at the dice table behind the Ledger House,
and is in debt to two of the people on this list. He does not fit through the personnel door
and uses the freight hatch, which requires someone to open it, which means someone always
knows when Buba arrives. He is apologetic about this roughly forty times a day.

### Missa Oleen · Ugnaught · fungus factor · `utterly boring`
Bruk's wife. She grows in the dark tunnels behind Kessek and sells at Dock Nine, and the
trip between is the only part of her week she talks about. Six varieties, four buyers,
prices unchanged in nine years by her own insistence. She thinks the Cartel's oil business
is loud and probably temporary.

She has not shared a shift with her husband since the Cartel bought the tower and does not
consider this remarkable; they are both working, and both alive, at the same time, in the
same place, which by the standards of her mother's generation is a good marriage. She brings
Ferrin a portion every trip and has never told Bruk.

### Sen Ilva · Selkath · dock medic · `tender`
Sen's suit hisses. It vents mist on a cycle and the sound arrives before she does, so nobody
at Dock Nine has ever been surprised by the medic. She keeps kolto-era habits in a
bacta-era world and will explain the difference at length if you let her, which the night
gang do, on purpose, when they want to hear her talk.

She waits to be spoken to. It is not shyness — approaching someone who has given no sign of
wanting to speak is a rudeness she cannot make herself commit — and it means half the
injuries at Dock Nine reach her late. She has asked Adda to signal her when someone is hurt
and too proud to say so, and Adda does, and neither of them has mentioned it to anyone.

### Orrin Kwaad · human · gate scanner · `corrupt`
Orrin takes forty credits to look at a manifest for slightly less time. Forty. Not four
hundred. He has been doing it for six years and has never once escalated the price, which
several people find more unsettling than the theft. He is unfailingly pleasant, remembers
everyone's children, and is the reason three of the shipments through Dock Nine are not
what they say they are.

He and Buba have an arrangement neither describes as an arrangement: Buba's debts, Orrin's
blind eye, and a running joke about a dice game that neither of them has ever played
together. When the Cartel audited the dock last year, Orrin was the one who found the
discrepancy — a real one, someone else's — and was commended for it.

### Tikka Vosh · Rodian · sorter, day gang · `menacing`
Tikka wears a scent-damper and tells people the refinery air gives her headaches. It does
not. She is from a house that Jat Hessik's family owes, she has known he was at Kessek since
her third week, and she has said nothing for two years. She is very good at her job. She is
pleasant to everyone.

What she is waiting for is not clear, including possibly to her. She has twice been in a
position to mention his name to a Cartel factor and has twice not done so. She eats alone,
by choice, in sight of the Kessek gate road, at the same time every day, which is the hour
the night gang walk up.

### Pell Muun-Adden · Muun · dock tallyman, Ossuk's cousin · `grand`
Pell passed the third examination. He has mentioned this at Dock Nine perhaps two thousand
times. He is here anyway, which he explains as a strategic posting and which everyone else
understands to be a posting. He and Ossuk have not spoken since Pell's confirmation dinner
eleven years ago, an occasion Pell describes as warm.

He is scrupulously honest, follows the letter of every Cartel rule and none of the spirit,
and has cost the dock more time than Orrin has cost it money. He is also the only person at
Dock Nine who will not take Buba's dice money, on the grounds that it is wasteful, and who
consequently ends up lending it to him instead.

# THE LEDGER HOUSE — 8

### Factor Ummu Sekk · human · debt factor · `menacing`
Ummu is small, sixty, and speaks so quietly that people lean in, which she has never had to
learn to weaponise because it simply is what happens. She holds the paper on most of this
list. She has never raised her voice inside the Ledger House and has, twice, had someone
carried out of it.

She keeps a ledger by hand although the Cartel has better, because a hand ledger is a thing
one can be seen to write in. She has a granddaughter in a school offworld that costs more
than she earns, which is either the explanation for everything about her or the one thing
that has nothing to do with it. *"Pay, and you are family. Do not pay, and you are
inventory. There is no third column."*

### Denno Fash · Ithorian · assessor · `thwarted`
Denno's translator collar is old and mistimes by half a second, so every valuation he gives
arrives slightly after his mouth stops, which strangers find disconcerting and which he has
long since stopped explaining. He assesses salvage and he is scrupulous, and he will not
value anything that came out of a killing.

This costs the Ledger House real money and Ummu keeps him anyway, which is the closest thing
to sentiment anyone has caught her in. He is required, by his own law, to plant two for
every one taken; there is nothing here that grows. He keeps a tray of seedlings in the back
office that have never once survived to transplant, and he starts a new tray every season.

### Kesh Vurr · Chagrian · clerk of accounts, Tenno's nephew · `dutiful`
Kesh will not falsify a form. This has been explained to him as a career matter by three
separate people and he does not appear to have understood that it was a suggestion. He has
reported two Cartel factors, one correctly. He is twenty-six and has been passed over four
times.

He eats his capsules at the desk and works through what everyone else calls lunch, which is
read as ambition and is actually just that he has nowhere to be. His uncle Tenno sends food
up from Kessek every week — real food, cooked, for a nephew who cannot taste it — and Kesh
eats every bite of it, slowly, alone, because it was sent.

### Old Nabb · human · door · `utterly boring`
Nabb sits at the Ledger House door on a stool that is his. He has sat there for nineteen
years. He is not a guard and would be no use as one; his function is that people arriving to
beg for time see a bored old man first instead of Ummu, which takes something out of them.
He knows this is his function.

He has one story, about a shipment of grain that came in wrong in a year nobody else
remembers, and he tells it perhaps once a month, and it is not a good story. When a debtor
broke down in the doorway last season and could not stand up, Nabb helped him to the stool
and sat on the step himself until the man could walk, and then went back to the stool and
said nothing about it.

### Yesh Adden · Ortolan · house cook · `devoted`
Yesh feeds the Ledger House and considers this a sacred trust, mostly because he is fed for
it. He was traded to a household at seven, like all of them, and this is the fourth kitchen
he has run and the first where anyone has thanked him. Ummu thanked him once, in his second
year, and he has repeated the sentence to people for eleven years.

His hearing goes into the subsonic and the Ledger House sits above a pump gallery, so he
works in constant low pain that he has never mentioned to anyone and manages by cooking with
his hands in the food — absorbing through the fingertips, which is normal for him and which
the human staff find quietly revolting and have agreed not to mention.

### Iss the Quiet · Anzati · house factotum · `bizarre`
Nobody at the Ledger House can say when Iss started. Payroll has him at four years. Nabb,
who has sat at that door for nineteen, is fairly sure it is longer, and says so, and is not
pressed on it. Iss runs errands, carries messages, and is the second-best pair of hands in
the building at any task you name and never the best at any of them.

He is agreeable, unremarkable and instantly forgettable in a way that survives being pointed
out. He has no pulse — a Selkath medic noticed this once, at Dock Nine, said nothing to him,
and was transferred within the month at her own request. He is very fond of Old Nabb and
brings him tea he does not drink himself.

### Tobb the Ledger's Boy · Jawa · runner · `hilarious`
Four hems, which makes him about fourteen, and he has let two of them down himself, badly,
which is visible. He runs paper between the Ledger House, Dock Nine and Kessek, and he
overcharges for it on a sliding scale of his own invention based on heat, distance and how
much he thinks you need the paper. Ummu is aware. Ummu finds it funny, which frightens
everyone.

He is a thief, and is known to be a thief, and finds this an excellent professional
reputation. He is also the only being who moves freely between all three houses and is
therefore the fastest route for anything that should not be written down. His hood smells
strongly and there are insects in it, and he has explained, several times, at length, to
people who did not ask, that this is correct and that they are the ones doing it wrong.

### Hessa's Mother · human · unnamed on the roster · `heartbreaking`
She is on the Ledger House books as an outstanding balance with a work-lien attached and no
current posting. The lien is nine years old. The balance has grown. Kesh Vurr found the
entry two years ago while reconciling an unrelated account and has raised it four times, in
writing, correctly, and been told each time that the matter is closed.

Nobody at Kessek talks about the tower's water-runner's mother. Nabb, who was on the door
that season, has never once told his one story about that year. What the entry means is
knowable — the paper is all in the same building — and it has simply never been worth
anyone's while to walk down the corridor and read it.

## Notes for the tone check

- **Family spread:** Suffering 5 · Comic 5 · Strange 3 · Flat 4 · Dangerous 4 · Warm 4. No
  cast exceeds two from one family.
- **The dull ones were written first** (Wesh, Missa, Nabb), so the strange ones have
  something to stand against — §5.6 rule 7.
- **Relationships bind across all three places**, not within one: Bruk↔Ferrin↔Missa,
  Wesh↔Adda, Tenno↔Kesh, Ossuk↔Pell, Jat↔Tikka, Buba↔Orrin↔Pell, Sarrick↔Hessa↔her mother,
  Iss↔Nabb, Gand↔Sarrick. ⭐ **Nine of the twenty-five are only fully legible if you have met
  someone at another site.**
- **One unremarked tragedy per crowd**, unpointed: Jat's night shift · the seedling tray ·
  the ledger entry.
- ⛔ **No pyromaniacs.** Trouble-traits used: alcoholic (Ferrin), abrasive (Adda), gambling
  (Buba), and a set of compulsions that read as traits without being them.
- **Holes deliberately left unfilled:** the duel's terms · what Ossuk's third exam was ·
  Sarrick's mate · what Tikka is waiting for · how long Iss has been there · what happened
  to Hessa's mother.
