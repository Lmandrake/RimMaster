# `Inhabited` — cast 12: the Deepwater Compact

_Authored 2026-08-21. **Faith: `the Balance`**, read from the shipped def at
`src/Jawa/Jawa_Patches/Defs/FactionDefs/JawaDeepwaterCompact.xml`, not the roster table:_
> *"Water is not a weapon and we will not let it be made into one. We sell to the farmer,
> and we sell to the fleet that burned the farmer, and both hate us exactly as much — that
> is how we know the Balance is holding. Choose a side and the whole world dies of thirst.
> **Inside our walls no one raises a hand. Outside our walls, we do not go.**"*

Memes `Structure_Ideological · AM_WaterPrimacy · VME_Pacifist · Individualist · VME_Trader`.
`raidsForbidden` — wardens dehydrate away from water and **never** reach the player as a
raid. `leaderTitle` **High Warden**. Five settlements, teal on the map.

### 🔎 DECIDE's pass

✅ **The brief was *people for whom excess is the sin* (`INHABITED_DESIGN.md` §5.9), and the
faith does something none of the other eleven do: it makes NEUTRALITY the virtue, which
means every good person here is complicit in something.** They sold the water that put out
the fire and they sold the water that cooled the guns. That is the register, and the cast
is written so nobody is comfortable in it.
⭐ **Three things fell out of the def that were not in the brief:**
**(1)** *"Outside our walls, we do not go"* is not principle, it is **physiology** —
`raidsForbidden` exists because wardens dry out. ⇒ their pacifism is indistinguishable from
their inability to follow you, **and they know that, and it galls them.** Half this cast is
made of people defending a virtue they never had to pay for.
**(2)** `Individualist` sits in a faith about collective rationing, which reads as a
contradiction and is not one: **the Compact rations water and nothing else.** What you
believe, who you love and what you do with your own time is nobody's business — so the
faction is puritan about exactly one substance and permissive about everything else. Write
that; it is the most distinctive thing they have.
**(3)** ⚠️ Selkath claws are *"socially unacceptable and illegal to use in a fight"* in
their own lore. On a faction whose first law is **no violence inside the walls**, a Selkath
is a person carrying a weapon they were born with and may never use. One of them is here.
⚠️ **The homogenisation risk (§5.5) is `Ascetic`.** It is the obvious trait for every one of
these people and it is used **five** times out of twenty-five, deliberately. The faith is
about excess being a sin; the interesting characters are the ones who sin.
⛔ **Nobody in this cast is a pacifist by temperament.** Three of them are dangerous. The
Balance is a *law*, not a personality, and a faction of gentle people would make it cost
nothing.

Traits checked against §5.7b — no conflicting pairs.

---

**The four things a Compact child learns before they are allowed on a jetty.**
① **The measure is not yours.** You may drink what is drawn for you and not one swallow
past it, and nobody will stop you, and everybody will see.
② **Both hands, always, when you hand a cup over.** One hand is how you pass a thing you
do not care about.
③ **Inside the walls, no one raises a hand.** Not for an insult, not for a theft, not for a
death. You take it to a custodian or you take it nowhere.
④ **We do not ask what it is for.** The tanker that fills at our jetty is going somewhere,
and it is not our business where, and the day it becomes our business is the day the
Balance breaks and everyone inland dies.

⚠️ Two of the Compact's five holdings are uncast and named only:
**Coldquay**, the second Twilight Sea jetty, and **Anchor Deep**, Butora's sister on the
Scald shore. Cast them from the displaced pool if they are ever landed on.

## DEEPWATER HOLD
*The Twilight Sea, the largest standing water on Ash'karr, and the seat. The Accord is
signed here, the ledger of who was sold to is kept here, and nobody who works in this
building has ever seen the desert they are rationing.*

**High Warden Osso Reeth** · Mon Calamari · m · 64
`traits: Ascetic, GreatMemory, TooSmart`
childhood: third child of a jetty family, put to the ledger because he could not swim well.
adult: thirty-one years in the Accord chamber. Has never once broken the Balance.
> He can recite every sale the Hold has made to the Empire since he took the seat — the volume, the date, the officer who signed — and he does it, out loud, at the annual Accord, because he believes a thing you cannot say plainly has already gone wrong. It takes fifty minutes. Everyone stands. He will not shorten it, he has been asked twice, and the second time he asked the man to say which of the sales he would like forgotten.

**Nossara Vekk** · Quarren · f · 51
`traits: Abrasive, GreatMemory, FastLearner`
childhood: born inland, at Tidewatch, and does not let anyone forget it.
adult: keeps the measure for the whole Hold. Sets what every household draws.
> She is the only senior custodian who grew up on a sea that was dying and it has made her impossible — she has cut the Hold's household ration four times in nine years against no shortage at all, on the grounds that a people who have never been thirsty will not survive being thirsty. Osso Reeth has overruled her exactly once. She has been right about the Grey Sea for eleven years and says so.

**Chalu Bir-Nass** · Gungan · m · 38
`traits: AnnoyingVoice, Kind, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: hatched at the Hold, never left it, does not want to.
adult: harbourmaster. Every tanker that fills at this jetty goes through him.
> He greets Imperial quartermasters by name, asks after their families, and means it, and this is the single most disturbing thing about him — he has decided that if he must fill the fleet's tanks he will at least do it as a person and not as a machine, and the officers have started requesting the Hold specifically. Nossara Vekk thinks he is a collaborator. He thinks she has never met one of them.

**Shaelas Duun** · Selkath · f · 44
`traits: BodyMastery, Ascetic, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: taught at six what her claws were and what would happen if she ever used them.
adult: warden of the inner quays. Has broken up eleven fights and never touched anyone.
> She is the most dangerous person in the Hold and the only one who has never been in a fight, and those two facts are the same fact — she comes between people with her hands open and low where everyone can see them, because a Selkath who closes a fist has already lost the argument in front of witnesses. She practises the opening, alone, most mornings. She is forty-four and has never once been sure she would not.

**Perrik Osso-Vane** · human · m · 29
`traits: Jealous, TooSmart, Delicate`
childhood: adopted off a wrecked hauler at four; the Hold raised him and never pretended otherwise.
adult: second clerk of the ledger. Wants the first chair and is not subtle.
> He is better at the ledger than Osso Reeth ever was and everyone including Osso Reeth knows it, and it is not going to get him the seat, because the Compact does not promote a man who has told you twice that he deserves it. He has begun keeping a second private ledger of Nossara Vekk's ration cuts, cross-referenced against the harvest years, and it is meticulous, and it is going to be very useful to somebody eventually.

**Grand Tessek** · Quarren · m · 71
`traits: Gourmand, GreatMemory, Abrasive`
childhood: the Hold's kitchens, from nine.
adult: cooks the Accord dinner. Has cooked forty of them.
> ⭐ In a faith whose one sin is excess he is a glutton, openly, and it is tolerated because the Accord dinner is the only night of the year the Compact eats past its measure and he is the reason it is worth doing — the argument, made loudly and annually, is that a people who never once exceed have not chosen anything. Osso Reeth has never publicly agreed with him. He has also never missed a dinner.

**Ilma Sook** · Nautolan · f · 33
`traits: Kind, SlowLearner, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: one of nine, in a jetty house, and the slow one.
adult: runs the Sharing — the ceremonial water-gift to anyone who arrives in need.
> She gives water away for a living in a faction that counts every drop, and she has never been audited, because Nossara Vekk went through her books once in the first year and found them perfect — she cannot do arithmetic quickly but she has never in nine years given out a cup she could not account for. She remembers every face she has handed one to. There are four hundred and some.

**Warden-Captain Boruu Halt** · Herglic · m · 47
`traits: Brawler, GreatMemory, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: dockside, big early, and learned what that meant.
adult: commands the Hold's defence. Has never commanded it in anger.
> He has drilled a firing line for nineteen years against an attack the Compact's own law says he may not go out and prevent, and it has made him a strange kind of soldier — very good, entirely defensive, and quietly certain that the first day it is needed will also be the last day the Balance means anything. He is not wrong and Osso Reeth has stopped arguing with him about it.

**Old Duula** · Selkath · f · ~88
`traits: GreatMemory, Ascetic, CreepyBreathing`
childhood: nobody at the Hold is old enough to say.
adult: the last custodian who signed under the previous High Warden.
> She sits at the back of the Accord chamber and does not speak, and twice in the last decade she has raised one hand, and both times the chamber stopped and the motion did not pass. Nobody has established what she is objecting to on those occasions. Perrik has asked her directly. She told him the truth and he did not understand it, and he is too proud to ask again.

## BUTORA
*The Scald shore — fresh water in the hottest place on the planet, sitting on the Empire's
supply route. Butora is where the Balance costs something, and everyone posted here has
been told so before they came.*

**Warden Kesso Reeth** · Mon Calamari · f · 36
`traits: Ascetic, Abrasive, FastLearner`
childhood: the High Warden's niece, which she has spent her whole career outrunning.
adult: holds Butora. Youngest custodian ever given a shore.
> She signs the Empire's fills herself instead of delegating them, every one, because she decided early that if the name on the manifest was going to be a Reeth then it was going to be hers and not something that happened three desks down. She has not spoken to her uncle in two years. They agree about everything.

**Fenn Ottouk** · Nautolan · m · 24
`traits: Brawler, NaturalMood(Depressive), TooSmart`
childhood: Butora born, and watched the fills from the jetty steps as a boy.
adult: warden, second year. Applied for a transfer inland he knew would be refused.
> He wants to pick a side and he cannot articulate which one, which is what makes him dangerous rather than merely young — it is not that he loves the farmers or hates the Empire, it is that he has worked out that the Compact's neutrality is only a virtue because their legs give out past the shoreline, and he has not found anybody who will argue with him about it. Kesso Reeth has read his transfer application four times.

**Quartermaster Bel Nossik** · Quarren · m · 55
`traits: GreatMemory, Jealous, Delicate`
childhood: Coldquay, and never says why he left.
adult: runs the Butora manifest. Knows the tonnage of every Imperial ship on this coast.
> He has an exact figure for how much of the Empire's operational water on this hemisphere comes off his jetty, he has never told anyone what it is, and he is not sitting on it out of loyalty — he is sitting on it because it is the only thing he owns that the Hold does not have a copy of. He will trade it. He has not yet decided for what.

**Ashaa Ottouk** · Nautolan · f · 41
`traits: Kind, GreatMemory, TorturedArtist`
childhood: hauled water at Anchor Deep from eleven.
adult: keeps Butora's dead. Also keeps its songs.
> She has written a song for every warden who died at this posting, forty years back, and sings them in strict order once a year, and three of them are about people who died of the heat two hundred metres inland after chasing a thief they had been told not to chase. She does not editorialise. She just puts them in the sequence with everyone else and lets the shape of it say what it says.

**Sergeant Vurra Dask** · Chagrian · f · 39
`traits: Abrasive, BodyMastery, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: the Hold's drill yards, from fourteen.
adult: trains Butora's firing line. Will not be posted anywhere else.
> She is the only one on this shore who is glad of the law that keeps them behind the walls, and it is not cowardice — she has run the numbers on what a warden company can do against an Imperial landing and the answer is *delay it*, and she has decided she would rather train people for the thing that might work than for the thing that would feel better. She says this out loud. It does not make her popular.

**Tobb Grell** · Herglic · m · 33
`traits: Gourmand, SlowLearner, Kind`
childhood: a jetty family, and the biggest child in it by a wide margin.
adult: works the pumps. Sixteen hours when the fleet is in.
> He is the strongest person in Butora and eats accordingly, which is a standing scandal that recurs at every ration review and is settled the same way every time — Bel Nossik reads out the pump tonnage, someone does the arithmetic, and the review moves on to the next item. Tobb has never once defended himself in these meetings. He does not think of it as being about him.

**Illik Sar** · Bith · m · 46
`traits: TooSmart, VoidFascination, Delicate`
childhood: came off a trade hauler at nineteen and simply never re-boarded.
adult: keeps Butora's purifiers running. There is no second person who can.
> He is not Compact, he has never taken the Balance, and he stays because the Scald water is the strangest chemistry on the planet and he has not finished with it — the Compact tolerates a heretic on the machines because the machines are the settlement. Kesso Reeth has asked him once whether he would keep them running if the Empire held the town. He said he would need to see the water first.

**Ottik Bir-Nass** · Gungan · m · 19
`traits: AnnoyingVoice, NaturalMood(Sanguine), FastLearner`
childhood: sent up from the Hold at sixteen because his uncle asked.
adult: runs messages, mans a post, talks constantly.
> He is the only person in Butora who is straightforwardly happy to be there, and the whole shore has quietly organised itself around not being the one who explains to him what this posting is for. Fenn Ottouk has come closest, twice, and stopped both times. It has been three years.

## TIDEWATCH
*The Grey Sea — salt-encrusted, shrinking, and worth less every year. The Compact holds it
anyway. This is what the faith looks like when there is nothing left to ration.*

**Warden Sethro Vekk** · Quarren · m · 58
`traits: Ascetic, GreatMemory, NaturalMood(Depressive)`
childhood: Tidewatch, when the jetty still reached the water.
adult: has held this post twenty-two years and refused two transfers.
> He measures the shoreline himself on the first of every month, walks the stakes out to the waterline, and writes the number down — it has moved the wrong way every month for eleven years and he has all of them. His sister Nossara left as a girl and rations the Hold on what she saw here. He has never once written to her about the numbers. He assumes she knows.

**Marrek Ol-Duun** · Selkath · m · 62
`traits: Abrasive, TooSmart, Ascetic`
childhood: born on the Grey Sea when it was still called green.
adult: argues, at every Accord, that Tidewatch should be abandoned.
> He has made the same speech for nine years, it is a good speech, and he does not want it to succeed — he wants it *heard*, because he has worked out that a Compact which quietly lets a holding die without ever voting on it is a Compact that will do it again somewhere it matters. He votes against his own motion. He has explained why once and everybody pretended to understand.

**Hessa Kur** · human · f · 37
`traits: Kind, Delicate, TorturedArtist`
childhood: outlander stock, taken in at nine after a caravan died out here.
adult: schools the eleven children of Tidewatch. Teaches the four things.
> She teaches children a code about water on a shore where the water is leaving, and she has not changed a word of it, and when a child asked her why they should learn the measure if the sea goes, she said *because the measure is not about the sea* and then went and sat by herself for an hour. She is the only one of the eleven who came from outside. She is the one who will not go.

**Bosso Tharn** · Herglic · m · 50
`traits: Brawler, Gourmand, Abrasive`
childhood: Coldquay, and left for reasons the Hold has a file on.
adult: works the salt pans. The one industry Tidewatch has left.
> He is here because Tidewatch was the only posting that would take him after the thing at Coldquay, which he does not discuss and which was, by every account including his own, a fight inside the walls. Nine years of salt. He has not raised a hand since, and the reason he gives is not remorse — it is that he was made to stand in front of the Accord and hear what he had done read out, and he would rather work salt for the rest of his life than do that twice.

**Quell Ottouk** · Nautolan · f · 27
`traits: FastLearner, Jealous, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: Butora, transferred out at twenty as a punishment nobody names.
adult: keeps the Tidewatch purifiers. They are older than she is.
> She is the best technician on this coast and she is stuck on the one shore where that skill buys nothing, and she knows exactly whose fault it is and will not say, which is worse for everybody than if she did. She has kept a forty-year-old purification stack alive for seven years on parts she machines herself. Illik Sar at Butora writes to her. She has never answered.

**Old Wenneth** · Mon Calamari · f · ~79
`traits: GreatMemory, Ascetic, CreepyBreathing`
childhood: the Grey Sea, and she can describe the far shore from memory.
adult: was warden here before Sethro Vekk. Handed it over and stayed.
> She is the only living person who has seen the Grey Sea from the other side, and the children make her describe it, and she does, exactly the same way every time, down to the order of the hills — she has been doing it for forty years and Hessa Kur has written three versions down and they do not differ by a word. Sethro brings her the monthly stake numbers. She has never asked for them.

**Dabb Illo** · Duros · m · 44
`traits: TooSmart, Jealous, SlowLearner`
childhood: freighter-born, put ashore sick at twenty-six and never fully recovered.
adult: keeps Tidewatch's manifest, which fits on one sheet.
> He was a navigator and he is now a man who writes down four salt shipments a month, and he does it beautifully, in a hand nobody out here can read properly, with the tonnages set out as though they were headings — he is not pretending it is more than it is, he is refusing to let it become less. He has asked for a transfer every year for eighteen years. He would not take one.

**Sook Nossik** · Quarren · f · 22
`traits: Ascetic, Kind, FastLearner`
childhood: Tidewatch born, in the years when everybody was already leaving.
adult: warden, first year, and the only one under twenty-five on this shore.
> She has never seen a full sea and does not experience Tidewatch as a tragedy, which the older wardens find unbearable and cannot say so — to her the stakes going out is simply what a shoreline does, and the measure is the measure, and there is a post to stand. Sethro Vekk has caught himself twice about to explain to her what has been lost. He has not done it. He is not sure it would be a kindness and he is less sure it would be true.

## Cross-links, for the second meeting

**Nossara Vekk rations the Hold on a sea she left at fifteen**, and her brother Sethro
measures that sea's retreat every month and has never sent her a figure. Both are correct
about the other's job and neither has said so. Tell the player nothing; let them meet
Sethro second.

**Bel Nossik has a number that would be worth a great deal to the Empire, to the Hutts, or
to the player**, and he is not loyal enough to keep it forever — he is only proud enough to
want a price. ⭐ He is the Compact's one genuine security hole and he is not a traitor.

**Chalu Bir-Nass and Nossara Vekk are the two halves of the Balance's actual argument** —
he says you must sell to them as a person, she says selling to them at all is the sin
already and personhood is a decoration on it. Neither is written as wrong.

**Bosso Tharn broke the first law and is still here.** The Compact does not exile. It reads
what you did aloud, in front of everybody, once, and then lets you work — and that is the
harshest thing in this faith and it is not violence.

**Illik Sar keeps Butora alive and has not taken the Balance.** If the Empire takes that
shore, the settlement's survival depends on a man whose only stated loyalty is to the
water's chemistry.

**Quell Ottouk and Illik Sar are the two best technicians on the coast**, one letter-writing
and one silent, and the Compact has never thought to put them in a room.
