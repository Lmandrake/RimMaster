# `Inhabited` — cast 03: the Jawa Trade Moot

_Authored 2026-08-20. **Faith: The Salvation — shared with the player.** Moral climate per
`INHABITED_DESIGN.md` §5.9: communal, clannish, sharp trade, ⭐ thieving as a virtue._

**Age is in HEMS.** The robe is given when a child first walks and let down as it grows;
five or six hems is an adult, nine is a very old Jawa indeed. Years in parentheses for the
pawn editor.

### 🔎 DECIDE's pass
✅ **Accepted, and it is the most important cast in the project** — our own people seen from
outside. The clans read as protagonists of their own lives rather than as local colour,
which was the whole risk.
⭐ **Three outsiders are included on purpose** and they are the best structural idea in it:
the Ugnaught who cannot bear the Jawa trade philosophy, the farmer locked in the codependent
circle, and **the one outsider alive who broke the scent-language** — which is the single
most dangerous thing at Dune's Throat.
⚠️ **`Sakk Vor` and `Tekka Vor` both apply the trade doctrine to people.** Different shapes
(one prices survivors aloud, one brokers marriages) so both keep, but ⛔ no third.
Traits verified. No `Pyromaniac`.

## SANDCRAWLER *TUKKAT-NUR* — "Second Use"

**Chief Ghekk Ubb-Ubb** · Jawa · m · seven hems (54)
`traits: Abrasive, GreatMemory, Ascetic`
childhood: three hems old and already crying the auction of his own father's goods.
adult: forty years on the throne-crate, and no clan has ever taken the crawler out from under him.
> He *conducts*. Every haggle opens with both sleeves raised, held there until the customer stops talking — sometimes a minute, sometimes four, and he has stood like that through a sandstorm rather than lower them first. He owns exactly one thing that is not for sale, a bent spanner, and he will not tell you why, and he will absolutely tell you at length that he will not tell you.

**Wupp** · Jawa · m · six hems (33)
`traits: SlowLearner, Ascetic, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: mixed fuel.
adult: mixes fuel.
> He mixes to the ratio his uncle taught him, has never varied it, has never once been wrong, and has never in his life held an opinion about anything else. Ask him about the war, the Shaman, the price of coils — he answers about fuel. He is perfectly content. He is the only person on this crawler who is.

**Ossik the Outrider** · Jawa · m · five hems (26)
`traits: Brawler, ShootingAccuracy(TriggerHappy), NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: put on a stripped speeder bike at three hems because he bit a cousin.
adult: forward scout. Has never lost a run.
> He has not slept inside the hull in six years — he sleeps under it, in the tread-shadow, because the crawler "smells of everyone." He greets kin by biting the air beside their shoulder, hard, teeth audible. He always finds the wreck. He has never once come back with all of his equipment.

**Deggo Ateeka** · Jawa · m · six hems (47)
`traits: TorturedArtist, GreatMemory, Delicate`
childhood: handed a dead astromech at two hems and told to make it talk. It took him nine years.
adult: best restorer in four clans. Forty-one units a season, all sold, every one.
> He names them out loud the day they first move, and then he sells all of them, and he remembers all of them — designation, fault, buyer, price, thirty years back, in order. When a good one goes he weeps into the hood, which brings the insects out of the folds for the salt, and he lets them, and he keeps working.

**Bikk Nkik** · Jawa · m · five hems (24)
`traits: AnnoyingVoice, SlowLearner, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: learned Trade Talk off a protocol droid with a shot vocabulator.
adult: the crawler's outsider-speaker, a post nobody else wanted.
> He greets every human customer with "GREETINGS, DECEASED CUSTOMER" at enormous volume, and has for six years, because the droid that taught him had one corrupted greeting file. Corrections slide straight off. He believes he is the clan's diplomat, he practises in the tread-shadow, and — nobody can explain this — sales are up.

**Nurr the Hem-Cutter** · Jawa · m · seven hems (56)
`traits: GreatMemory, Ascetic, Delicate`
childhood: put to the needle at two hems and never taken off it.
adult: has let down every hem worn aboard this crawler for thirty years, and logged each one.
> He measures you by walking around you twice without ever touching you. In his sleeve is a bundle of cloth strips — every hem he has ever let down, two hundred and six of them, in order, including the eleven children who never reached three. He can name any strip in the dark by smell. He will not let you see the bundle and he will not explain why he carries it.

**Sakk Vor** · Jawa · m · six hems (41)
`traits: Psychopath, TooSmart, Cannibal`
childhood: taught the trade philosophy at four hems and took it further than intended.
adult: walks every wreck first, alone, before the ramp is lowered for anyone else.
> He prices what he finds — hull, drive, cargo, crew, survivors — and he says the numbers aloud in Trade Talk, in front of them, because it would be dishonest to whisper. Entirely pleasant company. Buys the orphans of dead ships their first robe out of his own share, and means absolutely nothing by it.

**Yubb Nagg** · Jawa · m · five hems (22)
`traits: NaturalMood(Sanguine), SlowLearner, Beauty(Ugly)`
childhood: inherited a grinding rig from a grandfather nobody liked.
adult: junior fitter, and the most wanted bachelor at the swap meet, in his own account.
> Three clans have made offers for him this season. All three are offers for the grinding rig. He has told everyone aboard, twice, and has begun practising a walk — a slow, rolling, dreadful walk — and nobody has had the heart, and now it has gone on far too long to fix.

**Kutt** · Jawa · m · eight hems (71)
`traits: Gourmand, Delicate, GreatMemory`
childhood: the storm year. He remembers the taste of the ration his mother did not eat.
adult: water-warden. Weighs every cup on a jeweller's balance.
> He measures out the crawler's water to the grain, and he is a Gourmand — he wants it, visibly, hands shaking over the balance — and he has taken no share of his own for eleven days because the run is long. He does this every long run. Everyone knows. He gets furious if it is mentioned and furious if it is not.

## THE SWAP-MEET GROUND AT DUNE'S THROAT — the last week before storm season

**Kwikk Tann** · Jawa · m · six hems (44)
`traits: TooSmart, Abrasive, GreatMemory`
childhood: memorised the shifting dunes before he could work a spanner.
adult: sells the season's navigation data to every clan at the meet.
> About one route in nine is invented, and he decides which clans get the nine — the ones who bought last year get real charts, the ones who haggled get scenery. He is not sorry. He will explain the loyalty scheme to your face, patiently, using your own clan as the worked example, while you are still holding his chart.

**Fussik Vukk** · Jawa · m · six hems (37)
`traits: NaturalMood(Optimist), SlowLearner, Delicate`
childhood: found it. Has never stopped finding it.
adult: eleven years at the meet with the same single item of stock.
> An intact proton warhead in a padded sling that he carries everywhere, including to meals, because he will not leave it in a tent. The price has come down twice. He is genuinely delightful to talk to — warm, funny, remembers your children — and every trader at Dune's Throat does business with him standing up, facing the exit.

**Rikk Nkik-Tal** · Jawa · m · seven hems (52)
`traits: AnnoyingVoice, TorturedArtist, Jealous`
childhood: sang for the Still House gate at three hems and was sent home.
adult: cries the auction, nine hours a day, without water.
> A voice like a bone saw finding a knot, and he can hold it all day, which is exactly why he has the job. He wanted to sing the Salvation litany. The Still House has refused him four times and he has memorised all four refusals word for word. Ask him to sing and he will, right now, and you will be sorry, and he will be watching your face while you are.

**Tekka Vor** · Jawa · m · six hems (49)
`traits: TooSmart, Gourmand, Jealous`
childhood: apprenticed to a broker who dealt in droids, and switched stock.
adult: the meet's marriage-broker. Forty-one matches last season.
> He wears a bandolier of stoppered scent-vials, one per marriageable kin of four clans, and he will let you smell the whole catalogue and then upsell you. His doctrine is the trade doctrine, said out loud, about people: *do not ask what use she is to you — imagine who else might find a use for her.* His own vial is at the end of the row. He has unstoppered it so often it has gone faint.

**Nommo Grish** · Ugnaught · m · 118
`traits: Abrasive, TooSmart, Ascetic`
childhood: won the right to the droid trade at twenty, tusks against a cousin, and left anyway.
adult: twenty-two years camped at the edge of a Jawa meet, which his own people consider a disgrace.
> Four feet of professional insult in a leather apron who will not sell a droid that does not work — a condition the clans regard as a tragic illness and treat with enormous, elaborate gentleness. He bows and waits for a purr, and the Jawa have all learned to purr for him. It sounds nothing like it is supposed to. He accepts it every time.

**Mardo Chell** · human · m · 58
`traits: Abrasive, NaturalMood(Pessimist), GreatMemory`
childhood: born on a moisture farm nine kilometres from where he will die.
adult: buys from them every season and complains for the other fifty-one weeks.
> He calls them thieving vermin to their hoods, in Basic, loudly, every year — and they adore him, because they can smell the affection underneath it and he has no idea they can. He has bought the same condenser coil off the same clan four separate times. It is his coil. They keep selling him his own coil, and he keeps saying it runs better than the last one.

**Vess Tikko** · Rodian · f · 30
`traits: TooSmart, GreatMemory, Ascetic`
childhood: a clan she will not name, a feud she will not describe.
adult: buys engine parts at Dune's Throat and does not haggle, ever.
> Antennae and a Rodian's nose — she is the only outsider alive who reads the hood-talk, the scent-layer under the Trade Talk, and the clans found out three seasons ago. Now every price spoken within four metres of her is honest and the whole ground resents it. She never says a word about it. She simply stands a little too close and lets the seller remember.

**Grutt the Once** · Jawa · m · eight hems (77)
`traits: GreatMemory, Gourmand, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: unremarkable and he skips it.
adult: sixty years of dining out on a single afternoon.
> He stole a landspeeder off an Imperial supply officer and put it into a sink-pit inside the hour, and he has told that story every night since, in a tent he does not own, and the meet feeds him for it. It has grown about four minutes longer per decade. It now runs the better part of an hour. It is, infuriatingly, still a good story.

**Ekk Nnn** · Jawa · m · six hems (35)
`traits: CreepyBreathing, Abrasive, GreatMemory`
childhood: normal, talkative, by every account.
adult: the meet's finest appraiser of drive systems. Has not used a word in nine years.
> No Jawaese, no Trade Talk, nothing — he trades purely in scent, which means outsiders must be handled by somebody else and kin must stand within a metre of him to hear him at all. Inside the hood he breathes through his mouth, wet and slow and clearly audible from three paces. Nobody knows what he is protesting, or whether he is. The theories are an entire industry at Dune's Throat and he sells nothing to any of them.

## THE STILL HOUSE OF KARR — the rock the women hold, which does not move

**Shaman Ikka Sur-Ateeka** · Jawa · f · nine hems (88)
`traits: VoidFascination, GreatMemory, Ascetic`
childhood: given to the House at two hems. Has never been more than a kilometre from this rock.
adult: sixty years overseeing four sandcrawlers she has never once seen.
> She knows her crawlers by the smell of the men who come off them, and she is never wrong about which one is dying. Her chamber is lined floor to ceiling with the vocabulators of nine hundred dead droids, and she plays them one at a time, all night, every night, and calls it listening to the Salvation. Chiefs return from a season away and she tells *them* what they did.

**Tekki Nkik** · Jawa · f · seven hems (61)
`traits: Ascetic, Kind, GreatMemory`
childhood: dug the pit for a machine she loved and never left the work.
adult: performs the Interment of Scrap for every machine four clans cannot save.
> A hole in the rock a kilometre deep, forty years of filling it, and she gives a broken hand-lamp the full rite at three in the morning if three in the morning is when it died. She can name every unit down there. She has no patience whatsoever for living people, tells them so, and has never been contradicted.

**Ubbi Sur** · Jawa · f · four hems (17)
`traits: FastLearner, Brawler, Abrasive`
childhood: marked for the House at birth, like every girl born here.
adult: not yet. She has strong feelings about that.
> Four hems, two of which she let down herself, badly, to look taller at the crawler-yard fence. She can strip a repulsorlift faster than any man on the *Tukkat-Nur*, she is forbidden to set foot on it, and she has three times hidden in the tread housing and been carried nine kilometres before anyone smelled her. She has already worked out what went wrong the third time.

**Grissa Ateeka** · Jawa · f · seven hems (63)
`traits: GreatMemory, Gourmand, Abrasive`
childhood: a crawler with ninety-one souls aboard.
adult: eleven years in the Still House kitchen, since it burned.
> She cooks for ninety-one people every single night, out of stores the House cannot spare, and the House lets her, and eats what it can, and buries the rest at dawn. She is not sad and will not be treated as sad — she is brisk, loud, faintly contemptuous of your knife technique, and if you stand in her kitchen looking sympathetic she will put a bowl of roots in your hands and tell you to chop.

**Hobb Tann** · Jawa · m · seven hems (58)
`traits: Abrasive, GreatMemory, SlowLearner`
childhood: taught to build a storm-cairn and never taught anything else.
adult: thirty-one years walking the ridge above the House.
> A storm-cairn takes nine stones. He walks the ridge correcting them to nine, and he will tell you about it, with dates, including the year a caravan used eight and what he said to them and what they said back. He has no other subject in the world. He is entirely correct about this one, and it has never once mattered to anybody.

**Vekk Grish** · Jawa · m · six hems (30)
`traits: Masochist, BodyMastery, Ascetic`
childhood: first into every hole. Praised for it early. Never stopped.
adult: goes into the reactor wrecks the droids will not enter.
> Eleven times, in a patched suit, and he comes out grinning and burnt and anointed for it twice. The House calls it devotion to the Salvation. He calls it nothing at all — but he books his next entry before he is out of the medbay, cheerfully, in front of the people treating him. His robe has been let down at the shoulder to fit around the scar tissue.

**Nokko Ubb** · Jawa · m · five hems (28)
`traits: Transhumanist, Jealous, TooSmart`
childhood: lost a hand at eleven and got a better one back.
adult: keeps the good salvage. All of it. On himself.
> Both hands off a protocol droid, one eye off an Imperial probe, and a written list of what he wants next, in order, pinned where every visitor to the House will see it. He delivers unasked verdicts on other people's prosthetics — *"that arm is wasted on you"* — and means it entirely as a compliment to the arm. Anyone at the meet wearing better hardware than his ruins his whole season.
