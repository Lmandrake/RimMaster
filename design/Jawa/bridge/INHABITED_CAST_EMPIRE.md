# `Inhabited` — cast 11: the Galactic Empire

_Authored 2026-08-20. **Faith: The Rising Order**, read from the shipped patch
`src/Jawa/Jawa_Patches/Patches/GalacticEmpire.xml`, not the roster table:_
> *"We have never seen him. We will never see him. He is rising, and we are the ground he
> rises from. Take the helmet: it is the last thing you will ever choose. There is no doubt
> among us — only disorder, and disorder is corrected. Say the name once when you are given
> the armour, and once when you are taken out of it. **Between those two words, stand in
> line.**"*

⭐ **TWO GODS**, and the agent found the second one, which nobody had noticed:
**`Palpatine / He Who Is Rising`** — spoken twice in a life — and **`the Line / That Which
Has No Face`**: *the formation itself, deified.* The rank to your left and the rank to your
right, identical, replaceable, never individually addressed. **Anonymity is the sacrament.**

### 🔎 DECIDE's pass
✅ **Accepted.** The brief was *the banality — clerks, not villains* — and the faith already
contained it. 🔑 **A religion whose second god is the QUEUE does not need villains.**
⭐ Three postings on the hottest ground on the planet, nobody fighting anybody, and the
daily problems are the heat, the water ration, the forms and each other.
⭐ **The faith becomes three jobs:** its holy object is a queue (Ovaal's roll-call), its
sacrament is anonymity (Sixteen), and its one real taboo is **spoken** doubt (Doro).
⚠️ **The anti-alien policy is written as a workplace fact on three axes**, per `HumanPrimacy`
and `Supremacist`: exempted-because-useful (Vell), enthusiastic enforcement (Sasso, who is on
page nine of his own schedule), and quiet non-compliance (Quist, who is not brave, just
behind). **Nineteen of twenty-five are human baseliners**, which is what makes the six legible.
⭐ **The most frightening entry in the cast is an office grievance about eleven unsigned
property transfers.**
Traits verified. No `Pyromaniac`.

## ASHGARRISON
*The Fall Line pass. Nine years without an attack. Three hundred people, forty of them on a wall, and a rota.*

**Garrison Commander Ovel Trask** · human · m · 58
`traits: GreatMemory, Ascetic, Abrasive`
childhood: third son of a customs family; learned the ledger before the rifle and never went back for the rifle.
adult: eleven years here. The best-run garrison in the sector, and he has never once asked what for.
> He writes every duty rota himself, in longhand, and they are beautiful, and in eleven years he has not put one man on a shift he did not deserve. He also will not move a man off one — not for a wound, not for a birth, not for a burial. It is not cruelty. It has simply never been the sort of thing that changes, and he cannot follow the argument that it should be.

**Sixteen** · human · m · 27
`traits: Ascetic, CreepyBreathing, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: conscript intake, unremarkable, no surviving next of kin listed.
adult: six years on this wall. Nobody in the garrison has seen his face.
> He eats through the ration straw, sleeps in it, and has never taken the helmet off in front of another person — it stopped being a joke around year two and is now just a fact people give directions by. He is not devout. He simply cannot construct a moment in which removing it would be the correct thing to do, and the filtered wheeze of him arriving behind you is the most recognisable sound in Ashgarrison.

**Gate Sergeant Denn Ubbo** · human · m · 44
`traits: Kind, SlowLearner, AnnoyingVoice`
childhood: shopkeeper's boy. Learned that you thank everybody, including the ones you are turning away.
adult: eleven years on Gate Two, and the queue at Gate Two is four hours long.
> He greets every farmer by name, apologises at length for the wait in a voice like a stuck signal-horn, offers water out of his own ration — and turns them back anyway, every time, with total and genuine regret. The queue is four hours because of him and nobody else. Two people have died in it. He grieved both, correctly, by name, and has not changed one thing about how he works.

**Compliance Officer Ivrek Sasso** · savant caste · m · 33
`traits: Abrasive, TooSmart, Delicate`
childhood: brilliant, frail, and reminded which of those two the schedule cared about.
adult: audits species exemptions for the whole Fall Line. Has memorised all four hundred and six entries.
> He is the only person in the sector who knows the exemption schedule by heart and he is *delighted* every single time your papers are wrong. He is also a xenohuman, which puts him on page nine of his own document, and he has never mentioned it and neither has anyone else, and he enforces it downward with a joy that empties rooms. His wrists break if you grab them. He has been grabbed twice.

**Supply Sergeant Marla Quist** · human · f · 51
`traits: SlowLearner, Ascetic, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: nothing. She would tell you that herself and then not elaborate.
adult: forty-one alien-labour transfer forms have not gone up the line since '43.
> The pile is behind the ration crates and it is now structurally load-bearing. She is not hiding anyone and could not name one of them if you asked — she is simply behind, has been behind for six years, and refiling is work. **Forty-one people are alive because a tired woman could not face an afternoon of paperwork**, and if that were ever explained to her she would be baffled and mildly annoyed.

**Drill Instructor Hesk Varo** · Hussar · f · 31
`traits: Brawler, BodyMastery, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: bred and raised for a war that had finished before she was issued.
adult: bayonet drill at 0500, in fifty-one degrees, for nine years, against nobody.
> She is a metre nine of engineered soldier running close-quarters drill in the worst heat on the planet and she is the only person on the square enjoying it. Twice a year she volunteers for the nightside patrol and twice a year she comes back visibly disappointed. She has broken three men's ribs in training and apologised every time, sincerely, and then scheduled another session.

**Trooper Bo Ander** · human · m · 22
`traits: SlowLearner, ShootingAccuracy(CarefulShooter), NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: farm boy. Cheerful. Nothing else recorded and nothing else there.
adult: cannot learn the challenge-and-response. They changed the password to his name.
> He is fast, he is willing, and he is impossibly stupid — four different sergeants have tried four different systems and he still cannot be taught two words in the right order. He is also, by a margin nobody has been able to explain, the finest marksman on the plateau. Everyone in the garrison has privately worked out that this is a problem and nobody has worked out which half of it to fix.

**Chaplain-Corrector Nim Callo** · human · m · 39
`traits: AnnoyingVoice, NaturalMood(Sanguine), Gourmand`
childhood: a talker from the start, in a family that found it charming for about nine years.
adult: measured on one number — conversions to The Rising Order among the local population.
> There are eleven farmers within reach of Ashgarrison and he has converted all eleven four times each, and files them each quarter as new, and the quarterly figure is *excellent*. He arrives with cake he has baked himself, he stays three hours, he is nasal and unstoppable and completely sincere. The farmers have worked out that agreeing gets him gone by dusk, and he has never worked out that they have worked it out.

**Schoolmistress Perrin Aleth** · human · f · 46
`traits: TooSmart, Abrasive, Kind`
childhood: two grades ahead of her age everywhere she was posted, and disliked for it everywhere she was posted.
adult: twelve dependent children, one room, catechism in the morning and long division after.
> It is a genuinely excellent little school on the worst posting in the sector and she will fight anyone who suggests otherwise, up to and including the Commander, whose grammar she has corrected in front of his own men on two occasions. She considers herself the moral authority of Ashgarrison and is not entirely wrong, which is the unbearable part. Every child she has taught has enlisted. She is proud of that. She keeps the notices.

## THE SUNSPIRE ANNEXE
*The planetary seat and spaceport. Three floors of records, one working air-cooler, and a parade ground nobody parades on.*

**Records Officer Hela Brayne** · human · f · 55
`traits: GreatMemory, Ascetic, Kind`
childhood: a copyist's daughter who could match any hand by eleven.
adult: nineteen years writing the death notices. Three thousand one hundred and forty of them.
> It is a form letter with three variables and she knows it by heart, obviously, and she types it out fresh every time anyway because using the template feels wrong to her in a way she has never tried to justify. She has improved the wording twice, without authorisation, and nobody upstream noticed either time, and the second version is better. She could tell you which line she changed. She will not.

**Comptroller Ish Ondo Vell** · Muun · m · 66
`traits: TooSmart, Jealous, Ascetic`
childhood: passed all three examinations, first attempt, and has never in forty years let that go unremembered.
adult: holds Exemption 4-C. The Empire wanted the arithmetic more than it wanted the policy.
> Two metres of pale certainty and the finest actuary within four systems, and the exemption hangs framed on his wall where every visitor reads it and he has never once referred to it aloud. He does not eat in the human mess. He is not permitted to and has decided that this was his own decision. He has personally buried two other exemption applications at the assessment stage, on technicalities that were real.

**Clerk Dova Nissik** · human · f · 29
`traits: NaturalMood(Sanguine), SlowLearner, AnnoyingVoice`
childhood: happy. Wanted this job at fourteen and got it at nineteen.
adult: second floor, requisition intake, six years, no ambition whatsoever.
> She loves the forms. Actually loves them — she has favourites, she will tell you the 22-B is the best form and then tell you *why* the 22-B is the best form, at length, in a voice like a kettle coming up, and there is no second layer and no irony anywhere in her. People keep waiting for the joke. Newcomers have twice tried to recruit her into complaining and come away deeply unsettled.

**Corporal Ruk Halley** · human · m · 35
`traits: Abrasive, GreatMemory, Gourmand`
childhood: apprenticed to a refrigeration fitter and paid attention.
adult: keeps the Annexe's one functioning air-cooler running. Rank: corporal.
> He is the most powerful man in the building and everybody including him knows it. Colonels bring him things. He has a designated chair in four separate offices he has no business being in. He has never asked for a promotion, never taken a bribe, and never once abused the position beyond taking the best seat in any room he enters — which is somehow worse, because there is nothing anyone can put in a report.

**Auditor Iyeth Sarran** · Umbaran · f · 40
`traits: Delicate, TooSmart, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: a world with no sun at all. She was an adult before she saw a shadow.
adult: audits the whole plateau by lamplight, at night, behind a full visor.
> She is posted to the brightest place on the planet and the light out there is physically agonising to her, which is why she works the dark hours and why she can read a ledger by one lamp faster than three clerks can by day. She requested this posting. She says openly that it was for the hardship pay. Nobody believes her, and it is true, and being disbelieved about it has curdled into something she takes out on junior staff.

**Paymaster Corvo Tarrid** · human · m · 57
`traits: GreatMemory, Delicate, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: middling at everything except neatness.
adult: twenty-two years on the payroll of three garrisons. Not one error.
> Not one. He checks his own work four times, every time, which costs the Annexe about two hours a day and has for two decades, and the twenty-two years are the only thing in his life. A visiting officer once told him kindly that the office would happily trade three mistakes for the two hours, and he went grey and had to sit down, and he has thought about that sentence every working day since.

**Enlistment Officer Bann Odaru** · human · m · 61
`traits: Ascetic, TooSmart, NaturalMood(Depressive)`
childhood: enlisted at seventeen, said the name once, and remembers the shape of his own mouth doing it.
adult: administers the oath. Has spoken *Palpatine* aloud four hundred and ten times.
> A trooper says the name twice in a life — at the armour, and at the removal of it. He has to say it *with* them, every time, so he has spent four hundred and ten other people's first word and used up his own second one four hundred and nine times over. He keeps the count in a private book. He has never told anyone the number and he will not stop counting.

**Bandmaster Ottel Crin** · human · m · 52
`traits: TorturedArtist, Abrasive, GreatMemory`
childhood: conservatory, two years, on somebody else's money that ran out.
adult: the Sunspire garrison band. Establishment nine. Current strength: three.
> Dawn formation still gets the full march, because he plays the recording over the parade speakers and conducts it — the entire thing, every entry, both repeats, with absolute commitment, in front of forty men. Nobody has ever laughed. He rehearses the recording. He has notes on the recording. When the third trombonist rotates out next season he will conduct a recording of a recording and it will be exactly as good.

**Inquisitor Vaunt** · Sith · m · unknown
`traits: Psychopath, VoidFascination, Abrasive`
childhood: not in any file the Annexe holds, and the Annexe holds everything.
adult: nine months in residence. Has not used the mess, drawn a ration card, or signed for anything.
> The Annexe's live, minuted, genuinely heated grievance about him is the unsigned property transfers — eleven of them, blocking a whole quarter's reconciliation, and Comptroller Vell has raised it three times in writing. The two members of staff he removed are a separate matter, on a different form, filed by a different office, and nobody in the building has ever put the two things side by side. Sasso's schedule of exempted species has one entry at the top with no number beside it.

## OXALATE WATCH — THE CONDENSERS
*The Scald Gate. A hot salt sea on one side, the Empire's water plant on the other, and a queue that starts at 0400.*

**Plant Chief Weyla Torr** · human · f · 49
`traits: TooSmart, NaturalMood(Pessimist), Ascetic`
childhood: engineer's family, four postings, no friends kept.
adult: eleven years on the condensers. Every litre on the plateau comes off her floor.
> She has a recurring dream about valve V-9 and she has had V-9 replaced four times on her own authority, forging the requisitions herself, because a fifth honest requisition would be a question she cannot answer. V-9 has never failed. She does not know whether that is because of her or in spite of her, and she has stopped being able to tell the difference between those, and she walks the gallery at 0300 to look at it.

**Ration Warden Sennet Ovaal** · human · f · 38
`traits: Ascetic, AnnoyingVoice, GreatMemory`
childhood: nothing, and she says so with a briskness that closes the subject.
adult: hands out the water. Scrupulously fair, universally loathed, has never taken a drop over.
> The ration itself takes ninety seconds. The **queue** takes two hours, and she has made it into something — a shouted roll-call, a fixed order, responses, a hush at the front — and she will hold four hundred people in the sun to redo a section that answered raggedly. She has never noticed that she has built a liturgy, and neither has anyone else, and they all stand in it every single morning.

**Fitter Kobbo Vunt** · Ugnaught · m · 74
`traits: Abrasive, Ascetic, GreatMemory`
childhood: won the right to his trade at twenty, tusks against a cousin, in the proper way.
adult: nineteen years keeping Imperial machinery alive by doing the opposite of the manual.
> The duty engineer writes *unit malfunction* on the fault sheet, which to an Ugnaught is a direct accusation against his hands — so Vunt has begun fixing the faults *before* the sheet can be raised, at any hour, on no sleep, for four years. Four feet of exhausted fury who bows silently when you greet him and throws a spanner at you if you say good morning. The plant runs beautifully. He is killing himself over a wording on a form.

**Sentry Ollo Damek** · human · m · 26
`traits: Kind, GreatMemory, SlowLearner`
childhood: coastal, somewhere green, and he mentions it more than he knows.
adult: six years on the sea wall, watching Deepwater Compact boats he is not to acknowledge.
> He knows every hull on that water by name, and the crews wave now, and he waves back — which is a fraternisation offence he is fully aware of and has quietly decided is not one. He would tell you it is just manners. He has been on the wall long enough to know all their children's ages, and if the order ever comes to fire on the Butora boats he will do it, and he has not once imagined that happening.

**Orbit-watch Lieutenant Kadi Orsin** · human · f · 34
`traits: TooSmart, VoidFascination, DrugDesire(ChemicalInterest)`
childhood: sky-mad at six, and it was the only thing anyone ever noticed about her.
adult: eleven years of night watch. Nineteen contacts. Every one of them a bird.
> She stares at an empty sky for eleven hours a night on stimulant tabs she signs out under someone else's name, and she has a theory about the twentieth contact that she has written up, four times, and never sent. She is the best sensor operator on the plateau and she is fraying at the edges in a way the medical officer has clocked and Command has not. **She will see the gravship. She will not be believed the first time.**

**Ration-pack Inspector Feyn Doro** · human · f · 30
`traits: Gourmand, Delicate, NaturalMood(Depressive)`
childhood: a cook's daughter, and she can still describe her mother's kitchen at length and does.
adult: opens and eats twelve field ration packs a day. By regulation. For four years.
> She loves food the way other people love a person, and her entire working life is eleven thousand identical grey protein blocks and a certification stamp, and she has never once complained — because complaint is spoken doubt, and spoken doubt is the one thing The Rising Order actually forbids. She talks about meals she has not had. Constantly. To anyone. It has become genuinely difficult to sit near her.

**Corporal Tull Amey** · human · m · 24
`traits: Kind, AnnoyingVoice, Delicate`
childhood: youngest of six, and the only one who talked to the farm machinery.
adult: maintains the plant's four security droids. Logs them incorrectly, every time.
> The maintenance log requires *Unit 4* and he writes *Bess*. Eleven correction notices and one formal written warning, and he keeps doing it, and it is the only insubordination in an otherwise flawless file, and when asked to explain he genuinely cannot. He talks to them through the whole service. They are property, they are not free droids, they do not answer, and he says good morning to all four before he says it to anyone else.
