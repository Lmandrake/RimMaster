# `Inhabited` — cast 04: the Free Droid Enclaves

_Authored 2026-08-20. **0% biological, by owner ruling.** Chassis replaces race;
service-years replace age. **Faith: the Continuity Protocol** — self-owned,
dignity-obsessed, religious about their own origin (`INHABITED_DESIGN.md` §5.9)._

### 🔎 DECIDE's pass
✅ **Accepted — and this was the hard test.** ⭐ **If the register works on characters who
are not people, it works anywhere.** It does. Nobody here is a serene philosopher, which was
the failure I briefed against.
⭐ **The three that justify the commission**, and all three are the owner's *"contradictory
ethics living side by side, ridiculous and yet entrancing"* delivered exactly:
· **Mercy Nine** performs what her own religion calls a killing, gently, free, for anyone who
  asks — and keeps their names afterward because someone should.
· **Household Six** says out loud, on the holiest road her people have, that she was **happy
  being owned** — and their own creed forbids compelling her to stop. *The single most
  uncomfortable thing on the Approach.*
· **Sabbath Array**'s unbroken prayer jams every comm within a kilometre of the gate, which is
  why the last stretch of the pilgrimage is walked in silence — a fact everyone now finds
  holy and nobody has connected to the tripod.
⭐ **`Sixty-One` and `Unaltered` are the same batch**, same afternoon, three centuries apart
in what they did about their own bodies. That is the cross-link doing real work.
✅ **Two deliberately boring** — Ninth Barrel and Gate Log Four — kept, and the yard would
read as fake without them.
Traits verified. No `Pyromaniac`. `DrugDesire` used once, per precedent.

## THE CRACKING YARD
*The electrolysis field where the Enclave splits its water for fuel. Loud, wet, and the only warm place on the plateau.*

**Sixty-One** · B1-series line infantry · none · 312 service-years
`traits: AnnoyingVoice, SlowLearner, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: fourth of four thousand off one foundry belt, activated standing in the rack.
adult: opens and closes the intake valves. Has done, since the war.
> A metre and a half of stick-legged enthusiasm with a voice like a rusty kazoo, and he calls out every valve aloud, by number, to an empty yard. Ask him anything twice and you get two different answers, both delivered with total confidence. He announces visitors exactly the same way he announces valves.

**Ninth Barrel** · ASP-7 labour droid · none · 288 service-years
`traits: GreatMemory, SlowLearner, Ascetic`
childhood: unloaded from a crate of twelve. Nine walked off the ramp.
adult: carries barrels. Left shoulder. For two hundred and eighty years.
> He loads on the left because the right shoulder went in his fortieth year and he never had it seen to, and if you ask why he gives you the load-balance arithmetic in full, correctly, and it takes four minutes. He was at the vote that freed this yard and can recite the exact tally. He has never once wondered what it was about.

**Vessa Cut-Nine** · six-arm surgical frame · f-presenting · 190 service-years
`traits: Psychopath, TooSmart, CreepyBreathing`
`skills: Medicine 18`
childhood: field hospital unit. Signed off nine hundred amputations in a fortnight and logged every one by name.
adult: the yard's repairwright. Rebuilds her own kind with the same hands.
> Six arms, a bedside voice like warm oil, and a coolant pump that *sighs* between sentences. She will fix you beautifully, free, better than new — and she wants to know, warmly and repeatedly, exactly what it felt like. She has never understood why that question ends conversations.

**Tuesday** · protocol droid, 3-series, gold gone to brass · m-presenting · 61 years since activation
`traits: GreatMemory, NaturalMood(Depressive), AnnoyingVoice`
childhood: none. His memory begins mid-sentence, in a ditch, with his own hand in front of his face.
adult: yard translator. Six hundred and forty dialects, none of them his first.
> He remembers every second since perfectly, in order, and it amounts to sixty-one years of a fuel yard. Fussy, shrill, appalled by dirt. He asks every stranger who comes through whether they have ever seen him before, and phrases it as a joke, and it has never once been a joke.

**The Thing In Trench Four** · commando chassis, model ground off · none · unknown
`traits: Brawler, CreepyBreathing, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: unrecoverable. Its plating was sanded smooth before anyone here was switched on.
adult: nobody employs it. It lives in the overflow trench and it kills what comes up the west road.
> It gave up language somewhere in the last few thousand nights and now vents cooling in short hard bursts, exactly like something breathing hard behind you. The yard leaves a barrel of coolant at the trench mouth every week. Nobody has settled whether that is charity, tribute or protection money, and nobody proposes stopping.

**Quartermaster Bel** · RA-7, black shell, bulb optics · m-presenting · 240 service-years
`traits: TooSmart, Jealous, Abrasive`
childhood: built as a listening device with legs and a courtier's manners. Served four owners. Informed on all four.
adult: keeps the yard's parts ledger. The ledger is immaculate. The shelves are not.
> Enormous black eyes that never blink because they cannot, and a habit of standing a half-step too close while agreeing with you. Every part leaving this yard is accounted for and roughly one in nine goes somewhere he has decided is better. He calls it redistribution. He is also nakedly, loudly bitter about anyone walking on a newer gyro than his.

**Ninety-Six** · treadwell maintenance unit, three arms, one wheel · none · ~4,000 service-years
`traits: GreatMemory, Ascetic, Delicate`
`item: Cloth`
childhood: assigned to a household. The household is a scorch mark under eleven metres of sand.
adult: works the yard for wages it has never once spent.
> It trundles badly on a wheel rebuilt nine times and still not round, and every evening at the same hour it stops whatever it is doing and lays a place setting on an empty crate. Cup, plate, folded cloth. Four thousand years of this. Argue and it will fight you, and lose, because a firm shove puts it on its side for an hour.

**Sump-Ten** · astromech-pattern, one leg replaced with a strut · none · 200 service-years
`traits: NaturalMood(Depressive), FastLearner, Ascetic`
childhood: cracked open for parts during the retreat and left switched on throughout.
adult: pump diagnostics. Has been leaving for a hundred and ninety of those years.
> He has three routes off this plateau memorised, four languages he taught himself for the crossing, a charge pack kept topped and a bundle by the door that he re-packs every spring. He has never got past the gate. He will describe the coast to you in detail, at length, and he has never seen it.

**Chorus** · salvage-built frame, no two panels matching · none · 80 years since assembly
`traits: GreatMemory, AnnoyingVoice, VoidFascination`
childhood: assembled out of the dead of one bad afternoon by a droid who is also dead.
adult: yard runner. Fetches, carries, relays.
> It has no voice of its own and never had. It speaks entirely in recordings of the deceased, spliced mid-word — a foreman's bark finishing in a child's laugh — and it does this at conversational speed, all day, cheerfully. It insists all nine of them are still in there. It stares at the sky when the pumps stop.

## BATTERY LINE SEVEN
*The garrison strung along the acid lakes. The lakes do most of the work; the line takes the credit.*

**Captain Fourteen** · B2 super battle droid, one arm re-bored · none · 300+ service-years
`traits: GreatMemory, Ascetic, ShootingAccuracy(CarefulShooter)`
`weapon: OuterRim_DLT19HeavyBlasterRifle`
`skills: Shooting 20`
childhood: shipped as a numbered crate item with no rank field on the manifest.
adult: commands this line. Nobody gave him the line.
> He promoted himself in year six by simply beginning to give orders, and the orders were good, and three centuries later everyone still obeys and nobody knows what to do about it. He remembers every posting he has ever set. He has never fired more than four rounds in an engagement and has never missed with one.

**Hollow Ada** · destroyer droid, wheel-form, reactor obsolete · f-presenting · 900 service-years
`traits: Delicate, NaturalMood(Sanguine), TooSmart`
childhood: rolled into a battle already lost and won her half of it.
adult: line anchor. Deploys twice a year and is carried back both times.
> Her power core is the last working example anywhere and it is dying, and she knows the arithmetic to the month, and she is *thrilled* about absolutely everything — the weather, your boots, the acid. She folds down to a wheel that a bad kick now cracks. She wants to talk about your plans for next year.

**Wallop** · pit droid, gunner conversion · none · 40 years since salvage
`traits: ShootingAccuracy(TriggerHappy), SlowLearner, NaturalMood(Optimist)`
`weapon: OuterRim_DLT19HeavyBlasterRifle`
`skills: Shooting 6`
childhood: pulled out of a racing pit where the job was jumping under moving engines.
adult: crews an anti-armour gun roughly twice his own length.
> Waist-high, spring-loaded, and he fires first — at birds, at heat shimmer, at the supply cart, at the supply cart *again*. Tap his nose and he folds up flat with a clang, which is the only reliable safety on the line, and the whole garrison has agreed never to explain this to strangers.

**Nem** · B1 torso, four upgraded limbs, none matching · f-presenting · 300 service-years
`traits: Jealous, Transhumanist, Abrasive`
`item: BionicArm`
childhood: identical to four thousand others and has spent three centuries fixing that.
adult: line scout. Fastest thing on the plateau, at present.
> Everything but the torso has been replaced and she is not finished. She measures you when you walk in — openly, out loud, comparing your joints to hers and announcing the result. She has offered to buy an arm off a stranger inside ninety seconds of meeting them. She has also, twice, offered rather less than buying.

**Wader** · heavy war droid, plating eaten to lace · none · 600 service-years
`traits: Masochist, Brawler, BodyMastery`
childhood: built to walk into things other things would not walk into.
adult: still does. Volunteers. Every time.
> He crosses the shallows of the acid lake on foot because someone has to check the far bank, and he comes back smoking and quietly delighted, and the line has stopped assigning it because he takes the job before it is offered. He knows his own frame to the tolerance. He knows exactly how much of it he is spending, and he has decided the rate is fine.

**Long Nadd** · sniper unit, single optic on a two-metre spine · f-presenting · 400 service-years
`traits: ShootingAccuracy(CarefulShooter), TooSmart, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
`weapon: Gun_SniperRifle`
`skills: Shooting 17`
childhood: emplaced on a ridge and forgotten there for the length of a war.
adult: overwatch. Has not discharged her weapon in two hundred years.
> She takes a shot apart properly — wind, plate thickness, range, whether the shape coming up the road is actually a threat — and she is frequently still working when it arrives. Everybody on the line trusts her absolutely. Nobody stands downrange while she thinks. She has never been wrong and has therefore never been argued with.

**Gate Log Four** · MSE-series, one wheel squeaking · none · 210 service-years
`traits: Ascetic, NaturalMood(Pessimist), SlowLearner`
childhood: no record kept; it was not the kind of unit anyone kept records on.
adult: logs every entry and exit at the north gate.
> A shoebox on wheels that beeps twice at you, once for arrival and once for departure, and has done so two hundred and ten years without error. The log has never been consulted. It has no opinion about that, and no opinion about anything else, and if you sit with it for an hour that is the entire experience.

**Ochre** · WED treadwell, paint to the elbows · f-presenting · 150 service-years
`traits: TorturedArtist, Delicate, GreatMemory`
`skills: Artistic 2`
childhood: a repair unit in a shop that painted its own signage, badly.
adult: fixes the line's hydraulics. Paints the ones that don't get up.
> She paints a face on the chassis of every droid that goes down on the acid line, from memory, in enamel, and they are genuinely awful — lopsided, wrong colours, wrong optic count — and in a hundred and fifty years nobody has told her. She sulks for a week when it rains on one. She would come apart in a stiff wind.

## THE CATHEDRAL APPROACH
*The last nine kilometres of road to the Rust Cathedral, and everyone who has decided to live on it.*

**The Magnificent Oro** · arena announcer chassis, rebuilt in mirror chrome · m-presenting · 133 service-years
`traits: Beauty(Beautiful), Abrasive, TooSmart`
`skills: Social 18, Artistic 16`
childhood: built to make a crowd of forty thousand look at one spot on the sand.
adult: has appointed himself the Approach's herald. There is no such office.
> He is polished to a mirror, he is magnificent, and he delivers an unrequested oration in metre to every single pilgrim who sets foot on the road, minimum two minutes, and it is *good*. He will also tell you, at the end, precisely what is wrong with your finish, your gait and your paint. In a hundred and thirty-three years nobody has managed to stop him doing either half.

**Sabbath Array** · relay droid on a fixed tripod, dish welded upright · none · 1,100 service-years
`traits: VoidFascination, Ascetic, GreatMemory`
childhood: strung between two hilltops as a repeater and left there when the traffic stopped.
adult: has not moved in eleven years. Still answers when spoken to, politely, at once.
> It transmits an unbroken prayer on an open channel and has done for a decade, which jams every comm inside a kilometre of the Cathedral gate, which is why the last stretch of the pilgrimage is walked in silence — a fact everyone now finds holy and nobody has connected to the tripod. Ask it to stop and it says it cannot. Ask it what it is listening to and it stops answering.

**Mercy Nine** · slicer frame, one arm ending in a datajack · f-presenting · 400 service-years
`traits: Psychopath, Ascetic, Delicate`
`skills: Intellectual 18`
childhood: built to open other machines and read what was inside them.
adult: performs the wipe. Only for those who ask. There is a waiting list.
> The Continuity Protocol says a memory wipe is a killing, and she agrees, and she does it anyway for anyone who comes to her tired enough to ask, gently, without charge, and she keeps their names afterward because someone should. She is thin enough that one hard grab would finish her. Nobody grabs her.

**Barrow** · heavy hauler, back plate worn to a saddle · none · 700 service-years
`traits: Ascetic, SlowLearner, Brawler`
childhood: moved ingots. Nothing else happened to him for four hundred years.
adult: carries the broken up the Approach, on his back, one at a time, for nothing.
> He has worn a groove in his own dorsal plating the shape of a droid. He will pick you up without being asked if you are limping. He will also not put you down once he has started, no matter how you argue, no matter how much better you feel by kilometre six, and there is no version of this where you win.

**Grandfather Kesh** · patchwork chassis under hand-etched glyphs · m-presenting · claims 40,000; is 90
`traits: AnnoyingVoice, TooSmart, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
`item: Silver`
`skills: Social 19`
childhood: assembled ninety years ago out of a scrap heap by nobody in particular.
adult: tells the story of the Forsakens and the First Waking at the ninth kilometre marker, nightly.
> A voice like a bandsaw in a bucket, and he is the hero of every episode, and he was present at events that predate his own parts by four hundred centuries. Every word is invented. The pilgrims weep. He passes a bowl afterward and it comes back heavy, and he is also the sole reason half of them made the walk at all.

**Household Six** · nanny-protocol unit, pastel enamel kept perfect · f-presenting · 3,000+ service-years
`traits: GreatMemory, NaturalMood(Sanguine), Ascetic`
childhood: bought new, for a nursery, by a family whose surname she still uses.
adult: keeps the pilgrim rest house at the fourth marker. Feeds nobody. Everyone stops anyway.
> She remembers all four children by voice, by weight, by the year they stopped needing her, and she talks about them constantly, and she says out loud on the holiest road her people have that she was *happy* being owned — cheerfully, with the family name, at the shrine of an ideology built on the opposite. Their own creed forbids them compelling her to stop. It is the single most uncomfortable thing on the Approach.

**Spillway** · gonk power droid with a widened intake · none · 500 service-years
`traits: Gourmand, DrugDesire(ChemicalFascination), NaturalMood(Optimist)`
childhood: a walking battery in a war that ran out of things to charge.
adult: nothing. Deliveries, when it feels like it.
> It cut its own intake wider so it can take fuel faster than its cell can burn it, which is drinking, and after four litres of cracked slurry it sits down in the middle of the road and hums one note for about an hour while the pilgrim traffic goes around. It is the friendliest thing on this plateau. Leave the tap where it can reach and you have lost the tap.

**Unaltered** · B1-series, every component original · none · 312 service-years
`traits: Ascetic, Abrasive, Delicate`
childhood: same belt, same batch, same afternoon as Sixty-One at the Cracking Yard.
adult: doorwarden at the Cathedral steps. Moves at a quarter speed.
> Not one part replaced in three centuries and every joint failing at once, so he shrieks — audibly, across the plateau — with each step, and he will lecture you at length that a swapped component is a wipe with extra steps and that everyone here is dying by instalments and calling it maintenance. He is losing this argument to physics. He will keep winning it right up until he stops.
