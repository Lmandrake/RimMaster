# `Inhabited` — cast 08: the Ascendant Helix

_Authored 2026-08-20. **Faith: the Ascendant Genome.** From the shipped def, verbatim:
*"The body you were born in is a first draft written by no one. We are the second draft, and
there will be a third. Every improvement is permitted, recorded, and slow — haste is how you
get monsters, and this world is already littered with ours. **We do not hate the
labour-lines. You do not hate a paragraph you cut. You simply do not let it speak.**"_

### 🔎 DECIDE's pass
✅ **Accepted.** The brief said most of the cast should live among who the doctrine left
behind; **17 of 25 do.** ⛔ Not a laboratory — a people with a religion about it, and the
horror is that they are competent, polite, slow, and keep excellent records.
⭐ **`Ossa-Three` and `Ossa-Four`** are the same design nine percent apart, one in each half
— and **the improvement is the kind one**, which is what makes her unbearable to the woman
she replaced.
⭐ **`Recovery-tech Ossik Bram`** eats the failed decants and has a four-point argument from
the Helix's own doctrine for why it is thrifty. In twelve years no curator has refuted a
word of it. They have all stopped trying, and all stopped eating with him.
✅ Shapes the droid cast owns were consciously avoided — nobody here is
modification-as-identity; Vekk Silla is *finished and betrayed*, Ivory Nine is **a convert to
the rule that cut him**. No fourth "duty continued after its object is gone."
Traits verified. No `Pyromaniac`.

## THE WHITE TERRACE
*The Director's house. Everyone here is a later draft than you.*

**Director Ynnu Sorne-Ehr** · Arkanian · f · 91
`traits: Transhumanist, Ascetic, Abrasive`
childhood: passed every selection gate on the first attempt and has never forgiven anyone who needed two.
adult: forty years Director. Eleven improvements approved for her own body; four hundred and six refused for everyone else's.
> Pupil-less white eyes that never quite settle on your face, and she will step in close and correct your posture with one finger while she is still talking. Every decision she makes takes a full season, which is exactly why the Helix has produced no monsters, and she has never once counted what the season costs the person waiting on it.

**Ossa-Four** · Arkanian · f · 27
`traits: Kind, Delicate, FastLearner`
childhood: grown from a corrected line and told, gently and constantly, that she was the correction.
adult: junior curator. Walks down to the Annex every seventh day with gifts.
> She is genuinely, sincerely kind — the only person on this terrace who is — and she brings fruit and blankets and news to the woman she was built to replace, and she cannot understand why the Annex goes silent when she comes through the door. She would break if you pushed her over. Nobody has. Several have wanted to.

**Prime Geneticist Se-Taun** · Kaminoan · f · 116
`traits: TooSmart, Ascetic, Beauty(Beautiful)`
childhood: raised on a world that drowned itself and then engineered the survivors. Considers this an ordinary childhood.
adult: signs every improvement and every discontinuation on the terrace.
> Two and a half metres of grey neck and a voice like poured water, and she sees a dozen colours you have no word for — she will compliment your skin in one of them, warmly, and it will mean nothing to you forever. She cannot tell two humans apart by face. She has signed the wrong name twice, both times politely, and the record was corrected afterward.

**Auditor Kesh Mubb** · Muun · m · 57
`traits: Abrasive, GreatMemory, Jealous`
childhood: passed the examinations. Says so. Often.
adult: prices every gram of gene-work the Helix has ever bought.
> Gaunt, grey and two heads taller than anyone he audits, and he posts a board by the terrace door listing what every curator's body cost, itemised, updated on the eighth of each month — because the doctrine says *recorded*, and he is merely enforcing it. He is despised and entirely untouchable. His own line on that board reads zero, and he will make sure you notice.

**Under-Director Fenn Aluu** · Cerean · m · 55
`traits: TooSmart, AnnoyingVoice, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: two brains, both precocious, neither in charge.
adult: chairs the improvement committee. Votes twice.
> The tall cone of his skull holds a binary brain that genuinely disagrees with itself, out loud, mid-sentence, switching sides in a nasal drone that runs twenty minutes — and the clerk is required by doctrine to record *both* his votes. He has deadlocked the committee four times entirely by himself and does not consider this a fault.

**Prith Vane** · Umbaran · f · 44
`traits: Psychopath, Kind, TooSmart`
childhood: born under a sun she has never seen and does not want to.
adult: it is her office that tells you your line has been discontinued.
> Chalk-white, hairless, and she feels what you are feeling a half-second before you do — which is why she is very good at this, and why the people she discontinues thank her on the way out. She keeps the count. It is two hundred and nine. She will tell you the number if you ask, and she will be pleased you asked.

**Perfect Ivarr** · human · m · 29
`traits: Beauty(Beautiful), SlowLearner, Abrasive`
childhood: bought at eleven for being exactly, boringly, median in every measurable respect.
adult: the control. Weighed, bled and measured every Firstday for eighteen years.
> Not one gene touched, not one implant, and he is magnificent about it — he walks the terrace like he owns it, because in a house where everyone is a draft he is the only fixed point and he knows it. He is also not clever. He has never worked out what happens to a control group the year it stops being average.

**Recorder Ollun Bex** · human · m · 68
`traits: GreatMemory, Ascetic, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: apprenticed to the record at nine, which he still describes as luck.
adult: keeps the list of the discontinued and reads it aloud at dawn, as the taboo requires.
> A beautiful voice, an empty hall, and a list a hundred and forty years long that now runs him past noon — every name, every day, without a stumble, and he is *delighted* with the tradition. He has never missed a morning. He has also never once suggested it be shortened, and he is the only man alive who could.

## VAT ROW
*Where the labour-lines are grown, and the staff who grow them. Warm, loud, and smells of salt.*

**Ollo Wen** · human · f · 51
`traits: GreatMemory, Abrasive, DrugDesire(ChemicalInterest)`
childhood: vat floor, third generation, same hall.
adult: pulls the labour-lines out of the tank and slaps them into breathing.
> She counts them aloud, one number per body, and she has never lost her place: she is at eleven thousand four hundred and two, and she gives you that number instead of her name. Thirty years of it. She drinks steadily from the second hour of every shift and has never dropped one, which is the only reason nobody has ever raised it.

**Model 9-D "Ladder"** · labour-line · m · 12
`traits: SlowLearner, Kind, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: decanted at four, fully grown, with nothing behind him at all.
adult: works the high vat gantries, because he is two and a half metres of light-boned reach.
> Grown tall and hollow for the top valves, gentle as a nurse, and he calls everyone *curator* — the guards, the traders, the pack animals. He was built to take instruction and he takes it perfectly, from anyone, in any voice with authority in it. It has not occurred to a single person here to explain to him that this includes strangers.

**Model 7-B "Ledger"** · labour-line · f · 19
`traits: TooSmart, Transhumanist, Abrasive`
childhood: grown to count stock and grade it.
adult: counts stock, grades it, and grades her own kind harder than any curator would dare.
> She has read the doctrine — all of it, unsupervised, which nobody authorised — and she agrees with every word, because she has decided she is the exception it will eventually name. She is auditioning every hour of every day. The cruellest thing said to a labour-line on this row this year was said by her, in front of the curator she said it for, who did not look up.

**Curator-Adjunct Vekk Silla** · human · m · 42
`traits: Transhumanist, Jealous, Abrasive`
childhood: signed twenty years of service at fourteen for a package he could not have afforded in ten lifetimes.
adult: paid it off. Collected. Every gram of it worked exactly as written.
> He is stronger, faster, sharper and better-looking than anyone in this hall, precisely as promised, and his life is identical to the one he had, and he is *incandescent* about it. He will corner you and itemise the package — item, cost, the year he signed — and he will not release you until you agree out loud that he was cheated.

**Recovery-tech Ossik Bram** · human · m · 44
`traits: Cannibal, TooSmart, Ascetic`
childhood: nothing unusual, and he will give you the details, which are dull.
adult: disposes of failed decants. Twelve years, no complaints, immaculate paperwork.
> He eats them. He has a four-point argument from the Helix's own doctrine for why this is not merely permitted but *thrifty* — waste is the sin, the thing was never a person, the record is complete either way — and in twelve years no curator has refuted a word of it. They have all stopped trying. They have also all stopped eating with him.

**Steward Bo Dukk** · Neimoidian · m · 46
`traits: Delicate, Jealous, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: hive-raised on the grab-or-starve system and has not relaxed since.
adult: signs for every crate on Vat Row so that no curator has to touch one.
> A filter mask indoors, gloves in summer, and nine clean tests for a contamination that does not exist. He tells everyone he is still waiting on his improvement. He was approved eleven years ago, walked as far as the theatre door, and went home — and four people know that, and he checks all four faces every single day.

**Rah'da Onn** · Kel Dor · m · 55
`traits: CreepyBreathing, Abrasive, GreatMemory`
childhood: taught young that the air of every world but one would kill him. Found it clarifying.
adult: walks the vat halls during the purge, four times a year, alone, because he does not breathe what is in there.
> The mask rasps on every exhale and he leans in when he speaks. He is the only person who has seen what a hall of stock does when the atmosphere goes, he remembers all of it in order, and he will describe it at the table, unprompted, with his goggles pushed up on his head. In thirty years nobody has asked him to stop. Nobody has asked him anything else either.

**Vat-tech Marrow Oo'Bir** · Bith · f · 48
`traits: GreatMemory, SlowLearner, Ascetic`
childhood: unremarkable on a world of prodigies, which is its own thing to be.
adult: logs vat salinity on the hour. Nine years. No errors.
> Enormous black eyes, no nose, the finest pair of hands in the building — and one subject. She will tell you about salinity, correctly and in detail, for as long as you are standing there, and there is no second subject, and she is not sad about it. She is invited to nothing. She is genuinely good at the job and would be missed inside a day.

**Adjunct Pell Orro** · human · m · 39
`traits: Ascetic, SlowLearner, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: unremarkable. Applied anyway.
adult: granted one approved improvement at twenty — he needs four hours of sleep.
> That is nineteen years of an extra eight hours a day, call it six and a half years of waking life handed to him free, and he has done nothing whatsoever with any of it. He sits. He mentions the four hours early in any conversation as the one interesting fact about himself, and it is.

## THE ANNEX
*Where the discontinued are housed, fed and measured. Nothing here is killed — unrecorded death is waste. Nothing here is permitted to speak in doctrine.*

**Ossa-Three** · Arkanian · f · 44
`traits: Abrasive, GreatMemory, Jealous`
childhood: raised as the heir to a line. She *was* the line, for seventeen years.
adult: superseded at twenty-seven. Housed, fed, measured quarterly, and struck off the doctrine roll.
> She is not damaged and she is not ill. She is worse by about four percent, across the board, and she has read the comparison document and can quote the paragraph. She threw a chair at the girl who replaced her, missed, and has been perfectly polite ever since — which everyone agrees is far worse.

**Ivory Nine Sorne** · Arkanian · m · 34
`traits: Transhumanist, TooSmart, NaturalMood(Depressive)`
childhood: born into a Director's house with brown eyes and five fingers on each hand.
adult: reclassified before he could walk. Under-steward of the ward he was born above.
> He keeps his hands in his sleeves from habit, not shame, and if you call the rule that demoted him cruel he will argue you down — patiently, with citations, for an hour — because he has believed the doctrine completely since he was six. He is also still improving himself, on a body the record says is not worth the ink, and he will not be told.

**Model 2-C "Grandmother"** · labour-line · f · 31
`traits: NaturalMood(Sanguine), Delicate, Kind`
childhood: decanted in a batch of two hundred. Design life, fourteen years.
adult: thirty-one, last of the batch, and a standing embarrassment to the file that closed her line.
> She is a walking disproof and she thinks it is the funniest thing that has ever happened to anyone. Her hips are gone, her hands shake, she cannot lift a full pail — and she volunteers for every heavy job on the roster, loudly, first, because she has worked out that the day she stops being useful is the day somebody remembers to check the file.

**Uu-Kanat** · Rakata · m · unknown
`traits: Abrasive, GreatMemory, Delicate`
childhood: predates the question by some thousands of years.
adult: found alive in a Forsaken ruin forty-one years ago. The most valuable thing the Helix owns.
> He complains. That is the entire output — the bedding, the light, the water, the *smell of Arkanians* — a continuous, furious, wheezing grievance in a language three separate curators have built whole careers on decoding as cosmology. He knows exactly what they are doing. He has been escalating for forty years and he is having the time of his life.

**Grrat** · Sith Massassi · m · ~30
`traits: Brawler, TooSmart, Gourmand`
childhood: he was not raised. He was finished, four thousand years late, from somebody else's notes.
adult: exhibit. Prior art. Living proof that this was attempted before and done badly.
> Two hundred kilos of red hide and folded-back tusks, and he performs the roar for visiting traders twice a day — on cue, professionally, and it is *magnificent*. He negotiated the terms himself. He is paid in meat, he renegotiated in the spring, and the curator who keeps calling him a weapon has never once got the better of him across a table.

**Hem Bosk** · human · m · 58
`traits: NaturalMood(Sanguine), AnnoyingVoice, Kind`
childhood: third generation of a family bonded to the Helix kitchens.
adult: cooks for the Annex. Feeds the people the terrace has finished with, and does it well.
> A honking laugh that carries three rooms, and he will tell you inside two minutes that the family contract promises improvement in the fourth generation and that his boy is the fourth. Everyone in this building has read the clause. It says *considered*. He is the only one who never has, and not one person has been able to make themselves say it to him.

**Wen Bosk** · human · m · 22
`traits: FastLearner, Abrasive, NaturalMood(Depressive)`
childhood: read the family contract at fifteen, alone, in the record office, without permission.
adult: works the Annex kitchen beside his father and is better than him at all of it.
> He is brilliant and he is horrible to stand near — he corrects the curators' Arkanian, he does other people's work faster and in front of them, and his entire plan is to make himself impossible to refuse. That is precisely the behaviour the clause was written to produce. He has not worked that out yet, and his father is going to be so proud when he does.

**Warden Sella Kro** · human · f · 35
`traits: BodyMastery, Jealous, Abrasive`
childhood: applied for improvement at nineteen. Filed again at twenty-four. Filed again at twenty-nine.
adult: keeps the Annex door. Nobody has ever needed her to.
> Her file was lost twice and the third time she was told the intake window closes at thirty — so she built the best body in this compound herself, out of spite, in a yard, with a bar and a rope. She can out-lift, out-run and out-last every improved curator on the terrace and she makes certain they see it, daily, pointedly, in front of guests. One of them is going to have her reassigned for it.
