# `Inhabited` — cast 06: the Blackstar Company

_Authored 2026-08-20. **Faith: `the Contract`** — read from `faction_religions_spec.md` §10,
not the roster table. *"A contract is the only honest thing two strangers can hold between
them. It names one person, one price, one ending, and everyone not written into it walks
away untouched. We do not pillage — pillage is confessing you could not find the one you
came for."* Memes: `VME_Bushido` · `VME_Anonymity` · `Individualist` · `Guilty`._

🔴 **`permanentEnemy` is KEPT — the player will only ever meet these people in a fight**, so
every one of them is written to land in the two seconds before or during violence.
⚠️ **They are not jolly.** The comedy is structural — procurement applied to murder — and
several are meant to frighten. Nobody here thinks any of it is funny.

### 🔎 DECIDE's pass
✅ **Accepted. The most disciplined cast so far**, and the only one that consciously
de-duplicated its *species* shapes against the earlier files: the Chagrian is the
rule-follower half of the species entry rather than the tasteless one, the Rodian is the
theatre half rather than the clan-secret one, the Muun is contract-sacred rather than
exam-wounded, the Togorian is the female engineer rather than the wandering male. That is
the cross-cast discipline a fan-out normally cannot do for itself.
⭐ **Vidd Anselm is the owner's own example, delivered** — *fast, lean, impossibly stupid* —
and made worse in exactly the right way: he cannot read, will not say so, draws the mark's
face on his forearm each morning from memory, and it **drifts**.
⭐ **The claims office is the best structural invention in the project so far.** Blackstar is
the only faction on this world that **pays its victims**, and it is not mercy, it is a
liability. That one idea turns a pirate reskin into a society.
Traits verified. No `Pyromaniac`.

---

**The three clauses every hunter can recite.** ① **One name.** A contract names one person;
anyone not named is out of scope, and killing out of scope is not a crime, it is a *breach* —
which is worse, because a crime can be paid off. ② **Served, then opened.** The scope of
works is nailed to the outside of the door before the door comes off. ③ **The face is not the
person. The word is the person** — which is why the sworn do not remove the helmet, and why
a broken contract is uncleanliness that does not wash off.

There is a dispute-resolution clause. It has a staff. It rules against the Company about one
time in nine, and when it does, somebody Blackstar is shot by somebody Blackstar, on paper,
with a receipt.

## THE SIGNING HOUSE
*A landing spur and a stone room. Nobody leaves here armed until the scope has been read back aloud to the client, in full, with the parties named. The reading is the sacrament.*

**Captain Aureth Sar-Vell** · human · f · 57
`traits: GreatMemory, Ascetic, ShootingAccuracy(CarefulShooter)`
childhood: courier for a shipping line that dismissed her at nineteen over four crates she had signed for.
adult: has not done one hour of work in thirty-eight years that was not written down first.
> Silver-cropped, unhurried, and she will stop a firefight in progress to shout an amendment across it — *"the loader is not named, put him DOWN"* — and her own people obey that faster than they take cover. She has never fired before the clause is read. Eleven seconds of it, on a bad day, out loud, while her hunters die in the doorway waiting for her to finish.

**Brother-Adjutant Hoxx Tenneb** · Chagrian · m · 46
`traits: TooSmart, Abrasive, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: raised comfortable and rule-bound, in a house that flooded on schedule and treated it as weather.
adult: prosecutes breaches. **Against his own side. That is the entire job.**
> Blue, horned, and he files his lethorns to a mirror finish while you make your defence — the rasp is the only sound in the room and it does not stop when you talk. He has had four Blackstar hunters shot for over-collection, he is physically unable to let a violation pass unremarked, and he does not want the Captain's chair, which is precisely why nobody can manage him.

**Nel Sunta Rukh, the Crier** · Rodian · m · 33
`traits: AnnoyingVoice, TorturedArtist, GreatMemory`
childhood: nine years in a clan troupe, playing the herald, which is the part nobody wants.
adult: declaims the contract at the breach. Every word of it, every time, and it is art to him.
> He stands in the open doorway with both arms wide and performs your scope of works at you in a voice like a split reed — the recital of the parties, the fee, the *ending* — and he will not be hurried and will not skip. Two hunters have been shot behind him waiting for him to reach *"and thirdly"*. He thought about that for a week and then decided the fault was theirs.

**Registrar Ivve Odo** · human · f · 39
`traits: SlowLearner, Abrasive, Delicate`
childhood: nothing. A schooling, a certificate, a post.
adult: fourteen years of contract numbers. Ink cuffs, a rubber thimble, the flattest voice on the spur.
> She is genuinely boring and would not thank you for pretending otherwise. She closes the register at the eighteenth hour and does not reopen it, so an entire raid once sat on the pad overnight, fuelled and armed, because a countersignature arrived at nineteen. She was asked about it. She said the hour is the hour.

**Ma'kesh Bruul** · Whiphid · m · 61
`traits: Gourmand, Brawler, NaturalMood(Depressive)`
childhood: an ice world, a first kill at eleven, and a rule that says a hunter eats what he takes and nothing else.
adult: takes only night contracts, and only *alive and by hand* — which doubles the fee and is not negotiable.
> Two and a half metres of matted white fur under a cooling shroud that fails twice a shift, tusks yellowed, permanently and visibly miserable in the heat of a world he will not leave. He is the most expensive hunter on the roster because his honour is a line item, and he eats the fee's worth of meat in a week, and he has never once been talked into a rifle.

**Vidd Anselm** · human · m · 29
`traits: SlowLearner, ShootingAccuracy(TriggerHappy), NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: docks, gangs, and a cheerfulness nobody has ever managed to dent.
adult: three contracts signed. He has read none of them.
> He is fast, he is lean, and he is impossibly stupid — the sort of stupid that gets other people killed while he is still smiling. He cannot read, will not say so, and instead draws the mark's face on his own forearm every morning from memory. It drifts. By the fourth day it is a different man, and he shoots the first thing that moves in the doorway, and twice that has been the client.

**Teska Vaunt, Grand Sworn** · human · f · 44
`traits: Ascetic, BodyMastery, Abrasive`
childhood: fostered into a creed-house at nine; took the vow at nineteen, the ordinary age.
adult: twenty-five years under the helmet. Eats through a valve, sleeps in it, will die in it.
> Nobody in this House has seen her face and she speaks of it in the past tense, as a thing she used to have. She is the best body in the Company and knows the exact number of seconds she is good for. The vow is not sentiment to her, it is a *clause*, and she has twice refused to identify a dead friend because doing it would have required the helmet to come off in front of witnesses.

**Quartermaster Dolm Petrek** · human · m · 51
`traits: GreatMemory, AnnoyingVoice, Delicate`
childhood: an armoury clerk's son, in an armoury.
adult: issues everything the Company shoots, against a signed chit, no exceptions, ever.
> A nasal drone, a bad back, and a cage full of ordnance he treats as his own children. During the raid on the spur he stood at the counter with the alarm going and refused magazines to five armed hunters because the duty chit was unsigned, and they went out with what they had, and one of them did not come back. He is not sorry. He has the chit. It is filed.

**Wex Rhoda, "Clause Nine"** · Duros · m · 74
`traits: TooSmart, NaturalMood(Pessimist), Ascetic`
childhood: born in transit, as half his people are. Has never lived a full year on a surface.
adult: fifty years flying for the Company and has never been in the room when anyone was killed.
> Red-eyed, lipless, immaculately calm, and the reason is **ninth clause of his own contract**, negotiated at twenty-four — *the party of the second part shall not be present at execution* — which he will produce from his flight jacket and show you, unasked, whenever anyone is dying nearby. He has flown three hundred people to their deaths. He will tell you, patiently, that this is a different thing.

## THE BOARDING CREW OF THE *COUNTERSIGN*
*The ship that arrives with the paperwork already served. They come in through the wall, and the scope of works is nailed to the outside of the breach where the survivors can read it afterwards.*

**Bosun Vurgo Nakk** · Besalisk · m · 43
`traits: Abrasive, Brawler, GreatMemory`
childhood: eleven siblings, a cold world, and a father who ran a loading crew badly.
adult: sets the frame, blows the wall, and serves the notice. In that order, and the order is doctrine.
> Four arms and a wattled roar you hear through a hull: two on the breaching frame, one on the charge, and the fourth holding the served copy flat against your ship with a spike driven through it. He will not open a compartment that is not named in the annex — he has stood outside a door with his hand flat on it, listening to his own crew being killed in the next bay, because that bay was in scope and this one was not.

**Sszik Vhan** · Barabel · f · 38
`traits: Cannibal, Brawler, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: a forest world, dark, and a first hunt she describes as a meal because it was one.
adult: point of the breach. Signs Rider Four on every contract: *the mark arrives whole.*
> Black scales, a grin with too much in it, and she reads Rider Four aloud in front of everyone before she signs it, every single time, cheerfully, because she thinks the ritual helps. It has held on all but two contracts. The Company still gives her the point position, because on the other thirty-one it worked, and because nothing on a boarding ramp has ever slowed her down.

**Tobbet Grale, the Penalty** · human · m · 36
`traits: Masochist, SlowLearner, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: a farm, a beating culture, and no idea that either was unusual.
adult: **when the Company breaches a term, the client is owed a punishment. He takes it, for a percentage.**
> Broad, placid, wrongly scarred, and he *volunteers* — walks into the claims room rubbing his hands, asks what the injured party would like, and negotiates the percentage first. Everybody has agreed not to look directly at how much he wants this. The Company's breach rate has quietly risen since he started, and nobody has been able to prove why.

**Ruune Adlai** · human · f · 52
`traits: ShootingAccuracy(CarefulShooter), Ascetic, Abrasive`
childhood: a marksman father who counted her shots and charged her for the misses.
adult: nineteen contracts. Nineteen rounds fired. One barrel, one bag, one blanket.
> Thin, grey, eats standing, and she announces before every job — flatly, to the whole crew, so that nobody can claim afterwards that they did not know — that she will not fire on anyone who is not in the writ. Not for cover. Not for them. She has watched a boarding crew get cut apart across her sightline while the named man walked into her crosshair, and she took him, and she has never lost a night over it.

**Nekk Arda** · Arcona · m · 31
`traits: DrugDesire(ChemicalFascination), TooSmart, Delicate`
childhood: bright, tested, sent offworld young to a technical school he never finished.
adult: opens doors and locks for the crew. The best slicer the Company has.
> Hammer-headed, spindly, and his eyes have gone the colour of poured gold, which in his species means salt and means everyone can see it. He is worth four hunters at a sealed door and he has twice **amended a contract in the archive** for a handful of salt — the one sin the Contract cannot forgive — and the second one has not been found yet, and he has begun sweating in front of Hoxx Tenneb.

**Yenevva Poll** · human · f · 33
`traits: Kind, GreatMemory, Delicate`
childhood: a hospice family; she was taught to hold hands before she was taught to read.
adult: boarding medic. **The mark is the deliverable. The crew are not.**
> She steps over her own bleeding people to stabilise the man they came for, because that is what the contract says, and she apologises the entire way across the deck — by name, to each of them, remembering the names for years afterwards. She is the gentlest person on the ship and the crew cannot stand to be treated by her, and she knows exactly why, and she keeps doing it.

**Mirri Sett** · Togorian · f · 40
`traits: Abrasive, BodyMastery, Jealous`
childhood: a city, a technical guild, and a mother who built the solar arrays over three districts.
adult: keeps the *Countersign* flying. Itemises every repair. Bills the crew individually.
> Two metres of black fur in a machinist's harness, digitigrade, silent on a deck plate, and utterly contemptuous of the wandering males of her species and the romance people attach to them. She invoices Vurgo Nakk for the frame mounts he bends every raid, line by line, with a surcharge, and she has never been talked out of a single line — and she watches who gets paid before her, and remembers.

**Beggo Thrune** · human · m · 60
`traits: Ascetic, GreatMemory, Delicate`
childhood: unremarkable and he will confirm it if pressed, briefly, and then change the subject.
adult: twenty-nine years carrying and setting the boarding ramp. Has never fired a weapon.
> He is the ramp man. He sets it, he holds it, he pulls it in, and he is paid on the shooters' scale because the ramp is written into the scope of works and the scope of works is the scope of works. New hunters raise this about once a year. He regards the discussion as vulgar and will simply walk away from you mid-sentence, and everyone senior lets him.

**Ubo Tass** · Gran · m · 34
`traits: Jealous, AnnoyingVoice, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: a herd household of thirty, where being alone in a room was a punishment.
adult: second through the breach. Counts the crew, aloud, continuously, for the whole action.
> Three eyes on stalks and a bleating voice that carries over gunfire, calling the head-count through the entire boarding — *"six, six, six, five — FIVE"* — which the crew now navigate by and which the people being boarded hear coming down the corridor. He physically cannot bear being the only one of anything, and if the count reaches one he stops working entirely.

**Kessa Ryn Wode** · human · f · 22
`traits: Transhumanist, FastLearner, Beauty(Beautiful)`
childhood: a good family, an expensive face, and a decision at seventeen nobody in it understood.
adult: four bought pieces in four years, each one paid for by a named contract.
> She is beautiful and half of her is manufactured, and she names the parts after the marks that bought them — *"this hand is Serrat Doon, the eye is a woman called Ferrow"* — and she says it to people she is about to kill, conversationally, as an introduction. She is learning faster than anyone in the Company, she already knows which contract buys the spine, and she has started looking at people as prices.

## THE CLAIMS OFFICE
*Two rooms above the spur where the Company pays out. The Contract says everyone not named walks away untouched — so when they do not, someone in here writes a cheque. Blackstar is the only faction on this world that pays its victims, and it is not mercy, it is a liability.*

**Adjuster Ushet Kel Ba** · Ishi Tib · m · 55
`traits: TooSmart, Abrasive, Ascetic`
childhood: raised over water, in a trade where a wrong figure is a personal disgrace.
adult: settles collateral claims brought by the people Blackstar raided.
> A hooked beak, eyes out on stalks, and he conducts the entire hearing sitting chest-deep in a brine tub in the middle of the office because his hide splits in this air, and he is *not* embarrassed and will not have it mentioned. He will pay a farmer for a door, a wall, a dead animal, a spoiled well. He will not pay for the son, because the son picked up a weapon, and that made him a party.

**Underwriter Vessine Roal** · Muun · f · 63
`traits: TooSmart, Abrasive, Delicate`
childhood: examined, passed, and bored by it.
adult: prices contracts before they are offered. Her numbers are the reason the Company is solvent.
> Two metres of pale, gaunt precision who will quote your death to your face — itemised, with the escort discount, the terrain loading and the fee for taking you indoors — and she is never wrong by more than four percent. She is genuinely puzzled when people react badly to hearing it, and she has explained twice, patiently, that a Muun does not cheat.

**Kiv Torrun** · human · m · 34
`traits: SlowLearner, Gourmand, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: nothing happened to him. He is not withholding; nothing happened.
adult: indexes closed contracts by year, then by client, then by ending.
> He is the most boring man in the Company and the file room is his whole life: he has one anecdote, about a cabinet that would not shut, and he tells it at lunch, which he takes seriously and at length. He has processed the paperwork on somewhere over two thousand deaths and has never once thought about a single one of them, and that is not a pose — the thought has never arrived.

**Sub-Adjutant Nesh Hollo** · Quarren · m · 48
`traits: Psychopath, GreatMemory, CreepyBreathing`
childhood: deep water, a species that finds the surface trades distasteful, and a boy who left anyway.
adult: **service of notice.** He arrives before the raid, in person, with a copy, and asks you to sign for receipt.
> Face-tentacles under a rebreather hood that clicks wetly every second breath, and he knocks politely, three days out, and waits to be invited in. He remembers your children's names from the survey and asks after them while you read. He has never harmed anyone in his life and he is the single most frightening thing this Company sends, and he cannot understand why people cry at a delivery.

**Ippo Nuum** · Toydarian · m · 89
`traits: GreatMemory, Jealous, AnnoyingVoice`
childhood: a market world, a family of brokers, and no shame about any of it.
adult: holds the deposit on every live contract. Releases it only with both parties present.
> A snout, a wing-buzz that has irritated four Captains, and an absolutely unbudgeable rule: **both** parties, in the room, or the strongbox stays shut. He is proud beyond reason of being unpersuadable — his people cannot be leaned on and he brings it up constantly — and there are now three hundred and eleven deposits he cannot release, because one party to each is dead, and he can recite every one of them by amount.

**Adann Ferro, the Recoverer** · human · m · 41
`traits: Brawler, Abrasive, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: debt collection, from about eleven, for a father who was bad at it.
adult: when a contract is voided, he goes and takes the fee back off the **client**.
> Heavy-shouldered, broken-knuckled, in a good coat he keeps well, and he is the reason people pay Blackstar in advance without arguing. He has never failed a recovery. He is also quietly, corrosively bitter that thirty years of loyalty bought him the job of shaking down the Company's own customers, and he has begun saying so where hunters can hear him.
