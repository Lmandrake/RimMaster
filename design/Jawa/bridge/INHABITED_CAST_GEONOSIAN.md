# `Inhabited` — cast 02: the Geonosian Foundry Hive

_Authored 2026-08-20. Written against `INHABITED_CAST_HUTT.md` (format),
`INHABITED_SPECIES_TEXTURE.md` (species work-rules) and the 2026-08-17 ruling in
`FACTION_SPEC.md` on the hive's two outposts._

**Faith: `Meckgin`** — duty and caste as cosmology; work unfinished is the beginning of the
end of the world. Per `INHABITED_DESIGN.md` §5.9, that is the moral climate this cast must
show: ⭐ **people who cannot stop, and are not unhappy about it.**

> *Two outposts, one hive, one queen who would not leave. Every one of these is legally
> extinct.*

---

## 🔎 DECIDE's editorial pass, 2026-08-20

✅ **Accepted.** It clears the bar the first Hutt pass missed: these are people you would
recognise again, not people you would admire the writing of. The tone note held — the
faction most at risk of collapsing into grim industrial misery came back **funny**, and the
horror in it (the press, the seam, the plate) is unremarked rather than pointed at.

✅ **§5.9 satisfied without having been briefed on it.** The commission predates that rule,
and the cast embodies Meckgin anyway: a clerk filing to a dead company, a boy maintaining a
reception dock for employers who will never come, a picador keeping a post whose beasts died
thirty years ago.

⚠️ **ONE SHAPE REPEATS THREE TIMES — flagged, not cut.** *Ovv'gan* (quarterly reports to a
burnt orbital), *Little Poggur* (the welcome banner) and *Picador Ghhen* (the dead massif)
are the same structure: **a duty continued after its object is gone.** Three of twenty-five.
🔑 **Kept, because that structure IS this faction's faith** — it is the correct tic for
Meckgin and the wrong tic for anyone else. ⛔ **But it must not appear in any other cast**,
and if the hive is ever extended, no fourth.
*(`Antenna-keeper Rhud` looks like a fourth and is not — his shape is "does the work without
the belief", which is its own thing.)*

⭐ **The two that most justify the whole system:** *Ovv'gan* and *Poggur* interlock —
the boy believes the employers are returning **because the clerk files the reports, and you
do not file reports into nothing.** Neither entry explains the other. That is the
recurring-character effect working inside a single cast.
⭐ And **Old Umma** is the Geonosian–Enclave alliance (`ASHKARR_WORLD_DEFINITION.md` §12.5b)
rendered as one person: she speaks droid cant with a seventy-year-dead accent and calls free
droids by the company's serial prefixes, and not one of them has ever corrected her.

**Traits verified against RimSage 2026-08-20** — `Kind`, `VoidFascination`, `BodyMastery`,
`CreepyBreathing`, `AnnoyingVoice`, `Transhumanist`, `TorturedArtist` all real. No
`Pyromaniac`. Every hook is trait-backed.

## THE ORE SEAMS — the old company site

**Foreman Sk'rrikk** · Geonosian (overseer caste) · m · 51
`traits: Abrasive, Psychopath, GreatMemory`
childhood: hatched into the shift he still works, four hundred metres down.
adult: eighty-one drones under him and he has never once raised his voice.
> He will click his outer mandibles at you twice inside the first minute of meeting you — *work harder* — and he does not care that you are not his. He has clicked both sets four times in thirty years, remembers all four names, and recites them the way other people recite a good harvest.

**Clerk Ovv'gan** · Geonosian (drone) · m · 47
`traits: GreatMemory, TooSmart, Ascetic`
childhood: given the ledger at nine because his mandibles were too weak for the smelt floor.
adult: files a quarterly tonnage report to a company that dissolved ninety-one years ago.
> The address is a burnt orbital that no longer exists and he transmits to it on the fourth day of every quarter, on time, forever. He knows the company is gone. He will explain, patiently, that this is not relevant: *the seams are not finished, therefore the report is due.* He has never missed one.

**Ttun** · Geonosian (drone) · m · 43
`traits: SlowLearner, Ascetic, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: none recorded, by him or anyone.
adult: opens the number-four sluice, waits, closes the number-four sluice. Thirty-five years.
> There is genuinely nothing else in there. Visitors keep probing him for the hidden depth — the buried grief, the secret art — and come away shaken, because he is completely happy and completely empty and he thinks you are odd for asking. His single volunteered opinion, ever, was that the number-three sluice is worse.

**Gizzek Vor** · Geonosian (drone) · m · 38
`traits: Transhumanist, NaturalMood(Sanguine), FastLearner`
childhood: lost the right arm at eleven to an unguarded press, as was normal.
adult: lost the left at nineteen, both legs below the knee at thirty. Promoted twice for it.
> Four alloy sockets and a torso, walking on machine-shop legs he built better than the ones he was born with, and he is delighted. He asks — cheerfully, repeatedly, of anyone with a cutting torch — whether they'd take the remaining hand, because the socket is *so much faster.* Nobody has told him no in a way he accepted.

**Rrekk the Returned** · Geonosian (warrior) · m · 33
`traits: Brawler, Masochist, ShootingAccuracy(Trigger-happy)`
childhood: volunteered for the arena at fifteen, the one route up that a drone gets.
adult: survived it, bought passage offworld, saw four planets, and came back.
> Scarred to the plate and grinning about it, the only Geonosian on this rock who has stood under a foreign sun — and he came home to a dead mine on purpose and cannot explain why to anyone's satisfaction, including his own. He starts fights he has no interest in winning and thanks you afterward.

**Ssuura Bell** · Geonosian (drone) · f · 29
`traits: DrugDesire(ChemicalFascination), Gourmand, AnnoyingVoice`
childhood: found the fungus at eight in a flooded gallery and told nobody for a year.
adult: grows it, sells it, and is comfortably her own best customer.
> You smell her three corridors out and it is *wonderful* — a hot sweet euphoric reek that makes strangers involuntarily happy and then resentful about it. She talks at a pitch that peels enamel, never stops, and hands you a fistful of grey fungus while explaining that this is a gift and also that you owe her.

**Nurse-drone Ipp** · Geonosian (drone) · f · 56
`traits: Kind, Delicate, SlowLearner`
childhood: sorted the crushed from the salvageable before she was ten. Kept sorting.
adult: reattaches limbs badly, lovingly, and with total confidence.
> She hums while she works and her stitching is genuinely poor and every drone on the floor would rather have her than the machine. She weighs nothing, bruises if you grip her, and has carried men twice her mass up a ladder on adrenaline she pays for in bed for a week.

**Vekka of No Hive** · Geonosian (drone) · f · 40
`traits: NaturalMood(Depressive), GreatMemory, Delicate`
childhood: on a world the Empire sterilised. She is the only one who got off it.
adult: taken in here, given work, given quarters. Works the night gang alone by choice.
> She can smell that this queen is the wrong queen. Not hostile — *wrong*, the way a note is wrong — and she has lived inside that wrongness for eleven years and functions perfectly and sleeps four hours. Ask her about her own hive and she will tell you the name of every drone in it, in order, for as long as you let her.

**The Thing in Seam Nine** · Geonosian (drone) · m · 44
`traits: Cannibal, Brawler, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: ordinary. Genuinely, boringly ordinary.
adult: went down a collapsed seam at thirty-two and came up six years later.
> He works. He hits quota. He clicks correctly at his foreman and he has hurt no one since he came back up. He also will not stand in a lit room, eats standing in the dark with his back to the wall, and everyone knows exactly how he survived six years and nobody has ever said the word aloud.

**Attendant Qu'raa** · Geonosian (aristocrat) · f · 63
`traits: Ascetic, GreatMemory, Jealous`
childhood: selected at four for the chamber. Has not been outdoors since.
adult: grooms the queen, feeds the queen, and speaks in the queen's cadence without noticing.
> Pale as unfired clay from twenty years underground, wings gone chalky from disuse, and she is the closest thing this hive has to a mouth — when she says *there was too much left undone*, she is not quoting, she thinks she is saying it. She has had two rival attendants reassigned to the smelt floor on paperwork she personally filed.

**Little Poggur** · Geonosian (warrior) · m · 22
`traits: SlowLearner, NaturalMood(Sanguine), GreatMemory`
childhood: hatched eleven years after the company ships left.
adult: maintains the reception dock, the guest quarters and the welcome banner. Alone.
> He has never seen a human employer and is *certain* they are coming back next quarter, because Clerk Ovv'gan files the reports and you do not file reports into nothing. He has the whole arrival memorised — who stands where, what is said — and he has rehearsed it for eleven years and he will be so pleased to show you.

**Quartermaster Traxx** · Geonosian (overseer caste) · m · 58
`traits: Jealous, TooSmart, Gourmand`
childhood: counted crates before he could fly, and was very good at it.
adult: the ore that goes to the droids is weighed by him, and the weights are his own.
> He skims maybe four percent and has convinced himself it is a handling allowance, and he will produce the arithmetic unprompted, at length, to people who did not accuse him. He is enormous by drone standards, eats sitting down like an aristocrat, and is bitterly aware that no amount of fungus-fat will grow him wings.

**Picador Ghhen** · Geonosian (picador caste) · m · 67
`traits: AnnoyingVoice, GreatMemory, Ascetic`
childhood: handed a goad at six.
adult: kept the beasts. There have been no beasts for thirty years. He kept the post.
> He has one subject and it is a massif called Drenn that died before most of the hive hatched, and he will give you Drenn's feeding weights by season in a voice like a saw going through wet bone. He is not sad about it. He simply has nothing else and has never once suspected that this is a problem.

## THE PLATEAU — beside the Rust Cathedral

**Archduke Sun Vekk** · Geonosian (aristocrat) · m · 49
`traits: TooSmart, VoidFascination, Abrasive`
childhood: winged, high-caste, brilliant, and told so constantly.
adult: led the splinter onto the hottest ground on the planet to speak with a god.
> Two and a half metres of iridescent black chitin with a wingspan he opens *for emphasis* mid-sentence, and he is genuinely the smartest thing for a thousand kilometres. He has been nine days from contact with the Rakatan AI for nine years. He announces the revised date each Meckgin and the crowd cheers and he believes it every time.

**Oracle Nnimm** · Geonosian (drone) · f · 26
`traits: VoidFascination, TorturedArtist, NaturalMood(Depressive)`
childhood: touched a live conduit at seven and has been special ever since.
adult: the hive's only confirmed voice of the god. She has heard nothing.
> She goes rigid, the wings buzz to a flat drone, and for twenty minutes she speaks fluent structured *something* that is beautiful and rhythmic and has been analysed to death and means absolutely nothing. She knows. She has known since she was nine. Six hundred people arrange their lives around her and she cannot find the door out of it.

**Slagwright Ebb'ka** · Geonosian (drone) · f · 34
`traits: TorturedArtist, VoidFascination, Masochist`
childhood: apprenticed to the crucible; never left the heat.
adult: casts the god's face in cooling slag, at night, at the pour lip.
> She has made two thousand of them and destroyed two thousand of them, always within the hour, always by hand, always into the crucible she just took them from. Her forearms are a map of every one. When you finally see a finished one you will understand why she keeps going and also why she cannot stop breaking them.

**Communicant Zzir** · Geonosian (drone) · m · 31
`traits: Transhumanist, VoidFascination, Delicate`
childhood: unremarkable until he read a Rakatan glyph and could not put it down.
adult: replaced his own mandibles, twice, with alloy cut from the ruin.
> He cannot eat solid food any more and does not consider this a cost. The new mandibles click in tones no Geonosian throat makes, which he insists is the god's own supervisory tongue, and when he uses them near the old hive drones they go pale and back away, because whatever he is saying, some of it lands.

**Hollow Kess** · Geonosian (drone) · f · 37
`traits: Ascetic, BodyMastery, CreepyBreathing`
childhood: normal appetite, normal weight, normal everything.
adult: has not eaten in four years. Lives beside the fungus vats and breathes.
> Translucent, weightless, luminous where the plateau sun goes through the plate, and *unnaturally* well — she outworks men twice her mass and her heart rate does not move. She smells her meals now. She stands in the vat mist at dawn with her spiracles wide open making a sound like slow paper tearing, and it takes eleven minutes.

**Antenna-keeper Rhud** · Geonosian (drone) · m · 45
`traits: Ascetic, SlowLearner, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: nothing.
adult: maintains the array that the entire theological project depends on.
> He does not believe in the god. He has never believed in the god. He is not a dissident about it — the question simply does not interest him, whereas the solder does. He will talk about solder. He will keep talking about solder. He is the only reason anyone up here can transmit at all and no one has ever thanked him.

**Verifier Grask** · Geonosian (warrior) · m · 41
`traits: Psychopath, Masochist, Abrasive`
childhood: arena washout — survived, unpromoted, which is its own caste.
adult: tests every claim of communion, personally, on the hot plate.
> He puts his own palm down first, every time, for a count of five, so that no one can say the test is unfair — and then he is perfectly calm about your turn. Eleven false oracles confessed under him. Nnimm has never been tested and he has asked to test her every year for six years, in writing, politely.

**Proof-drone Ixxa** · Geonosian (drone) · f · 28
`traits: TooSmart, Abrasive, NaturalMood(Depressive)`
childhood: out-calculated her instructors at ten and was disliked for it at eleven.
adult: has a proof, four hundred pages, that the Rakatan AI is dead and has been for millennia.
> It is correct. It has been correct for three years. She has read it aloud at four consecutive Meckgins to a polite, cheering, entirely unlistening crowd, and she keeps rewriting it shorter, as though the problem were the length. She is unbearable at dinner and she is *right*.

**Old Umma** · Geonosian (drone) · f · 71
`traits: Kind, SlowLearner, GreatMemory`
childhood: worked the company floor beside droids who were property, same as her.
adult: meets the Enclave traders at the boundary stones with oil and very bad binary.
> She learned droid cant seventy years ago from units that have since been scrapped, so she speaks it with a dead accent, ungrammatically, and slowly — and the free droids come to the stones early to hear it. She calls them by the serial prefixes the company used. Not one of them has ever corrected her.

**Meckgin-master Bo'ol** · Geonosian (drone) · m · 39
`traits: NaturalMood(Sanguine), AnnoyingVoice, Gourmand`
childhood: put in charge of the festival at nineteen and never relinquished it.
adult: eleven days of mandatory celebration of the virtues of industry, and he is *thrilled.*
> He wakes the plateau with a horn he made himself and a shriek of pure joy that carries two kilometres, and the festival is a genuine, ridiculous, beautiful spectacle — slag fireworks, quota recitations, a parade of tools. He has extended it by one day every four years on his own authority. Nobody has stopped him. Nobody quite wants to.

**Hatchling Tikk** · Geonosian (drone) · m · 5
`traits: FastLearner, Delicate, VoidFascination`
childhood: is currently it. Hatched on the plateau, six hundred kilometres from the queen.
adult: —
> He is the first of his hive never to have smelled a queen, and he asks. He asks everyone, constantly, in the same four words — *what does she smell like* — and the adults give him answers that do not agree with each other and he collects and compares them like ore samples. He has started asking the ruin the same question.

**Deep-walker Onn** · Geonosian (warrior) · m · 36
`traits: GreatMemory, VoidFascination, CreepyBreathing`
childhood: strong, obedient, nothing special.
adult: went eight days into the Rakatan ruin alone and walked back out on the ninth.
> He remembers all of it. Perfectly, in order, and he has tried to tell it maybe two hundred times and gets four or five sentences in before the words come out as something that is not a language. His breathing changed down there and never changed back. Vekk keeps him close. Grask wants him tested. Nnimm will not be in the same room.
