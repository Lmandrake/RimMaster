**The three rules a Junker learns before his name.** ① **The shadow claims it.** A wreck
belongs to whoever's shadow touches it at first light — not first hand, first *shadow*.
② **Nothing leaves whole.** One fault is put into everything before it is sold, so the buyer
comes back; the man who puts it in is a licensed trade. ③ **You never ask what a man's plate
came off. You may ask what it weighs** — and asking the weight is the warmest thing one
Junker can say to another.

Their saint is **the Bolt That Missed**, and their miracle is the near-miss: not the man who
was spared, the *centimetre*. It is nailed to a post at Grave Nine and the ledger of misses
is kept in longhand.

## GRAVE NINE
*The wreck-field camp, in the scatter of something enormous. Nobody speaks aloud inside a hull that has not been declared dead; you talk in taps.*

**Fenzik Trawl** · Chadra-Fan · m · 29
`traits: TooSmart, AnnoyingVoice, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: a metre tall, in a species that is old at forty. Read the claim-code at nine.
adult: owns more of Grave Nine than the Scraplord and has stripped almost none of it.
> The smallest man on the field, four metres up on strapped stilts every dawn, screaming his claim across the scatter in a voice like a drill going into tin. He worked out that the code says *shadow*, not hand, and he has never once worked out that a claim you cannot strip is a debt — he holds eleven of them and is genuinely thrilled about it.

**Ossa Grell** · Snivvian · f · 44
`traits: GreatMemory, Abrasive, TorturedArtist`
childhood: carved her first tally at six and has not stopped carving since.
adult: keeps the ledger of the Bolt That Missed. Decides whose survival counts.
> Grey, jowled, permanently disgusted, with a knife and a plate of soft alloy always in her lap. She will interrogate you for an hour about the centimetre that spared you and then carve it in — but if you laughed while telling it she strikes it out, and there is no appeal, and two men have gone off the field over this.

**Weft** · human · f · 19
`traits: Brawler, CreepyBreathing, SlowLearner`
childhood: eight years alone in a sealed compartment. They cut her out at eleven.
adult: nobody's crew. Fed by everyone. Owns a spanner and a blanket.
> She breathes like something in a wall — she learned to do it quietly when quiet was survival and cannot unlearn it, so she is *audible* in a way you feel in your teeth. She has about forty words. She will not be given a weapon, because she goes hands and teeth immediately, and twice a year she climbs back into the compartment and stays there for a week.

**Hakko Dunn** · Klatooinian · m · 38
`traits: GreatMemory, Ascetic, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: born under an oath his great-grandfather swore to a kajidic that no longer exists.
adult: counts fasteners off the line. Still sends a tithe to an address that is rubble.
> He is not interesting and he has never tried to be. He counts, he sleeps, he sends a quarter of everything he earns to a dead Hutt house because the oath does not have an expiry clause, and he has no opinion about that either. He will halt a forty-man lift over a discrepancy of four bolts. He is always right, and it always costs a day.

**Kaad'ro Tenth-Breath** · Kel Dor · m · 55
`traits: VoidFascination, Ascetic, Delicate`
childhood: two hundred years of family in the upper atmosphere. He came down in a hull.
adult: cutter, day shift. Climbs the standing spine every single night.
> He cannot breathe your air and never forgets it — the mask, the goggles, the ninety-second margin if either cracks. Every night he free-climbs sixty metres of unsecured wreck to sit on the highest plate on the field and look straight up, and he has been told what a fall would do to him, and he goes up anyway, and he is the gentlest man in the camp about everything else.

**Illiwa Suum** · Nautolan · f · 33
`traits: Kind, NaturalMood(Depressive), Delicate`
childhood: raised where every feeling in the room arrived through the water before anyone spoke.
adult: pulls people out from under collapses. Best hands on the field.
> Her head-tentacles read emotion through moisture and there is no moisture here, so she is the only person in Grave Nine who has to *ask* how you are, out loud, like a stranger — and she asks, every time, in front of people, and hates it every time. She goes into unshored collapses she has been ordered out of, and one of them is going to close.

**Skorrsk the Tally** · Trandoshan · m · 41
`traits: GreatMemory, Abrasive, Jealous`
childhood: raised to earn jagannath points from the Scorekeeper by the quality of the hunt.
adult: has invented a system by which salvage scores. It is comprehensive. It is not accepted.
> Scaled, huge, and carrying a scratched slate with two thousand entries on it — plate by mass, depth of recovery, a multiplier for hazard. He knows perfectly well that his goddess counts kills and not cargo. He recites his total to new arrivals inside a minute, and if yours is higher by his own rules he goes very still and very quiet and does not let it go.

**Vagh Oro** · Feeorin · m · 214
`traits: Psychopath, BodyMastery, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: a century of it. His people count that as most of a boyhood.
adult: the Scraplord's collector. Has never been beaten and expects another eighty years of that.
> Two hundred and fourteen and getting *stronger* — that is what his species does, and he is delighted by it in the open, cheerful way a man is delighted by good weather. He remembers your name, asks after your knee, and breaks the arm because the arm was the arrangement. There is no cruelty in it anywhere, which is the part people cannot get past.

**Grunda Pell** · Ugnaught · f · 88
`traits: Ascetic, Abrasive, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: won her trade at twenty, tusks against a cousin, in the proper way.
adult: six years down the same shaft, working it beautifully.
> Four feet of ferocious pride in a shaft that everybody else in camp knows is a sewage tender. Telling an Ugnaught her work is worthless is an insult you do not survive socially, so nobody has, for six years, and the lie has grown into a full camp custom with its own polite noises. She comes up every evening filthy and satisfied and tells you what she found.

## THE LONG STRIP
*The stripping yard, where hulls are taken to bones. The cutting torches are tuned. Yes, tuned.*

**Vesh'kaa** · Kubaz · m · 36
`traits: TooSmart, CreepyBreathing, Gourmand`
childhood: informed on his own household at eleven and was paid, and remembers the amount.
adult: **last-fault man** — licensed to put the one flaw into everything the yard sells.
> Snouted, goggled, and audible from three metres because of how the snout works. He takes the yard's fee to break one part, and then a second fee from the buyer to be told *which* part, and considers this two honest trades rather than one dishonest one. He farms grubs in a flooded compartment and will offer you a handful in the middle of negotiating.

**Modd Ryel** · Bith · m · 50
`traits: TorturedArtist, Abrasive, GreatMemory`
childhood: nine years of formal music and one audition, failed, never mentioned.
adult: sets the cut-line. Has tuned all eleven torches to a chord.
> Bulb-headed, huge black eyes, hands like a surgeon's — and the eleven torches on the Long Strip are tuned, because he tuned them, and the yard's whole shift sounds like something. He will stop ninety people mid-cut because number six has gone flat. He has been told what an hour costs. He does not accept that this is the relevant question.

**Grutha Bakk** · Gamorrean · f · 45
`traits: GreatMemory, Brawler, Ascetic`
childhood: her brothers fought each other for sport while she learned the rota.
adult: runs the Long Strip. Her husband is listed as running the Long Strip.
> She holds the entire yard — every shift, every crew, every debt, no slate anywhere — and her husband stands at the gate in good armour agreeing with things. She is not being oppressed and she is not being clever; this is simply how the work is divided, and the first outsider who congratulated her on secretly being in charge got put through a bulkhead for it.

**Kessavor** · Gand · m · unknown
`traits: TooSmart, Abrasive, CreepyBreathing`
childhood: nameless, like all of them. Earned a surname at a hull nobody else could find.
adult: earned the given name too, and then the judgement. **He is permitted to say "I."**
> He will tell you this. He will tell you within ninety seconds, in that dry gas-through-chitin voice, and he will use the pronoun deliberately, and enjoy it. What he does not tell you is that a bad enough failure means giving it all back — that shame reduces a Gand's name by his own hand — and he has been slightly wrong about two finds this season and he can feel the floor under him.

**Bohrra Zeel** · Lasat · m · 62
`traits: Kind, Ascetic, Brawler`
childhood: taught the old warrior-code by people who by then had almost nobody left to teach.
adult: carries the injured out. Sings over every hull before the first cut.
> Eight feet of purple and grey who will not let a torch touch a ship until he has stood at the nose and sung the thing that is owed to something that carried people. It takes eleven minutes. It happens every time. Three foremen have tried to cut through it and all three were lifted off their feet and set down somewhere else, very gently, still talking.

**Nossik Ryl** · Mon Calamari · m · 58
`traits: TooSmart, NaturalMood(Depressive), Delicate`
childhood: apprenticed to men who *grew* ships rather than built them.
adult: the best pair of eyes on the Strip, employed exclusively to take ships apart.
> He can name every hull that comes in — yard, year, who flew her, what the scarring means — and his entire job is to say where to cut. Once a season he petitions the Scraplord to be allowed to restore one, any one, on his own time, and once a season he is refused, and he has filed nineteen of them and keeps the refusals.

**Perra Tolm** · human · f · 51
`traits: SlowLearner, AnnoyingVoice, Ascetic`
childhood: unremarkable. She would agree.
adult: twenty-two years on the same cut-line. Second-best welder in the yard.
> She takes her break at the same minute of every shift, eats the same thing, and has exactly one opinion, which is about gloves, and which she will deliver to you in a voice like a bandsaw hitting a nail. She has never wanted to be first-best and finds the ambition faintly embarrassing in others. She has outlasted six foremen without noticing.

**Ketris Vahn** · Iridonian · m · 34
`traits: Masochist, BodyMastery, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: first facial marking at fourteen, for something he refuses to describe as brave.
adult: **tests the shore.** Stands under it first so ninety others do not have to.
> Horned, and inked from hairline to collar — every mark on his face is a thing that fell on him and stopped, and he is the single most decorated near-miss in Ossa Grell's ledger. He walks into the unshored bay grinning, and the grin is not courage, it is *appetite*, and everybody has quietly agreed not to look at that too closely because they need him to keep doing it.

**Prah Sook** · Weequay · m · 47
`traits: Cannibal, Kind, CreepyBreathing`
childhood: spoke to his clan in scent and to outsiders in a name he made up for them.
adult: processes the dead. Nothing is wasted in the warrens — not the plate, not the meat.
> Leathery, unhurried, and the kindest presence on the Long Strip: he remembers your mother's health, he gives away his water, he sits with the frightened. He also cuts up the people who did not come out, because the Weight says so, and he sees no contradiction anywhere in that and would be baffled to be asked. He breathes like a bellows with a hole in it.

## THE SIXTEEN HOOKS
*The salvage market — a rail with sixteen hooks. The sixteenth is always empty, for the saint. Bribes are legal here, and weighed: a bribe must physically outweigh what it buys.*

**Rute Baan** · Neimoidian · m · 44
`traits: TooSmart, Delicate, GreatMemory`
childhood: hive-raised in a grub pit, fed by rank, and has never got the fear out.
adult: **the Weigher.** Holds the scales on every bribe taken at the Hooks.
> Green, wattled, immaculate, and absolutely incorruptible in a job that consists entirely of corruption — the scale is honest because a false weight would eventually be found and finding is death and he cannot make himself risk it. He will not stand beside the arm during a heavy weighing in case something comes down. He audibly whimpers. He has never once been wrong.

**Atai Vosk** · Zygerrian · m · 52
`traits: Jealous, AnnoyingVoice, Ascetic`
childhood: raised on a world that measured a family by how many people it owned.
adult: freed all eleven of them, followed one here, and now works for her.
> He fetches, he carries, he sleeps outside her stall in a voice like a cat in a grinder announcing that he is fine out here, and Yenna Rill has told him to leave for nine years and cannot make him. He cannot bear anyone else running her errands and will elbow a child out of the way to do it. He has never once apologised, because apologising would be about him.

**Nib Gurro** · Sullustan · m · 30
`traits: GreatMemory, AnnoyingVoice, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: cave-born, with the perfect three-dimensional recall his whole species has.
adult: licensed market guide. The market is thirty metres across.
> Enormous eyes, jowls, and a genuine, unfakeable superpower: blindfold him, spin him, and he will walk you to any stall at the Hooks. There are thirty-one stalls. You can see all of them. He charges four credits, he has business cards he made himself, and he explains the service at length in a voice like a squeezed toy to people who are already standing at the stall they wanted.

**Nrrsh** · Defel · f · 39
`traits: GreatMemory, Jealous, CreepyBreathing`
childhood: sunlight is agony to her kind. She has never seen this world in daylight.
adult: trades from the ninth hook, after dark. Nobody has ever seen her face.
> In visible light she is a shadow with a voice in it, and she sees into the ultraviolet — which means she can read the scoured-off unit marks on any plate in the market and knows exactly whose body every piece came off. That is the one thing a Junker never asks. She has begun, in the last year, to mention it: quietly, accurately, to the wrong people, when she is passed over.

**Olo Venn** · Pantoran · m · 41
`traits: GreatMemory, Delicate, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: nothing happened. He got here on a barge and got off it.
adult: sells fasteners. Sorted by thread, then by length, then by alloy.
> Blue, thin, and bruises if you clap him on the back, which people learn once. He has four hundred trays and knows all of them, and if you describe a bolt he will produce it in under nine seconds. He has no story and does not pretend to. He is quietly, immovably proud of the sort, and if you disturb a tray he will re-do the whole rank before serving anyone else.

**Grumm Oda** · Aqualish · m · 91
`traits: GreatMemory, Abrasive, DrugDesire(ChemicalFascination)`
childhood: tusks, a bad temper, and a barge crew that took him because he could lift.
adult: **the man the Bolt missed.** The saint. The relic on the post is his.
> They wheel him out at the turn of the season and he is furious about it, because he remembers that morning with total clarity and it was not a miracle — he was drunk, he was asleep in the wrong bay, and the bolt went past his ear because he was lying down. He has said this in public for seventy years. It has been folded into the liturgy as the saint's humility. Leave a bottle in sight and you lose him for three days.

**Ziri Nask** · Twi'lek · f · 17
`traits: FastLearner, Abrasive, Beauty(Pretty)`
childhood: sold twice before she was nine, and remembers the price both times.
adult: runs the sixth hook for Yenna Rill and is learning the whole trade in about a year.
> Smiling, immaculately polite, agreeing with everything you say — while her lekku, six inches behind her head, are telling her boss precisely what you are and what your offer is worth, in a language you were never going to read. She is very good and she knows it. The mouth has started catching up with the lekku, out loud, in front of customers, and it is going to cost her the stall.
