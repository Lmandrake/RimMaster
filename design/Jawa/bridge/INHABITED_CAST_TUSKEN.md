# `Inhabited` — cast 10: the Deep Desert Tribes

_Authored 2026-08-20. **Faith: `the Sun-Debt`**, read from the shipped def at
`src/Jawa/Jawa_Patches/Patches/DeepDesertTribes.xml`, not the roster table:_
> *"The sun lends and the sand collects… A machine that pulls water out of the air is a
> thief standing between us and what we owe — **it does not steal the water, it steals the
> debt.** We take back what was drawn. We never take more than was drawn."*

Memes `Structure_Animist · NaturePrimacy · Raider · Technophobia · PainIsVirtue`. Precepts:
offworld tech abhorrent — **destroy it, do not use it** · every drop accounted · **prisoners: no**.
`permanentEnemy false` — hostile, convertible via adoption.

### 🔎 DECIDE's pass
✅ **Accepted.** The brief was *zealots who are not villains*, and it holds: raiding a farm is
**righteous** here, because the farm is stealing from the sky and the sky is owed.
⭐ **Two things fell out of the def that the agent found and DECIDE had not:**
**(1)** interest is paid in **walking** and in **cut marks**, so a raider drinks hard and
walks little ⇒ **warriors are the most indebted people in the tribe and herders are the
aristocracy.** The War Chief owes more than any living person.
**(2)** *prisoners: no* is not cruelty — it means there are exactly two outcomes on the
sand: **kin, or the ground.** Adoption is the alternative to killing, not mercy.
⚠️ Their neighbours the Homestead League are decent people who farm moisture, and for these
people that is literal sacrilege. **Neither faith has ever heard the other's version, and
both are right.** Nothing here is written as though the farmers are wrong.
Traits verified. No `Pyromaniac`.

---

**The four things a Tusken knows before he can walk.** ① **The sun lends; the sand
collects.** ② **We take back what was drawn — and never more.** A raid is an audit, and
somebody on it has the job of calling the halt. ③ **A vaporator does not steal the water. It
steals the DEBT.** ④ **There are no prisoners. There is kin, or there is the ground.**

## KHOR VAAL
*The camp, sited in the fixed shade of the east wall — on a world whose sun does not move, a shadow is a permanent address.*

**Ghossa Tal-Vaar** · Tusken · f · 71
`traits: Masochist, Ascetic, GreatMemory`
childhood: walked the Waterless at eleven with her mother's band and came back owing nothing.
adult: sixty years in credit, and has never once spent any of it on herself.
> Both arms from wrist to shoulder are somebody else's arrears — she buys a young raider's debt and cuts his marks into her own skin, and she holds the knife herself, and she is smiling under the wrappings the whole time. She has about four inches of unmarked hide left, she is seventy-one, and she will not close the waiting list.

**Bok'ur** · Tusken · m · 36
`traits: Gourmand, NaturalMood(Sanguine), SlowLearner`
childhood: the fattest uli-ah anyone could remember, in a camp that had nothing.
adult: butchers for Khor Vaal. Eats about a third of it.
> The sun lends water and *only* water — so meat is free, fat is free, marrow is free, and Bok'ur worked this out young and has lived inside the loophole ever since like a man in a house. He drinks his ration in one swallow, chews steadily for the rest of the day, is enormous in a camp of wire-thin people, and is genuinely unable to understand why anyone resents a man who has committed no sin at all.

**Ish'aal** · Tusken · f · 29
`traits: TorturedArtist, Delicate, GreatMemory`
childhood: held the bowl for the cutter before her from the age of six.
adult: cuts the debt-marks. Every mark in this camp under thirty years old is hers.
> A mark is a receipt — a number, cut plain, so the sand can read it — and hers are not: hers spiral, hers braid, hers run down a forearm like water going somewhere, and half the camp now waits three seasons for her hand rather than take a plain one. She has begun turning away work she finds ugly, which leaves men in arrears uncut, which under the law is far worse than anything she has been accused of, and she will not hear it.

**Ruum'ka** · Tusken · m · 58
`traits: VoidFascination, AnnoyingVoice, Abrasive`
childhood: told at seven that the sun is a lender, and asked, reasonably, what it looks like.
adult: blind at thirty-one. Still faces it.
> Somebody ought to look the creditor in the face — that was the entire argument, he won it, and it cost him both eyes — so he sits on the west stones with his ruined face turned up and *argues*, out loud, all day, in a cracked hacking bark that carries the length of the canyon. He has been losing this argument for twenty-seven years. He will tell you exactly what the sun said last, and he will not be interrupted.

**Tha'raan** · Tusken · m · 44
`traits: Ascetic, Kind, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: put with the obbak herd at nine because he was too slow for the band.
adult: has not stopped walking since. The herd gives milk only while it is moving.
> Sun-cracked to the exact colour of the ground and thinner than the animals, and he is one of the richest men alive by the only measure his people keep — thirty-five years of walking against a herder's ration. He has missed both his daughters' markings, his mother's cairn and every rite of the last two decades, because the herd does not keep the calendar and the camp does, and he will not hand it to anyone else, because he knows precisely what the trade was and he made it on purpose.

**Corrin Aleff** · human · m · 31
`traits: Kind, GreatMemory, NaturalMood(Depressive)`
childhood: born on a vaporator flat. Taken as uli-ah at nine, in a raid he can still recite the litre-count from.
adult: twenty-two years kin. Carries water, mends wrappings, is loved.
> He says *please* and *thank you*, out loud, in Basic, every single time — after twenty-two years it still stops a conversation dead, and the young ones now do it back to him as a joke he has decided to enjoy. He still thinks in litres instead of walks. He has never once been asked to raid his own district, and the whole camp has agreed to call that a kindness rather than what it is.

**Vaas'ka** · Tusken · f · 66
`traits: Ascetic, Abrasive, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: her band lost eleven in a season and took nobody in to replace them. That was a decision, and she made it.
adult: the camp's oldest voice on the count, and its most consistent.
> Every mouth added to a tribe is a debt added to a tribe — that is arithmetic, not malice — so she has not eaten at a fire with an adopted person in thirty years, addresses each of them with great politeness in the third person as *the guest*, and has never said one thing you could write down as an insult. Corrin Aleff has carried her water twice a day for eleven years. She thanks the air beside him.

**Dhakk Orr** · Tusken · m · 40
`traits: Cannibal, Brawler, NaturalMood(Depressive)`
childhood: crossed the Waterless at nineteen with four others.
adult: crossed it again at thirty-three with two others. Came back alone both times.
> A body is water and water belongs to the tribe, the law on this is explicit, and it absolved him twice — read the wording yourself, he keeps it cut into a plate at his belt and will hand it to you. He is enormous, he is quiet, he volunteers for every crossing there is, and Khor Vaal has stopped asking who is going with him.

**Ikri Nass** · Tusken · f · 27
`traits: Transhumanist, TooSmart, Jealous`
childhood: handed the breaking-hammer at fourteen, the honour that goes to the clever ones.
adult: eleven years destroying offworld tech. The best breaker the band has produced.
> Under the left wrapping is a droid's forearm, taken off a unit she was sent to smash and fitted to a stump she got honestly, and she has shown it to no elder and to six people under twenty. She is openly, embarrassingly envious of machines — their hands, their tolerances, never being thirsty — and says so at the fire, and the bundle under her bedroll is getting bigger.

## THE MOTHER-CISTERN
*Cut into rock under a false floor. The count is kept here, every drop of it, and no one who is not kin has ever been shown the way in.*

**Nashra Kul** · Tusken · f · 34
`traits: TooSmart, Abrasive, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: kept her band's draw-figures because she was the only one who could hold them all.
adult: keeps them for nine bands now, forty years back — and has added them up.
> A vaporator draws four hundred litres in a season and the raid takes back eleven hundred; she has the plates, the arithmetic is not in dispute, and by the tribe's own law that makes every band on this ridge a thief. She has said it to the War Chief. She says it in front of the young ones, in a flat furious bark, and nobody has yet worked out what to do about a heretic whose heresy is that they are not devout enough.

**Harra Ghul** · Tusken · f · 61
`traits: Ascetic, Jealous, GreatMemory`
childhood: her mother kept the cistern, and her mother's mother.
adult: forty years on the measure. Sets the rate at which arrears accrue.
> She has never drunk from the Mother-Cistern in her life — she takes her ration from the common cup at the back of the line, in front of everyone, and would die before being seen to do otherwise. She also cannot stand another pair of hands on the measure, has driven off three apprentices in nine years, and raised the interest on arrears in a drought year without consulting a soul, which is the reason Bharr Nu will die owing.

**Uli Sheek** · Tusken · m · 39
`traits: Kind, AnnoyingVoice, NaturalMood(Optimist)`
childhood: an uli-ah who never grew out of the word, which the camp thinks is funny and he does not mind.
adult: rides ahead of the raid, alone and unarmed, to give the farm its chance to pay.
> He arrives at the gate with the farm's own draw figures chalked on a slate and explains the arithmetic in eager, painstaking, terrible Basic through a mask, in a voice like a hinge being forced, and he has been shot four times doing it and fully expects a fifth. He knows perfectly well that his arriving costs the band its surprise. He is waiting for one of them to say yes, and he believes one of them will.

**Ghurr Vex** · Nikto · m · 47
`traits: TooSmart, Abrasive, Ascetic`
childhood: sold offworld twice before he was twenty and discusses neither.
adult: claimed on a raid at twenty-six. Forges every gaffi stick in three camps.
> He measures you the way a tailor does — reach, grip, how you stand when you are tired — and the weapon that comes back is yours alone and will still be yours in thirty years. He has also refused three warriors outright on the grounds of their posture, in public, at length, and will not be overruled by the War Chief or anybody else, and he and Torr'gan have not spoken since spring.

**Sheel Ivarr** · Iktotchi · f · 26
`traits: TooSmart, NaturalMood(Depressive), Delicate`
childhood: a Homestead warden's daughter, outriding between vaporator arrays at fourteen, warning farms that raiders were coming.
adult: claimed on the sand at nineteen. Same job, pointed the other way.
> Her people feel a thing coming about twenty minutes before it arrives and she has never found the switch for it, so now she stands at the cistern mouth and tells a Tusken band which flat will be undefended, and she is right, and she is very good at it, and she is twenty-six. Twice she has given the warning late enough to matter. Nobody has proved it, and she has not slept properly since the second time.

**Mekka Ru** · Tusken · f · 24
`traits: Ascetic, Kind, Delicate`
childhood: married at seventeen into a band that was in arrears before she got there.
adult: nursing. The rite grants her a double ration, and she has never once drunk it.
> At every measure, in front of the whole line, she pours half the granted portion into the sand at the cistern mouth — slowly, so that everybody can watch it go — and then carries the child away without a word. Half the camp calls it the most devout thing they have seen in a decade and half call it a performance aimed at their consciences, and both halves are right, and she is getting visibly thinner in public and will not be spoken to about it.

**Hurrum** · Tusken · m · 52
`traits: Ascetic, SlowLearner, GreatMemory`
childhood: nothing much, and he would say so before you did.
adult: taps the whole cistern lining with a bone mallet every cycle, listening for the note to change.
> Nineteen years; the note has changed twice; both times he was right and the tribe kept its water — and that is the entire content of the man, there is nothing else in there, and he is not hiding anything. He also refuses to certify the cistern after any raid that hurried him, on principle, and the last time it happened the camp went on half-measure for six days over a point of procedure nobody could argue with.

**Bharr Nu** · Tusken · m · 45
`traits: Jealous, Abrasive, GreatMemory`
childhood: unremarkable, and he will tell you so bitterly, as evidence.
adult: took four litres out of the Mother-Cistern eleven years ago and has paid interest on them ever since.
> Four hundred and six marks, and he will show you every one of them working up the arm in order, and he is nowhere near the end and you both know it — the debt outran him in year six. The tribe does not exile a thief, it *charges* him, so he eats at your fire and walks in your band and argues the terms of his own damnation at you for as long as you will stand still. The maddening part is that he is right about the rate. Harra Ghul did change it.

## THE SHORT WALK
*A raiding band, named for what it costs. You drink hard and you walk little — which is why the warriors of a water-worshipping people are the deepest in debt among them.*

**War Chief Torr'gan** · Tusken · m · 57
`traits: Brawler, Ascetic, GreatMemory`
childhood: first raid at twelve, on a flat that is now a wind-scoured slab.
adult: thirty-one years of raiding. Thirty-one years of drinking hard and walking little.
> He can read a farm's draw-plate from the saddle at a glance and call the litres to within twenty, and he is the deepest-indebted living person in the nine camps, and both facts are public. Ghossa Tal-Vaar has offered four times to take his arrears onto her arms; he has refused four times, because a chief who has been forgiven cannot make anybody else pay. He walks his interest out past the picket while the camp sleeps — forty laps of the ridge, at fifty-seven — and it is killing him.

**Ann'shu** · Tusken · f · 38
`traits: Psychopath, GreatMemory, Ascetic`
childhood: could hold the count at five, which is a thing they test for.
adult: **the Measure of the raid.** Calls the litres aloud as they load, and calls the halt.
> A flat bark, no hurry, counting containers off the line while the shooting is still going — and the moment the count matches the farm's draw she calls the halt and the band goes, and she has called it with three of her own bleeding on the ground behind her. There is nothing sadistic anywhere in her; she simply is not moved by any of it, and the tribe has correctly identified that as the best possible qualification for the job.

**Kuvv Raan** · Tusken · m · 33
`traits: TooSmart, Abrasive, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: took apart everything he was given, and was beaten for it until somebody worked out what he was for.
adult: breaks the machines. Nobody has ever broken them faster or enjoyed it more.
> He is *delighted* by vaporators — warmly, genuinely delighted, the way another man is delighted by a good animal — and he opens them at the seams while telling their owners, loudly, over the noise of the raid, exactly which three decisions the manufacturer got wrong. Twice now he has held the band on a burning flat well past the point of sense because he had not finished the sentence.

**Orr'gash** · Tusken · m · 49
`traits: Kind, GreatMemory, Abrasive`
childhood: raided the same flats his father raided, off the same list, in the same order.
adult: eleven raids on the Sooley place. The Sooley place is still standing.
> He takes what the machines drew and not one drop past it, so the house cistern goes untouched every single time — that is the law and he is scrupulous about the law — and the woman who farms it has taken to chalking the season's draw figure on the door for him, and in eleven years neither of them has spoken a word to the other. He will not go in on a farm with children on it. He has aborted two raids over it, in front of everyone, and said why.

**Shaa Nel** · Tusken · f · 30
`traits: ShootingAccuracy(CarefulShooter), Ascetic, Jealous`
childhood: given a cycler at fifteen and told what it was for, which was not people.
adult: eight years putting rounds through condensers at six hundred metres. No kills.
> Three districts of moisture farmers know her by the hole — one round, dead centre of the condenser, and the raid arrives forty minutes later — because killing a farmer recovers nothing, and what recovers nothing is waste, and waste is the sin. She has begun to want one anyway. She talks around it at the fire, in the abstract, more often than she used to, and every man in the band has noticed and none of them has said anything.

**Vurrk** · Tusken · m · 43
`traits: CreepyBreathing, Brawler, NaturalMood(Pessimist)`
childhood: mask crushed in a rockfall at sixteen and re-formed badly by someone doing their best.
adult: goes through the door first. Has done for twenty years.
> The intake whistles on the draw — a low note and a bad high one, audible at thirty metres in still air — and there are farm children on this ridge who were raised on that sound as a story and have since heard it in person. He could have it fixed in a week. He won't, because the sound does half his work and he knows it and says so cheerfully, and he can no longer go anywhere on this planet unannounced, including the places he would rather.

**Ess'kan** · Tusken · m · 35
`traits: Kind, Brawler, Abrasive`
childhood: raised by a woman who had been claimed off the sand, and was not told until she died.
adult: has claimed eleven people in nine years. There are no prisoners — there is kin, or there is the ground.
> He cannot put down a person who is standing still; the arm simply does not go — so he claims them instead, on the spot, out loud, in front of the band, and a claim cannot be undone by anyone including him. Four of the eleven have run. Two are the best people in the camp. Khor Vaal is now full of half-kin who fit nowhere, he is proud of every one of them, and he will do it again on the next raid.

**Tann'ru** · Tusken · m · 28
`traits: Ascetic, GreatMemory, NaturalMood(Sanguine)`
childhood: middle of a large family, middle at everything.
adult: ninety-one raids. Neither the best nor the worst man in the band at anything at all.
> He has come out exactly even on the count ninety-one times — never in arrears, never in credit, not once — which the camp treats as either a small miracle or proof he is fiddling the tally, and it is neither: he does not want anything and never has. He holds no view on the Sun-Debt and has never been asked for one. He is who the War Chief puts on the flank when the flank has to hold, everybody sleeps better for it, and nobody can describe him.
