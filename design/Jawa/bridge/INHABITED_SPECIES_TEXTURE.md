# `Inhabited` — species texture, for authoring working-class characters

_Research pass 2026-08-19. Source: Wookieepedia wikitext via the Fandom API. **(C)** current
canon, **(L)** Legends — nearly all working-life detail lives in Legends._

**What this file is for.** `INHABITED_DESIGN.md` rule 4 says *one line of friction can
encode an entire social order*. This file is where those lines come from. Every entry below
is chosen because it changes how a member of that species **does a job, takes a break,
talks to a foreman, or gets paid** — not because it is interesting trivia.

⚠️ **Use, do not copy.** Every character we write is original. This is the register to write
IN, and the constraints to write AGAINST.

🔑 **The authoring pattern:** take the species' work-rule, collide it with a mundane job, and
the character is already half-written. *A Chagrian who cannot taste, on a food line. A Gand
with no name yet, doing a job that requires him to sign for deliveries.*

---

## Ugnaught — the benchmark for hard labour
Heavy industry, smelting, droidsmithing, the unglamorous plumbing of infrastructure.
- 🔑 **The "blood profession".** Parents teach children the family trade. If too many young
  come up in one trade, a **blood duel** is called at twenty — historically to the death,
  tusks as the weapon, survivor inherits the *right to work* (L).
- **Taboo:** telling an Ugnaught their work malfunctioned is a direct personal insult (C).
- **Etiquette:** bow silently and wait for a guttural purr. Using the common galactic
  greeting gets tools thrown at you (L).
- **Lifespan 200+**, so a "young" hand may be sixty. Children are *Ugletts*.
- ⭐ **Their food grows next to the workshop** — moulds and fungi farmed in dark side-tunnels
  off the living quarters.
- Whole tribes were sold into slavery; the free ones **bought their way out**.
- The register in one line: *"I bought my freedom through the skill of my hands and the
  labor of three of your human lifetimes."*

## Geonosian — caste is birth and is absolute
Factory, foundry and architecture, kilometres underground.
- 🔴 **Mandible speech is literal supervision.** Clicking the outer mandibles twice means
  *work harder*; clicking both sets means *die* (C). **The foreman does not shout.**
- **Mutilation is normal, not tragic.** Foundry safety regulations *"were minimal, and many
  drone workers were killed, maimed, or crushed by exposed machinery"* (L).
- Conditioned to **loathe separation from the hive**; most *"were content to remain within
  their caste until they died."*
- The one route upward is **volunteering for the arena**; survivors buy passage offworld.
- **Drinking is smelling** — a fungus that produces a euphoric body odour.
- They celebrate **Meckgin**, a festival of the virtues of industry. ⭐ Already load-bearing
  in this campaign — see `the_forgotten_war.md` R-W4.
- ⚠️ Post-sterilisation the species is **considered extinct**, so any working Geonosian is a
  refugee, possibly the last of a hive.

## Ithorian — the Law of Life follows them to work
Agriculture, ecology, botany, herbalism, trade.
- 🔑 **The Law of Life governs the job:** *"For every plant that was destroyed in the
  harvest, two must be planted to replace it"* — and it extends to any life taken. **They
  will refuse work that nets a life**, and *"refused to touch things that came as a result
  of bloodshed."*
- **Etiquette:** it is rude to air a grievance with anyone except the person who caused it.
  **No venting to co-workers.**
- ⭐ **Their throats physically cannot produce Basic** — every on-shift conversation is
  mediated by a translator collar. Two mouths, deep stereo rumble.
- **Reflexes slower than average humanoids** (L): wrong for fast reaction work, right for
  patient work.
- Violent individuals are exiled from the homeworld (C). "Hammerhead" is offensive;
  "leatherneck" is a deliberate slur. Many find them dull.

## Rodian — clan is everything and clan is secret
Merchants, technicians, artists and **theatre** — every clan keeps a troupe (L).
- 🔑 **Revealing your clan risks a feud**, so a Rodian worker dodges "where are you from".
  Many offworld Rodians are illegal emigrants or feud refugees who cannot go home.
- **Cold-blooded** — useless on an unheated night shift.
- **Infrared vision** and vibration-sensing antennae: excellent in darkness.
- **Suction-cup fingertips** — superb climbers, but human-designed tools fit them badly.
- ⭐ **Their pheromone encodes identity, lineage and breeding.** A rival can smell exactly
  who you are. Humans find the smell revolting; some wear perfume to make humans comfortable.
- Lisp in Basic; struggle with r, l and s. Greeting: open palm across the chest.

## Gand — no name until it is earned
The species trade is **finding things** — *findsmen*, trackers.
- 🔴 **A Gand has no name and speaks of itself in the third person.** Unproven Gands are
  called "Gand". Accomplishment earns a surname; mastering a craft earns a given name; only
  ritual judgement earns the right to say **"I."**
- ⭐ **Shame causes voluntary name reduction as penance** — a worker who botched a job drops
  back to "this Gand". *This is the best single mechanic in the file for a workplace story.*
- Leaving the homeworld is mildly apostate: *"The Gand should not leave Gand. It is not the
  way."*
- A findsman's divination **takes at least three hours** and is not negotiable before
  starting a job.
- **Needs a fraction of the sleep humans do and can "store" sleep** to work a long job.
- Chitin shrugs off blaster fire; **can regrow a limb** (new plate stays chalky pale).
- One subspecies needs an **ammonia respirator** offworld; the lungless subspecies does not
  breathe at all, is immune to gas, and **speaks by modulating gas through the exoskeleton**.
- Alcohol makes them depressed.

## Chagrian — cannot taste, so does not break for lunch
Service, hospitality, law, bureaucracy, and heavy tidal labour.
- 🔴 **They cannot taste.** Saltwater destroys it by adulthood; the tongue is used for
  **smell**, flicked out to assess food or air. ⇒ meals are *"at best an inconvenience"*,
  they carry **nutrition capsules to minimise eating time**, and **celebratory feasts are
  unheard of.** ⭐ **A Chagrian takes no lunch break and does not drink with the crew.**
- **The horns are status** — males spend much of the daily routine filing and decorating
  their lethorns, with uncharacteristic vanity.
- **Rule-followers to a fault.** Will not falsify a form, will not look away from a
  violation, and generally admits guilt when accurately charged.
- Can stay submerged for **weeks**; home settlements flood on schedule, treated as
  unremarkably as sunset. Green sweat when hot. Born as 30 cm tadpoles in a sealed tub.
- Raised provided-for, they are **easily disturbed by visible poverty** and try to fix it.

## Selkath — the claw taboo
Medicine, healing, ocean stewardship, the **kolto** industry.
- 🔴 **Extending the venom claws is a public declaration of madness** — *"considered
  dishonorable and a sign of madness."*
- **Conversational reticence:** rude to approach someone showing no sign of wanting to
  speak. They wait to be signalled.
- **The neutrality reflex:** their world survived by strict enforced neutrality — punish
  whoever *started* it, not whoever was right.
- Habit: **stroking the cephalic lobes while talking**, the way a human strokes a moustache.
- ⭐ **They must stay moist.** Armour is fitted with **misting vents**; a Selkath working dry
  is visibly miserable.
- A species with the memory of being **needed, milked and dropped** when the kolto market
  died. Bounty hunting is a disgrace to them.

## Togorian — split by sex, sharply (L)
- **Males:** nomadic hunters, rarely leave the homeworld. Those who do are pirates,
  mercenaries, or **men searching for a missing mate**. Their mastercrafted scimitars fetch
  high prices offworld.
- **Females:** live in cities, **built the planet's technology**, expertise in solar energy;
  the engineers, traders and managers who deal with outsiders.
- ⭐ **A male visits his mate roughly one month a year** — and they are monogamous and
  devoted. An eleven-month absence is unremarkable.
- Status without privilege: the Margrave lives as any other nomad.
- *"Since the only offworlders they met were smugglers, pirates, or slavers, they considered
  most of them no better than rossorworms."*
- Retractable claws on hands *and* feet, digitigrade, dense bone, sibilant voice.
  *"I don't advise shooting a Togorian. It only makes them angry."*

## Muun — the exam you failed
Finance, law, accounting, actuarial, engineering. Even a labourer is a numbers person.
- **The contract is sacred:** *"Muuns'll take you for everything you've got, but they never
  cheat."* Letter of the law, habitually not the spirit.
- 🔴 **The caste is exam-gated.** *"Commoners rarely left the surface of their planet as
  upward mobility was limited, reserved for only the most gifted of children who passed
  rigorous examinations."* ⭐ **An ordinary Muun labourer offworld either failed those exams
  or ran from them, and that is a wound.**
- *"If there is anything the Muun detest more than wasteful spending it is nonconformity"* —
  visibly appalled by waste and by anyone who will not dress like everyone else.
- Near 2 m, gaunt, pale from never going outside, **last two fingers of each hand markedly
  shorter**, **three hearts** — two consciously controlled and used to raise blood flow in
  the cold. Nasal speech. Reputed cowards.
- ⚠️ The Empire **exempted them from anti-alien persecution** because it wanted their skill:
  a second-class citizen who is nonetheless not being hunted.

## Anzati — the day job IS the cover
- 🔴 **The real skill is never being remarkable.** Never the best at anything, never staying
  long enough for anyone to notice they do not age.
- ⭐ **Parents give children no name**, letting them *"seek names that best blended in with
  their chosen prey"* — an Anzat's name is camouflage matched to the population.
- **No pulse and no body heat.** Any medical scan or biometric reads wrong.
- Centuries of lifespan; puberty around 100. Many produce **one** book, one painting, one
  piece of music and never a second — perfecting a craft is pointless when everyone who
  could judge it died a thousand years ago.
- Known to the galaxy only as folklore, and as "snot vampires".

## Herglic — too big for the furniture
Trade, shipping, hyperspace scouting, heavy freight.
- 🔴 **Gambling is the defining cultural weakness** — an addiction to games of chance. **An
  ordinary Herglic's wages are at risk every payday** and dice are the assumed social medium.
- ⭐ **Size self-consciousness governs their bearing.** *"Typically a Herglic would take up
  two seats in a restaurant, and the majority of doorways required some maneuvering"*, and
  they *"tended to feel somewhat self-conscious and out of place when removed from their
  community or their ships."*
- **Speech marker:** they breathe through a **blowhole**, and the *"hauum"* of clearing it
  became a loan-word across a sector — **a Herglic clears the blowhole before saying
  something that matters.**
- Under occupation they fought, were slaughtered, then pragmatically surrendered before the
  infrastructure was destroyed. Some **bought their way into the nobility** when titles went
  on sale to non-humans, and the old nobility never let them forget it.

## Ortolan — will take a bad contract if the pay is food
Cooks and musicians, in that order. Suction-cupped digits **absorb food through the
fingertips** as well as play instruments.
- 🔴 *"Handicapped in their business affairs by their obsession with food, sometimes
  accepting otherwise unfavorable contracts when promised enough to eat."*
- ⭐ **Pushed out at age seven.** An Ortolan is expected to support itself and is forced out
  of the family unit unless it has a valuable talent; **gifted children are traded to other
  families** for specialised education. Apprenticeship is a transaction between households.
- **They are not naturally blue** — they dye their fur, a fashion begun when a trader tried
  to sell them food dye and they decided dyeing *food* was a waste.
- **Floppy ears are fat-storage organs**, painfully sensitive, hearing into the subsonic —
  ⭐ **industrial noise genuinely hurts them.**
- Smell locates food at two kilometres. Eating constantly maintains blubber against a frigid
  climate — an Ortolan denied food is burning reserves it built on purpose.
- **Their economy runs on barter**; credits are what you use with offworlders. The instinct
  is to trade goods, not name a price.
- Treated as a figure of fun: *"the little blue dope."*
- ✅ `RimMandrakeOrtolan` is v1, done and confirmed — owner's ruling 2026-08-15.

## Jawa — us
Scavenging, salvage, droid refurbishment, trade. **Patch it up just well enough to sell**,
and run a codependent circle with farmers who dislike them and have no better supplier.
- 🔑 **The trade philosophy, and it is genuinely good:** do not look for a use in a salvaged
  item — **imagine someone else who might find a use for it.**
- **Language is deliberately opaque.** Jawaese uses **scent as well as words**, and the odour
  encodes identity, health, clan lineage, last meal, maturity and mood. ⭐ **A Jawa can hold
  a full parallel conversation with kin in front of a customer**, in a simplified scent-free
  **Trade Talk** they let outsiders learn.
- **Structure:** each sandcrawler is led by a male Clan-Chief, but the clan is overseen by a
  female **Shaman** who does not travel — in an otherwise patriarchal society.
- **The annual swap meet** before storm season: all clans trade salvage, exchange updated
  navigation data for the shifting desert, and arrange marriages as "marriage merchandise".
- ⭐ **Robes are given when a child starts walking and worn for life**, hemmed to the armpit
  and let down as they grow — **a Jawa's height is counted in hems.** Five or six for an
  adult.
- Body temperature 46 °C. Bathing wastes water; robes are dipped in a moisture-retaining
  solution, and the smell attracts insects into the folds of the hood. Polished orange
  gemstones in the hood shield the eyes.
- ⭐ Most non-Jawas regard them as thieves — **a description most Jawas find pleasing.**

## What good looks like — calibration, from canon background figures

Not to be copied. Read for the SHAPE, per `INHABITED_DESIGN.md` §5.6.

| shape | example |
|---|---|
| **the job IS the character** | a man whose whole job is striking an anvil at fixed hours so a town of scrappers knows when to wake — and who does it the morning the police arrive, until the whole street takes up the rhythm |
| **the unremarked tragedy** | an old man one shift from the end of his sentence, who has a stroke at the bench; the doctor quietly ends him because nobody is coming |
| **the horror has a break room** | a prisoner tries the far door of the beast pen to escape; on the other side, the two keepers are play-fighting over their lunch |
| **the worthless detail** | a pickpocket everyone calls "*the* Muftak" because a drunk once mistook his name for his species and it stuck |
| **competence with no exit** | a mechanic trusted with vehicles, droids and subordinates, paid a real salary — out of which his owner deducts room and board so precisely that he can never quite buy his freedom |
| **the deal that kills** | a scout who trades away more than he owns for a talisman he believes will make him fearless, while the seller quietly lifts the power cell from his blaster |
| **grand, for no reason** | a navigator who computes hyperspace jumps in his head, and spends every single evening at the same table with the same four drinkers |
| **kindness, unexplained** | a night bartender who warns the two thieves living in the pipes below whenever the landlord starts talking about "the infestation" |

## Sourcing caveats, recorded honestly

- ⛔ **"No costumes, just clothes" has no locatable primary source and must not be
  attributed to John Mollo.** The sourced equivalent is Lucas's brief as reported: *"I don't
  want the audience to notice any of the costumes. I just want to see light versus dark."*
- ⛔ **Do not attribute "used universe" to Gary Kurtz** — no direct quote is locatable. The
  doctrine traces to Lucas and to Roger Christian.
- Nearly all working-life detail above is **Legends**, not current canon. For a fan campaign
  that is fine; it is flagged so nobody is surprised.
- The research ran against an exhausted WebSearch budget and worked by direct API fetch.
  Two seams remain unmined: **M. John Harrison's "great clomping foot of nerdism"** argument
  against worldbuilding and its rebuttals, and **ILM creature-shop interviews** on the
  funny/ugly/sad balance in the cantina.
