# Folk Gesture Micro-Mechanics — the four promoted

_The rule that governs all four: a gesture is a **convenience and a whisper,
never a system**. Each is one small comp or animation, costs nearly nothing,
moves satiation by at most a drip, and is legible without a tooltip. If any
grows a UI panel, it has failed._

## 1. Zizzik's decoy (build first — richest hook)

A 1x1 "decoy heap" building (scrap-cheap): a deliberately broken thing kept in
a room. **Mechanic:** a room containing a decoy passes a small fraction of its
breakdown rolls to the decoy instead (the wrong spark lands somewhere
harmless); consuming the decoy's condition. Feeds the slumber ledger as a
standing S-tier offering (devotional_sacrifice_catalog.md's register).
**Signature:** when the decoy "takes" a spark, it visibly chars and the
chartreuse flicker crosses the room once. Jawa build it by instinct; other
cultures see junk.

## 2. Ta'Baa's leaving-bag (build second)

A 1x1 bunk-side "leaving-bag" (linkable to a bed like end tables). **Mechanic:**
each made bag slightly slows Ta'Baa's rooted-erosion clock (packed = ready =
not truly rooted), and on any evacuation/launch order its owner gains a brief
move-speed bonus (the bag was never unpacked). Cap the clock relief so bags
never substitute for actually flying. **Signature:** violet running-light blink
across bags at every launch warning.

## 3. Sh'kaar's shade-line pause (build third — pure presence)

**Mechanic: none.** An animation-only behavior: a Jawa pawn crossing from
shade into direct sunlight hesitates for a fraction of a second at the
boundary. No stat change, no job interruption, no stacking with drafting
emergencies (skip while drafted or fleeing). This is the cheapest "the world
IS different" purchase in the whole layer: the player *sees* the taboo lived
a hundred times a day. Optional S-drip: crossing under an active Sh'kaar
front adds his tiny meter tick — TUNE, default off.

## 4. Mob'Unloo's set-it-down (build LAST — touches item-transfer jobs)

**Mechanic:** Jawa-to-Jawa direct item handoffs (where vanilla jobs pass
items hand to hand) route through a set-down: the giver places the item one
cell away, the taker picks it up. Implement as a job-step substitution ONLY
where it cannot deadlock hauling chains — feed/rescue/medical carry are
exempt (the body's needs outrank the ledger; canon-consistent: Oomo's nursing
overrides). If the exemption list grows past five cases, demote this gesture
back to lore — the ruling promotes it, but the anti-frustration law (F-spine)
outranks a gesture.

## Build placement

All four ride the Ninefold mod's M1 (gesture layer) except the decoy, which
can ship in M0 as a plain building with its comp stubbed to lore-only.
Quicktest plans per the build spec's protocol; FOUNDRY has the bridge.
