Ash'karr — plant cherrypick

190 plants that can actually appear on this planet, of 669 installed. Default is KEEP. Only what you mark CUT goes on the Cherry Picker kill list — this is a blacklist, so anything you never look at stays in the game.
🔴 Rules I invented to pre-fill this. Overrule any of them in one line.
  • R1 — a TREE in the core desert that does not read as desert flora is CUT. This is your complaint, and it turns out to be only three plants.
  • R2 — cacti, saguaro, agave, palms and scrub STAY. They are the Tatooine register; a desert with no succulents reads as a parking lot.
  • R3 — nothing is cut if it is the last wood in a biome. Hard constraint, overrules R1. Cutting a rung out of a progression is how a tech tree ends up with a gap nobody notices.
  • R4 — flora confined to an exotic biome is left UNDECIDED on purpose. The real question there is whether Mycotic Jungle or Poison Forest belong on Ash'karr at all, and that is a biome call, not a plant call.
  • R5 — non-tree groundcover STAYS. Cutting it makes the desert emptier, not drier.
⭐ The finding that shrinks this job. Of 51 trees, only five reach Desert / ExtremeDesert / AridShrubland — and two of those are the pebble cactus and the saguaro, which you want. So the whole of your complaint is three plants: Plant_TreeDrago, BMT_Plant_TreeTwistingThornwood and BMT_Plant_TreeMartyr. Cutting all three costs no wood at all — pebble cactus and saguaro both drop WoodLog and both survive. The other 46 trees only exist in biomes that are themselves exotic.

⚠️ The one place this could break something: AB_RockyCrags is the largest biome on the planet at 4,703 tiles, and every wood source in it is Alpha Biomes toxic flora. Those are marked LAST WOOD and pre-set to keep.
