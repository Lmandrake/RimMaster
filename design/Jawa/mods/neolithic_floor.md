## 2. 🔴 The ruling: four of those seven come back

**Un-cut `Bow_Recurve`, `Bow_Great`, `Pila` and `MeleeWeapon_Ikwa`.**

**The reason.** The owner's cut was aimed at *industrial guns* — *"I like the vanilla
weapons being gone, to amplify the Star Wars flavor"* — and a bow is not a blaster's
competitor. It is the floor he has now named in as many words. These four went out as
collateral of a sweep that read "vanilla weapon" without reading the tech level.

**What it costs to leave them cut**, read off Core's own
`Data/Core/Defs/ThingDefs_Misc/Weapons/RangedNeolithic.xml`:

| def | vanilla tags | who loses it |
|---|---|---|
| `Bow_Recurve` | `NeolithicRangedDecent` | **the only vanilla carrier.** `Tribal_Hunter`, `Tribal_HeavyArcher` |
| `Bow_Great` | `NeolithicRangedHeavy`, `NeolithicRangedChief` | **the only vanilla carrier of Chief** |
| `Pila` | `NeolithicRangedHeavy` | — |
| `MeleeWeapon_Ikwa` | (melee, short stabbing blade) | it is a **knife** by any reading of the ruling |

⚠️ **With them cut, `NeolithicRangedDecent` resolves to six defs and not one is a bow:**
`NerveSpiker`, `AG_ForsakenBow`, `BMT_ThrumbungusShroom`, `BMT_BlastSpore`,
`BS_GiantPrimitiveBow`, `BS_OgreThrowinRock`. A tribal hunter asking for a decent
neolithic ranged weapon is handed a mushroom or an ogre's rock. That is the defect, and
no amount of cheapening fixes it.

⛔ **`Bow_Great_Unique` stays cut** — an Odyssey quest-unique, not a floor weapon.
⛔ **`MA_VerdantBow` and `VWE_Throwing_Rocks` stay cut** — modded exotica and literal
rocks; the floor does not need them and both sit above or beside carriers we keep.

✅ **What this does NOT change.** It does not re-open the eight vanilla **industrial**
guns; they stay cut. It does not touch mid or high tier pricing. It adds no mod.

## 3. 🔴 The pricing lever is NOT `MarketValue`, and the item said otherwise

`WEAPON_FLOOR_BOWS_KNIVES_1` says *"Lowering a weapon's `MarketValue` … is the sanctioned
move."* **For the vanilla floor that instruction cannot be carried out**, and a patch
written to it would match nothing and report success.

Measured — every vanilla floor weapon is **stuffed** and declares **no `MarketValue`
statBase at all**:

| def | stuff | `costStuffCount` | `WorkToMake` | `MarketValue` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `MeleeWeapon_Club` | Metallic·Woody·Stony | 40 | 1200 | *none* |
| `MeleeWeapon_Knife` | Metallic | 30 | 1800 | *none* |
| `Bow_Short` | Woody | 30 | 2400 | *none* |
| `MeleeWeapon_Spear` | Metallic·Woody | 75 | 12000 | *none* |
| `Bow_Recurve` | (costList: 40 `WoodLog`) | — | 5000 | *none* |
| `Bow_Great` | Woody | 50 | 9000 | *none* |

⇒ **The levers are `costStuffCount` / `costList` and `WorkToMake`.** A stuffed weapon's
price is derived from what it is made of and the work in it; there is no scalar to
discount. `MeleeWeapon_Club` at 40 wood over 1200 work is already the cheapest thing on
the planet, and `Bow_Short` at 30 wood over 2400 work is the cheapest bow.

⚠️ **And the engine compares the STUFFED price.** `PawnWeaponGenerator` filters on
`ThingStuffPair.Price`, which includes the stuff — so a club in plasteel is not a floor
weapon, and the wood-stuffed pairing is what has to fit the budget.

## 4. 🔴 An instrument trap, worth more than the ruling

**In the def dump, a Cherry-Picked weapon reads as `weaponTags: []` and
`MarketValue: 0`.** It is not removed from the dump. The correlation is exact across all
seven cut defs and holds inversely across the 35 survivors, which read real tags and
`MarketValue: none`.

⇒ **Tag-emptiness in the dump is the signature of a CUT, not of an untagged weapon**, and
any audit that reads it the other way reports the cut list back as a pile of bugs. It
cost this ruling one wrong finding: `Bow_Short` read tagless, and it is not — it carries
`NeolithicRangedBasic` in Core, and it read empty only because the dump predates its
un-cut. The dump is `2026-08-21T22:44:59Z`; the config is `2026-08-22T22:21:15Z`.

🔑 **So the dump is stale for every weapon cut or un-cut on 2026-08-22**, and the roster
script says so in its first line rather than quietly answering.

## 5. What is still UNMEASURED

**The silver figures.** A stuffed weapon's price is computed at runtime from stuff and
work, so "what each should cost" cannot be finished off a def dump — and the item's own
pass condition is a live census, not a price table:

    spawn every affected kind 5x through the bridge, read equipment back with jawa/pawn_get

That is CHECK's after the load, against the existing victim list. ⛔ Nothing here should
be reported as fixing a bare-handed kind until that census runs.

⚠️ **Three kinds are not a pricing problem and must not be counted as one:**
`Jawa_Droid_Leader`, `Jawa_Droid_Specialist` and `Jawa_TradeMoot_Specialist` carry **no
`weaponTags` field at all**, and Droid Grunt/Heavy run `weaponMoney 0-0`. Those need a tag
and a range. No discount reaches them.
