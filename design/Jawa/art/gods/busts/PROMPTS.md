### Zizzik the Spark-Maker (`zizzik_bust.png`)
> A reverent square bust portrait of Zizzik the Spark-Maker, a Jawa god (predates
> all spaceships): hooded and robed, furry rodent-like body, two glowing
> pupil-less eyes slanted in gleeful, chaotic delight in hood-shadow, strange
> furry black five-fingered hands from deep sleeves. Wrong sparks arc from
> broken salvaged tech clutched and dangling in the god's strange hands,
> malfunction and misfortune given form. Desert / scavenged tech / sand
> crawlers / droids as fits; NO spaceships. Painterly dark religious
> iconography, chiaroscuro; ground palette rust/bone/indigo + brass accent,
> this god's accent sickly spark-green. Tight framing on eyes and hands, 1:1
> square.

Result: 1254x1254 raw -> 1254x1245 tight cutout, 67% opaque, generated in 122s.

### Ozzik the Shamed (`ozzik_bust.png`)
> A reverent square bust portrait of Ozzik the Shamed, a Jawa god (predates all
> spaceships): hooded and robed, furry rodent-like body, two glowing
> pupil-less eyes both proud and grieving in hood-shadow, strange furry black
> five-fingered hands from deep sleeves. The god wears a broken, salvaged
> crown of scavenged metal atop the hood, robe grand and ornate but visibly
> torn, ambition and pride curdled into grief, the trap of it showing. Desert
> / scavenged tech / sand crawlers / droids as fits; NO spaceships. Painterly
> dark religious iconography, chiaroscuro; ground palette rust/bone/indigo +
> brass accent, this god's accent faded royal purple and tarnished gold.
> Tight framing on eyes and hands, 1:1 square.

Result: 1254x1254 raw -> 1254x1219 tight cutout, 58% opaque, generated in 70s.
