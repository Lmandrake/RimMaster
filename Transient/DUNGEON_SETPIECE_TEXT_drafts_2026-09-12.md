- OLD (superseded): "Every light in camp feels slightly too bright. Something that prefers not to be seen is being made to watch."
- NEW [Narrator]: "I have been round the lamps twice, and they are all somehow too bright. There is a guest in the dark who dislikes being lit, and the short ones have gone and lit him."

### ② Ohm — pleased
- OLD (superseded): "The machines run a shade smoother than maintenance explains. The current is satisfied."
- NEW [Narrator]: "The machinery hums in tune tonight — rather better than the little ones' tinkering strictly accounts for. Someone in the walls is content, and I have no intention of disturbing him."

### ② Ohm — displeased
- OLD (superseded): "Relays stutter without cause and tools sit wrong in the hand. The current has noticed the neglect."
- NEW [Narrator]: "Three relays cleared their throats this evening, and every tool in the house lies where it fell. The current keeps accounts, in my experience, and it is not I who will be settling them."

### ③ Oomo — pleased
- OLD (superseded): "The water tastes better than it should. Whatever counts the family has finished counting, and the count pleased it."
- NEW [Narrator]: "The water has come over sweet again, which the pipes cannot explain and I shall not try to. The tally of the household has been taken, and for once nothing was missing from it."

### ③ Oomo — displeased
- OLD (superseded): "The cisterns sound hollow even when full. Something keeps recounting the family and arriving at the gaps."
- NEW [Narrator]: "The cisterns have taken to sounding hollow, though I have checked, and they are full. Something is counting the little family over and over, and stopping each time at the same empty place."

### ④ Mob'Unloo — pleased
- OLD (superseded): "Small windfalls keep arriving — a fair price, a found coin. Somewhere a ledger has balanced, and approves."
- NEW [Narrator]: "A found coin in the corridor; a bargain nobody quite deserved. Small mercies keep arriving unsigned. Somewhere in my house a ledger has closed balanced, and I have learned to bow when it does."

### ④ Mob'Unloo — displeased
- OLD (superseded): "Everything traded lately feels a fraction underpaid. Somewhere a ledger is open, and the colony is on the wrong page."
- NEW [Narrator]: "Every trade the short ones bring home lately arrives a little light. There is an open ledger in this house, and I regret to report the household currently stands on its debit page."

### ⑤ Rekko — pleased
- OLD (superseded): "Old things hold. Hinges that should complain don't. The past is being kept, and it keeps back."
- NEW [Narrator]: "The old hinges have gone quiet out of what I can only call courtesy. The past is being kept in this house, and it keeps its side of the bargain — the one tenant I have never had to chase."

### ⑤ Rekko — displeased
- OLD (superseded): "Dust settles faster than it used to. What is not mended is being mourned, and mourning has a weight."
- NEW [Narrator]: "I cannot keep ahead of the dust this week, and I have kept ahead of dust for a very long time. What sits broken in this house is being grieved over, and grief settles on everything."

### ⑥ Ta'Baa — pleased
- OLD (superseded): "The wind carries road-smell through camp. Everything packable feels lighter than yesterday."
- NEW [Narrator]: "There is road-smell in my corridors, which is impertinent of the wind but not unwelcome. Everything the little ones own has grown lighter overnight. They will dream of doors tonight, and the doors will approve."

### ⑥ Ta'Baa — displeased
- OLD (superseded): "The horizon looks farther than it is. Something that lives at thresholds finds every door here closed."
- NEW [Narrator]: "The horizon has removed itself to a rude distance. Something that lives on thresholds has tried every door in this house and found them shut — and it is not a caller one leaves standing."

### ⑦ Zizzik — pleased
- OLD (superseded): "Static crackles off blankets and small things fail amusingly. The spark is fed, and playing."
- NEW [Narrator]: "The blankets are biting people again, and the small appliances have begun failing in ways I can only describe as witty. I would restore order, but I gather the disorder is spoken for."

### ⑦ Zizzik — displeased
- OLD (superseded): "Nothing has gone wrong in days. The quiet has a coiled quality. The spark is saving up."
- NEW [Narrator]: "Nothing has broken in days, and I confess I do not care for it. The quiet in my halls has the shape of a held breath. Something is putting mischief by, the way misers put by coin."

### ⑧ Sh'kaar — pleased
- OLD (superseded): "The heat lies flat and patient, like a fed animal. The sun's attention is elsewhere. This is what its approval feels like."
- NEW [Narrator]: "The heat has laid itself down flat across the sand, in the manner of a beast that has already dined. The sun's regard is elsewhere today, and one accepts such courtesies without inquiring after the price."

### ⑧ Sh'kaar — displeased
- OLD (superseded): "Shade feels thinner. The glare finds gaps it didn't find last week. Something overhead is running its schedule a little faster."
- NEW [Narrator]: "The shade has grown thin, and the glare has learned its way through gaps it never used to trouble. Whatever keeps the schedule overhead has quickened it, and I would keep the short ones under a roof."

### ⑨ Ozzik — pleased
- OLD (superseded): "The camp's one fine thing catches the light and holds it. Old grandeur, briefly remembered, stands a little straighter."
- NEW [Narrator]: "The camp's one fine thing is holding the light rather grandly this evening. Old pride, remembered, does straighten the spine — I have seen it in better houses than this one, and said so less kindly."

### ⑨ Ozzik — displeased
- OLD (superseded): "Everything looks smaller than it did — the tents, the walls, the plans. A shamed thing is comparing, and the camp loses."
- NEW [Narrator]: "Everything has looked smaller today — the tents, the walls, the ambitions, the little ones themselves. Something ashamed is making comparisons in my house, and the household, I am sorry to say, comes off worst."

## B. Per-vault hand-finish set-piece prose — six Forsaken vaults

Two candidates per vault; strike one or both. Where a candidate carries both a
Game string and a Narrator string they are SEPARATE strings (three streams,
never mixed). Register guards observed: "the Forsaken" exonym only; Rakata
endonym only in their own mouths; no tyranny pre-reveal; no agency attributed
to the Cathedral; no droid-mercy explanation; no Force language. These sit
ALONGSIDE §3.10's accepted-for-now units, as the still-held per-vault
hand-finish layer — not replacements for them.

### V1 — tile 678, Rust Cathedral core, type ① (garrison held), set-piece: the intact garrison seen whole

**Candidate V1-a**
- [Game]: "The garrison is at full strength. The patrol pattern has not varied in recorded memory. The inner stores are sealed."
- [Narrator]: "Nothing here has broken, rusted, or doubted. The sentries walk lines drawn by mouths long out of the world, and walk them beautifully. I kept a house once to half this standard, and even I find it frightening."

**Candidate V1-b**
- [Narrator]: "This door has been locked since before the little ones' language had a word for door. The garrison behind it has kept every order it was ever given, and stands listening for the next one. The little ones whispered here, without agreeing to. Wise of them — attentive things live behind these walls."

### V2 — tile 4000, Scorch, type ① (outer works, launch site), set-piece: the contested launch ground

**Candidate V2-a**
- [Game]: "This is an outer works of the vault system: a launch site. Its defenses are powered. Salvage factions contest the surrounding ground."
- [Narrator]: "Everyone in the ash has an opinion about this vault, and nobody has a key. The Enclaves watch it the way heirs watch a will — and the will, I can report, is in no hurry to be read."

**Candidate V2-b**
- [Narrator]: "Ships left this ground once, in great numbers and a great hurry; the scorch of their leaving still stains a country's worth of sky. What did not leave was locked, and what was locked is still lit. The little ones know good salvage when they see it. So, regrettably, does everyone else in the ash."

### V3 — tile 9167, Fall Line, type ① (garrison, Imperial chokepoint), set-piece: two armies ignoring each other

**Candidate V3-a**
- [Game]: "The garrison is active. Imperial patrols operate within sight of the vault. Neither force acknowledges the other."
- [Narrator]: "Two armies share this ridge: one that marches past in fresh paint, and one that has stood post since the sand lay differently. They ignore one another magnificently. The little ones went between them like a whisper through an argument."

**Candidate V3-b**
- [Narrator]: "The Empire drills at the foot of a hill it does not understand, and the hill keeps a garrison that has never heard of the Empire. Old sentries, new flags — I have watched this exchange before, in my own corridors, and it ends when one side finally knocks. I would rather it were not the little ones who knock first."

### V4 — tile 17461, Deadstone, type ② (flesh loose), set-piece: the breach and the trail

**Candidate V4-a**
- [Game]: "The vault is breached from the inside. The interior holds no defenders. A trail leads away from the breach, toward warmer ground."
- [Narrator]: "The strongest door on this ground was opened from the wrong side, patiently, a long time ago. What left it wanted warmth, and went looking. The little ones studied the trail and pronounced it old. Old, I did not say aloud, is not the same as gone."

**Candidate V4-b**
- [Narrator]: "A vault is a promise that something will stay put. This one is the sound of a promise breaking — walls peeled outward, racks stripped by nothing with hands, a doorway wearing the shape of what walked through it. I would advise against following the trail. I note, for the record, that the little ones are already following the trail."

### V5 — tile 37, Slough, type ② (flesh loose, second breach), set-piece: the ground digesting the vault

**Candidate V5-a**
- [Game]: "The vault is breached from the inside. The breach is recent, relative to the others. The surrounding terrain is consuming the exposed structure."
- [Narrator]: "Here the ground itself has ambitions. It has taken the doors in a slow grip and begun, quite unhurried, to swallow. Whatever broke out did so lately, as these things are measured, and the ground was still hungry afterward. The little ones did not linger. I thought the better of them for it."

**Candidate V5-b**
- [Narrator]: "On the line between the burning day and the frozen night, someone buried a secret, and the secret dug its way out. The Slough is closing over the evidence at its own pace — a scavenger too, in its way, only patient past anything with a heartbeat. The little ones took what records the ground had not yet digested, and kept their boots moving."

### V6 — tile 20853, Umbra, type ③ (frozen Forsaken), set-piece: the casket hall hand-finish

**Candidate V6-a** (alternate casket-hall unit, alongside §3.10's accepted text)
- [Game]: "The hall contains occupied cryptosleep caskets. The occupants are alive."
- [Narrator]: "I served things like these once — I am nearly sure of it; the memory has frost on it. Rows of them now, asleep under glass, each dreaming a war that ended without asking them. The little ones stood among the caskets and did not chatter. Some rooms tell even strangers to lower their voices. This one has been saying it to the dark for a very long time."

**Candidate V6-b** (WAKE-moment hand-finish, built outward from the owner's ruled words, canon.yml `rakata.woken_brutality`)
- [Game]: "The caskets are opening. The Forsaken are waking. They are not grateful."
- [Speaker — a woken Rakata, own voice]: "Report. Year, front, who holds the sky. You — no. A *scavenger* answers me? [OWNER'S OWN →] And hey... isn't that a colonizer ship you're riding in?! What are you doing with that? [← OWNER'S OWN] Bring it. Bring the ship. It was never yours to ride."
- [Narrator]: "And there it was, the first question out of the ice: not thanks — title. I had rather hoped the sleep would improve them."
