Retracted. The “45 landforms we own and never use” line in section 03 was measuring the wrong thing. Geological Landforms keeps its 49 landforms as graphs with their own tile requirements and evaluates them at map generation; a GL_* feature is computed at display time and never enters the tile’s stored mutator list, so the read that scored them could not see them. The mod is loaded and operational — Loaded 49 landforms — and the lever that changes which ones occur is hilliness and elevation, which is what the pass above moved.
Three things the bridge documentation did not say, found by doing this. One: world_landmarks_set’s isValidTile is evaluated after the add, so it reports the landmark it just placed — it is not a gate and not a usable pre-check. Two: the landmark is Ruins while the mutator is AncientRuins; nine placements failed on the assumption that one name served both. Three: a landmark’s own mutatorChances rolls bypass any category guard you apply to your own writes — that put a dry-river landmark on a live river tile and displaced its River mutator. Six of the seven displacements were correct specialisations; that one was a defect, and it was repaired.
01

The planet as it stands

02

Findings

F1

Three tiles in five have nothing on themCritical

13,308 of 21,872 tiles carry no mutator, no landmark, no road, no river and no settlement. On a globe that emptiness reads as majesty — the doctrine asks for “vast unbroken dune sheets” and it got them. But the player never sees the globe. They see one tile, and on 61% of the planet that tile is a flat colour with a caption.

empty by biome — Desert 2959 · MycoticJungle 1731 · ExtremeDesert 1366 · Wasteland 1202
HorrorWastes 1176 · RockyCrags 1127 · PropaneLakes 602 · Ocean 574

Fix Emptiness should be a place, not an absence. The lightest-touch mutators — VEE_HardToTraverse, VEE_RagingWind, VEE_MoreSolarPower, VEE_SkygazingSpot — cost nothing, need no art, and turn a blank hex into a hex that does something when you camp on it.

F2

The two commonest features on a scavenger planet are “there is nothing to find”Critical

Of 13,819 mutator instances, 3,204 — 23% — are purely subtractive. VEE_MineralDevoid and VEE_DeepOreDevoid alone account for 3,067 of those, tied at 1,533/1,534, which is the fingerprint of a blanket pass rather than a decision. They rank third and fourth on the whole planet, behind only Caves and Dunes.

1534 VEE_DeepOreDevoid · 1533 VEE_MineralDevoid · 92 Fish_Decreased · 16 PlantLife_Decimated
15 Fertility_Reduced · 8 NoTrees · 6 AnimalLife_Decreased

Fix Scarcity is the premise, so keep it — but say it once and then say something else. Halve the devoid pass and spend the freed tiles on VEE_DeepOrePoor (a gradient, not a wall) and on the salvage register in section 03. A Jawa world should reward the search, not pre-announce its failure.

F3

Landmarks are sprinkled more evenly than random chance would place themCritical

579 landmarks; only 43 of them (7%) have another landmark next door. A blind sprinkle of the same 579 tiles gives 15% ± 5%. So the placement pass carried a minimum-separation rule, and the planet now reads as seeded rather than formed. Real terrain clusters: crater fields, cenote lines along one fracture, a dune sea, a canyon system.

landmarks 579 · with a landmark neighbour 43 (7%)
random baseline, 200 trials, same count: 15% ± 5% ← the map is MORE dispersed than chance

Fix Pick six or eight anchors and grow small families around them — five VEE_Cenotes in a line, a crater field of four, a run of VEE_SerpentineCanyons. Fewer places, each worth walking to.

F4

The Scald wears a rainbow collarClosed by ruling

The crater lake is ruled circular and that is fine. What is not fine is the 79-tile ring hugging it, which carries ten different biomes one tile deep, and a second ring behind it repeating the same ten. Nothing on a real planet transitions through ten ecologies in two tiles. This is the concentric bullseye the owner’s own defect list rules out, drawn in miniature.

first ring (79 tiles): Badlands 26 · FeraliskJungle 12 · OcularForest 10 · DesertOasis 7 · CypreJungle 7
Volcano 6 · Grasslands 4 · Pyroclastic 3 · LavaField 3 · Desert 1
second ring: the same ten, reshuffled

Ruled “Leave the scald area alone” — owner, 2026-08-26. F4 is closed, not deferred. The reasoning below stands as written; the decision is not a technical question. The Scald, its rings, Scald Spine and Hollow Verge are not to be repainted.

Was Choose two, not ten. A crater lake in a volcanic province wants a shore (oasis/badlands) and a rim (volcano/lava), with the exotics — ocular forest, gelatinous ground — pushed off to their own pockets a few tiles out where they read as anomalies rather than as a paint ring.

F5

The living roads are as straight as the dead oneMajor

Measured by turn angle at every through-tile: the Empire’s AncientAsphaltHighway runs 93% dead straight, which is correct — a surveyed imperial road should insult the terrain. The problem is that the tracks worn by people follow almost the same geometry.

AncientAsphaltHighway 30 through-tiles · 28 dead straight (93%) ← keep StoneRoad 405 through-tiles · 117 dead straight (29%) ← fix DirtRoad 416 through-tiles · 94 dead straight (23%) ← fix DirtPath 247 through-tiles · 54 dead straight (22%) ← fix

Fix Do not straighten anything — bend the organic roads. Push StoneRoad/DirtRoad/DirtPath toward 60° steps and let them detour round hills and dry lakes. The contrast then tells the story: the ruler-straight line is the dead Empire’s, everything crooked is alive.

F6

The Rust Cathedral is one clean lozengeMajor

Edge ruggedness — a patch’s real border length over the border a compact disc of the same area would have — separates terrain that formed from terrain that was painted. AB_MechanoidIntrusion scores 1.63: a single 236-tile blob, no islands, no fingers, no outliers, one biome edge to edge. Beside it, Desert scores 5.52 and RockyCrags 6.85.

smoothest large patches MechanoidIntrusion 1.63 (1 blob) · Lake 1.68 · Volcano 1.77 FungalForest 1.88 · CrystalCaverns 2.06 · DesertOasis 1.48 confetti at the other end AridShrubland 85 blobs / 42 single-tile islands ZBiome_Badlands 59/19 · FeraliskJungle 31/10 · PropaneLakes 32/8

Fix Both ends need the same treatment. Give the Cathedral outliers — a scatter of intrusion tiles trailing downwind, a corridor of approach — so it has an edge you cross rather than a border you step over. And absorb the 42 single-tile AridShrubland islands: one hex of a different ecology surrounded by six of another is noise, not a place.

F7

Fifteen named regions contain nothing at allMajor

A name is a promise. These regions have zero landmarks, and most have zero of everything else — they are coloured hexes with a caption over them. The worst offender is the one with the best name on the planet.

Lantern Deeps 57 tiles · 100% CrystalCaverns · 0% mutated · 0 landmarks · 0 roads · 0 settlements Sporefields 255 tiles · 67% MycoticJungle · 0% mutated · 0 landmarks Rimewall 338 tiles · 76% RockyCrags · 27% mutated · 0 landmarks Scald 312 tiles · 100% Lake · 0 landmarks Blight 140 · Sunward Scrub 154 · Level 141 · Knuckles 78 · Pan 79 · Wither 72 Fanground 70 · Pyrelands 63 · Thornend 61 · Salt Gate 16 · Damp 230 · Sweatwood 93

Fix Section 04 puts a specific thing in each. Three of them name the exact landform they lack: Sinkground has no sinkholes, Fanground has no alluvial fan, Salt Gate is the delta and holds no delta.

F8

Fish on a planet that is 6.4% waterMajor

697 tiles carry a Fish_* mutator and only 35 of them — 5% — are coastal by real adjacency. Some of that is legitimate river fishing. The rest is a mutator pass that never checked the ground.

Fish_* tiles by biome: Lake 222 · Ocean 202 · CypreJungle 136 · SeaIce 48 · MiasmicMangrove 30 AridShrubland 17 · Grasslands 12 · OcularForest 6 · Scarlands 3 genuinely coastal by World.CoastDirectionAt: 35 / 697 (5%)

Fix Strip Fish_Increased from every tile with no water adjacency and no river link. Then, because water is the planet’s whole political economy, the surviving fisheries become worth fighting over — which is the Deepwater Compact’s entire reason to exist.

F9

The planet has no memory of its waterMajor

335 river tiles, and essentially all of them sit in seven regions clustered on the Scald and the Dune Sea. Three of eleven river systems die inland without reaching a sea — that part is deliberate and good. What is missing is everywhere else: a world whose story is the water left has 51 dry-river tiles and two alluvial fans.

river tiles by region Dune Sea 99 · Scald Spine 73 · Dew Belt 40 · Dew Horn 26 · Fever Wood 22 Ashfall Range 14 · Hollow Verge 13 · Anvil 12 · Rust Cathedral 10 · Scald 8 dry-water evidence VEE_DryRiver 51 · VEE_AlluvialFan 2 · VEE_RelictDelta 0 · DryLake 24

Fix VEE_RelictDelta is placed nowhere and is legal on 117 tiles — it is the single most Ash’karr object in the mod stack and it has never been used. Run dry channels out of the Ashfall Range and the Dew Horn, fan them into VEE_AlluvialFan, terminate them in VEE_SaltPlains. The Grey Sea is documented as shrinking; it should be leaving a bathtub ring of relict shorelines behind it.

F10

The nightside is 2,051 tiles and 20 landmarksMajor

Deadstone is the largest named block on the planet and is doctrinally “where you go when you cannot be found” — the Forgotten Arsenal’s heartland. It has one settlement, no rivers, 24 road tiles and 28% mutation, most of it the devoid pass from F2. Hiding is only dramatic if there is something worth hiding with.

Deadstone 2051 tiles · 28% mutated · 20 landmarks · 1 settlement · 24 road tiles Umbra 802 tiles · 37% mutated · 9 landmarks · 1 settlement · 0 road tiles Ammonia Flats 818 tiles · 24% mutated · 32 landmarks · 1 settlement Sunreach 795 tiles · 29% mutated · 10 landmarks · 0 settlements · 0 road tiles

Fix The salvage register in section 03 — VEE_BombardedSurface, AncientToxVent, VEE_MineableComponentSpacer, AB_DerelictBioLab — is all unused, all legal there, and all reads as war leftovers. Cache the Arsenal in the dark.

F11

All 72 regions use one FeatureDefNote

Every named region on the planet is a WB_MapLabelFeature. All 36 real FeatureDefs — Sea, MountainRange, Island, Bay, Peninsula, Desert — are unused. Cosmetically the labels draw fine; structurally the engine does not know the Twilight Sea is a sea or that the Ashfall Range is a range, and the label typography that vanilla varies by feature class is flat across the whole globe.

features 72 · distinct FeatureDefs in use: 1 (WB_MapLabelFeature) · unused: 36

Fix Low cost, low risk, and it makes the map read as a map: retype the eight seas/lakes, the eight named ranges and the archipelagos onto their proper FeatureDefs and leave the rest.

F12

The one Tatooine object in the stack is on one tileNote

sw_Sarlacc — the sarlacc pit, the single most recognisable piece of desert-planet iconography anyone shipped for this game — is placed once. sw_SarlaccLair twice. sw_DeadSarlacc four times. Meanwhile WildTattooinePlants appears on three tiles of a 21,872-tile desert world.

sw_Sarlacc 1 · sw_SarlaccLair 2 · sw_DeadSarlaccCave 8 · sw_DeadSarlacc (landmark) 4 WildTattooinePlants 3 · WildDantooinePlants 4 · WildGalacticPlants 2 WildRylothPlants / WildNabooPlants / WildFelucianPlants / WildAlderaanPlants: 0 legal tiles here

Fix Tattooine flora should be the default dayside ground cover, not a curiosity. And a sarlacc is a landmark you build a story around: put three or four in the deep Dune Sea and Long Sand, far from roads, with a dead one on the approach as a warning.

05

What I would do first

Ordered by story returned per tile written. Every one of these is a world_mutators_set / world_landmarks_set pass followed by world_commit — no XML, no rebuild, no game restart.

  • The solar pass. VEE_MoreSolarPower on the dayside, VEE_LessSolarPower past the terminator. One sweep, zero art, and it is the first time the planet’s geometry changes how you play rather than what colour the hex is.
  • Water memory. Dry channels out of the Ashfall Range and Dew Horn, fanned into VEE_AlluvialFan, terminated in salt. VEE_RelictDelta at Salt Gate and Pale Flats. This is the finding I would fix even if we fixed nothing else — it is the difference between a dry planet and a planet that dried.
  • Un-straighten the living roads and leave the Imperial highway exactly as it is. Cheapest realism win on the board, and it converts a defect into a piece of lore.
  • Cut the devoid pass in half and spend the freed tiles on the salvage register.
  • Cluster, don’t sprinkle. Pick eight anchors, grow a family of three to six landmarks around each, and delete some of the loose singletons to pay for it.
  • Fill the five named regions that name their own missing feature — Sinkground, Fanground, Salt Gate, Lantern Deeps, Sporefields.
  • Sarlaccs and Tattooine flora. Twenty minutes, and it is the first time the planet looks like the film it is quoting.

Two things I deliberately did not flag as defects, because the doctrine asks for them and the map delivers: the huge unbroken dune sheets, and the three river systems that die inland without reaching a sea. Both are right.
