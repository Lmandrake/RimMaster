## The one-paragraph verdict

The product is close and the process is the long pole. In 15 days this repo produced
3,070 commits, a shipped world, a working live bridge, and a curated mod product — and
also 15,447 words of governance prose, a queue ledger committed 526 times, and a
process share of commits that is **rising** (54% whole-history → 61% last 14 days).
The system's failure mode is not laziness or chaos; it is that **every incident becomes
a permanent written rule, every rule dilutes the others, and rigor is priced by how
scary the incident felt rather than by how expensive the mistake is to undo.** The fix
is structural, not disciplinary: a one-page charter, rules-as-hooks, rigor tiered by
reversibility, two resident windows instead of four, and skill curation moved out of
end-of-context sessions. Your own `research/agentic_workflows.md` already argued most
of this; the evidence now supports acting on it.
