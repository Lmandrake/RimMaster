# Gravship Campaign Design Notes (Summary for CoWork)

## Goal

Create a RimWorld Odyssey gravship campaign that feels like a true
mobile expedition rather than a normal colony that happens to own a
spaceship.

The desired fantasy is closer to:

-   Firefly
-   Battlestar Galactica
-   Oregon Trail in space
-   Scientific expedition crossing an unexplored world

The central gameplay loop should be:

> Land → choose objectives → establish temporary camp → explore → gather
> resources → improve the ship → enemy pressure rises → abandon the site
> → launch → repeat.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Campaign Rules

-   Start with the vanilla Gravship scenario.
-   Begin with only a barely functional ship.
-   Enable the Pursuing Mechanoids mechanic (or equivalent constant
    pursuit).
-   Never establish a permanent planetary base.
-   Temporary camps are allowed but are disposable.
-   Entire permanent colony lives aboard the ship.
-   Crew remains small (roughly 4--8 pawns).
-   Every crew member has a meaningful ship role.
-   Fuel remains a strategic resource.
-   Always keep emergency launch fuel.
-   Stay on a map only briefly (roughly 10--15 days maximum unless story
    demands otherwise).
-   Ship expands only after major milestones.
-   Avoid giant static killboxes.
-   Storage remains limited.
-   Accept losses during emergency launches.
-   Campaign ends with a major destination or final objective rather
    than endless wandering.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Philosophy

Movement should solve problems.

If staying forever is always optimal, the gravship becomes irrelevant.

Every mechanic should reinforce:

-   mobility
-   scarcity
-   exploration
-   difficult logistical choices
-   deciding what gets left behind

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Desired Exploration Experience

The planet should contain recognizable destinations visible from orbit.

The player should routinely ask:

-   Should we investigate the ruined city?
-   Should we detour to the strange fungal forest?
-   Is the psychic landmark worth the fuel?
-   Can we salvage enough before the mechanoids arrive?

The ideal information level is:

"You know WHY a destination is interesting."

You should NOT know exactly what rewards are waiting.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Recommended Exploration Mods

## Essential

-   Vanilla Landmarks Expanded
    -   Named landmarks
    -   Distinct environmental identities
    -   Interesting navigation decisions
-   Smart Odyssey
    -   Shows tile mutators on world map
    -   Makes route planning meaningful
-   Geological Landforms
    -   Dramatically different terrain layouts
-   Biome Transitions
    -   Mixed-biome landing sites

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Strong Additions

-   Ancient Urban Ruins
    -   Multi-day city salvage expeditions
    -   Exploration instead of tiny Ancient Dangers
-   RimWorld Exploration Mode
    -   Fog of war for the world map
    -   Discover destinations gradually
-   Go Explore!
    -   Lost cities
    -   Signals
    -   Ship captures
    -   Expedition objectives

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Optional Flavor

Choose ONE depending on desired tone.

### Alpha Biomes

Pros

-   Alien ecosystems
-   Strange biology
-   Science-fiction exploration feel

Cons

-   More fantastical
-   Larger gameplay changes

### Real Ruins

Pros

-   Archaeology
-   Authentic abandoned colonies
-   Interesting salvage

Cons

-   Needs loot reduction for balance

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Overall Recommendation

Small, carefully curated support pack.

Avoid hundreds of content mods.

Prioritize:

-   exploration
-   navigation decisions
-   visible destinations
-   temporary expeditions
-   meaningful logistics
-   ship evolution
-   continual movement

Everything should reinforce the feeling that the gravship itself is
home.
