## Deferred to v2

### 1. The KLUDGED middle tier — the single biggest saving

v1 has **two** states, not three: salvage → the donor's working machine. The
owner's rule (*"salvage can ONLY be replaced by a fixed version of the same
machine"*) has no room for a middle rung.

Per machine, deferring it saves: one ThingDef, **12 images**, a hand-chosen
restricted recipe subset, a second build-over step, and a second set of power and
heat numbers to balance.

⚠️ **This is a bigger saving than dropping the art**, and it was not the obvious
one. Worth remembering when v2 is scoped: the middle tier is where the cost is.

### 2. Our own art for machines beyond the pilot

`DESIGN.md` §5 measures full VFE-Factory coverage at **~138–207 images**. v1
draws none of them.

A salvage ThingDef does not need original art: `texPath` is a **path reference**,
so a salvage def can point at the donor mod's own texture and be tinted dead
through `graphicData.color`. It reads as *the same machine, dead*, which is
exactly the fiction.

⚠️ **The smelter is the exception and its art stays in v1.** Those 12 images are
already drawn, conformed, validated and committed — dropping them saves nothing
today and throws away the only A/B comparison against the donor's own style.
Deferring art is about machines 2–17.

### 3. The techprint gate

`DESIGN.md` §2 and `ship_deck_plan.md`'s three-gate chain (techprint → prototype
→ research) both want an **earned** techprint as the true lock. No techprint def
exists. v1 ships research-gated only.

⚠️ Carries a known hole, stated so it is not mistaken for an oversight: Research
Reinvented lets a player prototype a thing and then stop researching, so research
alone is a softer lock than intended.

### 4. Per-machine research projects

The owner's phrasing — *"how to fix that machine… and the associated research
item it is part of"* — names two things where RimWorld gives one dial. v1 uses
the one dial: studying salvage credits the research project that gates its
repair, so *"this machine"* and *"the research it belongs to"* are the same
object.

Genuinely separate machine-specific know-how needs a small `ResearchProjectDef`
per machine, roughly doubling the defs per machine. Deferred, not rejected.

### 5. The duplicate-machine problem

`DESIGN.md` §1 accepts, for testing only, that the player sees both VFE's smelter
and ours in the build menu. **Not acceptable at ship.** v2 must decide: retexture,
or replace VFE's def, or accept the duplicate.

### 6. Detonation

`design/V2_DREAMS.md` §1's energy-density explosion feature. The governing constraint is
already ratified (*power density explodes, not category membership*; a wreck is
discharged and takes no comp), but the feature itself is not v1 and is not this
mod's to build.

### 7. SACRED SCRAP — the wreck cannot be cleared

> "They will just be rubble for now in our initial v1." — owner, 2026-08-12

Deferred with the owner's eyes open, because this is the `ship_deck_plan.md`
ruling of 2026-08-06 and the reason the mod exists. In v1 every tier is ordinary
deconstructible, claimable rubble. Nothing stops a player bulldozing a wreck.

⚠️ **It cannot come back on its own.** `deconstructible=false` is the exact
condition that makes the repair impossible — see `DESIGN.md` §4. Sacred scrap and
build-over must be re-solved **together**, or the first restores the fiction and
silently removes the game.

The likely v2 shape, recorded so the work is not re-derived: keep
`deconstructible=true` so the vanilla `replaceTags` path keeps working, and
suppress *clearing* by other means — no haul, no leavings, and a designator
restriction — rather than by the one flag that also breaks the repair.

### 8. Studying the wreck specifically

Deferred with §7, and for the same reason: it is fiction, not loop. v1 ships **no**
`SpecialResearchOpportunityDef`. Research is plain `VFE_BasicFactories`, reached
however the player normally reaches it.

⚠️ **The investigation behind it is done and must not be repeated** —
`DESIGN.md` §2 carries the verified mechanism, the exact def, the reason
`Analyse` is right and `AnalyseProductionFacility` is wrong, and the tuning
numbers. It is roughly a five-line def when it comes back. What is missing is a
game cycle, not knowledge.

### 9. The repair loop itself — `replaceTags`

Stood down 2026-08-12 with everything else. This was v1's one irreducible
mechanism for about four hours.

It is **built and its def shape is verified**: all three tiers share
`<replaceTags><li>WM_AutomatedSmelter</li></replaceTags>`, and Core's furniture
proves the match is `replaceTags`→`replaceTags` (the four chairs interchange
carrying no `buildingTags` at all). Same size, same rotatability, same
designation category — the conditions Core's chairs interchange under.

⚠️ **Never tested at runtime.** The def shape is proven; that a blueprint
actually places over an existing tier in game is not. That is the **first thing
v2 should check**, and it needs a load, not analysis.

⚠️ **Carries the §7 coupling.** Do not restore sacred scrap alongside this
without reading §7 — `deconstructible=false` silently kills exactly this.
