WHY THIS IS SHORTER THAN BRIEF_1_WRECKED.md
=====================================================================================

The .md brief is the human's specification. This file is what the model reads.
Three deliberate differences:

1. NO NEGATIONS. The brief says "NO lights, NOTHING glowing, nothing may extend
   beyond the outline". Image models condition on the tokens present, so
   "no glowing lights" reliably produces glowing lights. Every constraint here
   is phrased as a thing to DRAW: dead grey glass, cracked lenses, soot.

2. CONSTRAINTS FIRST, ~150 words total. The brief is ~900 words and buries
   "same size, same canvas" under prose about scavengers. Early tokens carry
   more weight and long prose gets sampled, not followed.

3. NOTHING HUMAN-FACING. No shell commands, no rationale about game tiles, no
   explanation of why the rule exists. The model cannot act on any of it and it
   dilutes what is left.

Keep the .md — it is the record of WHY. Paste this.
