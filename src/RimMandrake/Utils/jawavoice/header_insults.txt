  Owner's call, 2026-08-12. Glosses were fluent English, which put a paragraph

  REDUPLICATION IS THE GRAMMAR, NOT A TIC
  =======================================
  Three of the Grade A canon phrases are reduplicative: Togo togu, Taa baa,
  M'um m'aloo. The corpus does it in 38.8% of lines: bab bab, mihsha mihshu,
  toobba toobbu, m'aahhat m'aahhau, rah rau. The pattern is a word repeated with
  its final vowel changed, usually a to u.

  This file treats that as REAL MORPHOLOGY: reduplication is an INTENSIFIER.

      sh'akka sh'akku   priced, and priced again  ->  "priced twice over"
      heeka heeku       stinks, and stinks        ->  "reeks"
      gaha gahu         sand, and sand            ->  "even the sand"

  It is the highest value change here, because a player who notices the doubling
  learns something true about the language rather than just hearing noise.

  WHAT MAKES A JAWA INSULT FUNNY
  ==============================
  Not anatomy and not parentage. A Jawa's contempt is COMMERCIAL, and the comedy
  is the mismatch: enormous mercantile outrage from a very small creature who
  would flee an actual fight. Four running jokes carry the file.

    * THE APPRAISAL. The worst thing about you is your resale value. "You were
      free and I still feel that I overpaid."
    * THE MOTIVATOR. R5-D4's bad motivator is the most famous Jawa joke in Star
      Wars. "Your motivator is bad" is a complete insult on its own.
    * THE SMELL. Jawa reek and know it, so "You reek, and I am a Jawa" is a real
      escalation, which is exactly why it lands.
    * UTINNI. The cry you give on finding something good. "Nobody in this desert
      has ever shouted that over you" is the cruellest line in the file.

  The Slights are the same voice with the volume off: the thing muttered while
  walking away. They run shorter, and several are a single shrug.
