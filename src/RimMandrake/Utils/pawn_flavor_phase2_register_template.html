The brief. PAWN_FLAVOR_PHASE2_PROSE_1 — lore-prose pass over every ThoughtDef, MentalBreakDef and XenotypeDef in the COMMON tier (403 + 22 + 72 = 497 rows) and the OCCASIONAL tier (1,262 + 11 + 13 = 1,286 rows) — 1,783 rows total, everything reachable (routinely or occasionally) in play on the live 585-mod set per infrastructure/output/pawn_flavor_phase2_census.csv. DORMANT stays OUT — essentially unreachable on this mod set, future work if ever. Every row already carries drafted Star-Wars/Jawa-scavenger replacement prose, current vs proposed side by side — you only disagree. Decisions: approve (ship the draft as-is) · tweak (right idea, wrong wording — say the fix in the note) · reject (redo later, not this pass) · skip (not reviewed — ships as drafted, same as approve, but distinguishable from an active approval).
Field shape varies by def type: ThoughtDef/XenotypeDef carry label+description. MentalBreakDef's actual prose lives on the linked MentalStateDef (label, the letter that announces it, the recovery message) — several entries have no linked MentalStateDef and carry only a label.
Posture: default is APPROVE — a row you never touch ships the drafted prose. Nothing is stripped by silence; this is a draft awaiting sign-off, not a whitelist. The tier filter below tells COMMON rows (first pass, reviewed once already) from OCCASIONAL rows (added in the extension pass, less-encountered content) if you want to focus your remaining time on one or the other.
How the drafts were made (overturn in one line): COMMON's 497 rows split across 9 parallel drafting passes (7 ThoughtDef batches by mod, 2 XenotypeDef batches) plus 22 MentalBreakDef rows hand-drafted against the real MentalStateDef fields via RimSage; 15 rows that came back too close to the original vanilla wording were rewritten by hand afterward. OCCASIONAL's 1,286 rows followed the same style guide at a tighter pass: 1,262 ThoughtDef rows split across 12 parallel drafting batches grouped by mod/theme, plus 11 MentalBreakDef + 13 XenotypeDef rows hand-drafted directly against the real MentalStateDef/XenotypeDef XML (RimSage where indexed, live mod XML on disk where not — several third-party mods, e.g. Alpha Memes and Vanilla Ideology Expanded, aren't in RimSage's index at all). Style guide throughout: desert scavenger register, mechanical meaning and mood direction preserved exactly, first-person present-tense for thoughts, Star Wars proper nouns used sparingly. Xenotype entries are re-voiced as the Jawa clan's own practical bestiary entries, not Wookieepedia paraphrase — species facts kept, framing changed.
