**Governing principle:** Adopt mods that change the *interface* freely (UI, search, smart hauling); gate mods that change the *limits* (capacity, automation). On a gravship the binding constraint is the ship's FIXED FLOOR AREA, not per-tile density.

**Anti-exponential principle (2026-08-02):** The gravship and its onboard industrial system are the campaign's ONLY scalable progression trees. Every other RimWorld advancement system (psycasts, genetics, ideology specialists, royal permits, mechanitors, transhumanism) is deliberately held to fixed identity / singular quest-earned exception / new-vulnerability, NOT a repeatable optimization economy. See `forbidden_mods.md` for the excluded player-progression systems and the 7-question evaluation test.

## ⭐ INDUSTRIAL CORE — the central campaign system (2026-08-02)

The gravship *carries* automated production. Colonists are explorers, soldiers, medics, researchers, negotiators, engineers — NOT full-time production specialists. The ship performs routine industrial labor, so the crew is dependent on power, cooling, conveyors, factory rooms, machine integrity, and replacement parts. Losing a production line should feel like losing a ship *organ*, not a workbench. Factory floor area directly competes with cabins, storage, life support, defenses, fuel, and future hull expansion.

### Vanilla Furniture Expanded - Factory — ✅ ACCEPTED, CENTRAL MOD ✔️VERIFIED 2026-08-02
- **Deps VERIFIED (About.xml):** Harmony (`brrainz.harmony`) + Vanilla Expanded Framework (`OskarPotocki.VanillaFactionsExpanded.Core`). packageId `VanillaExpanded.VFEFactory`. Supported version 1.6. **No Royalty dependency.** Authors: Oskar Potocki, Sarg Bjornson, Taranchuk.
- **Two research tiers VERIFIED (ResearchProjects_Various.xml — matches doc exactly):** `VFE_BasicFactories` (5,000 rsch, prereq `Machining`, tab Industrial); `VFE_ComplexFactories` (6,000 rsch, prereqs `VFE_BasicFactories` + `Fabrication`).
- **Why:** Turns the ship into a production *system* rather than a rack of benches. Bill-based UI ("do until you have X" / "do forever"), overclocking up to 500% (↑heat, ↑power), conveyor belts with mergers/splitters/filters + underground routing. Explicit tradeoffs baked in: large footprints, high power draw, exponential heat needing ventilation/heatsinks. No Project RimFactory bloat (excluded — see forbidden_mods.md).
- **⭐ WHAT IT ACTUALLY PRODUCES — VERIFIED full building list (answers "what would we do with it?"):**
  - **BASIC tier (`VFE_BasicFactories`):** Conveyor Oven (kibble, pemmican, **survival meals**, mass-produced meals, baby food, simple soup) · Automated Smelter (**slag / broken weapons / apparel / stone chunks / mech corpses → metal** — the salvage-loop engine) · Automated Biofuel Refinery (wood/organics → **chemfuel**, chemfuel → **astrofuel**) · Automated Drill Platform (deep-drill stone + metals) · Masonry Saw (blocks) · Autoloom (patchleather, synthread) · Automated Mincer (corpses → mince) · Conveyor Crematorium (destroy corpses/apparel/weapons/drugs) · Ammunition Press (artillery shells: HE, incendiary, EMP, firefoam, smoke, tox, etc.) · Autofarmer (auto sow/harvest).
  - **COMPLEX tier (`VFE_ComplexFactories`):** Automated Assembler (**components, advanced components**, uranium refining, hexagel) · Neutroamine Synthesizer (fat + chemfuel → neutroamine) · Automated Alloy Forge (**plasteel, ⚠️GRAVLITE, obsidian, bronze, skysteel, stainless steel**) · Medicine Granulator (**medicine** + psychite/drugs/antibiotics/penoxycyline/wake-up) · Automated Machining Bay (weapons/armor/equipment; shreds mechanoids/drones) · Automated Distillery (booze).
  - **Verdict:** demand map for a nomad crew is genuinely rich — fuel (chemfuel/astrofuel), survival food (the inherited oven), salvage→metal (smelter), the components chokepoint, medicine, plasteel/gravlite, and artillery ammo for defense. The factory earns its central spot; it converts *exploration + salvage* into *ship*.
- **⚠️ DESIGN TENSION 1 — gravlite has TWO routes now:** the Alloy Forge's `VFEFactory_AlloyGravlite` recipe (Complex tier) makes gravlite independently of Odyssey's "Advanced Gravtech" research. So Stage III gating (see custom mod) MUST cover BOTH the Odyssey research AND this recipe, or the player bypasses the gate through the factory. Flagged for the exclusion-patch design.
- **Biofuel Refinery / two-tier fuel leash (resolved under VGE):** with **VGE adopted** (see GRAVSHIP LAYER section), astrofuel is **the intended flight/generator fuel, refined from chemfuel**. Verified from VGE source: recipe `VGE_Make_AstrofuelFromChemfuel` = **70 chemfuel → 35 astrofuel (2:1 loss), 5000 work, BiofuelRefinery, gated behind BasicGravtech research**; astrofuel marketValue 20 vs chemfuel ~2.3. There's also a `VanillaChemfuelExpandedPatch.xml`. The two mods are DESIGNED to cooperate; no def conflict, no patch needed. This meaningfully de-risks running VGE + Factory together. Full detail + collision analysis in context.md.
- **Restrictions/config:** All permanent factory equipment stays aboard the gravship — no industrial colony at a landing site. Overclocking is for emergencies/short surges, not routine. Output stockpiles stay small (carry *capacity*, not years of goods). Rare-material recipes (neutroamine, plasteel, gravlite, medicine, advanced components) must arrive LATE and stay feedstock-constrained so exploration stays necessary.
- **Recommended starting state:** ONE inherited, already-installed provisioning line — a conveyor oven + minimal hoppers/conveyors, enough to make durable travel food. Crew can *operate* it but cannot *replace* it (VFE_BasicFactories locked). Establishes dependence without granting a full economy. Harsher variant: start with an automated smelter instead (oven → survival dependence; smelter → salvage dependence). Don't start with both unless the deck is extremely constrained.

### Belt Extractors — VFE Factory Patch — ✅ ACCEPTED (user, 2026-08-06)
- **Workshop ID 3694555789.** Small companion patch to VFE-Factory. Lets a conveyor belt auto-pull items from an *adjacent* storage building (shelf/chest): place a belt with its input face against the storage and it feeds the belt with **no configuration**.
- **Why accepted:** on a gravship every tile is scarce. This collapses the usual "stockpile → hauler → hopper" chain into "shelf → belt," which is exactly the deck-density win we need for a cramped hull. It's *routing/logistics*, not a new production tree — trivially clears the 7-question test (no new combat ceiling, no second industrial tree; it only makes VFE-Factory's own belts more compact).
- **Deps:** VFE-Factory (already central) + Harmony/VEF (already in stack). 🔎 confirm 1.6 supportedVersions + exact packageId in RimSort at the machine.
- **Design note:** pairs with VFE-Factory's native underground-belt routing (belts travel short distances under walls/corridors into hoppers & shelves — confirmed from the mod's own Workshop copy). Together these two are the backbone of any dense factory-on-a-ship layout, letting production cross corridors without spending surface deck on belt.
- **✔️ VERIFIED 2026-08-06 (Fetcher `2026-08-06_vfe_factory_layout_text_and_stills`, Workshop pages fetched):** author GroovyTaco; 781 KB; **Mod, 1.6**; hard-requires VFE-Factory (matches). Mechanic exactly as described (belt input-face against storage → auto-extract, graphic/label change to "extractor mode"). **Factory hoppers are EXCLUDED** (they already have native belt integration) — so this only helps pull from *shelves/chests*, which is still the deck-density win. **⚠️ Two caveats from comments (unconfirmed by author):** (1) a reported bug where extraction silently stops after some in-game time until the belt is rebuilt; (2) no pull-rate limit — a belt on a "critical" bin can drain it until belts/hoppers fill. Neither is a blocker but both are watch-items at playtest. Also note base VFE-Factory *already* lets you face a belt at a chest (per a comment) — Belt Extractors extends this to generic RimWorld storage.

## ⭐ CUSTOM LOCAL MOD — "Gravship Expedition Progression" (LIKELY NOT NEEDED — reframed 2026-08-02)

> **⚠️ REFRAME (2026-08-02): we probably do NOT need this as a heavy mod.** User asked whether a custom mod is still required and wants to avoid it. Mapping the original 7-job spec onto the lightest tool for each job: 6 of 7 collapse into vanilla behavior, a Configurable-Techprints settings panel, or a trivial one-file `PatchOperation`. Only ONE job genuinely needs a heavy custom mod: **deterministic, story-authored milestone quests with guaranteed pacing** (nothing off-the-shelf provides that). Job map:
> 1. Gate factory/gravtech research → Configurable Techprints (settings). No custom.
> 2. Techprints out of trader stock → Configurable Techprints (settings). No custom. *(load-bearing unknown — see below)*
> 3. Cap hull expansion → **vanilla already does it** (gravcore quest-only + 6-extender cap). → Expansion Authorization = **DROP**.
> 4. Close gravlite double-route → one-file micro-patch (add `Advanced Gravtech` prereq to `VFEFactory_AlloyGravlite`), or accept leak.
> 5. Gate/cap biofuel refinery → micro-patch or config, or accepted reframe.
> 6. Inherited operate-only start line → in-game scenario editor + at most a light patch.
> 7. Authored deterministic quest chain → the ONLY heavy-mod justification. Keep the spec below as a FALLBACK if we judge story-paced quests worth the build.
>
> **Cost of config-only:** techprints come from existing/emergent quests, not authored ones → pacing at the mercy of quest RNG (could stall or arrive early). Thematically fine, mechanically less controlled.
> **Load-bearing unknown (Fetcher `2026-08-02_configurable_techprints_and_quest_unlock_alternatives` filed):** with trader purchase disabled, are techprints STILL obtainable as quest rewards, or genuinely unobtainable? If still available via quests → config-only works. If not → need a light hand-out mechanism (small mod or Microelectronics-Chip-Quest-style unlock pattern).

> _Spec below is preserved as the fallback design if authored quest pacing is chosen._

A standalone local compatibility mod (NOT edits to Core/DLC/Workshop files) that makes quest-gated progression deterministic. Loads LAST, after every mod it patches. **XML-first** (research-project patches, techprint requirements, custom non-tradeable ThingDefs, QuestScriptDefs with fixed rewards, construction-cost patches, scenario starting-things, `PatchOperationFindMod` conditional patches); add C# only if a required interaction can't be done safely in XML.

**Two gate types (both needed):**
- **Knowledge gates** — a quest-earned techprint/data core required before research can *begin* (stops research labor manufacturing advanced capability from nowhere).
- **Capacity gates** — a quest-earned physical "Expansion Authorization" *consumed* when a major ship expansion is built (stops a lucky cache/trader/haul from allowing unlimited physical growth once tech is known). Each grav-field extender = 1 Authorization + normal gravcore + materials. A gravcore found elsewhere can power other systems but CANNOT enlarge the ship without the quest-earned Authorization.
  - **✔️VERIFIED Odyssey mechanics (2026-08-02) — good news, vanilla already half-does our job:** Grav field extenders "increase the area in which substructure can be built" and how much substructure a gravship supports; **a gravship supports at most SIX extenders** (natural hard cap on hull growth). Extenders are unlocked by the **`Basic Gravtech`** research and each requires a **Gravcore** — and gravcores are "**only obtained through quests.**" So Odyssey *already* quest-gates hull expansion via gravcores. Our "Expansion Authorization" layer is still worth adding IF we want expansion gated even harder than the vanilla gravcore-quest cadence, but it's now an ENHANCEMENT, not a fix for a hole. Decision point noted in context.md: keep the extra Authorization layer, or lean on vanilla gravcore scarcity + the 6-extender cap?

## Storage (restrictably fun)

### ⏳ STORAGE FOOTPRINT CAP — ratio-based (concept accepted, exact value TBD)
- **Concept (2026-08-02):** Storage is capped as a **RATIO of usable ship deck area**, not a fixed tile count — so it scales with hull growth and the squeeze stays constant as the ship expands. Denser shelving then only makes the *limited* hold tidy/searchable, never bigger. This is the single knob that keeps LWM/RimFridge/Adaptive Storage honest.
- **Now competes with the factory:** every storage tile is a tile the conveyor lines, hoppers, machine rooms, and cooling can't use. That competition is intentional — it's what forces the "what do we leave behind before launch?" moment.
- **Exact value still TBD, but the Fetcher results sharpen it (2026-08-02):** the factory is hopper-fed, so storage now has TWO competing jobs — (a) **input feedstock buffers** the conveyors pull from (wood for the refinery, slag/scrap for the smelter, steel/plasteel for the assembler, herbal/neutroamine for medicine), and (b) small **output stockpiles**. Both compete with the machines themselves (large footprints per the verified building list) AND cabins/fuel/defense. So the cap should be read as "storage + hopper buffers ≤ X% of deck," not just finished goods. Leading candidate still ≈ ¼ of interior floor; alternatives = hard tile count or a quest-gated storage module. Tune in the Phase-A playtest now that we know the real machine footprints.

## ⭐ GRAVSHIP DEPARTURE — NO-CRATER ASSUMPTION (user, 2026-08-05)

The **vanilla intended counter is the Grav Anchor** — a buildable (research: Basic Gravtech) that marks the tile permanent so launch doesn't destroy it; the wiki frames the anchor as "just a cost for using the ship." It's the *world-tile* that's lost/cratered, not the local map footprint. The user's want (leave freely, no crater) is well-served.
- **✅ VERIFIED 1.6 ROUTES (pick per taste — all confirmed via the Fetcher search snippets):**
  - **Non-Destructive Gravlaunch** (WS `3522708183`) — the most complete: prevents the *deletion* of the colony/tile on launch without needing a grav anchor. ⭐ **top pick** for "leave freely, tile stays intact."
  - **No Gravship Launch Site World Map** (WS `3522619335`) — cosmetic-only: **suppresses the ugly crater graphic** but still destroys the map reference (you'd still need a grav anchor to keep the tile). Use only if you want the vanilla rule but no crater art.
  - **NoGravAnchorNeeded** (WS `3522601536`) — launches stop destroying colonies without an anchor; renders anchors decorative (keeps them for compat). Pairs with `GravAnchorBoostsLaunchQuality` to repurpose anchors.
  - **Ber97 Crater Decay** (Nexus mod `635`, requires Odyssey) — different philosophy: keeps vanilla craters but auto-restores the tile after a ~15-day decay timer. ⭐ **thematically interesting alt** — a departed tile heals over time rather than never (fits "the desert reclaims it"), while preserving a short-term consequence.
  - **Directly Abandon Settlement** (WS `2010863958` / GitHub `Jellypowered/DirectlyAbandonSettlement`) — adjacent utility: skips/clears persistent abandoned-tile markers with an optional 0–30 day cooldown. Complements any of the above for world-map tidiness.
- **Recommendation:** **Non-Destructive Gravlaunch (`3522708183`)** as the primary — it directly delivers "abandon without cratering." If you'd rather keep a *soft* consequence in-theme, **Ber97 Crater Decay** (scar heals over ~15 days) is the more flavorful pick.
