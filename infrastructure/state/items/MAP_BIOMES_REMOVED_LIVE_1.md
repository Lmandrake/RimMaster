⛔ **Do not "fix" this by editing the Cherry Picker list.** Its contents are right. The

### 🔴 CAUSE — CONFIRMED 2026-08-22 23:1x. It is OUR OWN generated patch.

`src\Jawa\Jawa_Patches\Patches\BiomeCast_Ashkarr.xml`, generated by
`design\Jawa\fauna\gen_cast_patch.py`, replaces `wildAnimals` on **26 BiomeDefs** —
the exact 26 that vanished — writing 746 records in this shape:

```xml
<li><animal>AA_Skyeel</animal><commonality>1.0</commonality></li>
```

`BiomeAnimalRecord.LoadDataFromXmlCustom` does
`commonality = ParseHelper.FromString<float>(xmlRoot.FirstChild.Value)`. It takes the
**node NAME** as the animal and the node's **TEXT** as the commonality. With an `<li>`
wrapper, `FirstChild` is the `<animal>` *element*, whose `.Value` is null →
`ArgumentNullException` → **RimWorld discards the whole BiomeDef.**

**Measured in this load's `Player.log`:**

- **22** × `Exception loading def from file Biomes_*.xml: System.ArgumentNullException`,
  with `at RimWorld.BiomeAnimalRecord.LoadDataFromXmlCustom` in the stack
  (`Biomes_Cold.xml`, `Biomes_WarmArid.xml`, `Biomes_NewArid.xml`, `Biomes_Volcano.xml`,
  `Biomes_Wasteland.xml`, `Biomes_PoisonForest.xml`, the ten Alpha Biomes files, the
  `ZBiome_*` files …)
- **26** × `Failed to find RimWorld.BiomeDef named <X>. There are 54 defs of this type loaded.`

⭐ **The correct form is documented in the SAME FOLDER and the generator ignored it.**
`src\Jawa\Jawa_Patches\Patches\Ikee_Rename.xml` lines 37-46 quote that exact
`LoadDataFromXmlCustom` body and state the rule outright: *"`<AA_Eyeling>N</AA_Eyeling>`
is the only form that loads. The `<li>` form … would take FirstChild of an element and
misparse."*

### 🔑 The remedy, and it is one generator

Emit `<PawnKindDefName>commonality</PawnKindDefName>`, not `<li><animal>…`:

```xml
<AA_Skyeel>1.0</AA_Skyeel>
```

⚠️ `wildAnimals` takes a **PawnKindDef**, not a ThingDef — `Ikee_Rename.xml` line 48
flags that several defNames exist as both. Fix `gen_cast_patch.py`, regenerate
`BiomeCast_Ashkarr.xml` from `cast_assignment.csv`, redeploy, reload.

### ⚠️ THIS IS THE SECOND GENERATOR WITH THE SAME DEFECT, ON THE SAME DAY

§5 F2 of `EXPECTED_FAILURES_next_load.md` tracked `SkillDef named li`, which was **101**
before today and **0** after: `cast_to_xml.py` had been emitting `<li><skill>…` into a
custom-loader field and RimWorld was discarding the defs. That was fixed and shipped
tonight. `gen_cast_patch.py` carries the identical bug against a different custom loader
and shipped in the same window. 🔑 **The lesson is not "fix this file" — it is that any
generator writing into a field with `LoadDataFromXmlCustom` must be checked against that
loader's body, because the failure discards the ENTIRE def and the patch itself reports
nothing.**

### Why it blocks v1

`world\ASHKARR_WORLDMAP_tiles.csv` is the frozen, hand-authored planet
(`WORLD_FROZEN_RETHINK_PLANET_1`). Stamping it through the bridge writes
`Tile.PrimaryBiome` per tile; a biome that does not resolve cannot be written and the
failure is **silent** — §12.4 of `ASHKARR_WORLD_DEFINITION.md` already requires the
importer to refuse loudly on a tile-count mismatch, but nothing checks the biome roster.

**3,037 against a baseline of 25**, almost all of it
`No RimWorld.BiomeDef named <one of these> found to give to RimWorld.AnimalBiomeRecord`,
and 99% of the 84 MB log is the candidate dump attached to those.
