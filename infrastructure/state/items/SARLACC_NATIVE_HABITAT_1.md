# SARLACC_NATIVE_HABITAT_1

🔴 **Owner ruling, 2026-09-05:** the sarlacc gets its own item — **three life-cycle stages**
and a **dungeon-like module** — and is deliberately kept OUT of the deep-desert biome sheet.

Its native habitat is **the deep desert** (`design/Jawa/worldbuilding/biomes/deep_desert.md`).

## The premise the biome hands it

The owner's framing, verbatim in intent: *"a massive deep reservoir of water permanently
grown in place like a cistern of living flesh."*

That makes the sarlacc **not a predator but a well — and the well is a mouth.** In a biome
where energy is free and water is money, it does not need to hunt, because everything must
eventually come to water. It does not digest for nutrition; over a thousand years it is
**wringing you out**.

Consequences the design should honour:

- **A sarlacc builds an ecosystem around itself.** It is the only reliable water for degrees
  of arc in any direction, so the local web exists because it exists.
- **Killing one is campaign-scale.** Hundreds of tons of water released — a Jawa clan that
  takes a sarlacc has struck a well — but the entire ecosystem that depended on it dies with
  it. That is a real choice, not a boss fight.
- It should read as **grown in place**, geological in age, and continuous with the ground —
  not as an animal that happens to be big.

## Owed

- The three life-cycle stages (undefined — this item's main design work).
- The dungeon module.
- How it interacts with the deep desert's rules: it is the one permanent surface water, and
