# GIDDYUP_KEEP_OR_CUT_1 — the mounted-animal ruling

Owner, 2026-09-12 (verbatim, AFK message): "These mods seem fun to ride Star
Wars animals no doubt. Yet they are a known bug confounder. They easily break
at least many years ago this was true. So including them is a risk. Now that
you see the scenario evaluate the pros and cons of including them for their
content and gameplay features. We shouldn't carry every complexity possible,
but if it's worth it and if the mod is now better written then sure let's
keep it."

## spec
Background analysis (BENCH, Fable agent), then owner cards:
1. Identify exactly which Giddy-Up mod(s)/lineage the stack carries
   (memegoddess.giddyup, memegoddess.runandgun — which rewrite, whose code).
2. Code-quality-now question: is the current lineage the old Roolo-era
   breaker or the Owlchemist rewrite line; what does its patch surface touch
   (rendering, jobs, pathing, caravans); read the source where shipped.
3. Evidence from OUR game: the two 2026-09-12 crashes were OUR cast data
   defects that Giddy-Up's eager all-biome cache SURFACED first (canary, not
   culprit) — weigh that honestly.
4. Gameplay value in THIS campaign: Jawas riding banthas/dewbacks/rontos/
   eopies; caravan speed on a desert world; mounted NPC raids/visitors.
5. Compat matrix risk against our heavy hitters (Vehicle Framework, CAI-5000,
   facial animation, large pawns, melee animation, Yayo's Combat).
6. Options with trades, card-style: keep as-is / keep with settings trimmed /
   cut. A recommendation, clearly argued.
