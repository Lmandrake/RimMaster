  `RSW_DW_Part_{Leg,Manipulator,Sensor,Motivator,Servo,PowerCell,Frame}_Primitive`.
  Mass/MaxHitPoints/MarketValue each set to 40% of the matching donor
  Standard part (`Parts_Droidworks.xml`), rounded to sane whole numbers —

- **Install effects, deliberately flat, not a 3-tier system**
  (`HediffDefs/Effects_Droidworks_Primitive.xml`,
  `RecipeDefs/PartRecipes_Droidworks_Primitive.xml`): **FOUNDRY's own call,
  option B of the two the item brief offered** — there is no principled
  "40% of a -0.15 offset" (the donor Standard tier IS zero, nothing
  positive to shrink), so Primitive parts drop the Inferior/Standard/
  Superior grading entirely. Each of the 5 new install RecipeDefs
  (`RSW_DW_Install*_Primitive`) points its `DroidPartEffectExtension`'s
  `inferior`/`standard`/`superior` fields all at the SAME def — one flat
  "grossly inferior" hediff regardless of the installed part's own
  `CompQuality` roll. Magnitude: **1.5x the matching donor Inferior tier**
  (e.g. Leg Moving offset -0.225 vs donor Inferior's -0.15) — worse than
  the worst a fine part can ever roll, which is what "below every donor
  module" has to mean once Standard/Superior are strictly better than
  Inferior. No new C# — reuses `Recipe_InstallDroidPartAtBench` verbatim.
  Wired onto `DW_Race_Base.recipes` (`Races_Base.xml`) alongside the fine
  tier's own 5 — universal, not gated to the Primitive chassis (ruling 2's
  "use superior or inferior parts... be Jawa" economy applies to any
  droid).
- **3 Primitive modules** (`ThingDefs/Modules_Droidworks_Primitive.xml` +
  one paired HediffDef in `Effects_Droidworks_Primitive.xml`): one per slot
  this mod's own donor absorption ships (Hardware/Software/Sensor),

## Assumptions recorded (nothing in the design doc gives a number for any of these)
- Family differentiator: `baseHealthScale 0.6` (fragility), `powerFallPerDay
  0.6` (crude power draw), `energyDensity 0` at the family level (neither
  chassis explodes by default — Junker overrides its own to 3).
- 40% scaling applies to item Mass/MaxHitPoints/MarketValue (the concrete,
  arithmetically-checkable "quality" axis); Flammability left unscaled
  (not a quality axis).
- Install-effect magnitude: 1.5x the donor Inferior tier, not a bigger
  multiplier — a droid that can barely stand is a worse story beat than
  one that would rather have had the fine part, not one broken enough it
  might as well have none.
- Quality-tier system bypassed for Primitive install effects (flat,
  always-worst), rather than scaling a 3-tier trio — there is no
  principled 40%-of-zero for the donor Standard baseline.
- Skill requirements: Crafting 1 for all Primitive parts/install recipes
  except the Frame (Crafting 2) and the 3 modules (Crafting 2) — "very
  primitive" work needs little expertise, but a frame/module still has
  real stakes.
- G2 `MoveSpeed 3.2`, Junker `MoveSpeed 1.8` — flavor values, not measured
  against anything.
- Junker `combatPower 20` (G2 mirrors the house `99999` utility-droid
  sentinel already used by DUM/FX7/MSE/SalvageAssist — not independently
  re-derived, just matched).
