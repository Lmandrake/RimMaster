Owner, 2026-08-29: "The suggestion of Vault-based dungeons." Sibling of
[[ASSAILANT_FLESH_DUNGEON_1]].

The Rakatan vaults are already canon anchors: sleeper caskets (the war
generation, ANCIENTS_AS_RAKATA_SPEC), the Forsaken Arsenal still fighting, and
now the vault-guardian turret tier (Singularity Cannon, GravRail Artillery
assigned "Forsaken vaults" on the turret canon). A vault dungeon composes
these into an explorable place: what a crew fights through, what wakes, and
what the vault was FOR. Design with the owner; mechanism candidates
(quests/sites/prefabs) come after the fiction.

## Sitting brief (BENCH draft 2026-08-30 — proposals for the owner, nothing ruled)

Canon already rules the triad (reconciled_lore/03_deep_history.md, owner
2026-08-15): ① mechanoid garrisons — the vault held; ② flesh weapons loose —
the vault fell (Anomaly toolbox NOT allowed here — the Assailant dungeon holds

The vault siege pieces are already canon turrets assigned "Forsaken vaults"
(grav rail artillery 31k, Singularity Cannon 72k — vault doors are guarded by
world-enders, which IS the promise of the loot).

### To rule at the sitting
1. COUNT + SITING: how many vault sites on the frozen world, which tiles
   (near/under the Rust Cathedral patch? nightside?), and the ①/②/③ mix —
   PROPOSAL: 5-7 sites, one ③ only, discovered not shown.
2. STRUCTURE: one map per vault vs multi-layer descent. PROPOSAL: single map,
   concentric — outer works (breached), garrison ring (the fight), core
   (the payoff), so a raid-in-and-leave is possible but the core costs.
3. PAYOFF LADDER per type: ① tech/parts (Forgotten Arsenal parts are
   incompatible with droids by canon — loot is materials/weapons, not chassis);
   ② nothing but survival + the route to ③ knowledge; ③ the scene — what CAN
   the player do with frozen Rakata? (wake/loot/leave — each a campaign-
   shaping choice; ties to sleeper-backstory sympathy arc).
4. GATING: ship draws toward vaults by canon (substrate kinship) — do vault
   discoveries ride the cradle-memory reveal channel like the Assailant
   dungeon? PROPOSAL: yes — one reveal economy, two dungeon families.
5. AUTHORING ROUTE: KCSG/layout templates via bridge (rimworld-layout-layers
   lint applies) vs hand-built once on the frozen map.
