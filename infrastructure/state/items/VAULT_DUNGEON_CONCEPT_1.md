# VAULT_DUNGEON_CONCEPT_1 — Forsaken-vault dungeons

Owner, 2026-08-29: "The suggestion of Vault-based dungeons." Sibling of
[[ASSAILANT_FLESH_DUNGEON_1]].

The Rakatan vaults are already canon anchors: sleeper caskets (the war
generation, ANCIENTS_AS_RAKATA_SPEC), the Forsaken Arsenal still fighting, and
now the vault-guardian turret tier (Singularity Cannon, GravRail Artillery
assigned "Forsaken vaults" on the turret canon). A vault dungeon composes
these into an explorable place: what a crew fights through, what wakes, and
what the vault was FOR. Design with the owner; mechanism candidates
(quests/sites/prefabs) come after the fiction.
