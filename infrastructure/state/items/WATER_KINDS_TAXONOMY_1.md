# WATER_KINDS_TAXONOMY_1 — many kinds of water, and the transmutations between them

Owner, 2026-09-06, verbatim in intent: *"Seems like we need to have a LOT of kinds of
water. Not just the original distilled, potable, fouled, or toxic. It depends what's in it,
and the transmutations necessary to turn one into another."*

## spec
- Inventory every water the sheets have already produced, with what is IN it:
  - **red water** (the Contagion): spores + alien toxins → sunned a day → potable.
  - **slime rain / slime flood** (the Slime): potable as-is; slime *water* counts as toxic
    water for distillation purposes (owner).
  - **the Sheen** (the Rot): fungal reproductive emission — not water; reclaims into the
    milk ponds (not-water).
  - **hydrocarbon rime / etchfall / the Unveiling's aftermath** (the Crags): chemical, not
    water; melt-trickle during warm pulses is the twilight's only liquid water.
  - **brine / hypersaline pools** (the Wasteland): dead-river concentrate, radiologically
    live; the brine batteries.
  - **fog** (the Shrubland): the stormwall's last clean water on the approach.
  - **the terminator seas**; **scald meltwater** off the peaks (R-H1/R-H7 — potable once
    cooled/sterilized); **dead-river termini**.
- Define the taxonomy as DATA (owner rule: rules must be data, not prose): a table of
  water kinds × contents × hazards × the transmutation that turns each into the next
  (sun, distillation, cooling, boiling, the Slime's rendering, the Rot's toxins…).
- Map onto the live water/hygiene systems in the stack before inventing item defs
  (verify what the stack already distinguishes — do not assume Bad Hygiene's four kinds
  are the ceiling).
- Every biome sheet's "uniquely available" water line then cites the taxonomy.

## verify
The table exists, is data, and every water mentioned in a sheet resolves to a row; the
owner has ruled the transmutation list.
