🔴 **"Reskin a terrestrial animal with glowing plates" is REJECTED as a
solution class, not just for these two rows.** Verbatim on Enhydriodon:
*"This is just some kind of lava beast, boring. Don't need."* On Gorilla:
*"Again, these 'make a terrestrial shape made out of glowing plates' isn't a
good solution."* Both rejected. In chat, generalized further: *"We shouldn't
just 'reskin terrestrials' anymore, it's not productive."* **Any future
redraw of a "too terrestrial" creature must find an actual different body
plan / silhouette, not a material reskin of the existing terrestrial shape.**

- **Enhydriodon** — REJECTED. Redraw needed; do not reuse the cracked-obsidian
  reskin approach.
- **Gorilla** — REJECTED. Same note as Enhydriodon.

- **AA_Atispec** — 🔴 **CANCELLED, not parked.** *"Revenant and Atispec are
