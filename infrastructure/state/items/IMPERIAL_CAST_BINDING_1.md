## spec
`src/Jawa/Inhabited/Defs/CastRosters/CastRoster_EMPIRE.xml` ships **26**
`Inhabited.CharacterDef`s across Ashgarrison, The Sunspire Annexe and Oxalate Watch. Its own
header (`:5-11`) records that xenotype, pawnKind and apparel are **deliberately absent**.
⇒ the named Imperial cast and the Imperial raid roster are currently two unrelated
populations, and a named Imperial can generate as anything.

Decide, and write into the roster generator's input rather than by hand:
- which of the four `Jawa_Empire_*` kinds each named character generates as;
- xenotype per character, against `canon.yml > empire.xenotype_mix`
  (Baseliner 0.411 · Echani 0.411 · Chiss 0.137 · Chadra-Fan 0.041) — ⛔ **not** against
  `faction_roster_v2.md:711`, which lost;
- apparel: whether named Imperials wear the stormtrooper set, the officer uniform, or
  neither.

⚠️ Depends on `IMPERIAL_RAID_ROSTER_1` — bind to the four kinds only once they have a spawn
route, or this binds to defs nothing else uses.

## verify
`CastRoster_EMPIRE.xml` carries a xenotype and a pawnKind on every one of the 26 entries,
and its header no longer says they are absent.

## criteria
A named Imperial met in the world looks like the Imperials that raid.

## ruling
🔴 **DECIDE, 2026-08-21 — DO NOT EXECUTE THIS AS WRITTEN. It is not an Empire problem.**

Binding the Empire's named cast to a xenotype would have been a lookup, not a guess — every
race string maps to a real def in the **2026-08-21 578-mod dump**. ⚠️ *Not* the
2026-08-15 dump, which has 251 XenotypeDefs and none of ours; the fresh one has 139 and
carries them all. **Reading the older dump nearly produced a "these xenotypes do not exist"
finding that was pure staleness.**

### 🔴 What stops it: `useFactionXenotypes` REJECTS an out-of-set xenotype

All four `Jawa_Empire_*` kinds carry `useFactionXenotypes: true`
(`JawaFactionRoster.xml:37,60,79,98`). Read from `PawnGenerator.cs`:

- `:1751` — **generation**: `if (kind.useFactionXenotypes && factionDef2?.xenotypeSet != null)`
  ⇒ the xenotype is drawn *from the faction's set*.
- `:518` — **validation**: a candidate pawn is **rejected** when
  `useFactionXenotypes && !faction.def.xenotypeSet.Contains(pawn.genes.Xenotype)`.

⇒ **A named Imperial whose race is outside the Empire's four-xenotype set cannot be
generated by those kinds, and cannot be reused under them either.** Six of the Empire's 25
are outside it: `Hussar` · `Muun` · `Umbaran` · `Ugnaught` · `Sith` · `savant caste`.
🔑 `request.ForcedXenotype` (`:511`) is the escape hatch, but it fights the kind's own set
rather than cooperating with it.

### ⇒ And the Empire is not unusual. Scanned all twelve casts:

| cast | named characters whose race their faction cannot generate |
|---|---|
| JUNKERS · BLACKSTAR | **10 each** |
| HUTT | 7 |
| EMPIRE · HOMESTEAD | 6 each |
| WILDSTEAM | 4 |
| JAWA · HELIX | 3 each |
| TUSKEN | 2 |

⭐ **51 of 269 — 19% of the authored cast.**
⚠️ **That figure is an upper bound, and honestly so.** It comes from a naive
prose→defName mapping, and two of the strings are not species at all — `savant caste` is an
Imperial rank and `labour-line` is a Helix vat caste. The exact count needs a per-string
pass. **The class of problem is confirmed on every faction; the number is not final.**

### The ruling

⛔ **This item is superseded.** Binding one faction's cast in isolation would settle a
design-wide mechanism question quietly, for a twelfth of the cast, which is exactly what
`CAST_RACE_AND_KIT_FIELDS_1` forbade when the owner left `xenotype` and `pawnKind`
deliberately unruled.

🔑 **The real question, and it is one question for all 269:** *is a named character a sample
from their faction's distribution, or an individual who overrides it?* The design already
answers it — §4 of `INHABITED_DESIGN.md` has named people **drift between factions** through
the displaced pool, so a Muun in Imperial service is the setting working, not a defect. ⇒ the
mechanism must let a named character keep their authored race, which means **not binding
them to a kind whose `useFactionXenotypes` is true.**

⚠️ **And one number wants the owner's eye rather than mine:** the Empire's matrix says
**41% Echani** and its named cast contains **zero** Echani. Named and generated Imperials
will look like two different peoples standing in the same room. That is a content
observation, not a bug, and it is his call whether it matters.
